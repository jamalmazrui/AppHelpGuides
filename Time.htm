Subscriptions and accounts

Access the TIME Digital Magazine

  • All subscribers can access the digital edition on an iOS device by downloading the TIME Magazine App from the App Store and signing in using the email address you used when you subscribed, or your TIME Account Number.
  • For USA/Canada, if you have a print or digital subscription you can go here​ to sign in and access your Digital Magazines, or click the Digital Magazine link in the Time.com site footer. ​
  • International subscribers should use one of the following links depending upon the address on your TIME subscription account: Asia, Pacific​ or Rest of World (excluding USA/Canada)
  • If it’s the first time you are accessing your Digital Library, or you have logged out from your library, the system will ask for your email and we will email you a direct link to your Library.
  • If you are having trouble signing in, please contact your local TIME Customer Service centre. See the tab Contact Us for details.

Changing Your Mailing or Email Address

US/Canada users can update both your email and mailing address online.

For your email address:

  • Log in to Manage Account.

  • On the left navigation bar, select “Add/Change My Email Address”

  • Enter your email address, then select “save”. For your mailing address:

  • Log in to Manage Account.

  • On the left navigation bar, select “Change My Address.”

  • Enter your new mailing address, then select “save.” Note:​ It takes 2-4 weeks for the change of address to take effect. If you have any trouble changing your email or mailing address, you may contact customer care.

If you are located outside of the US/Canada

Please visit our Online Customer Service Centre for Asia and Pacific​ or Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Latin America

Alternatively, you can email our subscription customer care for Asia and Pacific​ or Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Latin America

Note: It takes 2-4 weeks for a change of address to take effect.

Contact us

Reaching Customer Care

If you need additional help, please do not hesitate to contact our customer care team. To better assist you, please include your account number and/or mailing address in your inquiry to customer care.

For customers based in the United States, Puerto Rico​ or Canada, please contact customer care: time-customerservice@time.com.

You can also reach customer care at 1-800-843-8463.

Customer Care Hours of Operation

  • Monday – Friday: 8 a.m. - 10:30 p.m. ET
  • Saturday: 9 a.m. - 7 p.m. ET For customers based in Europe, the Middle East, Africa​ or Latin America, please contact customer care: time@subscription.co.uk.

For customers based in Asia​ and South Pacific, please contact customer care: time@timeasiasubs.com.

Creating a digital account and signing in

Creating a free digital account will allow you to access articles on TIME.com and give you the ability to update your account preferences whenever you like. ​ Additional benefits include:

  • The Brief newsletter, a daily roundup of the TIME’s top stories curated by editors
  • The ability to manage your digital account
  • Invitations to exclusive events
  • Special offers, including discounts at the TIME Cover Store To login to your existing account, please go here. If you wish to create and account one can do so here

You can reset your TIME.com password anytime under My Account.

  • If you have forgotten your password, you can reset it here. ​
  • If you have any difficulty receiving your password email, please: Check your “spam” folder, or if you use Gmail, check your “promotions” folder. ​ Check any other email inboxes associated with the email address you used to sign up for your account. ​ Add support@accounts.time.com to your address book or contacts then request another password reset email. If you are still unable to find your password email, you can contact us here.

Finding Your Print Subscription Account Number

US/Canada users you can find your print subscription account number on the mailing label of your magazine.

It is the 10 digits directly above your name following #TIM.

You can also look up your account number in Manage Account​ using your mailing address.

If you are located outside of the US/Canada you can find your 12 digit account number on your magazine wrapper above your address.

Managing Your Subscription

US/Canada users:​ Your print subscription comes with many additional benefits that allow you to keep up with current news digitally, including:

  • Instant access to TIME digital magazines​ while you wait for your first issue
  • The Brief & Inside TIME newsletter
  • Access to TIME for Health​ digital wellness magazine collection
  • Special offers, including discounts at the TIME Cover Store
  • Access to The Vault—our digital archive of past magazine issues Your print subscription will automatically renew annually from the date of the first issue you receive.

You can make changes to your personal preferences, including mailing address, billing information and vacation pause in Manage Account.​

If you are located outside of the US/Canada

Please visit our Online Customer Service Centre for Asia and Pacific​ or Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Latin America

Alternatively, you can email our subscription customer care for Asia and Pacific​ or Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Latin America

Note:​ It takes 2-4 weeks for a change of address to take effect.

Print Magazine Delivery

  • When to expect your first issue: Issue delivery times will depend on location. United States: It will take 4-6 weeks for your first print issue of the magazine to arrive.

Canada: It will take 6-7 weeks for your first print issue of the magazine to arrive.

Other countries: It will take 4-6 weeks for your first print issue of the magazine to arrive.

  • Request a replacement issue: If you have missed multiple issues, to confirm your subscription or to receive replacement issues, contact customer care.​ Please let us know the last issue you received or the issue that you are missing, if you know it.
