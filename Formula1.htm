Formula 1 half-answered — and I found why the repeats kept happening

Its help is Salesforce, not Zendesk, and four settled estates were never marked settled

2026-08-04

What was new

Only Formula 1. The Accessibility Insights archive is byte-identical to the one I built from this morning, and Fantasy Premier League, Pandora and ScribeMe are the same estates gathered yet again.

Shopify did not arrive, and no getBrowserHelp.log came with the upload. If that run is still going, or if it failed, the log would say which.

The repeats were my fault, and they are fixed

I have been telling you the harvester was out of date. It was not — I never marked four estates settled. Fantasy Premier League, Pandora, ScribeMe and MLB were all built or refused days ago, and every one of them was still flagged open in the configuration, so every plain run dutifully gathered them again.

That is why the same four archives have kept arriving. Version 29 marks all four with their reasons, so a plain run now skips sixteen estates rather than twelve.

I should have caught this when the second identical upload arrived. Instead I blamed your copy of the script twice.

Formula 1 — half an answer, and a fault of mine underneath

The render worked and produced something genuinely useful: the whole contents list, six categories deep — Get Started, Watch F1 TV, Manage Account, Manage Subscriptions, Fix a Problem, and the F1 TV plan comparisons. Location availability, supported devices, pairing to a TV, streaming quality, commentary language, error codes, payment problems, cancelling and pausing a subscription.

But not one article address is in the document. Thirty-one links, and they are stylesheets, media, the cookie policy and six other-language editions. The article titles are drawn as text and their addresses arrive when a reader clicks — the Duolingo, Audible and Whole Foods shape.

And the address shape misled me again

f1help.formula1.com/hc/en-us answers “Invalid Page”. The /hc/ path was mine, not Formula 1’s — I read a Zendesk shape into a redirect that had dropped its path. What the estate actually runs on is Salesforce Lightning: its markup is full of Lightning design-system stylesheets and /sfsites/ media.

That is the third time an address shape has misled me about a publishing system, after the New York Times and Curiosity Stream. The rule I keep writing down — read the page, not the address — I keep needing.

One short run settles it either way

Version 29 seeds the host root rather than the invented /hc/ path, and fences out the six other-language editions:

python getBrowserHelp.py formula1

If the root renders its links, Formula 1 becomes a volume of perhaps sixty articles. If it renders the same contents list with no addresses, it is a refusal — and the sports question closes for good, since every other league has already answered nothing.

I would not normally ask for a second attempt at something that looks like a refusal. I am asking here because the page I read was reached through a redirect from an address I got wrong, so it is not a fair test of the estate.
