Account Support

Can I use a different email for Patreon than my Vox Membership?

Yes. You can use a different email address for each account.

When you create or manage your Vox.com or Patreon memberships, you can choose whichever email you prefer on each platform.

Can I use an existing Patreon account to join Vox?

Yes.

If you’re already an annual Vox.com member, you can redeem your free year of Vox on Patreon using your existing Patreon account.

If you already have a Patreon account, you can also become a patron of Vox by navigating to Patreon.com/vox directly through that same account—no need to create a new one.

How do I link my Vox and Patreon accounts?

If you’re a paying member on Vox’s Patreon, go to the Memberships tab on Patreon to find a link that lets you redeem your Vox.com membership.

If you’re an annual Vox.com member, check your email for a special link to redeem your free year of Vox on Patreon.

Please note that monthly Vox.com members who joined after November 17, 2025 aren’t eligible for Patreon access.

If you would like to upgrade to an annual subscription to receive access to Patreon, please reach out to customer support at membership@vox.com.

How do refunds or payment issues work?

For refunds related to your Vox membership, please see our refund policy here.

For any questions related to Patreon refunds, please follow the refund policy highlighted here.

I connected the wrong Patreon account — how do I fix it?

If you linked the wrong Patreon account, please contact our customer service team for help. Include the following information in your message:

  • The email address of the Patreon account you redeemed your link with
  • The email address of the Patreon account you intended to use
  • The email address associated with your Vox.com membership You’ll need to be able to verify ownership of all email addresses before we can make any changes.

I just cancelled my patreon membership, what happens to my Vox.com membership?

If you signed up first through Patreon, then your Vox.com membership expires the day you cancelled your membership.

You can renew your Vox.com access by either resubscribing on Patreon or by subscribing through Vox here.

I’m having trouble signing in — what should I do?

First, confirm you’re using the same email address associated with your Vox Membership.

If issues persist, contact Vox Support for assistance here.

Who should I contact for billing or technical support — Vox or Patreon?

For Vox Membership billing, contact Vox Support here.

For Patreon payment issues, log in to Patreon and use their Help Center.

Ad-Free Podcasts

Do I need to change feeds to get ad-free podcasts?

Yes, you will need to add the ad-free feed and remove the existing feed that includes ads. You can differentiate between the ad-free and regular feed through our custom thumbnail art,

Do I need to individually add the ad-free version of each of my Vox podcasts?

Yes. While the setup process is the same for all Vox podcasts, you must individually select and set up each ad-free podcast you want to listen to.

How can I listen to my ad-free podcasts? (Desktop Version)

To access ad-free podcasts, you must be a Vox member.

  • If you’re already a member, simply sign in and skip to Step 2 If you’re not a Vox member yet, follow these steps to get started:
  1. Become a Vox Member

Visit vox.com/support-now to become a member. Once you complete the signup process and select your monthly or annual membership plan, you’ll be directed to a confirmation screen.

  1. Access Your Podcast Page

Go to your account page and navigate to the podcasts section. This page should resemble the example shown below.

  1. Set Up Your Podcast

Click the “Set Up” button next to the podcast you’d like to listen to without ads.

Please note that you will need to set up the feed individually for each podcast you want to listen to ad-free.

  1. Choose Your Listening Method

On this page, you can either:

- Scan the QR code to set up your ad-free feed directly on your phone, or send the RSS feed to your phone via an SMS link for easy setup.
- Directly integrate the ad-free feed with Spotify, Apple Podcasts or YouTube Music
- Copy the RSS feed and manually add it to your preferred podcast app
  1. Start Listening!

Your feed will appear under a new icon for you in your app library. Be sure to delete the regular version if you were previously subscribed, as all of your episodes will now come into the premium ad-free feed.

Cancel Membership

Cancellation Policy

Memberships to Vox.com may be canceled at any time. You may cancel your membership through the account page or by reaching out to membership@vox.com directly.

Members will maintain access to all membership content until the end of their current billing cycle. The membership will not automatically renew and digital access will expire on the last day of the monthly or annual plan. To request a refund, please see our refund policies.

How to cancel a membership online?

In order to cancel a membership online, please follow the steps below:

  • Visit vox.com/account/membership
  • Click on manage my membership
  • In the newly opened webpage, click on cancel plan
  • On the next page, confirm this decision by clicking cancel plan again.

How to Cancel Membership With Customer Support

If you do not see the option to cancel your membership on your account page, you may send a request to cancel to Membership@vox.com. Please include your full name and/or your email address and account number when sending your request.

Current Vox Members

Do I already have access to Vox on Patreon?

All active Vox Members as of November 17, 2025, and all new annual members thereafter, receive one year of complimentary Patreon access.

Access is free at launch, and any future changes will be communicated in accordance with our Terms of Use policy.

How do I redeem or activate my complimentary Patreon access?

If you’re a Vox Member, you should have received an email with instructions to activate your complimentary Patreon access.

  • Click the link in that email to redeem your free year.
  • Already have a Patreon account? Sign in using that email.
  • Don’t have one yet? Create an account using the same email where you received your Vox offer. Can’t find the email? Check your spam folder or contact membership@vox.com and we’ll resend your link.

Once activated, you’ll get access to Vox’s exclusive Patreon posts, videos, and other member benefits — free for one year.

I just upgraded from a monthly membership to an annual membership, how do I redeem my patreon benefits?

Please reach out to our support team here to request your Patreon link.

If I cancel my Vox Membership, will I lose Patreon access?

Yes. Patreon access is tied to your Vox Membership. Once a membership ends, complimentary Patreon access will automatically be removed.

What if I didn’t receive my Patreon activation email?

If you’re an active Vox Member and haven’t received your activation email within 24 hours of launch or signing up for your membership, check your spam or promotions folder.

Still can’t find it? Reach out to Vox support here, and we’ll resend your activation link right away.

What’s included with my Patreon access, and how long does it last?

Before November 17, 2025:

All existing Vox.com members automatically received one year of Patreon access as part of their membership.

Starting November 17, 2025:

Members who sign up directly on Patreon.com/Vox, or those who sign up for annual subscriptions on Vox.com have access to Vox on patreon.

If you are a Vox member on a monthly plan who would like to upgrade to an annual subscription to receive access to Patreon, please reach out to customer support at membership@vox.com.

Patreon access includes:

  • Monthly deep-dive
  • Live roundtable events with Vox journalists and guests
  • Behind-the-scenes reporter extras and subscriber mailbags
  • Quick takes on major news moments
  • Ad-free video collections

Will I be billed separately for Patreon?

No. Your complimentary Patreon access is included in your Vox Membership at no extra cost.

General Overview

Can I support Vox without joining Patreon?

Absolutely. You can continue supporting Vox through your existing Vox Membership program.

How does Vox on Patreon work with my Vox Membership?

Patreon is an extension of Vox Membership, not a replacement.

If you’re already a Vox Member, you’ll continue to enjoy everything you have today — unlimited access to Vox.com, The Highlight magazine, ad-free podcasts, and exclusive newsletters.

Patreon simply adds more:

  • Monthly deep-dive videos (The Docket, What’s Working)
  • Monthly livestreams
  • Weekly Reporter extras and behind-the-scenes moments
  • Ad-free video collections
  • Access to community chats and reporter interactions.

What is Vox on Patreon?

Vox on Patreon is a new home for exclusive Vox videos, ad-free podcasts, and behind-the-scenes updates.

It’s where you can support our journalism directly while getting even closer to the stories and creators you love. Members will gain access to brand-new video series, weekly reporter extras, monthly live events, and community features that bring you inside our newsroom and reporting process.

What kind of content will I find on Vox’s Patreon?

You’ll find a mix of exclusive videos, podcasts, and interactive community experiences, including:

  • Monthly deep-dive video explainers like The Docket (on Supreme Court decisions) or What’s Working (on policy solutions making progress)
  • Weekly Reporter Extras — short, timely dispatches and behind-the-scenes clips from Vox journalists
  • Ad-free podcast feeds and curated video collections
  • Monthly livestreams and AMAs with Vox hosts and reporters
  • Member-only community posts and chats

Why is Vox launching on Patreon?

Patreon allows Vox to offer video-first and community-driven experiences for our most passionate supporters. It helps us deepen relationships with our audience while providing new, sustainable ways to fund explanatory journalism.

Will my Vox Membership benefits change with this launch?

No. Your Vox Membership stays the same — you still get everything you enjoy today. Patreon adds extra benefits for eligible members: exclusive videos, live events, community access, and reporter extras.

Will Patreon replace Vox’s YouTube or podcast channels?

No. Patreon adds to — not replaces — our existing channels. You’ll still be able to watch Vox videos on YouTube and listen to our podcasts everywhere you usually do.

Patreon simply offers more: exclusive series, ad-free listening, live stream events, and behind-the-scenes content you can’t find anywhere else.

Gift Links

Can others share my gift link, and how do I track usage?

Yes. Gift links work no matter who shares them, as long as the link hasn’t expired.

Each time you click the gift icon, you’ll see how many gift links you have remaining.Your allowance resets on the 1st of every month, and unused gift links do not roll over.

How do I create and share a gift link?

Open any Vox.com article and click the gift icon in the article lead.

A popover will confirm your link is copied and show how many gift links you have left. You can share the link anywhere—text, email, social media, forums, and more.

What are gift links and who can use them?

Gift links let paying Vox.com members share up to 10 articles per month with non-members.

Each gift link makes the article free to read for 14 days. Only logged-in Vox.com members can create them.

Vox History

When Vox was founded in 2014, it was animated by a simple observation: The media did a good job of reporting the news and commenting on it, but there was a disconnect between that work and the audience truly understanding why something happened. Vox started as — and remains — an organization dedicated to addressing that gap, which not only persists but has grown. We are proud to have popularized explanatory journalism in many forms, across many mediums.

Our work has been used to educate people everywhere from elementary schools to college classrooms to vaccination sites in Taiwan to footnotes in congressional memos. We’ve received numerous awards, including News and Documentary Emmys, Online Journalism Awards, and Loeb Awards. We were a finalist for the Pulitzer Prize for our collaboration with ProPublica on maternal mortality. Read more about what we’ve been up to in our press room.

Vox’s staff of over 100 journalists and subject-matter experts research, report and produce articles, videos, and podcasts that make complex ideas accessible. Whether it’s policy, culture, the Supreme Court, meatless meat, political extremism, biodiversity, artificial intelligence, the climate crisis, or something else, we give context to what’s happening today. Our goal is to ensure that everyone, regardless of income or status, can access accurate information that empowers them.
