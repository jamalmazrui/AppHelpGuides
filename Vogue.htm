Managing your Vogue account

Account Status

Account Status

Not sure when your subscription expires? If your payment has been received? Click here to check your account status online! As always, if you do not find the information you are looking for, please contact us by e-mail, phone, or mail.

•

Billing/Payment

Billing/Payment

If you would like to pay your bill in full using your credit card, click here to go to our secure payment page. You may also call 1-800-234-2347 if you prefer to pay over the phone.

Payments may also be mailed to:

Vogue, PO Box 37686, Boone, IA 50037-0686

If you would like to check your payment status, please click here.

If you have already paid your bill, but are still receiving invoices, please keep in mind that it can take up to two weeks for us to receive and process your payment. If it has been less than two weeks since you sent your payment, please disregard any invoices that you receive, as we may be unable to intercept them in the mail. If it has been more than two weeks since you remitted payment, please contact us by e-mail, phone, or mail.

•

Cancellation

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription online by clicking here . You may also contact us by e-mail , phone, or mail. We will promptly stop your service and issue a refund for any unmailed copies.

If you canceled your subscription, but are still receiving bills, please note that it takes a few days for a cancellation to become effective. Therefore, if you receive any invoices after you have canceled, please ignore them.

If you require further assistance, please contact us by e-mail, phone, or mail.

•

Change of Address

Change of Address

US & Canadian subscribers:

You can quickly and easily change your address online by clicking here! You may also contact us by e-mail, phone, or mail.

Foreign Subscribers:

You may contact us by e-mail, phone, or mail.

Subscribers: If you notify us that your subscription is undeliverable or the Post Office alerts us that your magazine is undeliverable, we have no further obligation unless we receive a corrected address and/or email within one year.

Vogue’s regular annual rate is currently $84.00. Vogue publishes monthly, except for two planned combined issues and other combined issues which count as two, as indicated on the issue’s cover.

Late or Missed Issues

Late or Missed Issues

If you have missed an issue, we may have an incorrect address on file. Please click here to verify your address. Your magazines may also have stopped being delivered due to nonpayment. Click here to check the payment status on your account. If you would like to pay in full for your subscription, please click here to go to our secure payment page.

Vogue Digital subscriptions

How can I cancel my subscription? (Vogue Digital subscriptions)

You can cancel you subscription by clicking here. If you canceled your subscription, but are still receiving bills, please note that it takes a few days for a cancellation to become effective. Therefore, if you receive any invoices after you have canceled, please disregard them. If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Digital Customer Service toll-free at 1-877-791-9274 or email us.

How can I contact the editor of Vogue Digital Magazine?

Send us your comments, questions, and suggestions. If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Digital Customer Service toll-free at 1-877-791-9274 or email us.

How can I make payments or check the status of a payment? (Vogue Digital subscriptions)

You can make a payment and/or check the status of a payment. If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Digital Customer Service toll-free at 1-877-791-9274 or email us.

How can I renew my Vogue Digital Magazine subscription?

From time to time, you will receive a mailed notification regarding your subscription renewal. You can also renew online by clicking here. If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Digital Customer Service toll-free at 1-877-791-9274 or email us.

How do I change my Customer Care login? (Vogue Digital subscriptions)

To update the email address and password you use as your Customer Care login, please contact Vogue Digital Customer Service toll-free, at 1-877-791-9274.

Where can I find my expiration date? (Vogue Digital subscriptions)

View the expiration date of your subscription, e-mail us, or contact Customer Care at 1-877-791-9274 for further assistance.

Why do I get billed after I have already sent payment? (Vogue Digital subscriptions)

If you have already paid your bill, but are still receiving invoices, please keep in mind that it can take up to two weeks for us to receive and process your payment. If it has been less than two weeks since you sent your payment, please disregard any invoices that you receive, as we may be unable to intercept them in the mail. If it has been more than two weeks since you remitted payment, please check your last payment date. If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Digital Customer Service toll-free at 1-877-791-9274 or email us.

Why must I enter my email address? (Vogue Digital subscriptions)

In order for us to serve you better, from time to time we will ask for your email address. Providing us with your email address will allow you to receive important messages from Vogue Digital regarding your subscription. Check the email address where you will receive your subscription notices. If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Digital Customer Service toll-free, at 1-877-791-9274, or email us.

One of my Vogue issues was never delivered. What should I do?

Vogue Magazine is published 12 times a year and your first issue will arrive between 6 - 8 weeks of order receipt. Thereafter, you should receive each issue a week prior to the cover date (for example, your May issue will arrive the last week in April). For subscribers outside of the United States, each issue may deliver up to two weeks after the cover date. You can check the most recent issues our records show that you should have received.

If your issue frequently arrives damaged and your mailbox is sufficiently sized, then check with the post office to see if the magazine arrives in good condition.

If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Customer Service toll-free at 1-800-234-2347 or email us.

Where can I find my expiration date? (Vogue print subscriptions)

The month end expiration date of your subscription can be found on your magazine label above your name. For example, February 2011 is represented as FEB11, and the double issue July/August 2011 is represented as JUL/AUG 11. You can also view your expiration information. If you have questions or need further assistance, contact Vogue Customer Service toll-free at 1-800-234-2347 or email us.
