PiccyBot

A small app worth having, and the manual is inside it

2026-08-03

PiccyBot

The right app to ask for. PiccyBot by Martijn van der Spek (Sparkling Apps), free on iOS, iPadOS, macOS and Android, built expressly for blind and low-vision users: it turns a photo or a video into a spoken description, takes follow-up questions, and accepts anything shared to it from another app.

He also wrote Talking Goggles, which the community knew before this. PiccyBot is the more capable of the two, and it is genuinely a described-media tool rather than an object recogniser — which is why it sits differently from Seeing AI. The community’s own comparison is that Seeing AI feels like watching the video while PiccyBot feels like someone reporting back on it.

What it actually publishes, and it is not much

  • piccybot.com — the official site. A plain request gets a title, a meta-description and nothing else. The whole site is drawn in the browser.
  • sparklingapps.com/piccybot-privacy.html and piccybot-eula.html — ordinary HTML, gatherable, and legal rather than instructional.
  • The App Store and Play listings — the feature list and, more usefully, a detailed version history in the developer’s own words.
  • A PDF manual for all PiccyBot features, added in version 1.76 in December

The manual is inside the app, and only you can fetch it

That is the whole story. The release note reads: “Included a pdf manual for all PiccyBot features to download and read at your leisure.” It is offered from within the app, and I can find no public address for it anywhere on the web.

This is the SoundForge situation again: the documentation exists, it is substantial, and it is a file rather than a website. Open PiccyBot, download the manual, and send it to me — that one file is almost certainly the whole guide, and everything else here is a footnote to it.

What I have set up meanwhile

getBrowserHelp.py version 9 carries a piccybot config: the drawn site plus the two legal pages in one run. 33 of 33 predicates pass, including two new ones that refuse piccybot.apps112.com and piccybot.soft112.com — download aggregators that mirror the developer’s text and are third parties under the scope rule.

Those aggregators are worth a word of warning. Reading one of them is how I found the official site and the manual’s existence, so they are useful for reconnaissance. But one of them files PiccyBot under “Business / PIMS & Calendars”, reports 35 downloads, and attributes it to a company in Kerala rather than to its Dutch author. An aggregator is a lead, never a source.

One scope question for you

The version history — twenty-odd releases, each with the developer’s own note about what changed — is real documentation of how the app works and when each feature arrived. “Copy and paste an image from Facebook or Instagram into PiccyBot” is a feature you would want to know about, and the release note is where it is explained.

But it is published on Apple’s and Google’s stores rather than on the developer’s own site. Under the scope rule that is the publisher’s own words hosted elsewhere, which I would normally keep — the same way Microsoft’s help is kept when it documents MSN.

Say whether you want it. If yes, it becomes a “What changed and when” category at the end of the guide, in the developer’s words, and it will roughly double a thin volume. If no, the guide is the manual and the legal pages.

Summary

  • PiccyBot confirmed and worth a guide: an AI photo and video describer written for this community, on iOS, macOS and Android.
  • Its website is drawn rather than served, so it needs the browser leg — getBrowserHelp.py v9 carries it, 33 of 33 predicates pass.
  • The real documentation is a PDF manual inside the app. Please download it from PiccyBot and send it; that file is the guide.
  • Download aggregators are refused as third parties — and one of them files this app under “PIMS & Calendars” and credits the wrong company. An aggregator is a lead, never a source.
  • One question for you: whether the developer’s version history, published on the app stores, belongs in the guide.
