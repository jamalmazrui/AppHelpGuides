Linktree Help Guide

This guide gathers Linktree’s official help centre. The text was taken from linktr.ee on 31 July 2026 and is Linktree’s own; nothing has been summarised or rewritten.

Linktree is the page people put behind a single address so a résumé, a portfolio, a shop and a handful of social accounts can all be reached from one link – on a profile, a business card, or the back of a programme. It is edited in a browser and in apps for iPhone and Android; there is no Windows program and no Alexa skill.

The sections are Linktree’s own collections, in alphabetical order with Miscellaneous last. Most were read from the collection pages themselves, which name the articles they hold; the rest from the link each article carries back to its collection.

A note on how this was gathered, because the first attempt found almost nothing. Linktree’s collection pages are built by script from a block of data inside the page, and every article address lives in that block rather than in an ordinary link. A first run that read only the links came away with four pages; the how-to collection alone names ninety-eight articles and none of them was found. Reading the whole page rather than only its links found them all.

One group needed a judgement. Linktree’s Social Planner articles – the scheduling tool it bought as Plann – belong to a collection whose own page was not among the pages gathered, so they arrived with nothing attached. They are gathered under a section of that name, which is editorial rather than Linktree’s own wording.

Illustrations do not survive as pictures; where one carried a description it is kept as a short italic note. Tables have become lists. The rating strip at the foot of every article, and the related-article panel, have been removed.

Headings run six deep at most. Level one is this title, level two is a section, level three is an article, and anything below that came from inside the article itself.

Can I change the Linktree URL to a custom domain?

Linktree makes it easy to share all your content with one link. Our simple tools let you build your page, customize themes, and add social icons—creating a space that showcases your online ecosystem. Each Linktree page uses a unique linktr.ee/username URL.

We don’t offer the option to use a custom domain to replace the linktr.ee URL. We also don’t provide developer tools for setting up domain forwarding.

If you’d prefer to direct people to your own website, a Redirect link might be the right solution.

To successfully connect your account, your Instagram must be a Creator or Business account.

How to check your Instagram account type

  1. Head to your Instagram profile on the app.
  2. Click on Edit Profile.
  3. If there’s an option to Switch to a professional account, you’re currently using a personal account and will need to switch to a Creator or Business account.

How to switch your Instagram account type

A. Switch your Personal account to a Creator account:

  1. Head to your Instagram profile on the app.
  2. Tap on Edit Profile.
  3. Choose Switch to a professional account and follow the on-screen steps.
  4. Pick a category that best describes you and then select Done.
  5. Select account type as Creator and then select Next.
  6. You’re all set! Your Instagram account is now a Creator account.

B. Switch your Personal account to a Business account:

  1. Head to your Instagram profile on the app.
  2. Tap on Edit Profile.
  3. Choose Switch to a professional account and follow the on-screen steps.
  4. Pick a category that best describes your business and then select Done.
  5. Select account type as Business and then select Next.
  6. Review your contact info and select Next.
  7. You’ve now converted your Instagram account to a Business type. ​

FAQs

Will I need a different login for each Linktree?

No, you can manage all your profiles using the same login method as your first Linktree.

What if I want to add a Linktree using a different email address?

If you’d like to create a Linktree account with a different email, log out before creating your new Linktree.

How do I manage multiple Linktrees?

If you’ve created more than one Linktree using the same email address, you can manage them all under one login. Just use the Switch Linktrees option in your account menu to toggle between profiles. Each Linktree stays separate, so you can customize and update them individually.

How to delete your Linktree account or profile

While we’re sorry to see you leave, the process to delete your Linktree accour or profile is straightforward. Keep in mind—this action will permanently remove your profile(s), links, and data.

💡 Tip: Don’t want to delete your Linktree entirely? You can cancel your paid plan instead.

⚠️ Important before you continue

Deleting your account or a profile is permanent. Once deleted:

  • Your Linktree URL will be deactivated.
  • Your profile(s), links, and data cannot be recovered.
  • You won’t be able to use the same username or email for 14 days.

How to delete a single Linktree profile

If you have multiple profiles under one Linktree account, you can delete just one without closing your entire account.

  1. Go to your Account settings in the Linktree Admin.
  2. Under the Account management section, find the profile you want to delete.
  3. Click the three dots (•••) to open the menu.
  4. Click Delete Linktree.
  5. Review the pop-up confirmation message.
  6. Click Continue to confirm deletion of that profile.

📝 Note: This action cannot be undone. Only the selected profile will be deleted; other profiles in your account will remain active.

How to delete your entire Linktree account and all profiles

  1. Go to your Account settings in the Linktree Admin.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the page to the Delete forever section.
  3. Click Delete account.
  4. Read the pop-up message carefully.
  5. When you’re ready, click Continue to confirm.

Protect your Linktree account with MFA (Multi-factor Authentication)

In today’s digital-first world, data is everywhere—accessed, used, and shared at an incredible pace. With that comes a rise in cyber threats, and at Linktree, we’re dedicated to protecting you and your content.

What is MFA?

Multi-factor Authentication (MFA) is a security feature that adds an extra layer of protection to your account. It requires more than just your username and password to verify your identity, making it harder for bad actors to gain access.

Why enable MFA?

MFA significantly reduces the risk of cyber-attacks. By adding another step to the login process, you’re safeguarding your Linktree account against unauthorized access.

MFA Options

When enabling MFA, you can choose between these two options:

  1. SMS Verification (Available in United States, Australia, United Kingdom and Canada only) With this option, you’ll receive a 6-digit verification code via text or voice call whenever you log in. It’s a straightforward choice for those who prefer simplicity and already have their phone handy.
  2. Authenticator App Use an app like Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator to generate time-based one-time codes. This method doesn’t rely on SMS, offering an added layer of security and flexibility, especially if you’re traveling or have limited cell service.

  1. Head to the My Account page in your Linktree Admin and scroll down to the Security and privacy section.

  2. Select SMS or an Authenticator App as your preferred MFA option.

  3. Tap Add and follow the steps to set it up.

  4. For SMS: Enter your country code and phone number. You’ll receive a 6-digit verification code via text or voice call.

  5. For Authenticator App: Follow the app instructions to link it to your account. 📝 If you’re not able to scan the code, tap Trouble Scanning? to receive a manual code that you may enter into your authenticator app.

That’s it! Next time you log in, you’ll need to enter a verification code to secure your account.

Why do I see a $1 charge after subscribing to Linktree?

If you’ve recently subscribed to a Linktree plan, you might notice a $1 pre-authorization charge on your credit card statement. Don’t worry—this isn’t an actual charge, and it will be reversed automatically.

What is a pre-authorization charge?

A pre-authorization charge is not actually a charge but a temporary hold placed by your bank to verify that your card is valid. This process helps ensure your payment method is active before any actual charges are made. Notably, the $1 hold is held by the bank and never collected by Linktree.

How long does a pre-authorization charge last?

The time it takes for the charge to be removed from your statement depends on your bank. In most cases, it will be removed within a few minutes to a few days, but in some cases, it may take up to a few weeks. If the charge is not removed within a reasonable time, we recommend contacting your bank for more details.

It’s also important to note that:

  • If the full transaction amount is successfully processed before the hold period ends, the pre-authorization charge will be removed from your statement and replaced by the full transaction amount.
  • If the full transaction amount is not successfully processed then the $1 pre-authorization charge will be released and returned to your account.

Your Linktree isn’t verified as your website

Stripe needs to know what you’re offering and confirm that your site is real.

Incomplete or mismatched information

  • Your ID is blurry, expired, or unsupported.
  • Proof of address doesn’t match what’s been submitted.
  • Bank account details don’t match the account holder info.
Missing beneficial ownership info

If your business has multiple owners, Stripe may need ID documents for anyone who owns or controls the business.

How to write great Link titles

Your Link Titles are the first thing people see - so make them count. A strong title can boost clicks, build trust, and guide your visitors to take action.

Here are three tips to help your Link titles stand out.

1. Make it clear

Your audience should instantly know what the link is about. Clarity builds trust and increases clicks.

If you’re promoting a link on social media, use similar wording on your Linktree. This keeps your message consistent and easier to follow.

2. Add a call-to-action

Call-to-actions (CTAs) prompt your audience to do something—click, watch, shop, or explore.

Start with a verb that matches the action you want them to take. Instead of a generic title like YouTube, try Watch my latest YouTube video.

💡 Tip: Make sure your CTA reflects what your audience will see or do after clicking.

3. Focus on your audience

Use titles that speak directly to your visitors’ needs or interests. The more relevant your title feels, the more likely they are to engage.

Make your Linktree private with Profile Lock (Beta)

Available on Starter, Pro and Premium plans

🧪 Beta feature: This feature is currently in development and isn’t available to all Linkers just yet. We’re working hard to roll it out to everyone soon.

Profile Lock lets you gate your entire Linktree behind an access requirement of your choosing, whether that’s a password, a code, or an email opt-in. Instead of locking individual links, it puts a single access gate over your whole Linktree, so you control exactly who sees your content and when.

What Profile Lock does

Anyone who visits a locked Linktree sees a lock screen and no content until they meet the condition you set.

You can choose from three lock types:

  • Password: Visitors enter a password you set.
  • Code: Visitors enter a 4-digit code you set.
  • Subscribe: Visitors enter their email to unlock, and you capture it as a lead in your Audience.

How to turn on Profile Lock

  1. Head to the Profile Access section in your Settings.
  2. Turn on Profile Lock.
  3. Choose a lock type: Password, Code, or Subscribe.
  4. Enter the value for your chosen lock type, or add a description if you picked Subscribe, then save your changes.
  5. Optionally add a custom lock screen message.

What visitors see

A visitor who lands on your locked Linktree sees a lock screen instead of your content. No links, social icons, or description load until they satisfy your lock condition.

For a Subscribe lock, a new visitor enters their email, completes a captcha, and agrees to the terms. Once they submit, your full Linktree loads for them.

Returning visitors who’ve already unlocked your Linktree don’t need to do this again right away. We store a cookie in their browser so they’re let straight through on their next visit.

FAQ

How is Profile Lock different from Link Lock?

Link Lock restricts access to a single link on your Linktree, using a Code, Age, Subscribe, or NFT Lock. Profile Lock restricts access to your entire Linktree, so nothing renders until a visitor meets your condition.

What happens to my Profile Lock settings if I downgrade to Free?

Your lock is removed and your Linktree becomes public again. You’ll need to upgrade and set up Profile Lock again if you want to re-enable it.

Do visitors have to unlock my Linktree every time they visit?

No. Once a visitor unlocks your Linktree, we store a cookie in their browser for 30 days so they don’t have to unlock it again during that window.

Use Instagram auto-reply to automatically share links with your audience

Instagram auto-reply let you share a link directly with anyone who comments on your Instagram post.   1. Go to the Instagram auto-reply page in your Linktree admin.
  2. Connect your Instagram account (if not already connected) by clicking Connect Instagram and following the prompts.
  3. Tap + Create an auto-reply.
  4. Choose the Instagram content that will trigger the DM:
  • One specific post or Reel: Send the DM when a specific post or Reel is commented on.
  • All posts or Reels (Available on Pro and Premium plans): Trigger the DM for any comment on any post or Reel.
  • Next post or Reel (Available on Pro and Premium plans): Apply the DM automatically to the next post or Reel you publish.
  1. Tap Next.
  2. Choose how the DM will be triggered:
  • Any comment: Send the DM whenever any comment is made.
  • Only comments with specific keywords: Send the DM when a comment contains specific words (e.g., “enter” for a giveaway). You’ll be asked to select the keywords.

  1. Tap Next.

Troubleshooting

Auto-replies aren’t sending

If people comment on your post or Reel but don’t receive a DM:

  1. Check that your Instagram account is public.

Instagram auto-replies only work with public accounts. 2. Confirm the correct post or Reel is selected. Make sure you’ve set the auto-reply to the right post, Reel, or to “All posts or Reels.” 3. Ensure the DM trigger is set correctly. Double-check if it’s set to “Any comment” or “Only comments with specific keywords.”

  1. 💡 Tip: If using keywords, make sure your followers are commenting with those exact words or use Smart match to catch variations (Available on Pro and Premium plans).
  2. Verify that your DM limit hasn’t been reached.

Plans have monthly DM limits. Go to the Instagram auto-reply page in your admin to check your usage. 5. Re-authenticate your Instagram connection.

If the connection between Linktree and Instagram was interrupted, disconnect and reconnect your Instagram account. You can do this from the Instagram auto-reply page in your Linktree admin.

My followers see a “Get Updates” button instead of the link

If someone opens your DM on a desktop browser, Instagram may show a “Get Updates” button instead of your custom button label.

📝 Note: The link will still work when they click the button, even if the label appears differently. To see your original button text, followers can open the message in the Instagram app on mobile.

Tax on Linktree plans in the U.S. and Canada

If you’re located in the United States or Canada, your Linktree plan price will show applicable taxes added at checkout. These may include:

  • U.S.: Sales tax
  • Canada: GST/HST, QST, and PST

📝 Note: If you’re outside the U.S. or Canada, this change doesn’t affect you.

Why is tax added?

Even though Linktree Pty Ltd is an Australian entity, we’re required to collect taxes in certain U.S. states and Canadian provinces where digital services are taxed. This helps us stay compliant with local tax laws.

When will the change take effect?

We’re introducing tax-exclusive pricing in stages. Here’s what to expect:

New subscriptions
  • U.S.: New plans will continue to be tax-exclusive.
  • Canada: From December 1, 2025, new plans will move to tax-exclusive pricing.
Existing subscriptions
  • Monthly plans in the U.S. and Canada: If your subscription was set up as tax-inclusive, it will switch to tax-exclusive on December 1, 2025.
  • Annual U.S. plans: These will become tax-exclusive at renewal from December 1, 2025.

📝 Note: Once your plan switches to tax-exclusive, your invoice will show tax as a separate line.

Will this affect what I pay?

Possibly. Your plan price stays the same, but tax will be added based on your location. That means your total charge may be higher than before, depending on your local tax rate.

How tax is calculated

We calculate tax based on your billing address, card country, or sign-up country. If tax applies in your area, it will be added at checkout.

📝 Note: If your location is tax-exempt, you won’t be charged tax.

Is this mandatory?

Only if your region requires it. If your area doesn’t charge tax on Linktree’s service, or if you qualify for an exemption, no tax will be added.

Will invoices and receipts change?

Yes. If you’re in the U.S. or Canada, your invoice or receipt will now show tax as a separate line item.

What about users outside the U.S. or Canada?

This change only applies to the U.S. and Canada. In other countries, VAT or GST will still be included in your plan price where required.

What happens with renewals?

If your plan renews after the change takes effect, tax will be added based on your location. Existing subscriptions may be prorated or continue under the old pricing until they renew.

Will this affect discounts or add-ons?

Nope. Any discounts or add-ons on your account won’t change. This update only affects how tax is displayed and applied.

Do I need to do anything?

No. You’ll see taxes added at checkout based on your region.

💡 Tip: If you think you’re tax-exempt (for example, a nonprofit or GST-registered business in Canada), you can share your documentation with our support team for review.

Understanding your Insights

The Insights page in your Linktree admin provides insights into the performance of your Linktree. The more you share, promote, and create campaigns with your Linktree, the more views and clicks you’ll receive on your links.

Key Definitions

Before diving into the analytics, let’s go over some key terms you’ll encounter:

  • Total Views: The number of times your Linktree profile has been accessed by visitors.
  • Unique Views: The number of distinct visitors who have viewed your Linktree, excluding repeat visits from the same person.
  • Total Clicks: The count of times visitors have clicked on any of your links.
  • Unique Clicks: The number of distinct visitors who have clicked on your links, counting each person only once regardless of how many times they click.
  • Click Rate: The percentage of views that resulted in clicks. This is determined by dividing the total number of clicks by the total number of views your profile has received. For example, if your profile has 1,000 clicks and 2,000 views, your click rate would be 50%. This is calculated by dividing 1,000 by 2,000 and then multiplying by 100.
  • Subscribers: Visitors who have signed up to receive updates or notifications through your Linktree using the Subscribe feature.
  • Sources: Channels or platforms (e.g., direct, social media, search engines) that visitors used to find your Linktree.
