Accessibility Statement

Our mission at Aira is to make a more accessible web space for our team, our clients, and our visitors.

One in four adults in the United States has a disability that impacts their ability to interact with the online world. At Aira, we strive to ensure that everything we create or add to our website is usable by people of all abilities. #### Accessibility at Aira

We have structured our website to allow those of all abilities to easily and quickly find the information they need. #### Ongoing Efforts to Ensure Accessibility

Our site follows Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 Level AA Success Criteria (and where possible Level AAA) Opens in new tab or window and the latest ICT Refresh (Section 508) guidelines Opens in new tab or window as they apply to the web as our primary goal. These guidelines include internationally agreed-upon standards and ICT Refresh Compliance Criteria that cover a wide range of recommendations and best practices for making content more accessible and ADA Compliant Opens in new tab or window. As new content and pages are created for our website. We apply these guidelines to all our designs, code, and content as new content and pages are created for our website.

Testing of our website is performed by accessibility experts using industry-standard tools such as AXE Accessibility Opens in new tab or window, WAVE Opens in new tab or window, NVDA Screen Reader Opens in new tab or window, Colour Contrast Analyser (CCA) Opens in new tab or window, additional browser extensions like HeadingsMap (verify page content structure) Opens in new tab or window, and keyboard-only navigation techniques. #### Accessibility Features at Aira

The following is a list of components and functions meant to improve our site’s accessibility:

  • The Aira design has incorporated a mobile-responsive version of its content.
  • We have selected colors for color contrast and meet AA requirements, even for small text.
  • Navigational aids are provided throughout our website. Skip navigation links have been incorporated to enable smooth keyboard navigation and to bypass repeated content.
  • The language of our website is declared in the header.
  • All images and other non-text elements are appropriately marked up to either reflect their purely decorative purpose or share informative content in their alternative text attributes.
  • Landmark and heading markups indicate expected navigation, section containers, headings, lists, and buttons in a more understandable and skimmable way for all users, regardless of device.
  • Forms are associated with labels or applicable Accessible Rich Internet Applications (ARIA) and include appropriate instructions on filling in form controls, with clearly understandable error states.
  • (ARIA) screen-reader only text is present when links, buttons, icons, or images need additional context to understand their meaning.
  • Warning text (readable by screen-readers) is displayed when links open in a new tab or window wherever possible. #### Third-party embeds, iframes, and/or Products & Services

Occasionally, at our discretion, we may include on our website links to sites offering third-party products or services. These third-party sites have separate and independent accessibility policies. We therefore have no responsibility or liability for the content and activities of these linked sites. Nonetheless, we seek to protect the integrity of our site and welcome any feedback about these sites. #### Accessibility Support Contact

Why Aira? Enhancing Independence with Professional Visual Interpreting

[Image: “Aira” logo on a dark teal background with concentric circular lines. Text on the right reads “Access is a human right,” with “human right” highlighted in teal.]

Standing in a busy airport or sitting at your desk with a complex document can bring a wave of uncertainty when it comes to access to visual information. You want to know exactly what kind of assistance you will get when you reach out.

Knowing that a trained professional is on the other end of the line changes everything. It turns a stressful moment into a seamless, predictable experience.

Aira supports access to visual information by providing consistent, professional visual interpreting whenever you need it. It is one essential tool in your toolbox to help you navigate your day with confidence.

The Professional Environment You Can Trust

When you connect with an Aira Visual Interpreter, you know exactly what to expect. Every professional works in a controlled environment with consistent noise levels, ensuring clear communication. You are not at risk of other people being present, chiming in, or creating disruption in the background.

While Aira never replaces traditional Orientation and Mobility training, our Visual Interpreters receive specialized training rooted in these principles to support your navigation. They understand the unique nuances of the blind and low vision community, so your experience is never treated as a novelty. You will never be asked inappropriate questions about your life or visual acuity.

Aira Interpreters integrate powerful tools like messaging, image sharing, and desktop access to streamline your tasks. Access Partners provide additional tools to our Visual Interpreters like detailed interior maps to support navigation and task completion.

“I have not used a volunteer service for a long time because Aira is there when I need it and I trust them more.” Gary K.

Keeping Your Personal Information Secure

Handling sensitive tasks requires absolute trust. Whether you are managing personal health information, reviewing financial documents, or logging into secure accounts, privacy and professionalism are paramount.

Visual Interpreters handle personal data, financial tasks, and health information with strict security protocols such as HIPAA compliance.

“I was out and I needed to switch money from savings to checking, and I booted up Aira and told the agent to transfer money. My friend said ‘You trust them to do that for you?’ and I said Yep.” – Randee B.

Empowerment Over Burden

Relying on friends, family, or strangers can sometimes feel like you are using up someone else’s time. Aira balances the scale by giving you an on-demand professional service. Our Visual Interpreters are full-time employees and if they answer a call, your task is their priority.

It reduces friction in daily tasks, allowing you to save your social capital for the moments that matter most. You can manage your day on your own terms without feeling like you are asking for a favor.

“Being out and about with Aira in my pocket is such a good feeling. Gives me independence and confidence on my own.” – Steven S.

Reducing Friction with Access Offers

Many organizations, universities, and businesses cover the cost of your calls. You can browse available Access Offers directly within the Aira Explorer app to find free visual interpreting locations and services near you.

Moving Forward Together

Our goal is to continue delivering reliable, high-quality visual interpreting that supports your daily life. Through ongoing innovation, new partnerships, and a growing community, we are dedicated to expanding your access.

Thank you for being a vital part of the Explorer community as we build a more accessible future together.

[Image: “Aira” logo on a dark teal background with concentric circular lines. Text on the right reads “Access is a human right,” with “human right” highlighted in teal.] #### AI at Aira: Built for Real-World Use

Artificial intelligence is evolving quickly, especially with real-time, interactive experiences becoming more capable. At Aira, these advancements are combined with live, professional Visual Interpreters to support access to visual information in a way that is secure, consistent, and reliable.

AI is not new to Aira. It has supported behind-the-scenes systems like scheduling and predictive services for years. More recently, AI has become a visible part of the Explorer experience, starting with Access AI.

This post walks through how Aira’s AI features work, how they fit alongside human interpreting, and what to keep in mind when using them responsibly. #### Meet Access AI

Access AI is Aira’s image description tool, designed to give you flexible, on-demand information about the world around you.

From the main screen, you can choose how you want information delivered:

  • Short, simple descriptions for quick understanding
  • More detailed responses when you want deeper context
  • A tone that matches your preference, from casual to more formal

You are in control of how information is shared. #### What You Can Do with Access AI

Access AI is designed for everyday use across a range of scenarios:

  • Get a “Quick Description” with a single tap
  • Use your device camera for real-time detailed image descriptions
  • Upload up to ten images at once for comparison or questions
  • Explore photos already in your library
  • Save and revisit results with chat history
  • Share descriptions with friends, family, or on social media

It is a flexible tool that supports access to visual information when and how you need it. #### Where People and AI Work Together

Aira stands apart in how it combines AI with human support.

If you want to confirm or expand on an AI-generated description, you can connect with a Visual Interpreter at any time:

  • Use the Verify feature to compare AI results with a live interpretation
  • Ask follow-up questions for added clarity
  • Switch from AI to a live call whenever needed

This combination helps reduce uncertainty and gives you options based on the moment. #### Designed with the Community in Mind

Access AI was shaped with input from blind and low vision users and built using principles from Visual Interpreter training.

That means the focus is not just on describing what is visible, but on:

  • Providing clear, useful information
  • Supporting orientation and understanding
  • Delivering descriptions in a way that is practical and easy to follow

The goal is simple: make information more usable, not just more available. #### Build AI: Shaping the Future Together

Vispero and Aira: The Right Stuff

Anyone striving to be an astronaut is said to have “the right stuff”. It’s a combination of personal skill and knowing how to use what’s out there to get things done. The same can be said for achieving accessibility. Two powerful additions to your accessibility tool kit are products from Vispero and professional visual interpreting from Aira. #### How both products work toward accessibility

Aira connects blind and low vision people to professional visual interpreters, called agents, via a smart phone app. Aira Agents read documents, assist with navigation, access computers remotely and provide any other visual information needed to accomplish a task.

Vispero produces the JAWS screen reader, ZoomText magnifier and Fusion, that combines these two Vispero software programs to support any level of vision. JAWS turns text and other screen features into audible output. The program can be customized to meet many complex work environments. ZoomText enlarges the computer screen in a variety of ways to meet the needs of people with different types of vision loss. The combination of both programs supports people as they move through progressive vision loss. Learning to use JAWS is one of the primary skills taught when someone begins blindness rehabilitation.

Having access to the computer is empowering. Blind people have used these tools in numerous careers at some of the top companies in the world. Government officials, factory workers, teachers, healthcare professionals, students and parents access the world through their computers and smart phones using Vispero products and Aira. OR Together, Vispero and Aira are a power tool. #### How do Vispero and Aira work together?

It’s rare that people use only one tool to achieve access. Often it’s a combination of technology and skills. For example, what do you do when your computer goes silent? Press any key? Nothing happens, no speech, no enlarged print, no sound at all. There’s a message on the screen but what does it say?

Sometimes you know what to do, up arrow three times then hit enter, etc. Wait, that didn’t work. That’s where the Vispero offer that many of us often refer to as the JAWS offer comes in. Using the Aira app, a person connects to an agent who can then read the computer screen. Agents can even connect remotely and save work or reactivate JAWS or ZoomText. Agents can also resolve inaccessible captchas. The minutes used are covered by Vispero.

“So I’m typing away and suddenly there’s that noise that means something’s wrong. The screen has something on it but I can’t see it because I’ve got no magnification and no screen reader. I called Aira and the agent guided me by using the camera in the phone app to start the remote access program. Then they connected and took over. They were able to save my work and restart Fusion. Project saved!”

Unfortunately there are still some of those verification captchas that don’t read using JAWS and are difficult to see even with screen enlarging. Aira can also help resolve them quickly and get you moving on with your task.

“I got to the very end of the web site shopping cart and couldn’t submit my order because there was one of those captchas where you have to write the letters and numbers you see. It didn’t have an audio option. Luckily Aira’s Vispero JAWS offer is available. Now we’ve got office supplies again.”

Aira Tech Tips & Troubleshooting with a Visual Interpreter

[Image: A man with short light hair looks at his phone and laptop sitting at a desk. “Aira Community: Aira Tech Tips & Troubleshooting.” The background includes the Aira logo and two decorative teal triangles.]

In our 2024 Explorer Survey, people identified three main categories of tasks: describing, reading, and technical assistance. Since 2020, the use of visual interpreting to assist with computer and other technical tasks has surged significantly. A whopping 62% of respondents reported using Aira to troubleshoot when screen readers or magnification software fail. Another 65% rely on our service to navigate inaccessible online forms, while 66% use visual interpreters to interact with inaccessible website elements or captchas. #### How does tech troubleshooting with Aira work?

Need visual assistance with a website or online task? Aira’s secure visual interpreters are available from either the Aira Explorer mobile app or our web app, accessible at in new tab or window. From either app, you can share your screen.

Our mobile app is limited to screen sharing on iPhones or iPads. Some Android phones support remote control, but this feature is becoming less common. On the web app, we have a screen-sharing function. To remotely control the computer, you’ll need a third-party solution.

Several options are available for remote computer control. For Windows, Microsoft Quick Assist is a popular choice. To enable Quick Assist, check the “Customize Your Call” button under the home screen of either app. We also use Team Viewer and Chrome Remote Desktop. In 2024, we launched the Remote Incident Manager (RIM) solution for both PCs and Macs.

Once you’ve connected the remote control solution to your visual interpreter, they can activate controls, fill in forms, and describe images. After completing the task, the interpreter will close the remote solution before ending the call. #### What if your computer is frozen or just not responding?

Our mobile app comes in handy when you need assistance with reading computer screens. Simply point your phone at the screen, as sometimes computer screens can be challenging to read using a smartphone camera.
