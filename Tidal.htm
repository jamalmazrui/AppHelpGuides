Claim Your Artist Profile

How do I claim my public artist profile in Tidal’s catalog?

You can now claim your public artist profile in Tidal’s catalog, which gives you access to manage your official artist page. This makes the process faster and supports a more direct connection between artists and their profiles. To claim your artist profile, please follow the steps below: • Log in at tidal.com • Search for your artist name • Tap or click your artist profile • Select the option to claim the profile • Choose how you’d like to verify your identity You can verify through a distributor (DistroKid, CD Baby, or TuneCore are currently supported). This is the fastest option and usually provides immediate approval and access. If you prefer, you can choose manual verification. This is reviewed by our team and can take up to 8 weeks to process. Once approved, your profile will be officially claimed. #### I already claimed my profile in Tidal Artist Home. Do I need to do it again? No. Your profile remains claimed and tied to your Tidal account. To manage it, log in at tidal.comand click your Profile icon in the top right corner of the screen (mobile) or top left corner (web/desktop). #### Can I submit more than one claim for the same profile? No. If there is already an active claim under review for a profile, new claims will be temporarily disabled until the current request is resolved. You can only claim one profile per Tidal account. #### Why don’t I see an option to claim a profile? If you don’t see the option to claim a profile, it may be for one of the following reasons: • There is already an active claim under review, which temporarily disables new requests • The profile is restricted to help prevent unauthorized claims • The profile has already been successfully claimed by someone else If you run into this, please reach out to Tidal Artist Support and we’ll be happy to help. #### What does it mean to merge artist profiles? Merging profiles is used when there is more than one artist page on Tidal for the same artist or project. A merge combines your music under one main profile so everything appears in a single place. #### What happens after profiles are merged? • Your music will be combined under one artist profile • Only one profile will remain active • Duplicate profile(s) will be closed #### Will my followers transfer when profiles are merged? For now, followers are tied to each profile. When profiles are merged, only your main profile remains active, and followers from closed profiles will not transfer. Your main profile becomes the single place for your audience to find you and grow organically. #### Will my artist name change when I claim or merge a profile? In some cases, yes. If the name on your profile does not match the name provided by your distributor, your profile name will be updated to match the distributor’s records. This ensures your profile reflects the official artist name associated with your releases. This can apply when claiming, moving content, or merging profiles and may include formatting updates such as capitalization and special characters.

Maintaining Content Integrity on Tidal

What is artificial streaming?

Artificial streaming refers to the use of bots, click farms, or other methods to inflate play counts. Artificially inflating play counts violates our Content Guidelines. Our primary goal is to prevent any misuse of the system and to protect all artists’ streaming activity. #### How does Tidal detect artificial streaming? To maintain platform integrity, we partner with fraud detection services to identify and address instances of fraudulent streaming. These tools help us detect patterns associated with artificial streaming, allowing us to remove manipulated content and streams to maintain a fair environment for all artists. #### Why was my content removed for artificial streaming? If your content was removed, it was flagged as being associated with fraudulent streaming activity, which violates our Content Guidelines. This decision is based on our commitment to ensuring that streaming data reflects genuine listens. If you have further questions, please reach out directly to your label or distributor. #### What happens to royalties from artificially streamed content? Royalties are not paid for tracks identified as having artificial streams. These tracks will also be excluded from royalty statements for the applicable accounting periods. #### Can I ask my fans to stream my music on repeat? It’s important to promote your music authentically. Urging fans to use methods like looping tracks or employing bots is discouraged. Such practices can trigger fraudulent activity and result in the removal of the content from Tidal. #### What should I do if I used a paid promotion service and my content was flagged for artificial streaming? We understand that some artists may have unintentionally engaged with third-party services that promise to boost streams or placements. Unfortunately, even if these services were used with the best intentions, the resulting activity still violates our Content Guidelines. We recommend focusing on organic promotion strategies going forward to build genuine fan engagement. If you have further questions, please reach out directly to your label or distributor. #### How can I organically boost my streams? Growing your streams organically involves connecting with your audience through genuine engagement. Share your music across social media, collaborate with other artists, perform live, and encourage your fans to add your songs to their playlists. Building a real fanbase that loves your music is the most effective way to achieve sustainable growth on Tidal.

Manage Roles and Access in Tidal Artist Home

Team roles in Tidal Artist Home allow artists to manage their profiles and catalogs effectively. By assigning specific roles and access levels, you can collaborate with team members, maintain control over your works, and ensure that your profile is managed according to your preferences. #### How do I invite a new team member? 1. Go to the Team tab and select Invite new team member. 2. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite. 3. Assign the appropriate role and access level. 4. The invited person will receive an email invitation to join your team.

How can I remove or change a team member’s access level?

  • To remove a team member, click the three dots next to their name and select Revoke access. If needed, you can re-invite them to the team.
  • To change a team member’s access level, click the three dots next to their name, select Edit, and choose the new role or access level.

How do I transfer ownership of an account?

  1. Navigate to Team > Edit > Owner under Manage Roles.
  2. Select Transfer and choose the Admin team member to whom you want to transfer ownership.
  3. Follow the prompts to confirm the transfer.

Note: There can only be one Owner per account. After the transfer, the previous Owner’s role changes to Admin. #### Access Level Descriptions ##### Owner: Artist Profile: - The Owner access level for an artist profile can be held by Artists, Managers, Group Members, and Labels. - Artists who are Owners of a profile have full access to Profile Management. They can invite and remove team members and change their access levels. - Managers, Group Members, and Labels who are Owners of a profile have full access to Profile Management. They can invite and remove team members and change their access levels. - The user who claims an artist profile is automatically assigned the Owner access level, though they can reassign Owner access to another user later on. Only one user can have Owner access at a time.

Admin:

Artist Profile: - The Admin access level for an artist profile can be held by Artists, Managers, Group Members, and Labels. - Artists who are Admins of a profile have access to Profile Management. - All other roles who are Admins of a profile have access to Profile Management. - All Admins can invite and remove team members at the Editor and Viewer access levels, as well as change the access level of Editors and Viewers.

Editor:

Artist Profile: - The Editor access level for an artist profile can be held by Managers, Group Members, Labels, or the “Other” role. Editors have access to Profile Management and can invite and remove team members at the Viewer access level.

Viewer:

The Viewer access level for artist profiles allows users to view Works and Profile Management. They cannot edit the artist profile, nor can they invite or remove users.

AI Policy

For listeners

Are AI-generated recordings allowed on Tidal?

Tidal allows AI-generated music on the platform, provided it complies with our AI Policy, Terms and Conditions of Use, Content Guidelines, and other applicable requirements. ##### What does Tidal consider AI-generated music? Tidal defines AI-generated recordings as recordings that are wholly generated using generative artificial intelligence. Due to limitations in AI-detection technology, and to avoid false positives, we will only be taking action with respect to wholly AI-generated recordings. As reliability of AI-detection technology improves, we will expand actions to recordings that are substantially AI-generated. We will be taking no actions as to AI-generated compositions. ##### Does Tidal label AI-generated music? Yes. Tidal labels recordings that it determines are wholly AI-generated. This will help provide transparency so listeners understand what kind of content they are listening to. ##### How do I report AI-generated recordings that aren’t tagged? If you believe a track was created entirely using AI but is not currently tagged as AI-generated, please submit a report through our support form. When completing the form, select: - I need help with a copyright or trademark violation or AI tagging - I want to file an AI flag review - This track wasn’t tagged as AI but should be

Can I block all AI-generated recordings?

Yes. You can turn off AI-labeled recordings in Settings. Once it’s off, AI-labeled recordings won’t play. They will also be removed from your personalized recommendations as they’re refreshed. Most recommendations update daily, while album recommendations may take up to a few days to update. Until then, you may still see some AI-labeled content, but it will be grayed out and won’t play. #### For artists and distributors ##### Can AI-generated recordings earn royalties on Tidal? Recordings that Tidal identifies as wholly AI-generated are not eligible for royalty attribution. ##### Can Tidal remove my AI-generated music? Yes. Tidal may block or remove AI-generated music that violates our policies or is associated with fraudulent activity, including content that deceives listeners, exploits an individual’s or group’s name or likeness, interferes with authentic artists and their audiences, or otherwise undermines the integrity of our platform. ##### What does Tidal consider fraudulent AI-generated music? Fraudulent activity may include AI-generated music that is intended to mislead listeners, impersonate artists, interfere with authentic artists and their audiences, or is associated with unusual upload or streaming activity. Tidal reserves the right to determine whether content is being distributed primarily for fraudulent purposes. ##### Does this policy apply to Tidal Upload? Yes. Tidal Upload content is subject to the same AI-generated music standards as catalog content. If Tidal determines that Upload recordings are wholly AI-generated, they may be labeled as AI-generated and will not be eligible for direct-to-fan monetization. ##### Why didn’t I receive royalties for my track that was created entirely with AI? If Tidal identifies a track as wholly AI-generated, it is not eligible for royalty attribution under Tidal’s current AI Policy. ##### What if my track is 99% AI-generated (or less)? We understand that focusing on wholly AI-generated content means some partially AI-generated content may remain unlabeled on Tidal. Our AI Policy is a living document and may evolve as technology advances. ##### What if my recording was incorrectly identified as wholly AI-generated? If you believe your track was incorrectly identified as wholly AI-generated and you did not use AI to create the recording, please submit an appeal through our support form. When completing the form, select: - I need help with a copyright or trademark violation or AI tagging - I want to file an AI flag review - This track was wrongly tagged as AI

How do I connect Tidal to AirPlay?

To connect Tidal via AirPlay, ensure you have: - An Apple device (iPhone, iPad, or Mac) - An AirPlay-enabled device (Apple TV, AirPlay-compatible speakers, or a smart TV with AirPlay support)

  • Open the Tidal app
  • Tap ‘Now Playing’ at the bottom of the screen
  • Click the speaker icon in the upper right-hand corner
  • Select AirPlay & Bluetooth

What types of tracks can I play on Tidal via AirPlay?

While connected to an AirPlay device, you can enjoy the following types of tracks: - Low - High - MAX - HiRes FLAC - Dolby Atmos

How do connect to AirPlay on my Mac? 

Make sure your MAC OS version is supported. Make sure that both devices are connected to the same network. If you want to use the AirPlay device as the sound output, you need to connect to it on your Mac sound output settings. To do that, please follow the steps below: - On the menu bar, click on the sound icon and select the Airplay device you’d like to connect. - Once the AirPlay device is selected, you can play music your Tidal app. - Make sure that ‘System Controlled’ is selected on your Tidal app as shown below. This means that once this is selected, Tidal is using the sound output selected in your Mac.

  • When connected to speakers via AirPlay, your mobile device will display specific controls and information tailored for a speaker setup. When connected to a TV via AirPlay, the interface adapts for an optimal viewing experience, suitable for larger screens.

Audio Format Updates

Can I still access music in MQA or 360 Reality Audio on Tidal?

No. As of July 24, 2024, music in the MQA or 360 Reality Audio formats is no longer accessible via any Tidal application or integration. For stereo sound, FLAC was chosen as the format we’ll support going forward because it is open source—meaning any artist can deliver their music in high quality directly to Tidal without the involvement of a third party. This makes it easier for their fans to experience music in the highest possible sound quality on all their devices. For immersive sound, Dolby Atmos was chosen as the format we will support going forward due to the number of compatible devices, catalog availability, and artist adoption of the format. Through our continued partnership with Dolby, we’ve given artists the opportunity to expand on their sonic vision, allowing their fans to experience their music like never before. #### How did these changes impact My Collection? For MQA: If you had an MQA track or album in your Collection – including in playlists – the track was automatically replaced by the highest quality FLAC version that has been distributed to Tidal. If you had an MQA track or album downloaded for offline access, you will need to update to at least Tidal app version 2.123.0. Once updated, you will receive a pop-up alert asking if you’d like redownload those tracks. This action will replace any previously downloaded MQA tracks with their FLAC versions. Please note you will only receive the prompt to redownload once. For 360 Reality Audio: If you had a 360 Reality Audio track or album in your Collection – including in a playlist or downloaded for offline access – the track or album will be grayed out and unavailable for streaming if you try to select it. #### Why am I seeing an error code: 15:1 when playing Sony 360 Reality Audio tracks? You may encounter the error code: 15:1 when attempting to play a 360 Reality Audio track from My Collection after updating to Tidal version 2.123. This error typically affects saved Sony 360 tracks. #### What should I do if I encounter this error? Try searching for the track again and attempt to play or redownload it. Please note, if the track is still unavailable, it means it is no longer accessible due to the removal of 360 Reality Audio from Tidal. #### Does Tidal have plans to make other changes to audio format availability in the future? We have no further plans to change our audio format offerings going forward, and we remain committed to providing our subscribers with exceptional audio quality. #### Are there any songs that are no longer available on Tidal now? While we have at least 16-bit, 44.1 kHz FLAC versions for nearly all MQA tracks today, we may not have a replacement for every single one. We are working hard to ensure all previously available MQA tracks will be replaced with a FLAC version in a timely manner. #### Why is my digital to analog converter (DAC) still showing tracks are being played in MQA? There were some tracks that were only delivered in the MQA format from content providers when we made this change. In these cases, the FLAC files available for streaming are produced from an MQA source file and certain DACs read them as MQA files. We’re working with content providers to replace these files with HiRes FLAC versions if available.

Change sound quality level

Change sound quality on Mobile:

  • When using mobile data: navigate to Settings > Audio & Playback > Mobile data streaming and select either Low, High, or Max. Your choice will take effect from the next track played.

Contributions

What are Contributions?

Tidal Contributions is a feature that allows artists to receive direct support from listeners when they upload music through Tidal. It offers artists a new way to build stronger fan connections and grow their careers on their own terms, and it gives fans a chance to support the artists they love. #### How do artists become eligible to receive Contributions? Artists must have a Tidal account, be based in the U.S., be at least 18 years old, and link a verified U.S. Cash App account that’s in good standing. To get started, go to your Profile > Settings and follow the prompts to connect your Cash App account. #### Who is eligible to send Contributions? Listeners must also be based in the U.S., be at least 18 years old, and link a verified U.S. Cash App account that’s in good standing. #### Where in Tidal can listeners contribute? After music is uploaded through Tidal, artists can connect their Cash App account directly from their artist profile. Listeners can then contribute from either the artist profile or the Now Playing view, depending on where the music is being viewed or played. Currently, Contributions are only supported via Cash App. #### Can artists add a custom message to the Contribution section on their profile? Yes! Artists who upload music to Tidal can add a short custom note that appears above the Send a contribution with Cash App button on their artist profile. This note is a great way to thank listeners, share updates, or highlight how their support helps. The message can be edited or removed by clicking the pencil icon. Contribution Notes must adhere to Tidal Content Guidelines. #### How do I set up a Cash App account? Cash App accounts are free to open. To get started, download the Cash App mobile application from your device’s app store and follow the instructions to create an account. To be eligible to give or receive Contributions, the account must successfully complete identity verification, be in good standing, and have no unresolved B-notices. For more on B-notices, visit cash.app/help/5642-bnotices-from-cash-app. Use of Cash App is subject to their Terms of Service andPrivacy Notice. #### Are Contributions anonymous? No. The sender’s $cashtag and account name will be visible to the receiver in Cash App. #### Can Contributions be split with collaborators or groups? Not right now. Contributions are sent to a single Cash App account linked to the artist profile. If a track features multiple artists, the Contribution option won’t appear in the Now Playing view. #### What if there’s a payment issue with a Contribution? All payments are processed through Cash App. For any questions or issues, please contact Cash App Support directly. #### Can Contributions be turned off? Yes. You can turn off Contributions by going to Profile > Edit and switching the Contributions toggle off. #### Is there anything to keep in mind when receiving Contributions? Yes. Contributions are sent at the discretion of the sender, and Tidal is not responsible for the transfer or its outcome. Any funds received belong solely to the recipient. If a message or description is included as part of the transaction within Cash App, it is for informational purposes only and is not verified or guaranteed by Tidal or Cash App. #### Contributions Guidelines We’re excited to offer Contributions as a way for listeners to support the artists they love. To make sure it stays a trusted, respectful feature, here are a few important guidelines for artists and listeners: - Make sure you’re following all applicable laws and Tidal’s Terms of Use - Any content you upload should follow Tidal’s Content Guidelines - Your linked Cash App account should be verified, in good standing, meet Cash App’s eligibility requirements, and have no unresolved B-notices. You can learn more about B-notices here. Cash App’s Terms of Service and Privacy Notice also apply. - Don’t offer perks, rewards, or incentives in exchange for Contributions - Don’t use Contributions to fundraise for political causes or anything illegal - Don’t use this feature to sell goods or services - Don’t collect Contributions on behalf of someone else without clear permission

Thanks for helping us keep Contributions safe, respectful, and open to everyone.

Daily Discovery

What is Daily Discovery?

Daily Discovery is a personalized playlist inspired by your listening, updated daily with 10 new tracks in the US morning timezone. #### Why don’t I see any songs in my Daily Discovery playlist yet?  If you’re new to Tidal, we need time to get to know you. Your Daily Discovery section will be populated once we have data on your listening habits. The more you listen, the quicker this process will be. #### What if my Daily Discovery isn’t updating regularly? If you notice that your Daily Discovery isn’t refreshing, it’s important to ensure you’ve been actively using the app. Our algorithm relies on your recent activity to generate new recommendations. The more you engage with Tidal, the more personalized and updated your Daily Discovery will be! #### Why do songs I already know appear in Daily Discovery?  This occasionally happens due to the timing of our recommendation process. There’s a brief period between when we filter out familiar tracks from your Daily Discovery and when these recommendations are actually updated in the Tidal app. If you happen to add a song to your favorites or playlists during this small window, it might still show up in your Daily Discovery.

HiRes FLAC audio

What audio formats does Tidal support? 

  • Max: High fidelity lossless audio up to 24-bit, 192 kHz with HiRes FLAC (Free Lossless Audio Codec), an open-source format that every artist can create and deliver their music in.
  • High: Lossless audio with FLAC (16-bit, 44.1 kHz)
  • Low (up to 320 kbps): Listen to the music you love using lower data usage with compressed AAC files.
  • Dolby Atmos: Learn more here.

What is HiRes FLAC?  

HiRes FLAC is any FLAC file greater than 16-bit, 44.1 kHz, which is standard CD quality. HiRes FLAC on Tidal can deliver superior sound quality in files as large as 24-bit, 192 kHz. #### What is the difference between FLAC and HiRes FLAC? HiRes FLAC is the distinction we make for any FLAC file that is greater than 16-bit, 44.1 kHz, the standard CD quality. FLAC is a two-channel, stereo format meaning music is mixed with a left and right speaker (or headphone) in mind. #### What devices can play HiRes FLAC? - HiRes FLAC is available for streaming directly from iOS, Android, Desktop or Web. - Select Tidal partners support HiRes FLAC. For more information on if your specific device is supported, please reference our supported devices page. - For Chromecast, the audio quality will default to High if your device does not support HiRes FLAC. Learn more about supported media on your Google device here: developers.google.com/cast/docs/media

How do I know the audio quality of the content I am listening to on mobile devices?

In the player below the progress bar, there will be a pill that says either FLAC, High, or Low, depending on both the audio quality level you’ve chosen in settings on that device and the formats of that specific song that have been delivered to Tidal. To view or make changes to the audio quality settings on Mobile: - When using mobile data: navigate to** Settings > Audio & Playback** > Mobile data streaming and select either Low, High, or Max. Your choice will take effect from the next track played. - When using WiFi: navigate to Settings > Audio & Playback > Wi-Fi streaming and select either Low, High, or Max. Your choice will take effect from the next track played. - When using downloaded content for offline playback:** navigate to Settings > Downloads > Audio** and select either Low, High, or Max. Your choice will only apply to future downloads. You will have to delete and re-download previously saved albums and playlists if you want to listen to them offline in a different quality.

How do I know the audio quality of the content I am listening to on Desktop or Web? 

To the right of the progress bar (bottom right corner) there will be a pill that either says FLAC, High, or Low depending on both the audio quality level you’ve chosen in settings on that device and the formats of that specific song that have been delivered to Tidal. To view or make changes to the audio quality settings on Desktop or Web: Navigate to Settings > Streaming > scroll to Audio Quality and select either Low, High, or Max. Your choice will take effect from the next track played. #### What do I need to ensure I’m getting the best experience with HiRes FLAC? The exact sound quality that artists and producers hear when they record music can be recreated through your speakers when listening in HiRes FLAC. To get the full experience, we suggest you research if the digital-to-analog converter (DAC) on your listening device can fully support the format (lossless 24-bit, 192 kHz) and use a wired or WiFi connection. #### How do I see bit depth and sample rate?  Subscribers can see the bit depth, sample rate, and format on High (FLAC 16-bit, 44.1 kHz) and see the transferring bit rate and format on Low (96/320 kbps AAC). You can view these details by tapping the sound format on the Now Playing screen. #### What file types are included in Max quality? You get the best quality version of any song on Tidal when you select Max quality. The source file you receive depends on the format(s) delivered to us by the artist. The hierarchy of availability for playback is HiRes FLAC, FLAC, then ACC. This means if we don’t have the file available in HiRes FLAC, the source file will be FLAC, and so on. #### What data speed does my internet connection need? For the best streaming experience when listening on High and Max quality, we recommend a steady Internet connection of at least 1.5 to 2 Mbps. Note that High quality audio usually consumes up to 10 MB of data per minute, and Max quality up to 40 MB of data per minute (varying based on the exact sample rates).

Understanding Duplicate Charges

Common Reasons for Suspected Overcharges:

  • You have two or more active Tidal subscriptions
  • You have a second account through Apple or Google Play
  • Understanding Tidal’s 30-day billing cycle
  • Attempts to collect failed payments

You have two or more active Tidal subscriptions:

A second charge indicates that there may be a second account registered to you under an alternate email address, or your email address but with a typo. #### You have a second account through Apple or Google Play: Tidal allows subscriptions through partners like Apple or Google Play, each managing its own billing system and login credentials. These subscriptions are distinct and not linked to any other Tidal accounts you may have, leading to charges from different sources if you have subscriptions with more than one provider. #### Understanding Tidal’s 30-day billing cycle: Tidal operates on a 30-day billing cycle. Given this framework, it’s possible to incur charges twice in one calendar month, such as on January 1 and again on January 31. This occurrence isn’t a mistake or duplicate charge; it’s a result of the subscription fees being due 30 days apart. #### Attempts to collect failed payments: Occasionally, payment requests might be declined by your bank or credit provider. To ensure uninterrupted service, Tidal may attempt the payment multiple times until successful. Consequently, the payment might be processed several days after the initial due date, followed by the next scheduled charge. This sequence might seem like a double charge within a short period, but it’s actually the settlement of a past-due amount followed by the charge for the upcoming service month. #### Clarifying True Double Charges: A genuine double charge is characterized by specific criteria: - Identical fees are charged on the same day. - The payment method for both charges is the same, for instance, two instances of billing through PayPal.

Identifying Charges from Multiple Accounts:

  • Charges are issued on different dates, indicating separate billing cycles.
  • Different payment methods are used for the charges, such as one through PayPal and another through your credit card, pointing to the existence of multiple accounts.

Direct-to-Fan Sales

What are the requirements to sell music on Tidal?

To sell tracks or albums on Tidal, you must be based in the United States and fully own or control all rights necessary to sell streaming and download access to the content. This includes: - The sound recording - The underlying musical composition - The lyrics - Any samples used - Artwork and related materials

Only original recordings may be sold unless you have proper written agreements with every rightsholder involved. You may not sell: - Covers without licenses from all applicable rightsholders - Tracks containing uncleared samples - Collaborations without documented agreements from all contributors - Content that violates Tidal’s Content Guidelines - Content previously removed for policy violations

You are responsible for ensuring you have all required permissions before offering content for sale. #### Do I need a Square account? Yes. Artists must complete Square onboarding to receive payouts for Direct-to-Fan Sales purchases. #### How do I get started selling music on Tidal through Square? To start selling music directly to fans on Tidal, follow the steps below: 1. Go to an uploaded album. 2. Tap Edit album. 3. Change album access from Unlisted to Paid. 4. Accept the Tidal Marketplace Terms to continue. 5. Create a free Square account and complete the sign-up process and identity verification.

Once setup is complete, you can begin selling eligible music and content directly to fans on Tidal. #### When and how will I receive payouts? Payouts for Direct-to-Fan Sales are securely processed through Square. Payout timing may vary depending on your bank and account status. #### What fees apply to my Paid Upload sales? Artists are responsible for all payment processing fees charged by Square. These fees are deducted from the sale proceeds before funds are made available for payout. #### Are purchases refundable? As outlined in the Tidal Upload Marketplace Terms, Upload purchases are final and non-refundable due to the nature of digital content, except where required by law. #### Are buyers charged a service fee? Buyers are not charged a Tidal service fee at checkout. The price displayed for a Paid Upload is the amount the buyer pays, excluding any applicable taxes or fees charged by their payment provider. #### I previously used Stripe. Do I have to switch to Square now? Artists with existing Stripe accounts can keep their accounts, but any new content offered for sale after June 4, 2026, will require a Square account. All future sales will be processed through Square, including sales of content that was previously processed through Stripe.
