About The Great Courses and The Great Courses Plus

Can I join more than one subscription service?

A: No, you can only have one subscription active at a time. If you want to start a different subscription, your current membership must be canceled first. For those interested in multiple subscriptions, The Great Courses Plus is the best option. It provides access to all courses across our smaller subscription services for the same price or less than subscribing to two of them individually.

Can I switch from one subscription to another?

Not immediately. You can switch to a different subscription by canceling your current membership and starting a new one once your paid access ends at the end of your billing period. If you would like to switch to The Great Courses Plus, you can upgrade at any time by clicking [HERE].

How does The Great Courses Plus free trial work?

Our free trial gives you access to all courses included in your subscription on your smart TV, computer, or phone. Once your free trial ends, your credit card will be billed based on the membership plan you’ve selected. If you’d prefer to cancel, simply visit the My Account page and cancel in a few simple steps. If you cancel during your free trial period, you’ll retain access until that period ends and won’t be billed.

How often will you add new content?

Our library is always expanding, with new courses and digitally remastered classics added to The Great Courses Plus each and every month.

What are the additional benefits of subscribing to The Great Courses Plus?

A paid subscription to The Great Courses Plus includes:

  • Purchase-to-own savings: Receive an additional 20% off all individually purchased courses
  • Downloadable materials: Access PDFs of course guidebooks or workbooks
  • Day-one access: Watch all new course releases as soon as they launch

What is the difference between The Great Courses Plus and The Great Courses?

The Great Courses Plus is a subscription service that gives you access to an extensive library of over 20,000 lectures produced by The Great Courses. You can purchase individual Great Courses in digital formats on this website , or you can subscribe to The Great Courses Plus and get unlimited access. All of your Great Courses purchases are available to watch on The Great Courses Plus website and apps.

What is the Great Courses Plus?

The Great Courses Plus is the streaming home of thousands of hours of engaging courses designed to enrich and improve your life. Academically comprehensive and relentlessly engaging, our courses bring lifelong learners face-to-face with trusted professors and subject matter experts on topics ranging from science and history to philosophy and religion to travel and professional growth. Always ad-free, the Great Courses Plus is the place to experience the pure joy of learning—anytime, anywhere.

Billing and Purchasing

Am I required to pay sales tax in states where The Great Courses does not collect tax?

In certain states where The Great Courses does not collect or remit sales tax, purchases are not exempt from sales taxes, and purchasers in those states are required to file the requisite sales or use tax returns and directly pay applicable taxes to the states. Additional information may be found on your state’s department of revenue website.

Can I purchase a digital course as a gift for someone else?

Yes! To gift a course, simply navigate to the course you are interested in and click the Buy Course button. At the top of the next window, click the Send as Gift button. From there, you can enter the recipient’s information, add a personal gift message, and choose your preferred delivery date. Once you place the order, we’ll take care of the rest!

Can I purchase an individual course without having a subscription?

Absolutely. Individual courses can be purchased by anyone, and purchased courses can be streamed anytime, regardless of subscription status. Active Plus subscribers receive an additional 20% discount on all course purchases.

Do I need to pay sales taxes on my purchases?

How can I review my next billing amount and date?

You can review the next billing date and next billing amount from the “My Account” section on our website. You can reach this page via the menu that appears when you hover over your username in the upper right corner of the website. Your next billing date and amount will appear on this page.

To view your payment history:

  • If you purchased a subscription through the mobile app for your iOS device
    • Open Settings icon on the iOS device
    • Click your name at the top of the screen
    • Click “Subscriptions”
    • If you have an active subscription it will appear under the “Active” section.
  • If you purchased a subscription through the mobile app for your Android device
    • Launch the Google Play Store app.
    • Tap Menu
    • Click on My Apps or Apps
    • Click on Subscriptions and tap on the app of the subscription you’d like to view
  • If you purchased a subscription through the Roku app
    • Go to roku.com and sign into your Roku.com account. Then go to the subscriptions section of your account.

How do I purchase courses to own?

You can purchase courses and digital transcript books directly on our website. The easiest way is to click SHOP at the top of the site to browse available courses. You can also purchase from individual course pages by selecting the Buy button. Digital transcript books can be added to your course purchase during checkout.

How do I redeem a digital course I received as a gift?

To redeem your gift, click the link provided in your gift email. Existing customers simply need to sign in (if not already) and click the Redeem Gift button, while new customers will first be prompted to create a free account. Once redeemed, you will be directed to your My Purchases page, where your new course will appear alongside any other courses in your account and you can start watching immediately!

What is a subscription to The Great Courses Plus going to cost me?

You’ve got options! We offer a monthly plan, a quarterly plan, or an annual plan. Every plan offers a free trial, unlimited streaming, and 20% of All Purchases, and it’s easy to cancel at any time. Visit our plans page if you want the details about each plan.

Why do I need a credit card to sign up for a free trial?

A valid credit card is required to ensure you are a real person and to help mitigate piracy during Free Trial Periods. Your card will not be charged as long as you cancel during your Trial Period. Cancelling during a Free Trial will end your membership immediately. You will not be entitled to another free trial if you join again. We advise cancelling at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Am I allowed to show videos to a group?

Content from The Great Courses—whether accessed through a subscription or purchased individually—is for personal or private use only. If you’d like to share courses with a group, please contact our customer service team at contactus@thegreatcourses.com

Can a subscription or membership be gifted?

Yes. You can gift The Great Courses Plus to anyone, whether they are new or already an active member. CLICK HERE to purchase a gift subscription.

How do I opt-out of marketing emails?

You may opt-out of marketing emails by setting your email preferences on the “My Account” section of the website.You can also unsubscribe using the link at the bottom of any marketing email from The Great Courses.

How do I update my membership account information?

Via website desktop or laptop:

  • Log into your account through the website, hover over your name (email) and select My Account.

Via website on mobile browser:

  • Tap Sign In at the top right of the screen and sign in.
  • Once signed in, tap your name (email) and select My Account.

The My Account section includes:

  • Changing your email address
  • Resetting your password
  • Updating your payment method
  • Viewing Billing and Order history
  • Accessing Downloads (previous purchases)
  • Managing your devices

I signed up for a Membership. How can I cancel my membership?

How you cancel, depends on how you signed up.

Via our website:

  • Hover over your name(email) on the top right corner of the page and select “My Account”
  • Click “Cancel membership” located under “Membership Plan Management”

Via iOS App:

  • Open the Settings icon on the iOS device
  • Click on “iTunes and App Store”
  • Tap on Apple ID at the top of the screen
  • Click “View Apple ID”, enter Apple ID password and Sign In
  • Scroll down to the “Subscriptions” section
  • If you have an active subscription created through the iOS app it will appear under the “Active” section.
  • Select the subscription you would like to cancel.

Via Android App:

  • Launch the Google Play Store app.
  • Tap Menu
  • Click on My Apps or Apps
  • Click on Subscriptions and tap on the app of the subscription you’d like to cancel.

Via Roku App:

  • Highlight the channel on your Roku
  • Click the “*” on the remote which is called Options
  • Select “Manage Subscription”

Via FireTV App:

  • Sign into your Amazon account with your Amazon email/mobile phone number and password.
  • Click on Accounts & Lists in the upper right corner.
  • Select Memberships & Subscriptions.
  • Locate subscription you would like to cancel.
  • Click the Cancel Subscription button that appears after your Payment method.

What is the cancellation policy for the Great Courses Plus?

You may cancel at anytime. No refunds are provided. You will have access to the content through the end of your billing period. If you cancel within the free trial period, your membership will remain active until the end of your free trial period. For more information, see our Terms of Service.

Where can I find My Purchases, Downloads, and Order History from The Great Courses?

You can find all of your purchases, eligible downloads, and order history in the Purchased Courses section of the My Account page on this website. You can access My Account from the account menu at the top right of your screen.

Technical

Does a single membership to The Great Courses Plus work on all of my devices or do I have to pay for multiple memberships - one for each device?

You only need one membership. Your account works across all your devices and computers, and we’ll keep track of your viewing progress so you can switch devices and pick up right where you left off.

How can I watch my previously purchased courses?

You can watch your previously purchased courses by logging in to your account on The Great Courses website or apps. On the website, click SHOP at the top of the page or visit Shop.TheGreatCourses.com and select My Purchases to access and stream all of the courses you own.

How do I access courses purchased using an old The Great Courses account?

If your purchases were made using the same email address as your current account, they will automatically sync and can be found in My Purchases within My Account when you sign in. If your purchases were made using a different email address, please contact our customer service team at contactus@TheGreatCourses.com, and they can help combine everything into your current account.
