The Audio Vault: how the site works

The Audio Vault is a volunteer, donation-funded project that collects the audio track of audio-described films and television so that blind and low-vision people can find described content that is otherwise scattered across platforms and countries. This guide gathers what the project publishes about using it: 25 answers and posts in its own words, covering registration and password resets, searching by language, the daily download limit, how to request a title, what an AudioVault Original is and how to make one, and how to volunteer as a describer.

This guide is not a catalogue and carries no download links. The site’s listings and its front-page featured titles were cut before anything was read, and any download address that appeared in the prose was reduced to its own words. If you want to know what is available, that is a question for the site’s own search, which needs an account.

The news posts are included because they carry operational detail — how download limits changed, how to help supply titles, where the community talks. Announcements about the description of one particular title were set aside; there were 5 of them.

Gathered on 04 August 2026. A volunteer project changes; check anything that matters against the site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I request titles to be put on the site?

Yes. You can either email us or post your request on our Discord server. If possible, include helpful details like the platform it’s on, the year of release, and whether you know if it has audio description.

Could I create AVOs?

Absolutely. Anyone with enough vision to describe what’s happening on screen can create one. The only requirement is that it should sound clear and professional. You can also visit our Discord server for guidance or help from others.

Do you accept donations?

Yes, though the site is free to use for everyone, donations are appreciated. If you’d like to support the project, you can make a donation here

Currently PayPal is what we use to process donations, but this may change in the future.

Do you have Audio description in languages other than English?

Yes, we do. We aim to include a variety of languages such as Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Turkish, and more. To find titles in a specific language, go to the “Movies” or “Shows” section and search by language name—like “Spanish”—to see all available content in that language.

Having troubles unzipping a TV show using an iPhone or iPad?

  1. On your iOS Device go to settings, apps settings, Safari and then go down to downloads.
  2. Clicking on downloads a new section will appear. Under the section (Store Downloaded Files on). You want to change the setting from iCloud to iPhone. If you have an iPad change it to iPad. The default is usually set to iCloud and this is what causes the issue.
  3. Instead of the files app, try using either of the following apps iZip and or Media Converter

How can I add AD to titles when watching with friends or family? and or, the AD does not match with the video I have.

Watching with friends or family, Add audio description tracks to your video files with describealign, a free and open source tool with a screen reader accessible GUI! Amaze loved ones with your computer wizardry as misalignments from missing scenes or frame rates become distant memories of a frustrating past. Available as an application binary on Mac or Windows or as a python package. Details available in the README. Version 2.0 with drag-and-drop and even more accessibility coming this month!

I have an audio description track that is not available on the vault. Can I submit this track to be added?

We are always looking for tracks that are not widely available, for example TV broadcasts, to add to the collection. You can submit tracks by uploading them to a filehost of your choice. and getting in contact with us.

I have forgotten my Password, What do I do?

Please Contact Us with the new password you would like to have and we will reset it for you.

Is there a download limit? What settings should I use if I am using a download manager?

  • Open Internet Download Manager (IDM).
  • Press Alt to activate the menu bar, then press D to open the Downloads menu, use arrow keys to select Options, and press Enter.
  • In the Options dialog, press CtrlTab to navigate to the Connection tab.
  • Locate the dropdown labeled Default max. connection number. With arrow keys, choose 1 to reduce simultaneous connections.
  • alternatively, there is a box below that where you can add an exception for audiovault.net for max 1 connection ** (Exceptions>New) **
  • Press Tab to reach the OK button, and press Enter to save.

I’m interested in helping out—can I volunteer to write or voice audio description tracks?

Absolutely, we’d be glad to have you on board! If you’d like to volunteer—whether it’s writing descriptions for shows or voicing scripts that have already been written—just get in touch. You can either email us or connect with us on our Discord server. Let us know how you’d like to contribute, and we’ll guide you from there.

What are Audio Vault Originals?

AudioVault Originals (AVOs) are exclusive tracks created by volunteers for the audiovault. These are personal or community-driven projects developed out of interest and shared for the benefit of everyone.

What does [New Description] mean in a title?

This tag indicates that the movie or show already had audio description, but a new track has been created for it—with the same accent but different style. For example, you might see [UK] and [UK New Description] to distinguish between two different versions.

What does [TTS] mean?

[TTS] stands for Text-To-Speech. It indicates that the descriptive narration in the audio track is generated using a synthesized voice.

What is Audiovault?

This site contains the audio only portion of audio described film and TV shows. It is not intended to deprive copyright holders or service providers of income. Rather it is a small Project designed to make it easier for blind people to find and access audio described content that is spread across multiple platforms and countries.

Why does a TV show have missing seasons?

Unfortunately a lot of TV shows don’t have audio description for every single season. As much as we would like to provide such files, we are not able to due to them not being available. Content providers seem to skip certain seasons if not current or just don’t describe their back catalog, and or lack of volunteers willing to take them on. If you believe your favorite show definitely got the back catalog described, feel free to let us know on our discord server.

Why is there missing films in a specific franchise?

Generally this is the same as missing seasons within a show. Certain movie franchises like Friday the 13th won’t have all the movies described. As much as we would like to provide such files, we cannot do to them not being described by the streamer/company. We always do try to have a complete set of films or TV show seasons. If you have any information on where some of these films can be found with AD, please let us know on our discord server.

Download Limits

July 20, 2022 Important update regarding server usage and download limits. Hello there,

Please go through this news post at least once, as it is very important for everyone. oweing to the rampent abuse of the server resources by a certain section of our users, we have had to implement IP blocks, which in simple words means we will be going through and blocking the people who abuse the service and make the experience worse for the rest of our users.

As a result, we have limited downloads per user to 25 downloads a day, exceeding which will result in a ban.>

We are however willing to make exceptions, as long as no harm and or abuse was intended. Should you feel that you have are inocent of any wrongdoing and or if you have been banned due to an error such as not being aware of the 25 downloads per day limit, Please Contact Us With your details, and we will help you out. We have always been against limits and or restrictions of any sort fron the start, however in order to insure that all of our users have equal access and a better experience with the site, we have been forced to go down this path.

We will of course take other measures should it become necessary.

Our hope is that all of us by using the vault farely will make the user experience better for everyone, and not have the website unusable for the rest just because of a few.

Have a great day ahead,

What the Audio Vault is, in its own words

Welcome to the Audiovault. This site contains the audio only portion of audio described film and TV shows. It is not intended to deprive copyright holders or service providers of income. Rather it is a small Project designed to make it easier for blind people to find and access audio described content that is spread across multiple platforms and countries.

No video is hosted on this site. Nobody profits from this endeavour. It operates on donations from the small userbase purely in order to maintain the site.
