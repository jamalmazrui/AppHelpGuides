Google News Help Guide

This guide gathers Google’s official help for Google News, the news reader that assembles headlines, local coverage, full coverage of a story from many outlets, and newspaper and magazine subscriptions. It was taken from Google’s own help centre on 28 July 2026.

Google News runs in a browser on a computer and as an app on iPhone, iPad and Android. There is no Windows program and no Alexa skill.

One feature of Google’s writing shapes this guide. Google writes a separate copy of each article for each platform and chooses which to show by a parameter on the address, so the same article arrives three times over — computer, Android, and iPhone and iPad. Rather than pick one and discard the others, or repeat the article three times, each article here holds all the versions Google publishes, one after another under a heading naming the platform. Where Google publishes a single version for all devices, there is just the one.

The sections below are mine, not Google’s. Google News files its articles under topics inside its help centre, but those topic pages do not survive being taken out of it, so the grouping here is by subject and that choice is my own.

Publisher material is excluded: Google keeps a separate Publisher Help Center for news organisations, which is a different audience. The community forum is excluded too — that is other readers talking rather than Google’s guidance.

Headings run three deep: this title, then a section, then an article, with the platform versions and each article’s own subheadings nested beneath. Every article is listed in the table of contents below.

Fixing problems

Fix problems with Google News

On a computer

If you have problems with Google News, try these solutions. You can also get help on the Google News Help Forum where readers share tips and knowledge about Google News.

Open Google News

After each step, try again to open Google News.

  1. Restart your computer.
  2. Check whether you can open other websites.
  3. Quit your browser, like Chrome or Safari. Then re-open it.
  4. Learn how to clear your browsing data.
    • Note: If you delete your site data, you’ll also delete any saved settings.
Can’t access Google News

If you’re signed in to a Google Workspace account:

  • Your administrator might have turned off access to Google News. For more info, contact your account administrator.
  • News.google.com won’t be accessible unless you sign out of the account.
  • To access Google News, sign out of the account or switch to a personal Gmail account.
Find your personalized content
  1. Make sure you’re signed in to your Google Account.
  2. If you recently renamed a conflicting account, try to sign in with the new name.
  3. Reset your Google News interests. Learn how to improve what you find.
Block or unblock people who share

Important: This feature is only available if you’re signed in to your Google Account.

If you’d like to stop someone who directly shares stories with you on Google News, you can block their account. Learn how to block or unblock people.

Report spam or inappropriate content
Spam or inappropriate content

If you think sites violate our quality guidelines for spam, offensive, or inappropriate content, you can send us feedback.

  1. Go to Google News.
  2. At the top right, click Help Send feedback.
Inaccurate or outdated content

News organizations publish the stories and images you find on Google News.

  • For content problems: Contact the publisher.
  • To share other feedback about Google News:
    1. Go to Google News.
    2. At the top right, click Help Send feedback.

Add & customize your Google News widget

Android

Get the latest news with the Google News app widget on your Home screen.

Add the Google News app widget
  1. On your Android device, touch and hold the Home screen.
  2. Tap Widgets .
  3. If there are multiple widgets available, tap the Down arrow in the Google News section to get the whole widget list.
  4. Touch and hold the chosen Google News widget.
    • Images of your Home screens appear.
  5. Slide the widget to where you want it and lift your finger.

Tip: You can change the shape and size of the Google News widget. Learn more about how to resize a widget.

Remove the Google News widget
  1. Touch and hold the Google News widget.
  2. Under the List view, tap Remove .

You might find options to Remove or Uninstall. To take the widget off your Home screen only, remove it. To take the widget off your phone, uninstall it. Learn how to remove an app, shortcut, widget, or group.

Manage your Google News notifications

Android

You can choose the amount and types of notifications you receive from Google News. In the Google News app, you can manage your notifications.

Check your notifications
  1. On your mobile device, open the Google News app .
  2. At the top right, tap your Profile picture or initial.
  3. Tap Notifications .
Change your notifications
  1. On your mobile device, open the Google News app .
  2. At the top right, tap your Profile picture or initial.
  3. Tap News settings .
  4. Under “Alerts,” tap Notifications.
    • To get notifications, turn on Get notifications.
    • To stop all notifications, turn off Get notifications.
  5. To control the amount of notifications you get, under “Number of notifications” tap an amount between “Low” and “High.”
    • To choose what type of notifications you get, next to each notification type, turn notifications on or off.
Update your daily briefing emails & notifications
  1. On your mobile device, open the Google News app .
  2. At the top right, tap your Profile picture or initial.
  3. Tap News settings .
  4. To get your daily briefing, under “Alerts,” turn on Daily briefing emails.

Tip: To receive daily briefing emails and notifications, you must sign in to your Google Account.

Customize what you find on Google News

On a computer

Important: This feature is only available if you’re signed in to your Google Account.

You can customize Google News to get stories about specific topics, like sports teams, technology, or space exploration. The updates from the interests you follow show up in the “Following” tab’s content feed.

To get more or less stories you want:

  • Follow or unfollow an interest like a topic, location, or source.
  • Like or unlike specific stories about a topic or from a source.
  • Hide stories from a source.
Choose what stories to get
Follow an interest
  1. At the top, search for the topic, location, or source you want to follow.
    • You can also select a topic from the menu at the top.
  2. At the top right, click Follow.

Tip: To find all the interests you follow, from the menu at the top, click Following.

Unfollow an interest

If you no longer want to get updates or news about a topic, location, or source:

  1. At the top, use the search bar to find the interest you want to unfollow.
  2. At the top, click Following.

Or

  1. From the menu on the left, click Following.
  2. Next to the interest you want to unfollow, click More Remove from library.
Like or unlike a story

If you want to get more or less stories about a certain topic or source:

  1. Point to a story’s headline.
  2. Under the headline, next to the name of the source, click More .
    • To get more stories like it: Click More stories like this.
    • To get fewer stories like it: Click Fewer stories like this.
Hide stories from a source
  1. Find a story from the source you no longer want to get updates from.
  2. Point to the story’s headline or title and click More Hide all stories from [source] .
How Google News uses your Web & App Activity

Your Google Account saves your searches and other activity across Google products to My Activity. Google News can personalize your news based on some of those activities. Learn how to customize what you find on Google Search.

Find what you want on Google News

On a computer

On Google News, you can find different kinds of news, and audio stories. Some sections cover the same subjects for readers. Some pick stories based on your interests.

Important:

  • Some features are available if you’re signed in to your Google Account.
  • If you share a paid story, others may not access it until they pay for it.
Get more headlines & perspectives

A computer algorithm finds and lists stories for you in Google News. If you’re interested in a story, you can click See more headlines & perspectives to get the full coverage available in Google News with more resources, perspectives, and context. The story page isn’t personalized for you, except for your language, region, and subscriptions.

To find all available sources for a story or topic: At the bottom of a story, click See more headlines & perspectives .

To find sources on “See more headlines & perspectives” that you subscribe to: Scroll to “From your subscribed sources.”

Search for specific content
  1. Go to Google News.
  2. At the top, click Search .
  3. Enter your search, like a topic to read about, place to follow, or source to read from.

Tip: To narrow your search results, click the Down arrow . You can enter an exact phrase, include or exclude words, pick a date range, or enter a publisher site (like nytimes.com).

Find content based on your interests

You can find stories picked by an algorithm that are based on your settings and past activity. Algorithms personalize your Google News settings based on your interests, sources, and past activity on Google, like in Google Search and YouTube. Learn how stories are selected or how to change settings and see past activity.

  1. Go to Google News.
  2. At the top, click For you.
Read your saved stories or subscriptions
  1. Go to Google News.
  2. At the top, click Following.
  3. At the top, click the content you want. For example:
    • Topics & sources
    • Saved searches
    • Saved stories
Improve what stories show for you

You can personalize Google News to show more of the kinds of stories you want. Learn how to personalize Google News.

Subscriptions and payments

Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play

Subscriptions on Google Play are for an indefinite term, and you’ll be charged at the beginning of each billing cycle according to your subscription terms (for example, weekly, annually, or another period), unless you unsubscribe.

You can use our self-help flow to cancel a subscription.

Cancel a subscription

Cancel a subscription on play.google.com

Important: When you uninstall an app, your subscription won’t cancel.

  1. On your computer, go to subscriptions in Google Play.
  2. For the subscription you want to cancel, click Manage.
  3. Click Cancel subscription.
  4. Within the confirmation pop-up, select a reason.
  5. Click Continue.

Tip: If you have a subscription with an app and it’s removed from Google Play, your future subscription will be cancelled. Your past subscriptions can’t be refunded, with some exceptions as specified in this article or in Google Play’s refund policies.

What happens after you cancel

Important: If you’ve committed to multiple payments for your subscription by purchasing with a payment plan, refer to the section below titled “What happens after you stop renewal on a payment plan” for information about canceling.

When you cancel a subscription, you’ll still be able to use your subscription for the time you’ve already paid. In some instances, you may be eligible for a refund. For more information, learn more about refunds on Google Play.

For example, if you buy a one-year subscription on January 1 for $10 and decide to cancel your subscription on July 1:

  • You’ll have access to the subscription until December 31.
  • You won’t be charged another yearly subscription of $10 the following January 1.

What happens after you cancel Play Pass

Any apps you installed from Play Pass and their data will remain on your device.

For content you obtained via Play Pass:

  • Paid apps will prompt you to buy the app or subscribe to Play Pass.
  • In-app purchases will be removed, but available for individual purchase.
  • Ads will reappear in apps where they were removed.
  • If you join Play Pass again, all this content will be unlocked again.

What happens after you cancel a prepaid plan

Prepaid plans last for a limited time, so you don’t need to cancel them. They automatically expire at the end of the billing period.

If your prepaid plan hasn’t been used, you can request a refund. Learn more about refunds on Google Play.

To cancel an unused prepaid subscription plan:

  1. On your computer, go to subscriptions on Google Play.
  2. Select the subscription or prepaid plan you want to cancel.
  3. Click Cancel subscription.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions.
What happens after you stop renewal on a payment plan

Once your payment method is charged for a payment plan, you can’t cancel any remaining payments for that payment plan. However, you can choose to stop your payment plan from auto-renewing. This means you won’t be charged on your next subscription renewal date (which you can view in Subscriptions on Google Play or on your email receipt), and you can continue to access your content until then. If you choose to stop your payment plan from renewing, you’ll still be responsible for any remaining payments on your current payment plan. Google Play will not issue refunds except as otherwise provided in Google Play’s Refund Policy.

See Subscribe to services or content for more information.

If you receive a refund for a subscription that was initially purchased with Split Payment, the funds return to your Google Play balance and the original payment method(s) used. Pause a subscription

  1. On your computer, go to subscriptions on Google Play.
  2. Select the subscription you want to pause.
  3. Click Manage Pause payments.

  5. Click Confirm.

Restart payments for a paused subscription

You can resume your subscription anytime.

  1. On your computer, go to subscriptions on Google Play.
  2. Select the subscription you want to start again.
  3. Click Manage Resume.
    • Your subscription will restart immediately.

Tip: If you can’t find your subscription, it may be on a different account. You may try to switch between accounts.

Change the payment method for a subscription

When your subscription renews, the same payment method you used to buy the subscription will be charged. An authorization hold may be placed on your form of payment up to 48 hours prior to the beginning of the next renewal period, or up to 5 days before the next renewal period if you’re based in India or Brazil.

  1. On your computer, go to subscriptions on Google Play.
  2. Next to the subscription you want to update, click Manage.
  3. Next to the primary payment method, click Update.

Important: If your payment method is declined or has insufficient funds, your subscription may be cancelled. If this happens, set up your subscription again.

Important:

  • The Split Payment option, which allows you to combine your Google Play balance with another payment method, applies only to the initial subscription sign up charge or an upgrade charge.
  • If you don’t use Split Payment at sign up, the full amount of all subsequent renewals of your subscription is charged entirely to the primary payment method or any backups you’ve configured.
  • Your email receipt for the initial subscription sign up or upgrade charge lists both payment methods used and reminds you that future renewals will use your primary payment method.

  4. Click Manage.
  5. Select the plan you want to change to.
  6. When a confirmation pop-up appears, click Yes.

Change between subscription plans or billing cycles

  • Monthly

  1. Open the app.

  3. Based on the subscription, you may have to pay the full price or the price difference of the new plan.

On a computer

You can subscribe for paid premium services and features on certain apps with Google Play.

When you subscribe:

  • The subscription is for an indefinite term, and you’ll be charged at the start of each billing period, according to your subscription terms, for example, weekly, annually, or another period.
  • If you accept a trial offer, you’ll get an email when your trial is about to end.
    • At the end of your trial period, the first billing period begins and you automatically get charged the amount in your subscriptions terms.
    • To avoid charges, cancel the subscription before the trial period expires.
  • An authorization hold may be placed on your form of payment up to 48 hours prior to the beginning of the next renewal period, or up to 5 days before the next renewal period if you’re based in India or Brazil. To remove the payment authorization, cancel the subscription before your next billing cycle begins.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time in Subscriptions on Google Play. When you cancel, you’ll lose access to the subscription at the end of your current billing period, unless you purchased your subscription with a payment plan. Refer to “Stop auto-renewal of a payment plan” below to understand how cancellation works for payment plans.
  • For certain jurisdictions, you have these options:
    • Cancel your subscription with immediate effect and lose access.
    • Cancel the subscription at the end of the current billing cycle. Learn how to cancel your subscription.
  • You can also add a second payment method to your Google Play account to help prevent interruptions to your subscription service in case your primary payment method expires or fails. Learn how to add a backup payment method.
  • If your payment is unsuccessful, you may have a grace period to update your payment method. During the grace period, you may still be able to use your subscription. When the grace period ends, or if there’s no grace period, your account may be placed on hold for up to 60 days during which you can’t use the subscription. During any grace period and account hold period, Play will periodically retry the payment. If the payment issue isn’t resolved before any grace period and account hold period ends, the subscription is automatically canceled.
  • Some apps and games are restricted based on the content rating because they target only users who are 18 and above or because app developers have chosen not to make them available to users under 18. This means app visibility and purchases, including subscription sign-ups, are limited to users who meet the age criteria. Developers may change their age criteria at any time and subscriptions are subject to cancellation if your account or developer settings change and no longer fit the criteria.
    • Learn more about Apps & Games content ratings on Google Play and how to set up parental controls on Google Play.
  • Google may share subscription data that doesn’t personally identify you with developers to help them offer subscriptions.
  • Promotional discounts tied to a subscription will be lost on subscription cancellation.

Pay with Split Payment for subscriptions

What is Split Payment for subscriptions?

When you initially subscribe to services or content or upgrade your subscription tier on Google Play, “Split Payment” allows you to combine your current Google Play balance with another payment method like a credit card or PayPal to cover the cost of your initial subscription sign up or upgrade payment. This allows you to use any remaining Google Play balance towards your purchase.

Applying your Google Play balance

If the cost of your initial subscription sign up or upgrade payment is greater than your available Google Play balance, your current Google Play balance will be applied by default to the initial payment. The remaining amount due will be charged to another payment method of your choice.

Payment combinations

You can combine your Google Play balance with any other accepted payment method. However, it isn’t possible to split a payment between two non-Google Play balance payment methods. You can’t choose to apply only a portion of your Google Play balance. You must apply the entire remaining balance.

Initial payment vs. renewals

The Split Payment option applies only to the initial subscription sign up or upgrade payment. For all subsequent subscription renewal payments, only your primary payment method and any backups you’ve configured will be charged without using the split payment method again.

How to use Split Payment

  1. Go to play.google.com.
  2. Make sure you’re signed in to the correct Google Account.
  3. Find and open an app that you want to subscribe to.
  4. Click Subscribe.
  5. Choose your subscription option.
  6. Review your subscription and plan details.
  7. If you have an available Google Play balance, your Google Play balance should automatically show up as one of the two payment methods in your purchase cart.
  8. If your available Google Play balance doesn’t show and you’d like to use Split Payment:
    1. To open the Payment Methods selection screen, click on the displayed payment method.
    2. Toggle on Google Play balance.
    3. If you don’t wish to use your Google Play balance, toggle it off.
  9. To complete checkout, click Subscribe.

When you can’t use Split Payment

Split Payment isn’t available for subscriptions that include a trial at no cost or other discount or promotion, or for prepaid subscription plans.

Subscribe with a season pass

A season pass is a recurring or non-recurring subscription that grants you access to a season of content. If you purchase a recurring season pass, it’ll automatically renew at the start of the next season, on the date set by the developer. Some season passes may be purchased with a payment plan.

You can cancel a season pass anytime. When you cancel your season pass, you’ll lose access to the subscription at the end of the current billing period or, if you purchased your subscription with a monthly payment plan, at the end of your payment plan. See section titled “Stop auto-renewal of a payment plan” below for more information on cancellation behavior for season passes on payment plans.

For more details on your season pass, visit Subscriptions on Google Play or contact the app developer.

Subscribe with a payment plan

When you subscribe to content using a payment plan, you commit to multiple payments over a specified period of time. If you purchase a recurring payment plan, it’ll automatically renew on your renewal date. You also have the flexibility to prepay your full outstanding amount anytime.

For information on payment plan availability, go to Location eligibility for subscription payment plans. If you are on a payment plan, visit Subscriptions on Google Play or review your email receipt for information about your plan. Payment Plans are subject to the Google Play Payment Plan Terms of Service.

Stop auto-renewal of a payment plan

Once your payment method is charged for a payment plan, you can’t cancel any remaining payments for that payment plan. However, you can choose to stop your payment plan from auto-renewing. This means you won’t be charged on your next subscription renewal date which you can view in Subscriptions on Google Play or on your email receipt, and you can continue to access your content until then. If you choose to stop your payment plan from renewing, you’ll still be responsible for any remaining payments on your current payment plan. Google Play will not issue refunds except as otherwise provided in Google Play’s Refund Policy.

Subscribe with a prepaid plan

Prepaid plans are subscriptions for a fixed duration of time offered by some apps, and they don’t automatically renew. If your payment method doesn’t support recurring subscriptions, a prepaid plan might be a good option for you.

Unlike regular subscriptions, prepaid plans are used to purchase access to subscriptions for a set amount of time. When the term ends, you lose access to the subscription.

If you want to continue your access to the subscription after the prepaid period ends, you can:

  • Extend the prepaid plan.
  • Switch the plan to a recurring subscription.

Not all apps have a prepaid plan option available. Prepaid plans don’t support trials at no cost.

  1. Go to play.google.com.
  2. Make sure you’re signed in to the correct Google Account.
  3. Find and open an app that offers prepaid plans for a fee.
  4. Click Subscribe.
  5. Choose the prepaid plan you want to purchase.
  6. Select a supported payment method.
  7. Click Buy.
Automatic, balance-based purchases

As an alternative to making individual purchases of an item, some developers may offer a plan that automatically purchases the item based on your usage. You can cancel these automatic purchase plans at any time in Subscriptions on Google Play. Learn more about automatic, balance-based purchases.

Start a subscription from a website
  1. On the website that offers a subscription, select Subscribe with your Google Account or Subscribe with Google.
  2. Make sure you’re signed in to the correct Google Account.
  3. Select a subscription offer, like yearly or monthly. This may depend on the types of subscription the website offers.
  4. Confirm your payment method and billing address.

Subscribe with Google

You can subscribe to online content from select newspapers directly from a browser on your mobile device or computer.

Add an existing subscription to your Google Account

If you already have a subscription but are asked to subscribe on a Google product like Google News, Newsstand, or Search, select Already a subscriber?. Then, sign in with the email address you used to buy it. You could see the option to add your subscription to your Google Account, which lets you see your subscription content anywhere you’re signed into your Google Account.

You can also add digital versions of your print subscriptions to Google Play or the Google News and Newsstand apps.Learn about digital subscriptions for print subscribers.

Learn how to see all your linked subscriptions in My Account. Subscriptions can include Google products, outside services, and those in the Play Store.

If you don’t want a subscription to show up in your Google Account:

  1. In your Google Account, go to the Subscriptions page.
  2. Select Unlink.

Your subscription won’t be canceled or changed. But you’ll no longer see content based on your subscription in Google.

Subscriptions in search results

You may see content from your linked subscriptions in your Google search results. You can turn this off in Search Settings.
