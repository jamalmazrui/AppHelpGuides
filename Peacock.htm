This guide gathers Peacock’s own help as it stood on 3 August 2026. The text is Peacock’s; nothing has been summarised or rewritten.

This is not television listings. Nothing here tells you what is on or when. It is the documentation for using Peacock: what a subscription costs, which devices work, signing in, payment and billing, and getting the app onto a Roku, a Fire Stick, a Chromecast or a smart TV.

This is a first edition, and it is incomplete. Here is exactly what is missing and why. Peacock draws its help centre in the browser and loads each category’s article list only as a reader scrolls down it. Two of its six categories – Video Playback and Technical Assistance – never gave up their lists to an automated reader, and Device Setup and Login gave up only part of its own. So this edition is strong on plans, payment and getting started, and holds nothing at all about playback problems, captions, audio description, or what to do when a video will not start. Those are exactly the articles most worth having, and they are the ones not here. A later edition should carry them.

The arrangement is Peacock’s own, taken from the trail at the top of each article rather than invented here.

Getting Started

Can I use Chromecast to stream Peacock from my mobile device to my TV?

If your devices have the right functionality, you can! We support this through Chromecast. For steps and assistance using this feature, please see below.

Steps to use Chromecast:

· Open the Peacock app.

· Select the content you wish to view.

· Select PLAY.

· Select the Chromecast icon at the top of the screen.

· Select your streaming device.

· When you are connected, the Chromecast icon will change color and the Peacock movie, show, or series will appear on your device.

Chromecast troubleshooting:

· Verify your Google Home app is installed on your mobile device.

· The Google Home app is available in the iTunes Store and Google Play Store.

· Make sure your Chromecast device is connected to your TV.

· Make sure the Chromecast device and your streaming device are both connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

How can I access Peacock on my Smart TV?

Please follow the below steps to access Peacock on your Smart TV.

  • Ensure your device is supported by Peacock. You can do so by checking if your device is listed here.
  • From your home screen, select the search bar and type “Peacock” to search in your installed applications.
    • If it does not show up, or is not available, select the graphic that says App Store on your home screen.
    • Select the search bar at the top of the screen and type “Peacock.” Press Select.
  • Select the Peacock app icon.
  • Select the download icon.
  • After the Peacock app installs, press the play icon, or Open.
  • If you have an existing account, select the “Sign In” button. If not, select “Pick a Plan”, you’ll then be prompted to sign up for Peacock.

To locate any content in Peacock, you can search using the Search box with the magnifying glass icon, or by selecting the icon of the specific content you wish to watch.

You can also browse specifically curated groups of content by visiting the Movies, TV Shows, Sports, or WWE hubs listed on the Home Screen of Peacock. To view the selection of live content available, you can visit the Channels section.

How can I use Peacock on my Amazon Firestick or Fire TV?

If you have not already signed up for Peacock on our website, you can sign up for Peacock on your Amazon Firestick or Fire TV by following the steps below.

  1. From your home screen, select the magnifying glass (Find).
  2. Select the graphic that says Appstore.
  3. Select the search bar at the top of the screen and type “Peacock.” Press select.
  4. Select the Peacock app icon.
  5. Select the download icon (Get).
  6. After the Peacock app installs, press the play icon (Open).
  7. Select Pick a Plan. You’ll then be prompted to sign up for Peacock.

After going through the sign-up process, you’ll be a Peacock subscriber and can start watching immediately.

How can I use Peacock on Roku?

If you have not already signed up for Peacock on our website, you can sign up for Peacock on Roku or Roku TV by following the steps below.

  1. From your Roku home screen, select Streaming Store.
  2. From the Streaming Store page, select Search.
  3. Type “Peacock” into the search and then select the Peacock icon.
  4. Select Add App. After it downloads, press OK.
  5. Select Open to start Peacock.
  6. Select Pick a Plan. You’ll then be prompted to sign up for Peacock.

After going through the sign-up process, you’ll be a Peacock subscriber and can start watching immediately.

How much does a Peacock subscription cost?

You can sign up for a Peacock Premium plan at $10.99 a month (+tax) or $109.99 per year (+tax).

If you are looking for all of our content with no ads (limited exclusions)*, you can sign up for a Peacock Premium Plus plan for $16.99 a month (+tax) or $169.99 per year (+tax).

*Certain pages are sponsored, and some programming will still contain ads (channels, live sports and events, and a few shows and movies).

Forgot Peacock Password: Reset Instructions

How to reset my forgotten Peacock password?

Whether you are looking to shake things up or you have just forgotten your password, resetting it is easy.

  1. Go to your account by clicking on Sign in at the top right corner of the website or app.
  2. Select “Forgot Password?”
  3. Enter the email address that you used when you signed up for Peacock and click on Continue
  4. We’ll email you a link to reset your password.
  5. Once you’ve received the password reset email, simply follow the instructions provided. The link will be live for 3 hours and you’ll only be able to use it once.

What happens if I can’t remember my Peacock email address I signed up with?

If you are signed in on your device, you will be able to locate this by navigating to your account in the application. Your email address is in the bottom left-hand corner. If you are not signed in, that’s ok. Try these tips to locate your login details or contact us and we’ll investigate for you.

** How do I change my Peacock password? **

  1. Go to your account by clicking on Sign in at the top right corner of the website.
  2. Once signed in, click on the icon in the top right corner of the website or app and select “Account”
  3. There are three tabs along the top, click “Settings”
  4. Scroll down until you see “Password”, “Reset Password”
  5. Click and follow the prompts

Tips for creating your Peacock password

  1. Passwords must be at least 10 characters long and must contain both letters and numbers.
  2. Use mixed characters, upper-case, lower-case, numbers, and symbols.
  3. Avoid using personal information such as your name or birthday.

How do I change my payment method or billing address on Peacock?

Got a shiny new payment card, or fancy new billing address? You can change those details easily online in your account.

Simply go to the Plans & Payment tab and then, under the Payment Method section, click on Update Payment Method.

To change your payment type to PayPal, click on the PayPal tab under “Payment Method”.

Once you’ve confirmed your new payment details, payments will be billed to your new payment method beginning with the next billing cycle.

How do I change my Peacock plan?

At some point, you may want to switch between Premium and Premium Plus subscriptions. The process for doing this depends on whether you’re subscribed through Peacock directly, or through a third party such as Roku, Apple, Amazon, or Google. Please see below for the processes for direct billing and third-party billing.

Changing Plans With Direct Billing

If you’re subscribed through Peacock directly, you will need to visit your Account on the web to make any changes to your plan.

Click on Change Plan to switch between Premium Plus and Premium plans.

Changing Plans With Third-Party Billing

If you’re subscribed through a third party, it will say so on your Account screen.

Click on “Change Plan” to adjust your plan or billing frequency.

Here, you will be able to change between Premium and Premium Plus. The toggle on the left will let you choose between monthly and annual billing for each plan.

How do I check charges on my Peacock account?

Step 1:

Go to your account by clicking on Sign In at the top right corner of the website.

Step 2:

Once you’ve signed in, go to the Plans & Payment section of your account. You’ll see any charges associated with this account under View Payment History.

How do I check that my promotional code was successfully applied?

You can verify that your promotional code was successfully applied by going to your account. If you successfully applied your promotional code, you will see your current promotional offer under Your Plan.

How do I find out if my credit card is associated with multiple Peacock accounts?

To check if your credit card is associated with multiple Peacock accounts, sign in with your email address (or email addresses) to see if they are registered on Peacock.

To review your billing information, follow the steps below:

  • Navigate to your account by clicking on Sign In at the top right corner of the website.
  • Once you’ve signed in, go to the Plans & Payment section of your account to see if there are any charges associated with your account.

If you still need assistance, we’re here to help. Select the Get in Touch button on this page and we’ll investigate for you.

How do I see if my Peacock subscription is being processed through third party in-app billing?

You can check all these details in your account. On the Plans & Payment tab, under Your Plan you will see your method of payment which will indicate if your subscription is paid through a third party like Apple.

How do I view my payment history or upcoming payments on Peacock?

For subscribers who have subscribed directly through Peacock, you can review your upcoming charges and payment history in the Plans & Payment section of our Account page. For subscribers who are subscribed to Peacock through a third-party (e.g., Apple, Google, Roku, etc.), you will need to contact the third-party for your payment information.

For direct billed subscribers to view your payment history, please follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Account page.

  2. Select View Payment History on the Plans & Payment tab of your Account page.

Why is my billing date changing for my Premium Plus account?

We are in the process of simplifying your billing so that you will get fewer charges per billing cycle. Previously, your charges for Peacock Premium and the Plus option may have been on different dates.

Beginning with your next billing date, we will charge you the total price for Premium Plus as one charge each month.

Your new billing date will be the later of your two current billing dates. Please note, any offer pricing you have will carry over until the offer expires, at which point you will be billed the current retail price for Premium Plus.

To see your billing information, please visit the Plans & Payment section of your Account.

If you have a Premium Annual subscription with the Plus Monthly Option, you will continue to be charged separately until further notice. Check your email for complete details.
