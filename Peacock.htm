Apple TV and Peacock Bundle

What is the Apple TV and Peacock Bundle?

The Apple TV and Peacock bundle is a streaming plan that gives you access to both platforms in one convenient plan.

With this bundle, you can enjoy Apple TV, including exclusive Apple Originals, and Peacock Premium content – including hit series, fan-favorite shows from NBC and Bravo, blockbuster movies, and live sports – all starting at $14.99 a month.

You can subscribe to both services in a single transaction, through either Peacock or Apple. To review the plans and pricing, please visit the Plan Picker page.

How much does the bundle cost?

There are two different Peacock and Apple TV subscription bundles available:

  • Peacock Premium + Apple TV/$14.99 a month

 

  • Peacock Premium Plus + Apple TV/$19.99 a month

Where can I watch content from both services?

You can stream Apple TV content in the Apple TV app, and Peacock content via the Peacock app. However, there will be a limited amount of Apple TV and Peacock episodes available to sample on both platforms.

Am I able to purchase the bundle if I am already subscribed to Peacock?

If you are already an existing Peacock subscriber, you are still able to upgrade to the Apple TV and Peacock bundle by signing up in the Apple TV app, the Peacock app on iOS, iPadOS and tvOS, or on tv.apple.com. To avoid having multiple subscriptions, you may need to manage any existing subscriptions before signing up for the bundle.

If your existing Peacock subscription is billed by Peacock directly, you may also purchase the bundle by signing into your Account on the Peacock website and navigating to the “Plans & Payment” page. Under “Your Plan”, you can select “Change Plan” to review the Apple TV and Peacock Bundle options. Please note, if you are currently on a Peacock Annual plan, your access to the bundle will not begin until your existing Annual plan expires.

Can I still purchase the monthly Plus add-on?

Effective April 1, 2025, we are no longer offering Plus as a monthly add-on subscription. Although the Plus monthly option will be discontinued, you can still enjoy the benefits of Premium Plus by upgrading your Premium plan to Premium Plus.

To upgrade, please visit the Plans & Payment section of your Account and select “Change Plan”, this will provide you with the option to select Premium Plus. You will be charged the then-current retail price for the Premium Plus subscription.

Once you complete the upgrade process, your subscription will be automatically adjusted to reflect the new Premium Plus plan.

If applicable, a refund will be issued to your original payment method on file for any unused days in your current Premium plan. The refunded amount should appear on your payment method within 7-10 business days.

Can I use Chromecast to stream Peacock from my mobile device to my TV?

If your devices have the right functionality, you can! We support this through Chromecast. For steps and assistance using this feature, please see below.

Steps to use Chromecast:

· Open the Peacock app.

· Select the content you wish to view.

· Select PLAY.

· Select the Chromecast icon at the top of the screen.

· Select your streaming device.

· When you are connected, the Chromecast icon will change color and the Peacock movie, show, or series will appear on your device.

Chromecast troubleshooting:

· Verify your Google Home app is installed on your mobile device.

· The Google Home app is available in the iTunes Store and Google Play Store.

· Make sure your Chromecast device is connected to your TV.

· Make sure the Chromecast device and your streaming device are both connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Do I have to use my Xfinity email address to register my Peacock account?

No! You can choose any email address to register your Peacock account. We recommend that you use an active email address that you will remember and can access.

How can I access Peacock on my Smart TV?

Please follow the below steps to access Peacock on your Smart TV.

  • Ensure your device is supported by Peacock. You can do so by checking if your device is listed here.
  • From your home screen, select the search bar and type “Peacock” to search in your installed applications.
    • If it does not show up, or is not available, select the graphic that says App Store on your home screen.
    • Select the search bar at the top of the screen and type “Peacock.” Press Select.
  • Select the Peacock app icon.
  • Select the download icon.
  • After the Peacock app installs, press the play icon, or Open.
  • If you have an existing account, select the “Sign In” button. If not, select “Pick a Plan”, you’ll then be prompted to sign up for Peacock.

To locate any content in Peacock, you can search using the Search box with the magnifying glass icon, or by selecting the icon of the specific content you wish to watch.

You can also browse specifically curated groups of content by visiting the Movies, TV Shows, Sports, or WWE hubs listed on the Home Screen of Peacock. To view the selection of live content available, you can visit the Channels section.

Peacock Gift Cards

Purchasing a Gift Card

Where can I buy a Peacock Gift Card?

You can purchase digital Peacock Gift Cards online. At this time, physical Gift Cards are not for sale.

What forms of payment can I use to purchase a Peacock Gift Card?

You may pay for a Peacock Gift Card via credit card (Visa/MasterCard/American Express), debit card, PayPal, or Google Pay.

Where is the Peacock Gift Card I ordered? / I didn’t receive my email with my Gift Card purchase.

Digital Gift Cards will be sent via email or text as indicated as checkout. If you have not received your Gift Card, please check your Spam filters or Junk folders.

Digital Peacock Gift Cards are sold through the third-party company, Buyatab. If you need further assistance, locating your order, you may also contact Buyatab directly at merchantemail@buyatab.com or (855)-818-9719.

Can I get the remaining balance on my Peacock Gift Card refunded to me?

Peacock Gift Cards are non-refundable unless required by applicable law. For additional information, please review the full Peacock Gift Card Terms.

Balance & Redemption

How do I check my Peacock Gift Card balance?

If you have redeemed part of your Peacock Gift Card, you are able to check the remaining balance by visiting your My Account page, under the “Plans & Payment” tab.

If you haven’t redeemed your Peacock Gift Card yet, you can check the balance by reaching out to Buyatab directly at merchantemail@buyatab.com or (855)-818-9719.

How do I redeem my Peacock Gift Card?

To redeem your Peacock Gift Card, please follow the steps below:

  • Follow the link provided in the email where your Gift Card was delivered or visit our Peacock Gift Cards page
  • Enter your Gift Card Number
  • Select “Get Started”
    • If you are a new subscriber, choose the plan you’d like to purchase (Premium or Premium Plus)
      • From there, you can enter your details and select “Create Account”
    • If you are an existing subscriber, select “Sign In”
      • If the balance of the Gift Card is enough to purchase the entire subscription, you can choose if you’d like to add another payment method to your account for future payments
      • If the balance of the Gift Card is not enough to purchase the entire subscription, you will need to add another payment method to complete the purchase
What can I use my Peacock Gift Cards for?

Peacock Gift Cards may be used to purchase Peacock Premium and Premium Plus subscriptions.

Do Peacock Gift Cards ever expire?

Peacock Gift Cards do not expire.

Can a current subscriber redeem a Peacock Gift Card?

Yes, existing subscribers who have purchased a Peacock Premium or Peacock Premium Plus subscription directly through Peacock can use a Peacock Gift Card toward the balance of a future payment. Once you add a Gift Card as a payment method option, it will be the primary payment method used until depleted, or until the balance due is no longer covered by the Gift Card on file.

Can Peacock Gift Cards be used to purchase Peacock subscriptions through other partners, such as Apple or Google?

No, they may only be used to purchase a Peacock Premium or Premium Plus subscription directly through Peacock. They cannot be used with any other websites or partners.

Why is Peacock increasing its price?

These price changes allow Peacock to continue to create the best experience for its viewers, remain competitive in the marketplace, and deliver unique content across all genres. When is it increasing?

How do I fix issues with slow loading or buffering on Peacock?

When you watch Peacock, the video transmission isn’t sent to your device in one continuous “stream”. Instead, it’s sent in bite-size “packets”. If there’s a delay in transmission, your show pauses while it waits for the next packet. This is known as buffering.


Quick buffering tips:

  • Reduce the number of devices in use on the same network. Having multiple devices simultaneously use the same network can slow loading time. Disconnecting other devices on your network could help speed up your Peacock streaming.
  • Stop any large simultaneous downloads on the same network. If you’re playing online games, downloading or uploading large files while trying to watch a Peacock show on the same network, this could cause buffering.
  • Test your internet connection speed. We recommend a minimum speed of 3.0 Mbps for you to be able to enjoy our content. However, to enjoy certain high-quality live events and ultra-high definition premium content, we recommend your Wi-Fi or 4G/5G/LTE speeds to be at least 8 Mbps.

Sign up here to watch all of our LIVE sports, sports shows, documentaries, classic matches, and more. You’ll also get tons of hit movies and TV shows, Originals, news, 24/7 channels, and current NBC & Bravo hits—Peacock is here for whatever you’re in the mood for.

Here’s what else you get with Peacock:
  • New movies straight from theaters—plus hundreds of movies from major Hollywood studios such as Universal, DreamWorks Animation, and Focus Features.
  • Thousands of hours of hit TV shows, including highly anticipated Peacock Originals and current hits from NBC & Bravo.
  • Peacock Channels playing your favorite movies, shows, and clips 24/7. Scroll less and stream more with NBC Sports on Peacock, SNL Vault, Fallon Tonight, TODAY All Day, and True Crime.
  • The LIVE sports you love all in one place, including Sunday Night Football and Premier League.
  • Kids’ movies and shows, including Where’s Waldo? and Curious George.
  • Hit Spanish-language TV shows and news from Telemundo.
