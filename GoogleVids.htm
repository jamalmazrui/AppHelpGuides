This guide holds Google’s own help for Google Vids, Google’s video maker for work, gathered from support.google.com on 14 November 2026. It covers starting a video, adding scenes, recordings, voiceovers and captions, customizing a video, and making one with Gemini. It is a young product, so this guide is short.

Google keeps the help for Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms and Vids in one help centre, and this guide holds the part of it about Vids. Google writes the same article once for each device it runs on, so where an article differs by platform the heading says which one it is for: Computer, or iPhone and iPad. Google’s Android editions are left out, as this collection covers Windows, the web, iPhone and iPad, and Alexa. The categories are Google’s own topics.

Add content to a video

Add & arrange text & shapes in Vids

Important: Learn which Google Account types have access to Google Vids and different Vids features.

You can add text, shapes, and other objects to your scenes in Google Vids.

Add text or objects to a scene

To insert texts or shapes to a scene in a video:

  • On your computer, open a video in Google Vids.

  • Select the scene where you want to add a text box or object.

  • From the sidebar, select Shapes or Text .

  • In the side panel, click the object you want to insert. To remove text or shapes from a scene in a video:

  • On your computer, open a video in Google Vids.

  • Select the text box or object you want to remove press Delete.

Arrange & align objects in a scene

To arrange objects on a scene’s canvas:

  • On your computer, open a video in Google Vids.
  • Select the scene where you want to arrange an object.
  • In the “Menu,” click Arrange.
  • Choose from these options:
    • Order: Put the object behind or in front of text, other objects, or images.
    • Align: When you select multiple objects, you can align their edges.
    • Distribute: When you select 3 or more objects, you can evenly distribute the space between them.
    • Center on page: Center objects vertically or horizontally in a scene.
    • Rotate: Change the orientation of an object.
    • Group: Lock multiple objects together to make them easier to move around and format.

Resize or rotate an object in a scene

To change the size or rotation of objects on a scene’s canvas:

  • On your computer, open a video in Google Vids.
  • Select the scene with the object or text box you want to change.
  • Click the object or text box.
  • Click on the object click Format options .
  • In the format options side panel, click Size & rotation.
  • Tips:
    • To keep the height and width proportions when you resize an object, select Lock aspect ratio.
    • In the format options side panel, you can also add customized formatting to text and shapes.
  • Make changes to the object or text box.

Google Vids storage, object & version history limits

Learn about the limits for Vids storage, objects, and version history. Limits are subject to change.

Vids storage limits

  • The maximum length of any video or audio clip created in the Vids recording studio is 30 minutes.
  • If your video is longer than 30 minutes, you can only make edits to shorten it. You can’t make any other edits until it’s less than 30 minutes.
  • You can import audio or video clips of up to 95 minutes or 4 GB, but until you edit them to 30 minutes or shorter, you can’t make any other edits.

Drive storage limits

You can’t add a media that’s larger than your available Drive storage, but you can still edit your video.

Object limits

For each video, you can add up to 50 video and 50 audio objects. This includes the individual clips of:

  • Music
  • Sound effects
  • Voiceovers

Version history limits

If you reach the version history limit in Google Vids, to continue your edits, make a new copy of your video.

    • To move the focus to the timeline, press the key combination for your operating system:

Use a template & add a background in Google Vids (Miscellaneous)

Important:

  • Learn which Google Account types have access to Google Vids and different Vids features.
  • If you access Vids in all languages, the placeholder text for templates is in English. You can create and use templates like meeting recaps, project updates, help center tutorials, and more to get started with video creation.

In this article, learn how to:

  • Create a new video with a template

  • Add a template to a video

  • Change colors and fonts of your template

  • Change the background of your scene Tips:

  • If you’re building a video using “Help me create,” templates will be suggested based on the prompt and any supporting documents you’ve added.

  • The placeholder text for templates is in English only for all Vids users at this time.

Create a new video with a template

You can create a new video from a template.

  • On your computer, open Google Vids.
  • In the start menu, select the aspect ratio of your Vid: Landscape.
  • Click Templates.
  • To narrow your template search, use filters.
    • Sort by Function, Design Style, Color, or Industry.
  • Click on a template in the template gallery to preview:
    • To add all scenes from the template to your video, click Insert all scenes.
    • To add the current scene from the template to your video, click Insert current scene.

Add a template to a video

You can add a template or an individual scene from a template to an existing video.

  • On your computer, open a video in Google Vids.
  • On the right, click Templates .
  • To narrow your template search, use filters.
    • Sort by Function, Design Style, Color, or Industry.
  • Select the preferred template.
  • A copy of the template opens in the side panel.
    • To add all scenes from the template, at the bottom, click Insert all scenes.
    • To add scenes individually, click the scene you want to add.

Change colors and fonts of your template

You can change the colors or fonts across your template using the Styles side panel.

  • On your computer, open a video in Google Vids.
  • In the toolbar, click Customize Styles .
  • To update the font color, on the right, click on the color or text you want to update.
  • Optional: To customize new shapes in your scenes, at the bottom, click New objects and scenes.

Change the background of your scene

A background is the picture or color behind your scene’s content. You can change the background color of one scene or all the scenes in a video.

  • On your computer, open a video in Google Vids.
  • Select a scene in the timeline at the bottom of your canvas.
  • Insert the image you want as background.
    • Learn how to add images, GIFs, or video clips to a video.
    • Learn how to generate an image with Gemini in Google Slides.
  • Right-click the image, then click Set image as background .

Add a background color:

  • To add a background color, right-click on the canvas Background .
  • Select a color.
  • Optional: To add a custom color, under “Custom,” click Add , select a hex code or adjust the color, hue, or transparency, Click OK.

Remove a background image

  • To remove a background image, right-click the background.
  • Click Detach image from background .
