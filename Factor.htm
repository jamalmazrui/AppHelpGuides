This guide gathers what Factor publishes in writing about its prepared-meal service: how the service works, menus and plans, the add-on range, the app, its nutrition and ingredient philosophy, nutrition coaching, and its sustainability programme. It was taken from Factor’s own site on 29 July 2026.

This is a short volume, and the reason is worth stating plainly. Factor keeps no help centre. Its Frequently Asked Questions page is not a page of answers but a chooser — five headings, Getting Started, Managing my Subscription, Nutrition and Ingredients, Receiving My Meals and Enjoying My Meals, with the answers appearing only when one is selected. Those answers are not in the page as delivered, so they are not here. What is here is the guidance Factor does publish as ordinary pages, and a good deal of it is genuinely useful: how the weekly cycle works, cut-off times, skipping and pausing, how meals arrive and how long they keep, portioning and heating, and what the dietary preferences mean.

Two more notes on the gathering. Factor serves every page under dozens of query variations — search terms, marketing test names, tracking parameters — so 141 saved files held only thirteen distinct pages; the fullest copy of each was kept. And the corporate pages, the press page, the careers page and the business-sales pages were left out: they are about the company rather than about using the service.

If you want the FAQ answers as well, they can be had, but it needs a script that opens each of those five headings in a browser rather than reading the page as delivered. Say the word and I will write it.

Headings run two deep here rather than three: this title, then a page. There are too few pages to justify sections.

Where can I find Factor’s bundles for purchase?

All of our bundles can be found with the Add-Ons. You must have a Factor meal subscription to purchase Add-Ons. Once you choose your meals, head over to the Add-Ons section and you can select and add a bundle to your cart.

Are Factor’s bundles always available?

We offer seasonal bundles that change every couple of weeks, so what you see today may not be available at a later time.

Can I purchase multiple bundles?

Of course! Just select the number that best meets your needs and add to your box.

Is it possible to have my bundle shipped to a different address than normal?

Yes. Your bundle will get delivered with the rest of your Factor meals, so it will ship to the address you have on file. Make sure your address is updated within your account by Wednesday before the menu closes. FAQs

Do I have to commit?

Nope. You can skip your weekly delivery, pause or cancel any time. Just be sure to do so by your cutoff: 11:59 pm CT 5 days before your delivery.

Do I have to cook anything?

None at all. All of our meals are dietitian-approved and chef-prepared so all you have to do is heat, eat, and enjoy.

Can I personalize my meals to suit my lifestyle?

Yes. You choose your Factor meals every week. What’s more, our plans are flexible, so you can switch your meals to suit your current lifestyle.

Will I receive my delivery if I’m not home?

We take steps to accommodate your busy schedule. Delivery times are from 8 am to 8 pm on the day you choose. And if you’re not at home, don’t worry—you can always add special delivery instructions to your account. You can also change your delivery day online if you need to.

How does my food stay fresh?

We carefully handpack all ingredients with special ice packs and insulation, so your meals keep cool—but do not freeze—on their way to you.

What if I have allergies or restrictions?

We make sure to post detailed nutritional information, including allergens, with every one of our meal descriptions. You can find this info online when you choose your meals.

What if I don’t need meals every week?

No worries. You can easily skip a week (or several) when you need to. Just be sure to do so by your cutoff: 11:59 pm CT, 5 days prior to your delivery.

Nutrition and ingredient philosophy

Frequently Asked Questions

Carbohydrates come from fruits, grains, vegetables, and dairy, and generally fall into simple and complex categories. Simple carbs are digested quickly, while complex carbs — especially those rich in fiber, like whole grains and legumes — break down more slowly to provide steadier energy throughout the day.

Protein supports everything from hair growth to enzyme function, organ health, and hormone production. When digested, it breaks down into amino acids — the building blocks your body depends on. Of the 20 types of amino acids, nine are “essential,“ meaning your body can’t produce them on its own and they must come from food.

Healthy fats benefit both your heart and your brain. Unsaturated fats—including monounsaturated and polyunsaturated varieties— help support healthy cholesterol levels and cardiovascular function. Great sources include salmon, tuna, eggs, avocado, chia seeds, flaxseed, nuts, seeds, olives, and olive oil

Micronutrients are vitamins and minerals — like vitamin A, vitamin C, potassium, and magnesium — that your body needs in small amounts to function. They support the brain, muscles, skin, bones, nerves, circulation, and immune system. Many are “essential,“ meaning your body can’t make them on its own. Together, they keep you healthy and performing at your best. Deficiencies are associated with heart disease, diabetes, certain cancers, osteoporosis, and more.

Factor offers a rotating weekly menu of ready-made, dietitian-designed meals designed for maximum flavor and nutrition. Options include, high-protein, low carb, fiber filled, calorie-smart, and vegetarian — all fully prepared by our chefs, so there’s no cooking required.

Yes — all Factor meals are delivered fresh, never frozen. Every meal is sealed for freshness and shipped in insulated packaging with ice packs to ensure it arrives safely chilled.

Yes, your body absolutely needs sodium because it is an essential nutrient that your cells, nerves, and muscles cannot function without. This mineral plays a critical role in regulating your internal fluid balance and maintaining proper blood volume. It also acts as an electrical conductor that allows your brain to communicate with the rest of your body and signals your muscles to contract properly. Since your body cannot produce sodium on its own and constantly loses it through daily sweat and urine, you must replenish it through the foods you eat. The key is finding the right balance, as getting the small amount your body requires supports optimal daily performance, while routinely getting too much can strain your cardiovascular system.

Sodium serves two important purposes in Factor meals. It’s an essential nutrient your body needs to support fluid balance, nerve function, and muscle contraction, and it also helps preserve the safety, quality, and flavor of our meals. Because Factor meals are delivered fresh rather than frozen, we use carefully measured amounts of sodium alongside vacuum-sealed packaging to maintain freshness while delivering restaurant quality taste. Sodium varies by recipe, and the nutrition facts for every meal are available on our weekly menu. For those looking to limit sodium, we also offer lower-sodium meal options that contain 700mg of sodium or less per serving, making it easy to choose meals that fit your individual nutrition goals.

Factor’s Sustainability Efforts

Renewable Electricity & Carbon

Factor is committed to sustainability every day of the year. We source 100% renewable electricity for our production sites and offices, and we offset 100% of the delivery emissions to your door

Sustainable Seafood

We are committed to seafood procurement in line with Monterey Bay Aquarium Seafood Watch rankings of Certified, Best Choice, or Good Alternative. Our Barramundi is Fair Trade Certified™.

Meal Donations

We donate plated meals and cooked surplus to minimize food waste and make our product available to those who are food insecure. In 2023, we donated more than 11.5 MM lbs of fresh surplus food to our food rescue partners.
