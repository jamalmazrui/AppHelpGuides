About Link

How do I create a Link account?

There are several ways to create an account:

  • Directly on the Link website.
  • During checkout when purchasing crypto.
  • Save your information for future use on any Link-supported site when making a purchase from a business that uses Link. This automatically creates a Link account.
  • Purchase a digital “Sold through Link” product. This automatically creates a Link account.

Depending on what you’re using Link for, you may need to provide additional information. See What do I need to do to verify my account? for more information.

How do I make a purchase with Link?

If you don’t have a Link account, you can either sign up through Link or create an account and save your payment information while you’re making a purchase on a Link-supported site.

For all future purchases on Link-supported sites, you’ll enter your email address and verify your phone number with a one-time code, and we’ll autofill your saved card details, billing, and shipping addresses. Manage your info and get support in your Link account.

How do I return an item or request a refund?

If you have an issue with a Link purchase you made, you’ll need to speak to the store or business where you made the purchase.

Go to the Activity section of your Link account and click Contact Business to get in touch with the business or store directly. Link can’t process refunds or returns on behalf of the business.

For “Sold through Link” Purchases

If your purchase is “Sold through Link” and shows up as “link.com *[business name]” on your bank or card statements, you won’t see the Contact Business option. Instead see Requesting a refund for a “Sold through Link” payment.

Link mobile app

Link offers a mobile application to access your Link account for both iOS and Android devices.

Download the Link app today to manage your payment information, view your subscriptions, and see your transaction history.

Availability

The Link app is free to use, and can be downloaded where Link is available.

Supported languages

The language of the app automatically defaults to the device language set in your system preferences. Check your device settings for the full list of supported languages and if you’d like to change this for the Link app.

Mobile app features

With the Link app, you’ll be able to take all the same actions that you can from the Link website today, including:

  • Create or login to a Link account
  • Manage your payment information
  • View subscriptions
  • See past purchases

In addition, you’ll be able to opt-in to receive push notifications for applicable Link features.

Troubleshooting mobile app issues

Link app functions best on the latest version. If you are experiencing issues with the app, we recommend first making sure that you have the latest update installed, before logging out and logging back in again.

Push notifications for Link mobile app

Push notifications are messages sent directly to your device from a mobile app to provide updates that may be relevant for your account.

Enabling push notifications

Follow these steps to enable push notifications on the Link mobile app on iOS or Android:

  • Tap on your name / profile icon on the top right of the screen to open the Settings page.
  • Click on Notifications.
  • Select the type of notifications you’d like to receive.
  • Toggle the Push button to turn this feature on.

Troubleshooting tips

If you are experiencing issues with push notification deliveries, try taking the following steps:

  • Check phone settings: Push notifications only work if you also allow notifications for the Link app in the general Settings of your Phone (Settings > Notifications). Read more about modifying your notification permissions for your operating system: iOS, Android.
  • Confirm you are on the latest app version: If you still aren’t receiving push notifications, check if you have the latest version of our app installed, and update if not.
  • Restart the app: Log out and log back in again.

What is Link?

Link is a digital wallet that provides effortless and secure online payments and digital transactions. With Link, you can enjoy convenience and peace of mind: it works on any device or browser and ensures seamless and quick payments by autofilling your saved payment methods, contact information, and shipping details everywhere Link is accepted.

Create a Link account on the Link website or by saving your information when checking out with a business that uses Link. You can manage your payment information and see past purchases by logging into your Link account.

Sold through Link

Sold through Link offers select businesses its merchant of record services to sell their digital products globally with ease under the “Sold through Link” brand.

When you’re shopping for digital products and see “Sold through Link” at checkout, and after placing an order for a digital product see “link.com*[Business User name]” (or similar) on your bank or card statement, your purchase is Sold through Link.

You can use Link to manage your orders and get support for orders purchased for digital products Sold through Link.

Why am I being asked to save my bank account with Link?

When checking out on a Link-enabled site, you may see the option to pay with your bank account on eligible purchases. If you would like to complete the purchase using this payment method, you’ll need to first connect your bank account with Link. Link will always let you know why you are being prompted to connect your bank account.

After connecting your bank account, you will be able to reuse the saved bank account on other Link-enabled businesses.

What are the steps to connect my bank account through Link?

To add a bank account to Link:

  • Log in to your Link account.
  • Go to Wallet and select “+” to Add a Bank under Add a payment method.
  • Click Agree and Continue to go through the authentication flow for your banking institution.

Alternatively, you can connect your bank account as a payment method with Link by following these steps during checkout:

  • Select your bank.
  • Enter your bank login credentials.
  • Select the bank account(s) you want to connect.

If you’re creating a new Link account, you’ll also need to agree to the Link End User Terms and Privacy Policy.

How does Link work with Stripe to connect my bank account?

Link was built by Stripe to provide an easy way to pay in 1-click. You can use your bank account as a payment method to complete a payment on eligible purchases when checking out on a Link-enabled site.

How does Stripe connect to my bank account?

Stripe can successfully establish a connection to your bank account in one of two ways:

  • When you enter your bank login credentials, you may be logging in to your bank or your bank’s service provider directly. In these cases, your login screen will either open in a new window or you will be redirected to the bank’s website. After you enter your credentials, your bank or your bank’s service provider will then provide Stripe with access through a token. Stripe will use the token to access information in order to help the business process your transaction.
  • In other instances, when you input your login credentials, you are providing your credentials to Stripe and one of our trusted service providers. Stripe and our service providers may store and use your credentials to regularly collect updated data. We do not share your login credentials with the business during check out.

What data does Stripe access from my connected bank account?

Stripe accesses and stores copies of the following financial information types from your account:

  • Account details, including your name and mailing address, account number and routing number, account type, and account nickname
  • Account balances, including current and pending balances
  • Account transactions, including each transaction’s amount, date, business from which you purchased, and description

Stripe and our service providers may also store your financial institution login credentials so we can connect to your bank account and regularly collect updated data that you’ve agreed to share. Stripe does not share your login credentials with the businesses.

Stripe only uses your bank account information as outlined in our Privacy Policy.

When you connect a bank account, you are granting data access to Stripe and the following trusted partners:

  • If you process a payment with Link, we’ll also share the relevant details with our bank partners to enable the transaction.

What is a passkey?

Passkeys are a verification method for logging into your Link account. Instead of using a one-time passcode, passkeys allow you to safely sign into Link using the same authentication method that you would use to unlock your device, such as a device PIN or a biometric identifier like Touch or Face ID.

Each passkey is tied to a specific account and platform such as the device or a key sharing mechanism like iCloud keychain.

Passkeys simplify the login process by removing the need for verification codes and remembering different passwords across multiple websites and applications. For more information, see how passkeys work with iOS or Chrome.

Adding a new passkey

To add a passkey to your Link account:

  • Log in to your Link account.
  • Navigate to the Settings section.
  • Select Passkeys under Security.
  • Click Add Passkey.
  • A series of device/browser specific instructions will appear. Follow the prompts to complete the passkey setup.

You’ll be able to use the Log in using passkey option the next time you log in to your Link account.

I don’t recognize a passkey on my account

If you don’t recognize a passkey, review the Activity section of your Link account to confirm your recent account activity. If you identify any past purchases that you did not make, immediately contact your bank to file a dispute.

Then follow the instructions below to remove the passkey from your account.

Removing a saved passkey

Removing a saved passkey only removes a passkey from your Link account. To remove the passkey from your device or browser entirely, you may need to take some additional steps. See the device/browser specific instructions like those for Google Account or iPhone.

To remove a passkey from your Link account:

  • Log in to your Link account.
  • Navigate to the Settings section.
  • Select the Passkeys option under Security.
  • Click Remove on the Passkey you’d like to remove.
  • Click Remove and Log out to confirm.

Removing a passkey will automatically log you out of Link on all devices.

What devices support passkeys?

Not all devices and web browsers support passkeys today, and you will only see the option to add a passkey when it is supportable.

See the devices and operating systems that are currently compatible with passkeys.

Troubleshooting passkey errors

If you are experiencing issues with a passkey, see Troubleshooting passkey errors to learn more about common reasons as to why your passkey might not be working.

Verifying your bank account

Why am I being asked to verify my bank account?

If you’ve previously saved your bank account with Link without having completed the authentication process, you may be asked to verify your bank account in order to use it on Link-enabled businesses. If a bank account requires verification, you will see a Verify badge next to it under the Wallets section on your Link account or a Verification required text as you’re checking out with a business using saved payment methods with Link.

How do I verify my bank account?

When making a purchase

During your checkout with the business, you may be prompted to go through the bank account authentication flow. Follow the on-screen steps here to enter your bank login credentials to complete the verification step and proceed with the bank payment using Link.

Depending on the business, you may also have the option to complete the verification step through micro-deposits instead. Micro-deposits are small deposits into your account that contain a unique code within the statement descriptor. This code is then used to complete the verification step. Note that this process can take between 1-3 business days, as it can take some time before the micro-deposits posts to your account

Link Account

You can also complete verification for your bank account directly from your Link account:

  • From the Wallets section of your Link account, select the bank account with the Verify badge.
  • Click Verify account and follow the on-screen steps to enter your bank login credentials to complete the verification step.
  • Once completed, the verify badge will be removed from the payment method indicating that this is now ready for use with Link.

Why do I see a duplicate entry after verifying my bank account?

Depending on the banking institution and its technical setup, you may see a duplicated payment method entry appear for the bank account after you’ve completed the verification process. The bank account entry without the verify badge indicates that the verification step is already complete, and you can simply delete the other entry with the verify tag still visible by removing that account from the wallet section.

You’ll continue to be able to use the remaining verified bank account entry as an available payment method for your Link account.

Why is my bank account not verifying?

In order for the bank account to verify, the exact same bank account must be selected. When completing the verification steps, please ensure that you’re selecting the correct banking institution, account type, and banking details requiring verification.

Why am I seeing 0000 for some purchases made with Link?

Your payment details are replaced with 0000 for certain Link purchases made with a US bank account. You can view past purchases and look up business contact information by navigating to the Activity section of your Link account:

The 0000 that you see in place of your payment details doesn’t denote any additional level of security for that payment.

Can you delete my Link cryptocurrency transaction records from a public blockchain?

Wallet funding and transaction information related to your use of Link may be recorded on a public blockchain. Many public block chains are open to forensic analysis which can lead to deanonymization and revelation of private financial information, especially when combined with other data. Because blockchains are decentralized or third-party networks that are not controlled or operated by Stripe or its affiliates, we are not able to erase, modify, or alter your personal data from such networks.

Fees when using Link to buy crypto

Standard fees

There are two types of fees associated with making a crypto purchase using Link:

  • Processing fees: These are payment processing fees charged based on factors such as your order value and the payment method you’ve used. These will normally amount to 3-5% of your order value and will be shown in more detail before you make a purchase.
  • Network fees: These gas fees fund the computations required to validate your purchase on the blockchain. They’re dynamically calculated based on blockchain network conditions.

Will I be charged a cash advance fee for my crypto purchase?

Some credit card issuers treat crypto purchases as cash advances. Because of this, you may be charged a cash advance fee on your crypto transaction.

While the exact amount varies, these fees are typically around 3%, sometimes with a 10 USD/EUR minimum.

To avoid paying this fee, we recommend using your bank account instead. If you choose to pay with a credit card, you should check with your credit card issuer before completing the transaction to see if a cash advance fee will be charged.

How long does it take for the crypto to land in my wallet?

It typically takes about 15 minutes after a purchase for a crypto asset to land in your wallet. This can vary depending on external factors such as network congestion.

If your crypto hasn’t appeared in your wallet after 24 hours, contact us for assistance.

What should I do if my crypto purchase is declined?

If your credit card payment is declined, try using a bank account (only in the US) or debit card, which are more likely to succeed.

If your debit card or bank account payment is declined, reach out to your bank or financial institution for assistance.

My payment was unsuccessful why haven’t I received my refund?

If your payment was unsuccessful, we immediately cancel the authorization made to your card.

If you see a debit on your bank statement, this will disappear in approximately 5 business days, however, the exact timing will depend on your bank. Unfortunately, there is nothing that can be done to expedite this timeframe.

If you are still seeing the charge on your statement, you will need to resolve this with your bank.

Why doesn’t the amount authorized on my card match the amount on my crypto receipt?

In order to account for price volatility, we may authorize your card for roughly 3% more than your purchase amount.

This authorization is temporary. We only debit you for the transaction amount specified in your purchase receipt.

Can I cancel my crypto purchase or get a refund once it has been completed?

No, once you complete your crypto purchase, we’re unable to cancel it or refund your payment.

Understanding stablecoins

Stablecoins are a type of digital money designed to hold a steady value. Most are tied to the US dollar, meaning 1 stablecoin is intended to be worth 1 USD.

You can think of stablecoins as digital dollars you can receive, hold, and send over the internet, even without a traditional bank account.

Unlike cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin or Ethereum, whose prices can change dramatically, stablecoins are built for everyday uses like getting paid, holding money, and sending it around the world.

How do stablecoins work?

Stablecoins run on blockchains, digital networks that securely record transactions and let people send money directly between wallets.

Many popular stablecoins, including USDC, are backed by reserves of cash and other highly liquid assets, such as short-term US government securities. These reserves help keep their value close to 1 USD.

When someone sends you stablecoins, they move directly to your digital wallet, often arriving in seconds or minutes, even when the sender is in another country.

Why use stablecoins?

Stablecoins combine the familiarity of dollars with the speed and convenience of the internet.

  • Dollar access: Hold and manage money in US dollars, wherever you are in the world.
  • Fast, global transfers: Send and receive money across borders, 24/7, unconstrained by banking hours.
  • More control: Manage your money directly through a digital wallet.
  • Lower costs: Sending stablecoins can cost less than bank transfers, including wires, depending on the network and service you use.

What can I do with stablecoins?

Stablecoins can be used for many of the same things you already do with money, whether that’s getting paid by an employer, holding a balance in dollars, or sending money to friends and family.

You can also exchange them for your local currency or spend them with businesses and apps that accept stablecoins.

Exactly what’s available depends on where you live and which services you use.

Are stablecoins safe?

Widely used stablecoins are backed by reserves of cash and other high-quality assets, helping them maintain a steady value close to 1 USD over time. While no financial asset is entirely risk-free, these reserves help make stablecoins a more predictable way to hold and move money.

What is USDC?

USDC (USD Coin) is a type of digital currency called a stablecoin. Unlike other cryptocurrencies that can fluctuate in value, USDC is designed to always be worth $1 USD. Each USDC is 100% backed by US dollar reserves (cash and short-dated U.S. Treasuries, all held in regulated U.S. financial institutions), and redeemable on a 1:1 basis.

Funds stored on your Link account are stored in USDC. When you withdraw to a bank account, your USDC is automatically converted to your local currency at the current exchange rate. No conversion occurs when you withdraw to an external crypto wallet.

Withdrawals

How do I withdraw my funds?

You can withdraw funds from your Link balance at any time after identity verification is completed. There’s no holding period or waiting requirement. As soon as funds arrive in your balance, they’re available for withdrawal to your bank account or external crypto wallet.

Withdraw to a bank account

  • Select Withdraw.
  • Select Bank Account as your destination (or add a new one).
    • Follow the steps to select your bank from the list and enter your account information. If your bank isn’t listed, we are currently unable to support it.
  • Enter the amount you want to withdraw.
  • Review the exchange rate, any fees, and the total amount you’ll receive in your local currency.
  • Confirm the withdrawal.

Withdraw to a crypto wallet

  • Select Withdraw.
  • Select Crypto Wallet as your destination (or add a new one).
  • Select the blockchain you want to receive USDC on. Confirm that the selected network is accurate.
  • Enter your wallet address on the selected chain. Confirm that your wallet address is correct and that the selected token is accepted by your wallet provider.
  • If required (especially for Stellar or some exchanges), enter a memo or tag.
  • Enter the amount you want to withdraw.
  • Review the transfer and confirm. There are no fees associated with this withdrawal type.

Ensure you use the right network and address for your withdrawal. Funds withdrawn to the wrong address or via an unsupported network may be permanently lost.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Bank withdrawals

Bank withdrawals generally take 3–5 business days. Weekends and local holidays do not count as business days and may extend the delivery time.

If you’ve not yet received your withdrawal to your bank account, wait the full 5 business days before requesting further assistance.

If five full business days have passed:

  • Confirm that the account details and account-holder name are correct.
  • Ask your bank to check for an incoming transfer.
  • If the bank cannot locate it, log in to your Link account for further assistance. Provide the withdrawal date, amount, and any information provided by your bank.

Crypto withdrawals

Crypto withdrawals usually arrive within a few minutes, but they can take up to approximately one hour. Network congestion may cause additional delays.

If the transfer is confirmed on the blockchain but does not appear in your receiving account, contact the wallet or exchange that received the transfer.

What are the withdrawal fees?
  • Bank withdrawals: The estimated exchange rate and any applicable fees are shown in the Link app before you confirm your withdrawal.
  • Crypto withdrawals: There is no fee.
Can I cancel a withdrawal?

Once you’ve created a withdrawal from your Link account, it can’t be canceled. You’ll need to wait for the outcome of the withdrawal.

If successful, the funds will need to be retrieved from the bank account or crypto wallet, whichever one your withdrawal was sent to.

If the withdrawal was unsuccessful, the funds will be returned to your Link balance.

My withdrawal failed. What should I do?

If your withdrawal to a bank account failed, common reasons include:

  • The name on your Link account doesn’t match the name on your bank account.
  • The address on your Link account doesn’t match the address on your bank account.
  • Your bank account details (account number or selected bank name) were entered incorrectly.
  • If your ID number was required for a withdrawal, it may be incorrect.

If your bank withdrawal fails, your funds are returned to your Link balance. They’re not lost. For help identifying the cause of the failure and correcting any information that needs updating, log in to your Link account.

If your withdrawal to a crypto wallet failed, common reasons include:

  • The wallet address was entered incorrectly.
  • The wallet doesn’t support USDC on the blockchain you selected.
  • A required memo or tag was missing.
  • If a crypto withdrawal fails before it is sent to the blockchain, your funds are returned to your Link balance. However, if the transaction was completed on the blockchain but went to an incorrect address or the token isn’t supported by your wallet provider, the funds cannot be recovered. Crypto transactions are irreversible by nature. Please always double-check your wallet address and chain before confirming.

Verifying your identity

Best practices for successful ID verification

Depending on what you use Link for, you may need to upload your ID to complete your identity verification.

Before you begin

You’ll need:

  • Your original, valid government-issued photo ID. A photocopy or an image of the document are not accepted.
  • A device with a camera. If possible, use a mobile device, as mobile cameras typically produce higher-quality photos than webcams.
  • A recent version of Chrome, Safari, or Microsoft Edge.

Make sure your ID:

  • Is an accepted document type.
  • Is current and not expired.
  • Belongs to the person whose identity is being verified.
  • Is not redacted, altered, or damaged.

Tips for taking successful photos

Image quality affects verification success. Follow these recommendations when photographing your ID and face:

Photographing your ID
  • Place your ID on a flat surface.
  • Make sure the entire document is visible. Don’t cover or block any part of it.
  • Hold your device steady and allow the camera to focus.
  • Make sure the image is clear and isn’t too dark or bright.
  • Avoid shadows and glare.
  • Use a color image.
  • If uploading an existing image file, use .png or .jpg.
Photographing your face
  • Make sure your entire face is visible.
  • Remove sunglasses, masks, and other accessories that obscure your face.
  • Choose a location with soft, ambient lighting.
  • Avoid strong overhead lighting that casts shadows on your face or ID.
  • Don’t sit directly in front of a bright light, which can wash out your face or create glare.

How do you verify my identity?

We verify your identity in three ways:

  • Verify the authenticity of your ID document: We review images you upload of your government-issued photo ID to ensure the document is authentic. This process is like a bank teller checking your ID document to confirm that it’s real. We’ve built automated identity verification technology that looks for patterns to help determine if an ID document is real or fake. See Best practices for successful ID verification.
  • Match your ID with photos of your face: We review photos of your face to confirm the photo ID belongs to you. This process is similar to a bank teller confirming that the photo on your ID document is you based on the shape of your nose or eyes. We’ve built automated identity verification technology that uses distinctive physiological characteristics of your face known as biometric identifiers to match the photos of your face with the photo on the ID document.
  • Validate your ID number against global databases: We collect your name, date of birth, and government ID number, like your Social Security number (SSN), and check this information against a global set of databases to confirm that it exists.
