What is an Experience?

Experiences are special reservations that restaurants create to highlight unique offerings, such as tasting menus, holiday events, chef’s tables, or themed nights. When you book an Experience, you know in advance what is included, how much it costs, and any special details the restaurant wants you to see.

With Experiences, you can:

  • Plan a special outing with fixed menus, pairings, or seating that match the occasion.
  • Avoid surprises by seeing price, inclusions, and policies before you confirm.
  • Discover something new when Experiences appear in search results and on restaurant pages. Back to top

How to book an Experience on opentable.com

  • Go to opentable.com and sign in to your account.
  • Search for a restaurant or browse by date, time, and party size.
  • In the results or on the restaurant’s page, look for sections or badges labeled Experiences.
  • Select the Experience that interests you to see details such as menu, price, dates, and any terms.
  • Choose a date, time, and party size from the options shown for that Experience.
  • If the Experience requires prepayment, enter your payment details and review the total.
  • Review the cancellation policy and any other notes, then confirm your booking. Back to top

How to book an Experience in the OpenTable app

  • Open the OpenTable app and sign in.
  • Search for a restaurant or browse nearby options.
  • On eligible restaurant pages, scroll to the Experiences section to see what is available.
  • Select the Experience to open its description, price, and available times.
  • Choose your date, time, and party size.
  • If prepayment is required, follow the prompts to pay with a saved card or enter a new one.
  • Check the policy details, then confirm your reservation. Back to top

What happens after you book an Experience

  • Your Experience reservation appears in your OpenTable account under Upcoming or My Reservations.
  • Your confirmation email or screen will show:
    • That the booking is an Experience (not a standard reservation).
    • What is included (for example, prix-fixe menu, welcome drink, service or fees).
    • Any cancellation rules, cutoffs, or non-refundable terms.
  • At the restaurant, staff will see that you booked an Experience and can seat and charge you accordingly. Back to top

Troubleshooting

I can’t find Experiences for a restaurant
  • Check the date, time, and party size; the restaurant may only offer Experiences on certain days or for specific group sizes.
  • Try searching a bit farther out; some Experiences are only offered on special dates or holidays.
  • If you previously saw an Experience that is now missing, it may be sold out or no longer active.
I can’t change or cancel my Experience reservation online
  • Open your reservation in Upcoming or My Reservations and look for Modify or Cancel options.
  • If no options appear, the restaurant may not allow changes or cancellations within a certain window, or the Experience may be non-refundable.
  • In those cases, use the contact information in your confirmation to reach the restaurant directly.
  • If additional add-on items were selected when booking the prepaid experience, you won’t be able to modify them through OpenTable. Please contact the restaurant to make changes to the reservation.
I was charged upfront and have questions about the amount
  • Open your Experience confirmation and review the description, taxes, and fees to see what the total includes.
  • If you still have questions or see something unexpected, contact the restaurant. They control Experience pricing and refunds. Back to top

Related frequently asked questions

Do Experiences cost more than standard reservations?

Experiences often have a fixed price or minimum spend because they include specific menus, events, or extras. The restaurant sets these prices. Standard reservations usually have no upfront cost and you pay at the restaurant based on what you order.

Can I earn Dining Points on an Experience?

Some Experience reservations may earn standard Dining Points if they follow the usual eligibility rules in your region. Experiences don’t add extra Bonus Points beyond what is shown in your confirmation.

Can I switch from a prepaid Experience to a regular reservation?

There is no direct way to convert an Experience to a standard reservation. If the Experience policy allows cancellations, you can cancel it and then make a new standard reservation for an available time.

What if I arrive late to my Experience?

Late arrivals are handled by the restaurant’s own policies. If you might arrive late, contact the restaurant as soon as possible using the details in your confirmation so they can advise you on options.

Overview and key benefits

OpenTable Dining Points are a way to earn rewards when you book and honor eligible reservations on the OpenTable app or on opentable.com.

  • Earn points when you book eligible reservations while signed in to your OpenTable account and, where available, enrolled in OpenTable Regulars.
  • Use your points for rewards such as dining credits, discounts on prepaid experiences, or other options that may be available in your region.
  • See your current balance, activity, and any upcoming expirations in the Rewards or Points area of your account.
  • Keep in mind that rewards and point values can vary by country and may change over time. The details you see in your account are always the most up-to-date.
What are OpenTable Points?

Rewards programs are subject to varying rules and regulations globally. Diners whose primary dining location is included in the countries listed can earn points while dining anywhere globally, and redeem them for rewards in the following locations:

  • Canada

How earning and redeeming Dining Points works

Before you start

To earn Dining Points on a reservation:

  • Make your reservation on the OpenTable app or on opentable.com in a country where Dining Points are available.
  • Sign in to your OpenTable account before you book.
    • Don’t have an OpenTable account? Sign up here!
  • Join OpenTable Regulars if that loyalty program is offered in your region.
  • Check the reservation details at the time of booking; when points are available, you’ll see how many points you can earn before you confirm.
Step 1 – Sign in and join the program
  • Open the OpenTable app or go to opentable.com.
  • Sign in to your OpenTable account or create one if you’re new.
  • If you see an option to join OpenTable Regulars or a Rewards hub, follow the prompts to enroll so eligible reservations can earn points.
Step 2 – Book an eligible reservation
  • Search for a restaurant, date, time, and party size on the OpenTable app or on opentable.com.
  • Choose an available time and complete your booking while you’re signed in.
  • On the confirmation page or in your confirmation email, look for a note about how many points the reservation can earn or whether it is not points-eligible.
Step 3 – Dine and wait for points to appear
  • Go to your reservation and dine as planned.
  • After your visit, the restaurant updates the reservation in the OpenTable system.
  • Points usually appear in your account within about 7 days.
  • Until then, the reservation may show as Pending in your points or rewards activity.
Step 4 – Check where Dining Points and rewards are available

Dining Points and rewards are only available in selected countries. If your primary dining location is in a supported country, you’ll see a Rewards or Points section in your account and eligible reservations will indicate how many points you can earn.

If you don’t see a rewards section or any redemptions listed, your country may not currently offer points or may offer only limited rewards.

Step 5 – Redeem your points for rewards
  • Open the OpenTable app or go to opentable.com and sign in.
  • Go to Rewards or your Points area to see your balance and the rewards available in your region.
  • Review the list of rewards, including any notes about where and how they can be used.
  • Choose a reward type (for example, dining credit, prepaid experience discount, or a partner gift card if available) and follow the prompts to confirm the redemption.
  • You’ll see how many points are required and what the reward is worth before you confirm. Those in-product details are the most up-to-date values. Back to top

Where can I find my dining points history?

Access points activity in your OpenTable account:

  • Log in to the OpenTable app or opentable.com.
  • Select Rewards.
  • In the OpenTable Regulars modal, select the Learn more button.
  • Scroll down and select Points history. This view will display your total number of points, the next time your points will expire, and how many points will expire. Select a tag at the top of the list to filter based on the type of points history you’d like to review!
    • All: will display all points earned, redeemed, and expired.
    • Earned: will display only a record of points earned from reservations or promotions.
    • Redeemed: will display only the record of points redeemed for rewards.
    • Expired: will display expired points.

Can my OpenTable points expire?

Points expire in the United States and Australia.

There are two ways that points expire:

  • Any unredeemed points expire three years after they were earned.
  • All points in an account expire if that account hasn’t had a seated reservation for 12 months. Note: When you redeem your points, OpenTable will create your redemption by selecting the oldest points earned first.
How do I know if my points will expire?

Sign in to OpenTable, and confirm in the Rewards Hub when your points will expire.

  • Log in to your dining account on the OpenTable dining app or on opentable.com.
  • Select the Rewards icon.
  • Under Rewards from points, the number of points set to expire and their expiration date will be displayed.
    • Example: 400 points expiring April 1, 2026 Additional details about dining point expirations:
  • OpenTable Points collected on or after March 31, 2018, must be redeemed within three (3) years from the end of the calendar quarter in which these points were collected.
    • For example, Points collected by a member on January 1, 2022, must be redeemed by 11:59 pm Pacific Time on March 31, 2025.
    • Calendar quarters end on March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31 of each year. Back to top

Troubleshooting

You don’t see any points after your visit
  • Check your Points or Rewards activity and look for the reservation.
  • It can take up to about 7 days after your visit for points to appear.
  • If the reservation was canceled or a no-show, points will not be added automatically.
  • If it has been more than 7 days and you believe your reservation should have earned points, contact OpenTable Support with your confirmation number so we can review it.
Your reservation was booked in a different way
  • Reservations booked from a restaurant’s own website using some widgets, or through certain partner sites and apps, may not be eligible for Dining Points even though the restaurant uses OpenTable.
  • To earn points next time, start your search on the OpenTable app or on opentable.com, sign in first, and check that your confirmation shows that the reservation can earn points.
You used a reward and expected to earn points as well
  • When you redeem points for dining credit or a similar reward and use it on a reservation, that reservation usually does not earn additional points.
  • You still receive the value of the reward you redeemed, but the visit will not add more Dining Points.
You don’t see rewards in your region
  • Make sure you’re signed in to the correct OpenTable account.
  • Go to Rewards or Points and check whether any options are listed.
  • If you don’t see rewards at all, your country may not currently offer redemptions, even if you can earn points.
Your friend in another country sees different options
  • Reward catalogs and point-to-value conversions are different between regions.
  • Each diner sees the rewards that apply to their primary dining location, so it’s normal for options and amounts to differ.
You redeemed points but aren’t sure what happens next
  • For dining credits or prepaid experience discounts, check your upcoming reservations or experience confirmation for details on how and when the credit is applied.
  • For Amazon gift cards, look for a separate email sent to the address on your OpenTable account. Delivery timelines can vary by region.
  • If you still don’t see the reward after the expected timeframe, contact OpenTable Support so we can review your account. Back to top

Related frequently asked questions

Where are Dining Points available?

Dining Points and rewards are available only in selected countries. If your primary dining location is in a supported country, you’ll see a Rewards or Points section in your account and eligible reservations will indicate how many points you can earn.

What can I redeem points for?

Redemption options vary by region and may include dining credits toward reservations, credit toward prepaid experiences, or other rewards such as partner gift cards. The Rewards area in your account shows the options available in your country and how many points each one requires.

How long does it take for points to appear?

Points usually appear within about 7 days after your reservation is seated or completed. If you still don’t see points after that and you believe your booking was eligible, contact OpenTable Support with your confirmation number so we can investigate.

Can I move points between accounts or regions?

In general, points stay with the OpenTable account that earned them and are tied to that account’s primary region. If you have questions about consolidating points or accounts, contact Support so we can review your account history and explain what is possible under current policy.

Why do my rewards look different from a friend’s in another country?

Rewards are offered and priced by region, so diners in different countries may see different options and point-to-value conversions. The Rewards hub in your account always shows the options tied to your primary dining location.

Bonus Points reservations help you collect dining points more quickly when you book certain times at select restaurants. Increase your rewards rate with bonus point reservations

Bonus Points reservations are special time slots that award more dining points than standard reservations when you book and honor them correctly.

  • Earn dining points faster by booking select times labeled as offering Bonus Points.
  • Use points toward rewards, depending on the programs available in your region.
  • Keep your usual booking experience while earning extra points on eligible reservations.
Important notes about earning bonus points
  • Bonus Points are only available on select dates and times at participating restaurants.
  • You must sign in to your OpenTable account and choose a reservation time that clearly shows the Bonus Points label.
  • The number of points you can earn displays on the booking screen and in your confirmation email. Back to top

How Bonus Points reservations work

Choose a time with the Bonus Points label
  • Search for a restaurant and date as usual on opentable.com or in the OpenTable app.
  • Look for reservation times that display a Bonus Points label, for example, a time that shows a higher point value than standard reservations.
  • Select one of those times and complete your booking.
  • The point value for your reservation appears on the confirmation page and in your confirmation email.
Changes and cancellations
  • If you change your Bonus Points reservation in any way, such as moving it to a different time or changing the party size, the system treats it as a cancellation and rebooking, and the reservation may be eligible for only 100 points.
  • If the new time doesn’t offer Bonus Points, your updated reservation will earn standard points instead.
  • If you need to keep Bonus Points, cancel the original reservation and book a different time that still shows the Bonus Points label.
No-shows and restaurant cancellations
  • If a restaurant cancels your reservation or marks you as a no‑show, you won’t earn points for that reservation.
  • When a restaurant marks you as a no‑show, you receive an email notification with a link you can use to dispute the no‑show if you believe it is incorrect. Back to top

Troubleshooting

I thought I booked a Bonus Points reservation, but I received standard points
  • Check your confirmation email and look at the point value shown for your reservation.
  • If the confirmation email shows that your reservation was only worth standard points, the time you chose wasn’t a Bonus Points time.
  • To earn Bonus Points next time, make sure you choose a time that clearly displays the Bonus Points label before you confirm your booking.
I received a Bonus Points confirmation email, but I only received standard points
  • If you modified the reservation after booking, you may have lost its bonus point qualification. For example, adjusting the time or party size.
  • When you change a reservation, it is like canceling and rebooking; if the new time doesn’t offer Bonus Points, your reservation will earn standard points.
  • If you didn’t change the reservation and your confirmation email shows a higher point value than what you received, contact Support and include your confirmation details so the team can review your account.
I booked a Bonus Points reservation, but I received 0 points
  • Check your email for any no‑show or cancellation messages about that reservation.
  • If you received a no‑show email and you believe you honored the reservation, use the link in that email to dispute the no‑show.
  • If you see a cancellation message from the restaurant and you believe this is incorrect, contact Support with your confirmation details so they can review what happened. Back to top

Related frequently asked questions

How do I know if a reservation offers Bonus Points?

Look for reservation times that display a higher point value or a Bonus Points label when you search on opentable.com or in the app. Only those labeled times offer the extra points; other times during the same general window may award standard points.

Do I still get Bonus Points if I change my reservation?

If you change your reservation to a time that doesn’t offer Bonus Points, your reservation will earn standard points instead. If you want to keep Bonus Points, cancel your existing reservation and book a new time that still shows the Bonus Points label before you confirm.

What happens to my Bonus Points if the restaurant cancels my reservation or marks me as a no-show?

If the restaurant cancels your reservation or marks you as a no‑show, you won’t earn points for that reservation. When you are marked as a no‑show, you receive an email with a link you can use to dispute the status if you believe it is incorrect.

What should I do if my Bonus Points still look wrong?

First, review your confirmation email to confirm how many points the reservation was worth and whether you changed or canceled it. If something still doesn’t look right after you check your confirmation and account, contact Support with your reservation and confirmation details so the team can review your case.

All monetary refunds for prepaid experiences are handled directly by the restaurant. OpenTable does not process these refunds on the restaurant’s behalf.

  • Wait for the refund to appear on your payment method.

  • Keep a copy of your refund confirmation or card statement showing the refund amount and restaurant.

  • Contact OpenTable Support and share:

Redeem points for credits toward reservations

Dining credits let you turn your Dining Points into a prepaid Mastercard you can use toward an eligible reservation at a participating restaurant. They are a flexible way to save on a future meal while continuing to earn points on eligible bookings.

Benefits include:

  • Use points for a fixed-value dining credit, such as a 10, 25, or higher-value reward depending on your balance.
  • Apply the dining credit to the bill at a participating restaurant when you pay with your Dining Credit.
  • Keep any remaining balance on the card for another eligible visit until the card expires. Availability: Dining credits are currently available for eligible diners booking at participating restaurants in the United States. In other regions, you may see other reward options such as Amazon gift cards instead.
Before you start
  • You must be signed in to your OpenTable account before you redeem points or use a dining credit.
  • Dining credits can’t be applied to a reservation that’s already booked. To use one on a different reservation, cancel first, then rebook through the dining credit flow.
  • Dining credits can’t be used toward prepaid experience reservations.
  • Your account must include both your full first and last name to redeem the reward. Back to top

How dining credits work

  • When you redeem points for a dining credit, OpenTable creates a dining reward token linked to your account.
  • You activate that token by booking a reservation through a special link; this attaches the dining credit to that reservation and creates your prepaid card.
  • Your Dining Credit card is a prepaid card that you bring to the restaurant, printed or on your phone, and use toward the bill.
  • The restaurant processes it like a debit card against the available balance.
  • If you spend less than the full amount, any remaining balance stays on the card until the expiration date, and you can use it again at participating US restaurants.

Understand the booking and payment policy before you book

When you book with OpenTable, each restaurant sets its own cancellation and no-show policy. Understanding how those policies work helps you avoid unexpected fees, keep your account in good standing, and make sure your visits count toward any points or rewards you might earn.

When you book
  • Check the restaurant’s policy. Look for the cancellation and no-show policy in your reservation confirmation email and on the restaurant’s OpenTable profile. Some restaurants allow same-day cancellations; others may require 24–48 hours’ notice.
  • Keep your confirmation email. It usually includes direct Modify or Cancel links and a reminder of any fees for late cancellation or no-shows.
  • Policies come from the restaurant. OpenTable helps share these details, but the restaurant decides when to charge a fee and how to handle exceptions. Back to top

How cancellations and no-shows work

How cancellations usually work

  • You can typically change or cancel a reservation from your confirmation email, on opentable.com, or in the OpenTable app.
  • If a restaurant has a stricter policy, such as no same-day cancellations, you may see fewer or no cancellation options online. In those cases, you may need to contact the restaurant directly.
  • When you cancel in time according to the restaurant’s policy, you generally won’t be charged a fee, and the reservation will be marked as canceled rather than a no-show.

When a reservation may be marked as a no-show

You may be marked as a no-show if:

  • You didn’t arrive for your reservation and didn’t cancel in time.
  • You arrived too late, and the restaurant couldn’t hold the table.
  • The restaurant didn’t receive your cancellation request; for example, you may have called a different number or their voicemail was full. If you accumulate multiple no-shows within a 12-month period, your OpenTable account may be temporarily deactivated so that restaurants can rely on the reservations they receive.

When a restaurant cancels your reservation

Sometimes a restaurant needs to cancel or move reservations because of weather, closures, or operational issues. In those cases, you may receive a cancellation email even if you didn’t cancel yourself. If the reservation has not happened yet, you can usually contact the restaurant directly to see whether they can rebook you.

What happens if you can’t change or cancel online

In some situations, you may not be able to change or cancel a reservation on OpenTable.

  • Cancellation window has passed: Restaurants can set how far in advance diners can cancel, often between 1 and 7 days before the reservation time. If you’re inside that window, you may not see a cancel option and will need to contact the restaurant directly.
  • No cancellations allowed: Some restaurants do not allow cancellations for certain reservations. In these cases, OpenTable will not display cancel options, and you’ll need to work directly with the restaurant.
  • Prepaid Experiences or prepaid add‑ons: For Experiences that require prepayment, restaurants usually manage cancellations and refunds themselves. You’ll typically need to contact the restaurant directly to ask about changes or refunds.
  • In‑house reservations: Reservations created directly by the restaurant in their own system may not be cancellable in your OpenTable account. Contact the restaurant to update or cancel these.
  • Deposits and fees: If the reservation includes a deposit or credit card guarantee, the restaurant may charge you when their cancellation or no‑show policy is violated. Any refunds or fee disputes are handled by the restaurant. Back to top

Restaurants may ask that you provide a credit card number to secure your reservation. If the reservation is modified outside of the time window set in the cancellation policy, the restaurant may use the credit card details to charge a fee.

Deposit policies

Restaurants may charge a deposit to secure a reservation. The deposit is paid with a credit card when the reservation is booked. This deposit amount will either be applied to the final bill or refunded.

This policy will be clearly outlined when booking the reservation and in its confirmation email.

Overview

Most OpenTable account issues are caused by a browser, device, or app problem rather than a broken account. By checking your connection, browser or app version, and stored data, you can quickly resolve many issues yourself.

Basic troubleshooting can help when you:

  • Have trouble signing in or receiving verification codes.
  • Notice pages or buttons are not loading correctly.
  • See errors when you use the app or try to update it.
Before you start

Before you follow the steps in this article, check these basics:

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of your browser or the OpenTable app.
  • If you use a VPN, turn it off while you troubleshoot.
  • If possible, keep another device nearby so you can try the same steps on web and app. Back to top

How to troubleshoot on opentable.com

Follow these steps if you are using OpenTable in a web browser and something does not look right.

  • Confirm which browser you are using.
    • OpenTable works best on up-to-date versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge.
  • Try a Private or Incognito window.
    • Open a Private or Incognito window in your browser and sign in to opentable.com from there.
    • Try the same action again and see if the issue continues.
  • Try another supported browser if you can.
    • If you started in Chrome, try Firefox, Safari, or Microsoft Edge instead.
  • Check for browser updates.
    • Open your browser settings and check for updates.
    • Install any updates, then restart the browser and try again.
  • Clear cache and cookies as a last step.
    • If the issue only affects one browser and goes away in a Private or Incognito window, clearing cache and cookies in that browser can help.
    • After you clear cache and cookies, close the browser completely, open it again, and sign back in to your OpenTable account. If you still see the same error after these steps, take a screenshot of what you see and contact OpenTable Support from the Help Center.

How to troubleshoot in the OpenTable app

Use these steps if you have issues with the OpenTable app on your phone or tablet.

  • Check your connection.
    • Make sure your device has a strong Wi‑Fi or cellular signal.
    • Try switching between Wi‑Fi and cellular data.
    • Turn Wi-Fi off for 30 secs, then Wi-Fi on.
    • Turn off any VPN while you use the app.
  • Restart the app and your device.
    • Close the OpenTable app completely.
    • Restart your device, then open the app again and sign in.
  • Check for app and device updates.
    • Open your device settings and check for iOS or Android updates.
    • Open the App Store or Google Play Store and check for OpenTable app updates.
    • Install any updates and try again.
  • Clear app data and reinstall if needed.
    • On Android, clear the OpenTable app cache from your device settings.
    • On iOS, offload the OpenTable app, then reinstall it from the App Store.
  • Try opentable.com in a mobile browser.
    • If the app still does not work on a supported device and OS, open a mobile browser, go to opentable.com, and sign in to your account there while the app issue is investigated. Back to top

  • Make sure the phone number on your account is correct.

  • Confirm you are signed in to the correct account, especially if you have more than one email address.
  • Compare what you see on web and in the app, if possible.

Frequently asked questions related to logging in

What to do if I can’t log in?
  • Double-check that your phone number was entered correctly. Typos are typically why verification codes don’t appear.
  • Double-check that you’re using the very latest code you’ve received. It’s possible to request multiple codes, but only the most recent will work.
  • If attempting to log in by requesting a verification code to your phone number isn’t working, try verifying by email instead.
  • Your account might be deactivated. If an account is deactivated, the verification code won’t provide access to the account and you won’t be able to log in. Learn more about how to unlock your account.
    • Accounts are deactivated when they accrue more than four no-show reservations within a 12-month window. We established this practice to protect our restaurant partners, who are severely harmed when a guest doesn’t show up for a scheduled reservation.
Can I log in with a password?

No, there is no way to log in to opentable.com or OpenTable apps with a password, you must use a verification code send to either your phone number or email address. There is no way to turn off verification codes.

Check if your account is deactivated

Your OpenTable account may be automatically deactivated if you are marked as a no-show for four reservations within a 12‑month period. This policy helps protect restaurants from repeated no‑shows and ensures tables are available for diners who plan to arrive or cancel in advance.

When your account is deactivated:

  • When you try to sign in or verify your account, you may see a message that your account is deactivated due to too many no‑shows.

  • When you try to make a reservation online, you may see a message like “You cannot currently make a reservation” and a link to review the requirements to book online again. If you think your account may be deactivated:

  • Check your email for no‑show notices

    • Any time a restaurant marks you as a no‑show, OpenTable sends an email to the address on your account. The email contains a way to automatically notify OpenTable if the no-show was a mistake.
    • Look for recent emails about no‑shows and check your Spam or Junk folder if you don’t see them in your inbox.
    • Learn about cancellation and no-show disputes.

  • Contact us for help unlocking your account

    • If you believe a no‑show was marked in error or need help reviewing your account status, scroll to the bottom of this page and select the Contact Us button.
    • Include the email address you use for OpenTable and any details about the reservation(s) you’re asking about. Note: If your account is deactivated, signing up for a new account with the same email will not restore your previous account’s history or status.

To help keep your account secure, OpenTable may send you a login activity alert email if:

What does it mean if I receive an “unable to send code” or “unable to send email” response when I try to sign in?

If you receive either of these error messages, “unable to send code” or “unable to send email”, it typically means a VPN is being used on the device you’re trying to sign in with. VPNs will block any request to send verification codes, stopping you from logging in for security reasons. Try signing in on a device that isn’t running a VPN, as you won’t be able to log in if you’re using a device with a VPN.

This article explains the most common reasons this happens, how to check your reservation, and what to do next so you can feel confident your plans are set.

How to address incomplete reservation history

This article helps you understand why a reservation might be missing from your OpenTable account and how to confirm whether it is still booked. It also explains what to do when a reservation was made in a way that does not link to your account.

  • Only reservations booked through the OpenTable app or opentable.com while you are signed in to your account appear in your reservation history.
  • Reservations made directly with a restaurant (by phone, in person, or in the restaurant’s own system) may still be valid but usually do not appear in your OpenTable account.
  • Your online reservation history shows a limited window of past activity, so some older reservations may no longer display. Back to top

How to check for a missing reservation

Follow these steps to check a reservation that is missing from your OpenTable account.

  • Open the OpenTable app or go to opentable.com and sign in using the email address you normally use for OpenTable.
  • Check your upcoming reservations.
  • Go to Upcoming reservations and look for the restaurant name, date, and time you expect.
  • Check other accounts or emails you might use.
  • If you do not see the reservation, think about whether you:
    • Ever used a different email address for OpenTable.
    • Signed in with a social login that uses another email address.
    • Share an email address with a partner or family member who might have booked instead of you.
  • Try signing out and signing back in using any other email address you might have used for OpenTable.
  • Search your email for the confirmation.
  • Search all your email folders, including spam or junk, for the subject line “Your Reservation Confirmation for [Restaurant’s name]”.
  • Check that the reservation was fully completed.
  • If you started a booking but closed the app or browser before selecting Complete reservation, the booking may not have finished and no reservation will appear in your account.
  • Call the restaurant if you are unsure.
  • Contact the restaurant directly and ask them to confirm whether they see a reservation under your name, date, time, and contact details.
    • If they do see it, your reservation is still valid even if it does not appear in your OpenTable account.
    • If they do not see it, you may need to book a new reservation.
  • Contact OpenTable Support if you still need help.
  • If you have a confirmation email or the restaurant has confirmed your reservation, but it still does not appear in your account, contact OpenTable Support from the Help Center. Include your name, email, restaurant name, city, date, time, and a copy or screenshot of your confirmation. Back to top

Troubleshooting

Use these tips if something still does not look right after you follow the steps above.

If the restaurant sees your reservation but you do not
  • The reservation was likely created directly by the restaurant or as a guest booking outside the signed-in OpenTable flow.
  • These reservations usually do not link to your OpenTable account, so they will not appear in your reservation history.
  • The reservation is still valid as long as the restaurant confirms it. If you need to change or cancel it, contact the restaurant directly.
  • For future visits, book through the OpenTable app or website while you are signed in so your reservations show in your account and earn any available rewards.
If you never received a confirmation email
  • Make sure you selected Complete reservation at the end of the booking flow. If this step did not complete, the reservation may not have been created.
  • Check your spam or junk folder for a confirmation email with the subject “Your Reservation Confirmation for [Restaurant’s name]”.
  • If you cannot find a confirmation and the restaurant does not see your booking, you will need to create a new reservation.
If older reservations are missing from your history
  • Your online reservation history usually displays roughly the last year of activity, and older reservations may drop off sooner for very frequent diners.
  • You can still use any Dining Feedback emails you received to review older visits if those emails are available in your inbox.
  • If you need a formal copy of your full reservation history for privacy or legal reasons, contact OpenTable Support from the Help Center so the team can review your request. Back to top

Related frequently asked questions

Can a restaurant-made reservation appear in my OpenTable account?

Reservations that a restaurant creates directly in its own system or on your behalf are usually not linked to your signed-in OpenTable account. Those bookings may still be valid, but they will not show in your OpenTable reservation history.

Why do I only see reservations from the last year?

Your OpenTable reservation history is designed to show a recent window of activity, roughly the last year, and very active diners may see older reservations fall off sooner. This helps keep your account easier to scan while the underlying data is managed securely.

What should I do if I have a confirmation email but no reservation in my account?

First, confirm you are signed in with the same email address that appears on the confirmation, and check your Upcoming reservations again. If the booking still does not appear, contact the restaurant to confirm they see your reservation, then contact OpenTable Support with your confirmation details so the team can review what happened and explain your options.

Can OpenTable move a past reservation into my account history?

Support can confirm how a reservation was created and help you understand why it does or does not appear in your history, but they typically cannot move or recreate past reservations into your account if they were booked outside the signed-in OpenTable flow. For future visits, always book while you are signed in so reservations and any eligible rewards appear correctly.
