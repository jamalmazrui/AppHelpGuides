I’m having problems signing in.

For iOS app users having issues, please download the latest update from the App Store. Avoid hard closing the app to maintain your sign in.

If you can’t remember your password, follow these instructions to reset your password.

Occasionally, we hear from members who report that the sign-in page is loading incompletely—they see a spinning wheel or get an error message. This is usually a browser-related problem. Try temporarily turning off any extensions or ad blockers you may have installed, or try a different browser.

If you are using software to block ads, please make sure to allow the following domains, which are services we use for authentication and consent management: tinypass.com, onetrust.com.

If you created your account using your Facebook, Yahoo, Google, or Twitter account, make sure to click the appropriate button to log in.

If you are having trouble staying signed into the Slate iOS app, please do not force close the app. You will currently stay logged in while the window is open in the background on your device. We are investigating how to maintain that sign in longer and appreciate your patience.

If you frequently need to enter your email and password while browsing Slate in browser, please open a support ticket below and let us know your operating system and which browser you are using.

For additional support, email plus@slate.com or click New support ticket on our support homepage. You can help us provide speedier service by including the following information in your message:

  • What steps you are taking
  • The problem you encountered, including any error messages you saw
  • Visit this page to help us identify your computer and browsing configuration. Copy and paste the information into your message.

I’m signed in, but I’m not being recognized as a member.

For website or app issues, one common cause of this problem is when members have more than one site account without realizing it; one is a standard Slate account, and the other is Slate Plus. Verify which email address you used to sign up, which may be different than the one on your receipt.

Make sure to access your account through the same log-in you used to sign up for Slate Plus—if you created your account using your Facebook, Yahoo, Google, Apple, or Twitter account, make sure to click the appropriate button to log in. After signing in, verify what email address appears on your account page to be sure you were linked to the correct account.

If so, but you’re still having trouble, first sign out of your account from the upper right corner. Then sign back in. That usually resolves any access issues.

If logging out doesn’t help. it’s also possible that you have enabled an extension, ad blocker, or certain browser settings that are interfering with the login system. Try disabling your ad blocker, adjusting your settings, or using an alternate browser.

If that doesn’t work, email plus@slate.com or click New support ticket on our support homepage. You can help us provide speedier service by including the following information in your message:

  • What steps you are taking
  • The problem you encountered, including any error messages you saw
  • Your receipt or Plus member welcome email confirming your sign up or renewal was successful.
  • Visit this page to help us identify your computer and browsing configuration. Copy and paste the information into your message.

Billing Questions

Can I pay for Slate Plus with my bank account?

Yes, if you have a U.S. bank account.* When you first purchase Slate Plus, choose the payment method option “Bank Account,” then click the button labeled GoCardless. It will open a new form to enter your information. After you submit that form, you will finish the checkout process.

*At this time, Slate can only process direct debit transactions in USD$ from U.S. bank accounts.

How to add a bank account to your existing Slate Plus subscription

If you are already a subscriber, you can add your U.S. bank account as a payment method for future transactions.

Step 1: Sign in to your Account
  • Visit slate.com/account; if you’re not already signed in, you will be prompted to do so.
Step 2: Add your bank information
  • Go to Subscriptions & Payments. In the Payment section of the page, click the link for “Add Direct Debit.” It will open a new form to enter your contact and bank account information. After you submit the form, it will close. Then click Save in the Payment section.
Step 3: Change your default payment method to direct debit
  • You can change your payment method from credit card or PayPal to direct debit in two ways:
    • click the link “Set as default” for the bank account you added
    • click the link “Choose payment method” in the Subscription section, then choose the bank account Did you find it helpful? Yes No

How do I stop my membership from auto-renewing?

To turn off renewal, visit your Slate Plus account page. You can get there by selecting Account, in the right corner of your browser. (If you’re not already signed in with your Slate Plus account, first click the Sign In link to sign in.)

Within your Account, go to the Subscription & Payment page, and toggle Auto-renew subscription to OFF.

This will turn off your membership at the end of its term, and you will not be charged again. Your access will continue for the rest of your paid period, After that, you will no longer have access to the ad-free or exclusive versions of Slate podcasts and will only be allowed access to a limited quantity of articles.

If you signed up for Slate Plus in Slate’s iOS app or in Apple Podcasts, follow these instructions to stop auto-renewing.

How do I sign in to Slate Plus?

In the top right corner of any Slate page, click Sign In.

If you created your account using your Facebook, Google, or Apple account, make sure to click the appropriate button to log in.

Otherwise, enter the email you used when you created your account, then click Next.

  • If your account does not have a password, you will be sent an email with a link and a code to sign in. You can either click the link in the email, or enter the code on the screen that appears. Using the code is the best way to sign into the Once signed in, you’ll have access to unlimited articles and all of your podcasts will automatically play the ad-free extended versions in place of the regular ones here: slate.com. Or you can subscribe to the Slate Plus version of any show in the podcast app of your choice.

How to sign in to Slate without a password

There are two ways to sign in to Slate.com with your email address: with and without a password.

First, click the Sign In link at the top of any page or go to slate.com/sign-in. Then enter your email address.

If your account does not require a password, you will be sent an email with a sign-in link and a confirmation code. Either click the link in the email or enter the confirmation code on the screen that appears next.

If you are prompted to enter a password at slate.com/sign-in then your account requires a password. Finish signing in by entering your password. You can also find more instructions here: How do I sign in to Slate Plus?

How to Add a Password to Your Account

If you would like to sign in with a password in the future, you may add one to your account. Once you do, you will always be prompted to enter both your email and a password.

  • Navigate to your Account at slate.com/account.
  • Under the Email field, click the link for Add Password.
  • Two password fields will appear; enter your new password in both.

Web Push Notifications

Web push notifications provides another way you can allow Slate to inform you of new articles, Slate Plus offers, and more.

How Do I Opt-Out?

Each browser has a unique way of unsubscribing from push notifications. Please follow the appropriate instructions for your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) and platform (Windows, Mac, Android).

Chrome

  • In your Chrome Browser, visit chrome://settings/content/notifications
  • The Block list are sites you have blocked from being prompted. The Allow list is the list of browsers you opted-in for push.
  • Find the site the notification was from in the list and click the 3 dots icon.
  • You can now Click Remove to allow the site to prompt you for push again. Or you can click Block to prevent the site from sending you push. Alternatively, You can unsubscribe from a notification directly by clicking the gear icon (PC) or “Settings” button (Mac) in the notification, which opens the browser notification preferences.

Chrome Android

To unsubscribe from Chrome on Android when you receive a notification:

  • Tap the notification when it is in your notification tray
  • Tap Site Settings
  • Tap Clear & Reset
  • Confirm Clear & Reset Safari

Safari doesn’t provide an option to unsubscribe from within a notification itself, so notifications must be disabled from within Safari’s Preferences.

  • Open Safari. Click Safari > Preferences
  • Under the Websites tab, click Notifications
  • Identify the site you want to unsubscribe from, and change Allow to Deny Firefox (PC)

Unsubscribe when receiving a notification in Firefox (PC):

  • Click the gear icon in the notification

  • Click Disable notifications from (site) Unsubscribe from a site you are on in Firefox (PC):

  • Click the info button to the left of the site’s URL

  • Click the ‘X’ next to the Permissions for Receive Notifications Unsubscribe from browser preferences in Firefox (PC):

  • Go to the browser privacy preferences by entering in the URL about:preferences#privacy

  • Scroll down to Permissions and click Settings, next to Notifications

  • Find the site you want to unsubscribe from and click Allow

  • Change Allow to Block, and then click Save Changes Firefox (Mac)

Unsubscribe when receiving a notification in Firefox (Mac)

  • Click the ‘…’ button in the notification

  • Click ‘Disable notification from (site).’ Unsubscribe from a site you are on in Firefox (Mac)

  • Click the info button to the left of the site’s URL.

  • Click the ‘X’ next to the Permissions for Receive Notifications Unsubscribe from browser preferences in Firefox (Mac)

  • Go to the browser privacy preferences by entering in the URL about:preferences#privacy

  • Scroll down to Permissions and click Settings…next to Notifications

  • Find the site you want to unsubscribe from and click Allow

  • Change Allow to Block, and then click Save Changes. Firefox (Android)

Apple Podcasts and iTunes

How to subscribe automatically in Apple Podcasts on your mobile device

  1. Visit your Account Page and click Podcasts (make sure you’re signed in to your account).

  2. Click Set Up next to the show name

  3. If you are on a mobile device, you’ll see a view like the image below. Just tap Apple Podcasts to subscribe.

If you’re on a desktop or laptop computer, it will look like this image. Enter your phone number and we’ll text a private link that you can click to open and then add the selected show or feed to your podcast app.

  1. Repeat for all the shows you’d like to add.

  2. Listen! Your Plus episodes will appear under the show’s new icon in your podcast app library.

How to subscribe automatically in iTunes or Apple Podcasts on your computer

  1. Visit the member podcast feed page (make sure you’re signed in to your account).

  2. Click Set Up next to the show name you want to subscribe to.

  3. Under “Listen on your computer” select the Apple Podcasts or iTunes button to subscribe.

  4. Repeat for every show you want to listen to.

How to manually subscribe to a podcast in Apple Podcasts

First, you’ll need to grab the unique feed URL for that podcast. You can find your unique feeds on the member podcast feed page (make sure you’re signed in to your account).  Click Set Up next to the show name, then find the section that says “Copy your ad-free link ..” and click the Copy button.

How do I access the ad-free podcasts and bonus podcast content?

You won’t find the Slate Plus versions of shows by searching in the public directories of iTunes or other podcast players. They’re special, private feeds that you’ll need to add into your podcast player—don’t worry, you can do so in just a couple of taps! (You can also listen inside Slate’s iOS app—log in once with your Slate Plus account and all member podcasts are available. Learn more about that here.)

  1. To subscribe in your podcast player visit your Account Page and click Podcasts (make sure you’re signed in to your account).

  2. Click Set Up next to the show name

  3. If you are on a mobile device, when you visit the link, you’ll see a view like the one below. Tap the name of your favorite podcast app to subscribe.

If you’re on a desktop or laptop computer you’ll see a view like this one, enter your phone number and we’ll text a private link that you can click to open and then add the selected show or feed to your podcast app.

  1. Repeat for each of the shows you’d like to add.

  2. Listen! Your Plus episodes will appear under the show’s new icon in your podcast app library.

You can also add a podcast feed to any player manually by copying and pasting the URL and then adding it, following that app’s instructions.

Using Slate Plus in Slate’s app

Accessing Slate Plus content outside the iOS app

If you purchased a membership through Slate’s iOS app, you’ll need to create a Slate account, or sign into an existing account, in order to access member benefits on Slate’s website.

(We can’t create your account automatically, or link to an existing one, because Apple doesn’t share your email address or other information with us.)

After you sign up for Slate Plus in the app, you’ll be presented with a screen prompting you to sign in to an existing Slate account or create a new account. If you follow that prompt, your Slate account will be connected to your Slate Plus membership.

If during this step, you clicked the Apple ID option, you will always need to click the Apple ID button on the sign in page to log into both the app and the website. (This does not associate your sign in with your Apple ID email address, but instead with an anonymous Apple sign in.) To change your sign in type, email us at plus@slate.com

If you don’t create an account when you first purchase a Slate Plus membership, you can connect one later. In the app, tap the menu and then Log In / Sign Up.

Once you have created a Slate Plus account, check out our instructions here to access the members-only podcasts in your favorite outside podcast player.

Billing if you purchased Slate Plus through the iOS app or Apple Podcasts

When you purchase Slate Plus through Slate’s iOS app or the Apple Podcasts app, your subscription is tied to your Apple ID.

Your account will renew automatically at the end of each month or year until you choose to end it. Payment is charged to the payment method you have associated with your Apple ID.

To turn off auto-renewal, go to your subscription settings in the App Store. When your current subscription expires, you won’t be billed again and your Slate Plus membership will end.

You must turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current subscription period, or your account will be billed for the next period. Unfortunately, Apple won’t let us cancel subscriptions during an active subscription period.

I’m having trouble accessing Slate Plus content in the app.

If you’re having trouble with the Slate app, please download the latest update from the App Store or Google Play Store. For sign in trouble, you might try minimizing the window rather than closing out of it and signing back into your account as needed.

If you are receiving an error “Problem Linking Account”, please close the error window, and you should find you are able to continue reading. This message is not a reflection of your Plus member status.

Listening to members-only podcasts in the Slate app (iOS, Android)

Select the Menu icon from the main screen (three parallel lines), then select Podcasts. Then tap Shows.

Tap the show you want to listen to, then tap the Slate logo under “Get your ad-free Slate Plus podcast:”

This will open a new window inside the app. On that page, you can tap to subscribe and listen to the show

in the podcast player of your choice; just follow the instructions here.

You can also choose to listen to an individual episode inside of this new window. Tap on an episode name to go to that episode and use the audio player on the page. Please note: this player cannot keep track of your position in the episode if you close and re-open the app. For the best listening experience, follow the instructions above to subscribe to your favorite shows in your preferred podcast player.
