Identify music

Identify a song using the Shazam app on headphones or earphones on iPhone or iPad

Use Shazam to identify music when you’re wearing headphones or earphones that are paired with your iPhone or iPad (or connected to it with a cable). Shazam can identify not only the music you’re listening to on your headphones or earphones, but also the music playing around you. To ensure the best experience, make sure the Shazam app has access to your device’s microphone. Note: Shazam can’t identify songs through headphones or earphones when you’re not connected to the internet. #### Identify songs while using headphones with iPhone or iPad 1. Make sure your headphones or earphones are connected to your device. 2. Do either of the following:Go to the Shazam app  home screen, then tap the Shazam button .(image: Shazam app home screen showing connected headphones) 3. Open Control Center, then tap (image: the Shazam Music Recognition button).See Identify songs using Control Center.

When you subscribe to Apple Music, you can open and play your identified songs in the Music app . You can also automatically add identified songs to a “My Shazam Tracks” playlist—or a playlist of your choice—in Apple Music. Additionally, you can open and play songs that Shazam identifies as classical in the Apple Music Classical app . Use the Shazam app or Shazam Music Recognition when you’re offline

On iPhone, iPad, or an Android device, you can use the Shazam app to identify a song when you’re offline (not connected to the internet). When you’re back online, the app remembers the request, identifies the song (if it’s available in the Shazam music catalog), then adds it to your Shazam app library. On iPhone or iPad, you can also use Shazam Music Recognition in Control Center when you’re offline. To receive a notification about an identified song after you’re back online, do either of the following: - After using the Shazam app when you’re offline on iPhone, iPad, or an Android device: Make sure Shazam app notifications are turned on. - After using Music Recognition in Control Center when you’re offline on iPhone: Make sure Music Recognition notifications are turned on.

Note: To add songs identified using Control Center to your Shazam library, make sure iCloud Sync is turned on in Shazam app settings.

Set up and get started

Before you use the Shazam app

This guide is for the Shazam app on iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS Tahoe, watchOS 26, visionOS 26, or later. This guide is also for the Shazam app on Android. To ensure the best possible Shazam experience, do the following: - Update your devices to the latest operating systems. - Get the Shazam app for all your devices, or if you’ve already downloaded the app, make sure it’s updated to the latest version. - On Apple devices: Sign in to the same Apple Account on all your devices and make sure your iCloud and Shazam app settings are correctly configured so that songs you identify using Control Center, Siri, or Shortcuts are synced to the Shazam app. - On Android devices: Sign in with your email or Google Account and get the Apple Music app for Android. - If you’re not already a subscriber, join Apple Music to listen to songs in full (on Apple devices) or song previews (on Android devices) in the Shazam app. As an Apple Music subscriber, you can listen to songs in full in Apple Music and Apple Music Classical, and add your identified songs to Apple Music playlists. - If you’re a classical music fan, get the Apple Music Classical app to quickly open identified songs in Apple Music Classical.Note: The availability of the App Store, subscriptions, and services vary by country or region. See the Apple Support article Availability of Apple Media Services.

Sync songs to the Shazam app using Control Center, Siri, and more

When your iCloud and Shazam app settings are correctly configured, songs you identify using Control Center, Siri, the Smart Stack on Apple Watch, or the Shortcuts app are automatically synced to the library in the Shazam app. iCloud securely stores your identified songs and keeps them updated across all your devices (when you’re signed in to the same Apple Account on all your devices)—without using any of your iCloud storage. To confirm that your identified songs are synced to the Shazam app on all your Apple devices, do the following: 1. Make sure you’ve updated the Shazam app to the latest version. 2. If you haven’t done so already, turn on two-factor authentication for your Apple Account:On iPhone or iPad: Go to Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security, then tap Two-Factor Authentication. Enter a trusted phone number (the number you’ll use to receive verification codes), tap Next, then follow the verification code instructions. 3. On Mac: Choose Apple menu  > System Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security, click Two-Factor Authentication, then follow the onscreen instructions. 4. Make sure Music Recognition and Shazam are turned on:On iPhone or iPad: Go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud, tap See All (next to Saved to iCloud), then in the list of apps, confirm that Music Recognition and Shazam are turned on. 5. On Mac: Choose Apple menu  > System Settings > [your name] > iCloud, click See All (next to Saved to iCloud), then in the list of apps, confirm that Music Recognition is turned on. 6. On Apple Vision Pro: Go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud, tap See All (next to Saved to iCloud), then in the list of apps, confirm that Music Recognition and Shazam are turned on. 7. Make sure iCloud is turned on in Shazam app settings:On iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro: Go to the Shazam app  home screen, tap , then confirm that iCloud Sync is turned on.

Miscellaneous

Listen to your identified songs in Spotify, Deezer, or another app on an Android device

On an Android device, you can automatically add songs you identify to a “My Shazam Tracks” playlist in Spotify, or open and play your identified songs in another app, such as Deezer or YouTube Music. You can also set a default music app for listening to your identified songs, letting you quickly open the song in your chosen app. Note: To use Spotify or other music apps with Shazam, the apps must be installed on your device, and you must be a subscriber. #### Sync your identified songs to a “My Shazam Tracks” playlist in Spotify on an Android device 1. Go to the Shazam app on an Android device, then tap (image: the Library button) (or swipe to the Library). 2. Tap (image: the Settings button), make sure Spotify is set as your default music app, then turn on Sync Shazam Library to Spotify.A “My Shazam Tracks” playlist is created in Spotify, and your identified songs are automatically added to the playlist.

Although you can’t add your identified songs to the “My Shazam Tracks” playlist in Apple Music and Spotify simultaneously, all of your songs are saved in your library in the Shazam app and are available on all of your devices (when you’re signed in). #### Open an identified song in Spotify, Deezer, or another music app on an Android device 1. Go to the Shazam app on an Android device, then tap (image: the Library button) (or swipe to the Library).To see all songs, tap Songs. 2. Do any of the following:Tap (image: the More button) on a song, then tap an option in the list. 3. Tap a song, tap “Open song in” in the track screen, tap an available music app, then tap Just Once or Always. (Always isn’t available for Apple Music Classical or YouTube.)If you tapped Always, the music app you chose is set as the default and the “Open song in” button is replaced by that app’s button. 4. If you’ve set a default music app (or connected Shazam to Spotify in Shazam app settings), tap the [music app name] button to open the song in that app.

Set your default music app on an Android device

  • Go to the Shazam app on an Android device, tap (image: the Library button) (or swipe to the Library), then do either of the following:Tap Songs, tap a song, tap “Open song in” in the track screen, tap an available music app, then tap Always.
  • Tap (image: the Settings button), tap “Open songs in other music apps,” tap an available music app, then tap “Set default.”Apple Music Classical and YouTube can’t be set as defaults.

The “Open song in” button in the track screen is replaced by a button for the music app you chose. You can easily change your default music app. #### Change your default music app on an Android device - Go to the Shazam app on an Android device, tap (image: the Library button) (or swipe to the Library), then do either of the following:Tap Songs, tap a song, tap and hold the [music app name] button in the track screen, tap an available music app, then tap Always. - Tap (image: the Settings button), tap “Default music app,” tap an available music app, then tap “Set default.”
