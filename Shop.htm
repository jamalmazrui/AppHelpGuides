Shop help

This guide holds Shopify’s own help for Shop, gathered from help.shop.app in November 2026. Shop is Shopify’s shopping and delivery tracking app, on the iPhone and on the web at shop.com. It covers getting started, shopping, tracking deliveries, managing orders, Shop Cash rewards, and reporting a problem.

Shop and Shop Pay share one help centre, so this collection splits it in two: Shop.md for the Shop app and ShopPay.md for paying with Shop Pay and Shop Pay Installments. Each section opens with Shopify’s own overview of it. Shopify’s help for store owners is a separate guide, Shopify.md. Android-only articles are left out, as this collection covers Windows, the web, iPhone and iPad, and Alexa.

Delivery tracking

Overview: Delivery tracking

You can track orders in the Shop app or on shop.com to view delivery status and location. Orders from Shopify stores and Shop Pay purchases populate automatically, without connecting an email account. To automatically track orders from other stores, you can connect a supported email account. You can also add tracking information to Shop manually.

Shop isn’t involved in the fulfillment or delivery process for your order. Your order is managed by the online store you purchased from. After an order is shipped, its delivery is managed by the shipping carrier associated with the tracking number for your order. If you have questions about your order status or delivery, then refer to Reporting an order issue and getting help.

If you’ve contacted the store and shipping carrier and haven’t been able to resolve the issue, then you can report an issue with your order.

Report incorrect order information

If an unexpected or incorrect order is displayed in the Shop app, then you can review its details, take steps to protect your account, or report the information as incorrect.

Review an unrecognized order in Shop

If you don’t recognize an order, then review its details and check your email for a matching order confirmation. To review the order details, tap the order from the Home or Orders tab.

Unrecognized orders

If you still don’t recognize the order after reviewing its details and checking your email, then check your bank account to confirm whether you were charged for the order:

  • If you weren’t charged for the order, then proceed to report the order as Not my order in Shop.
  • If you were charged for the order, then contact your bank to start a dispute. After you start the dispute, report the order as Not my order in Shop.

If you can’t match an order to an email confirmation, or you didn’t place an order that’s displayed in Shop, then you can report the order.

Shop Promise

The Shop Promise badge is displayed on products from US stores that meet the Shop Promise requirements. The badge names what you can count on when you buy that product:

  • Trusted delivery: Orders for this type of product from this store consistently reach the carrier quickly.
  • Free returns: You can return the product without paying for return shipping or a restocking fee.
  • Returns accepted: You can return the product under the store’s return policy, but fees might apply.

A product can display one or more of these benefits. An estimated arrival date might also be displayed with the badge. There are no subscription or membership fees for Shop Promise.

You can find the Shop Promise badge in the following places:

  • Product pages
  • Checkout
  • Order confirmation and order status pages
  • The Shop app

On the store’s online product pages, at checkout, on the order confirmation page, and on your order status page, the badge can display the store’s return window instead of Returns accepted, for example 30-day returns. This means that you can return the product within that number of days under the store’s return policy, but fees might apply. In the Shop app, the badge displays Free returns or Returns accepted instead of a return window.

If the products in your order have different return windows, then each product displays its own return window. If any product offers free returns, then no return windows are displayed. Final sale items can’t be returned. They display Final sale at checkout when you check out with Shop Pay, and on your order status page. The return window is still displayed on your order status page after the window ends.

Returns are handled by the store under its own return policy. If you have a question about a return, then contact the store.

Limited guarantee for earlier orders

Stores are moving to an updated Shop Promise. The updated Shop Promise doesn’t include a delivery guarantee, so orders placed after a store moved aren’t eligible for Shop Cash when they arrive late.

Some earlier orders are still covered under the previous terms. These orders were placed before the store moved to the updated Shop Promise, and qualified for the limited guarantee. If the first delivery attempt for one of these orders was later than its estimated delivery date, then you might be eligible to receive $5.00 USD in Shop Cash. You can spend Shop Cash in the Shop app or on the Shop website. Learn more about the eligibility requirements for the limited guarantee.

Claim your Shop Cash

How you claim Shop Cash for an eligible order depends on the payment method that you used at checkout. You can claim in the Shop app or on the Shop website.

Orders made using Shop Pay

If you paid with Shop Pay and your order arrived late, then you can check whether it qualifies and claim $5.00 USD in Shop Cash from the order.

Steps:
  • From the Shop app, go to the tab.
  • Tap the order that was delivered late.
  • On the Shop Promise card, tap Claim Shop Cash.
  • If you’re prompted to complete Shop Pay setup, then follow the prompts, and then return to the order and tap Claim Shop Cash again.
Orders made using other payment methods

If you didn’t pay with Shop Pay, then you can claim only if it’s your first claim with that store. You need a verified Shop account and Shop Pay account to make the claim. After you set up both accounts, follow the same steps to claim your Shop Cash.

Tracking your orders with Shop

You can use the Shop app or shop.com to track orders with a valid tracking number. After you add an order to Shop, Shop uses information from the carrier to provide you with tracking updates.

When tracking an order, the delivery details page displays the following information, if available:

  • A tracking map with the approximate location of your package during transit
  • The store name, order status, and shipping updates
  • The tracking number with a copy option
  • The carrier name
  • A tracking URL to the carrier site
  • A delivery date estimate that’s updated as your shipment progresses, labeled Predicted by Shop when it differs from the carrier’s date
  • A menu with more options to contact the online store and shipping carrier, edit or delete your order, and report incorrect tracking information

Automatic order tracking

Shop can track many of your orders automatically in either of the following ways:

  • Orders from Shopify stores and Shop Pay: These orders are tracked automatically. Shop doesn’t access your email inbox to track these orders.
  • Orders from other online stores: If you choose to connect a supported email account, then Shop can scan your inbox for these orders. Connecting an email account is optional.
Orders from Shopify stores and Shop Pay

Orders that you place using Shop Pay, or from a store that uses Shopify features, products, or services, are added to your Orders tab automatically when the email that you used for the order matches one of the email addresses on your Shop account. This works as soon as you set up a Shop account, without connecting an email account or giving Shop access to your inbox.

Orders from other stores

After you complete checkout on a participating online store, you might be prompted to track your order with Shop from the order confirmation or order status page. The prompt can open the Shop app, ask you to install the Shop app, or ask you to sign in so the order can be added to Shop.

To track orders from stores that don’t use Shopify, you can connect a supported email account to follow all of your online orders in one place. After you connect an email account, Shop scans your inbox for order and tracking emails from the last 30 days, and then keeps checking for new tracking emails. When Shop finds a tracking number, the delivery is added to your Orders tab.

For automatic order tracking with Gmail and Outlook accounts, Shop searches only for emails with keywords such as tracking number and track your package, and doesn’t read the rest of your inbox.

Not all orders from stores outside of the Shop network are automatically tracked, because Shop can’t read some tracking emails. If an order isn’t added, then you can add it to Shop manually.

You can manage or disconnect a connected account at any time from your connected accounts.

If an order doesn’t automatically populate in the Shop app, or you don’t want to connect an email account to Shop, then you can manually add a tracking number to Shop, or forward your order tracking emails.

  • From the Shop app, go to the tab.
  • Tap > Add order manually.
  • Optional: If you need to search a connected email for a tracking number, then tap Search email for a tracking number, select the email that you want to search, and then copy the tracking number.
  • Fill in the Tracking number, Package name, and Carrier fields.
  • Tap Add order.

shop.com

  • On a web browser, go to shop.com.
  • Click Sign in, and then follow the prompts to sign in to your Shop account.
  • Click the icon.
  • Click > Add order manually.
  • Fill in the Tracking number, Package name, and Carrier fields.
  • Click Add order.
Forward your order tracking emails to Shop

You can add an order to Shop to track manually by forwarding an email that’s in English and includes a tracking number. If you set up automatic email forwarding through your email service provider, then make sure to use your unique Shop forwarding email address as the recipient.

Steps:
  • From the Shop app, go to the tab.
  • Tap > Add order manually.
  • In the Forward shipping emails section, tap Open Email app, and then select an email app such as Gmail or Outlook to continue.
  • From your email inbox, find your shipping confirmation email containing a tracking number.
  • Forward the email to your unique Shop forwarding email address by pasting it from your clipboard.

If you forward an email containing a tracking number that you’re already tracking in Shop, then it won’t be added. If your delivery isn’t displayed in the Shop app after you forward an email, then it could be because the Shop app had issues tracking it. You can add the tracking number to Shop manually instead.

Understanding order status updates in Shop

You can view the status of your orders in the Orders tab of the Shop app, or on shop.com. Order statuses communicate the current state of your package.

On this page

  • Before your order ships
  • After your order ships
  • Canceled orders and refunds

Before your order ships

The following order status updates are based on information from the store that you purchased from, if the store is within the Shop network:

  • Waiting for details: A new order has been detected, but the Shop app is waiting for more information. The order might already have tracking details, but the carrier might not have updated the tracking information yet. If there are no tracking details, then refer to Reporting an order issue and getting help for guidance on contacting the store.
  • Order placed: Your order has been placed, but the store hasn’t shipped it yet.
  • Shipped: The store has added tracking information to your order, and the package is on its way to the shipping carrier.
  • Shipped partially or Partially shipped: The store has added tracking information, and some items have been shipped.
  • Ready for pickup: If you placed an order for in-store pickup, then it’s ready to be picked up.
  • Picked up: Your order status is displayed as picked up after you pick up your order. The store that you purchased from needs to send pickup notification and confirmation emails to update the order status to Ready for pickup or Picked up.

After your order ships

The following order statuses are based on information from the shipping carrier:

  • In transit: Your package is on its way to the shipping distribution center.
  • Out for delivery: Your package is on its way to you.
  • Delivered: Your package has been delivered, or you’ve marked the order as delivered in the Shop app. If there aren’t any details in the Shop app of where the carrier left your package, then check the carrier’s website.
  • Delivered partially: Some items in your order have been delivered. You’ll receive another update when the rest of your order is delivered.
  • Delayed: The delivery of your package is delayed.
  • Action required: An action is required for your shipment to proceed. For example, you might need to pay a customs fee.
  • Failure: The delivery carrier has reported a delivery failure. Refer to Reporting an order issue and getting help for guidance on contacting the carrier.

Canceled orders and refunds

The following order cancellation and refund statuses are displayed in the app if the store has a profile in Shop:

  • Canceled: The online store that you purchased from has canceled your order, and it won’t be fulfilled.
  • Partially refunded: The online store that you purchased from has partially refunded your order.
  • Refunded: The online store that you purchased from has fully refunded your order.

Tracking updates might be displayed in different time zones because they’re based on your delivery location at the time of each update.

Shop is a shopping destination and delivery tracking app created by Shopify. You can access Shop on the Shop app for iOS and Android, or on the web at shop.com. To learn how stores qualify to sell on Shop and how to review a store before you buy, refer to Shopping with confidence on Shop.

Discover and shop

You can use Shop to browse and discover products from participating Shopify stores, follow stores to get updates about new arrivals and sales, and save favorite products for later. You can make a purchase from a Shop store in the app or on shop.com. Some stores offer pricing and rewards through Shop offers.

Payment methods and rewards

Shop Pay is a wallet that stores your email, shipping addresses, and payment information. When you check out on a store that accepts Shop Pay, you pay with your saved payment details. You don’t have to enter them again. Some stores offer Shop Pay Installments, so you can split your purchase into multiple payments on eligible orders. Earn Shop Cash on eligible purchases and redeem your Shop Cash towards future orders in Shop.

You can track your deliveries in real-time and manage your orders from the Shop app. This lets you view order details and track your delivery journey. You can also review an order in Shop after you receive it.

You can keep your Shop account settings up to date with your current payment information, shipping addresses, and privacy preferences. You can also manage your Shop profile to save your shopping preferences and receive more personalized product recommendations.

Managing your orders in Shop

Common issues and troubleshooting steps for orders

Some issues that you might encounter with orders in the Shop app are related to how order information is displayed, rather than a problem with the order itself. This page covers the most common issues and how to resolve them. For issues that need the attention of the store or the Shop support team, refer to Reporting an order issue and getting help.

Pre-orders

Pre-orders might be displayed in your Past orders section even if they haven’t been delivered yet. The Shop app uses active tracking information to inform you about your order. Because pre-orders don’t usually provide tracking updates until the order is ready, the order might be displayed as inactive in the Shop app.

Shipped orders without tracking details

Orders from stores in the Shop network might have a Shipped status without tracking details. To learn why this happens and how to contact the store, refer to Reporting an order issue and getting help.

Order edits made by the store

The Shop app doesn’t currently reflect any order edits made by the store. This includes returns and removed items. Order exchanges might also cause the Shop app to display both the original product and the new product on your order details.

Order marked as delivered, but not received

If you accidentally mark an order as delivered in the Shop app, then you can unmark the order as delivered to continue tracking it.

The Unmark as delivered option disappears 7 days after the order was marked as delivered.

To cancel an order or request a refund, contact the store that you purchased from. The process for submitting a return depends on the store that you purchase from.

Refunds on orders that earned Shop Cash

If you earn Shop Cash on an order, and the order is later refunded, then the Shop Cash that you earned from that order is deducted from your Shop Cash balance. This includes Shop Cash that you’ve already spent.

Learn more about earning Shop Cash in the Shop Rewards Terms and Conditions.

Refunds on orders that were paid for using Shop Cash

When you receive a refund on an order where you used Shop Cash, the Shop Cash is returned to your balance. However, expired Shop Cash isn’t returned to your balance after you receive a refund.

Full refunds

With full refunds, you’re refunded in the same way you paid for the order, except for expired Shop Cash. Expired Shop Cash isn’t credited back to your balance after a refund is issued. The following is a detailed breakdown of how full refunds are processed when Shop Cash is involved:

  • Initial refund process: When you receive a full refund on an order that used Shop Cash as part of the payment, the refund is initially applied to your Shop Pay payment method.
  • Subsequent refunds: Any remaining amount after the initial refund is then returned to your Shop Cash balance, ensuring that you’re reimbursed appropriately.

Partial refunds

With partial refunds, the amount of Shop Cash you get back depends on the store’s refund process:

  • Refund allocation: The store decides the portion of the refund that goes back to your Shop Pay payment method and the amount that is restored to your Shop Cash balance. Refer to the store’s refund policy for more information about refunds.
  • Processing order refunds: If the refund is from an order where Shop Cash was applied without any special offer, then the refund will first apply to your payment card. Subsequently, any leftover amount will be credited back to your Shop Cash.
  • Refund limits: The refund amount credited to either your Shop Pay payment method or Shop Cash balance will not exceed the original amount used in each respective payment type.
  • Refund processing time: The refunded Shop Cash may take up to 48 hours to reflect in your balance once the online store has processed the refund.

Learn more about refunds on orders that were paid for using Shop Cash in the Shop Rewards Terms and Conditions.

Shop Cash expiration and refunds

Shop Cash might be removed from your balance either when it expires or if you receive a refund on an order where you had earned Shop Cash.

Shop Cash expiration

Shop Cash expires on a rolling basis, which means different amounts might have different expiration dates.

  • Unless stated otherwise, most Shop Cash expires 180 days after you earn it. In Canada, Ontario or Quebec residents can check the Shop Rewards Terms for certain exceptions.
  • If you receive Shop Cash as part of a promotion or giveaway, then that reward might have a different expiration period.
  • If you only redeem part of your Shop Cash on an order, then the expiration date on your remaining Shop Cash balance resets from the date of that purchase.

Shop Cash might be removed from your balance either when it expires, or if you receive a refund on an order where you earned Shop Cash.

After your Shop Cash expires, it’s removed from your Shop Cash balance, and can’t be recovered.

Applying a Shop offer to your order

If your order meets the requirements for a Shop offer, such as minimum order value, then it applies automatically to your cart at checkout. A Shop offer isn’t confirmed until the offer is applied to your cart at checkout.

Shop offers apply only when you check out on the Shop app or shop.com. Shop offers can’t be applied when you check out on an online store. If you view a product in Shop and are redirected to the online store checkout to purchase that product, then Shop offers don’t apply to your purchase.

Shop offer limits

Shop offers are subject to change, and are only available while quantities last or for a limited time. Stores may set limits on their offers, which are indicated on the offer. The following limitations might apply to a Shop offer:

  • You might need to meet a minimum order value to be eligible for an offer. If a Shop offer has a minimum order value, then eligibility is based on the order subtotal before shipping fees and taxes are applied. If your cart doesn’t meet the minimum order value, then you won’t be eligible to use that Shop offer.
  • There might be a limit on how much Shop Cash can be earned for an offer.

Shop offers and refunds

When you receive a refund for an order that included a Shop offer:

  • You receive a refund that matches the discounted price you paid for the items, whether you return some or all of your order.
  • The Shop offer amount (the discount you received at checkout) isn’t returned as Shop Cash or in any other form.
  • If you earned Shop Cash as part of the offer, then refer to Shop Cash refunds for more information.

Understanding tariffs and their impact on your purchases

Tariffs are taxes that might be imposed by the government on imported goods. This article explains tariffs and how they might affect your online shopping experience. For more information about tariffs and their implications, you can visit official government websites, or consult with trade experts.

On this page

  • Impact of tariffs on purchases
  • Identifying products impacted by tariffs
  • Exemptions from tariffs
  • Supporting local businesses

Impact of tariffs on purchases

Tariffs might impact your Shop purchases in the following ways:

  • Increased prices: Due to tariffs, stores might raise prices on imported goods to cover the additional costs. This means you might notice higher prices for certain products.
  • Shipping adjustments: Some stores might adjust their shipping fees or policies to account for tariffs. This might affect the shipping process or total cost of your order.

Identifying products impacted by tariffs

If you purchase items that are subject to tariffs, then the additional costs might be reflected at checkout, or as duties charges from the carrier upon delivery. Shop stores are encouraged to communicate these costs clearly to help you understand the total price.

If you have a specific question about tariffs, then contact the store that you want to purchase from.

Exemptions from tariffs

Certain goods may be exempt from tariffs, but this varies by product category. If you have questions about specific items that might be exempt from tariffs, then contact the store that you want to purchase from for more information.

Supporting local businesses

Purchasing from local stores or those within your own country may help you avoid additional tariff costs. However, products imported by these stores from another country may still be subject to tariffs.

When you search for products in the Shop app, you can use the Sells from filter to browse products and stores in your country. The Sells from filter uses your shipping address to find and feature brands near you.

If you have questions about tariffs on a store or product, including those you find using the Sells from filter in Shop, then you should contact the store that you want to purchase from for more information.
