PiccyBot help

This guide holds PiccyBot’s own words about using it, gathered from piccybot.com on 23 November 2026. PiccyBot describes photos and videos aloud on the iPhone and on Android, answers questions about them, and works through WhatsApp, including hands-free with Meta’s Ray-Ban glasses. It covers how the app works, its features, setting it up on WhatsApp and the glasses, and what the free and Pro plans include.

This is a short guide, because PiccyBot publishes its help on a single page. That page also lists common questions, but it shows each answer only when its question is opened, so the answers did not come with the page and the questions are left out rather than given without answers. Also left out: the welcome, what users say, the video showcase, honors, donations and the technology partner, which are not help. The categories are PiccyBot’s own headings.

Use with Meta Ray-Bans

Setup

  • Download PiccyBot (iOS / Android)
  • Register for WhatsApp service
  • Open MetaView → Settings → Communication
  • Select WhatsApp and connect

For Photos

“Hey Meta, take a photo and send it to PiccyBot.”

“Hey Meta, take a photo.” → After the click sound, say: “Send the latest photo to PiccyBot.”

For Videos

“Hey Meta, take a video.” → Say “Stop” to finish recording.

After stopping, say: “Send the latest video to PiccyBot.”

(Videos are limited to 15 seconds.)

What Happens Next?

  • Your image/video is sent to PiccyBot on WhatsApp.
  • Your glasses notify: “Voice message from PiccyBot.”
  • Say: “Play the voice message.” to hear the description.

Use PiccyBot on your Meta Raybans via Whatsapp

Follow these steps to connect your Meta Ray-Bans with PiccyBot:

  • Download PiccyBot: iOS or Android
  • (Optional) Subscribe for enhanced features
  • Register for WhatsApp service: piccybot.com/register
  • Save the contact “PiccyBot” on your WhatsApp

Connect Meta Ray-Bans to PiccyBot:

  • Open the MetaView app → Go to Settings → Communication
  • Select WhatsApp and connect

Hands-Free Commands for PiccyBot:

For Photos:
  • “Hey Meta, take a photo and send it to PiccyBot.”
  • “Hey Meta, take a photo.” → After the click sound, say: “Send the latest photo to PiccyBot.”
For Videos:
  • “Hey Meta, take a video.” → Say “Stop” to finish recording
  • After stopping, say: “Send the latest video to PiccyBot.”
  • (Videos are limited to 15 seconds.)

What Happens Next?

  • Your image/video is sent to PiccyBot on WhatsApp.
  • Your glasses will notify: “Voice message from PiccyBot.”
  • Say: “Play the voice message.” to hear the description.

How PiccyBot Works

Use Piccybot to describe photos and videos. Just follow these simple steps to get started.

1

Open the App

Get Piccybot from the App Store or Google Play Store and install it on your device.

2

Point Your Camera or Upload Image/Video

Open the app and point your camera at any image, object, or scene you want described.

3

Listen to Description

Piccybot will analyze what’s in the frame and provide a detailed audio description.

4

Ask Questions

If you need more details, ask follow-up questions about specific parts of the image.
