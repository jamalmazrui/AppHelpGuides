About the Smithsonian Digital Edition

About the Smithsonian Digital Edition

Does Smithsonian magazine have a digital edition?

Yes! You can order a stand-alone digital subscription or a print/digital combination subscription here.

I currently have a print subscription. Can I add digital?

Yes! If you have a print subscription and would like to add digital access, please call customer service at 1-800-766-2149. (Monday-Friday 7am-7pm).

Can I get the Smithsonian Digital Edition without having a print subscription?

Yes, you can buy the Smithsonian Digital Edition without receiving the print edition here.

What is included in the Smithsonian Digital Edition?

The Smithsonian Digital Edition includes all the editorial content and photos from the current print edition of Smithsonian magazine. It also comes with access to our full searchable archive of past issues of Smithsonian magazine.

Will the Digital Editions of Smithsonian magazine display correctly on my mobile device?

Our current Digital Edition is designed for desktop and tablet devices. We’ll be adding formatting for comfortable reading on phones soon.

Are prior issues included in my subscription?

Yes! The Digital Edition comes with access to our full searchable archive of past issues of Smithsonian magazine.

Do digital editions expire?

Yes. You will have access to Digital Editions of past and current issues of Smithsonian magazine as long as your subscription is active.

Are Digital Editions of Smithsonian magazine available on other platforms?

Yes. Smithsonian magazine is available in digital format on several other services (e.g. Zinio, App Store, etc.)

These services have been authorized to sell Smithsonian magazine, but they are independent. For any subscription issues on those services, please contact the platform directly for support.

For additional help, please contact us here or call 1-800-766-2149.

Billing/Payment

Billing/Payment

If you would like to pay your bill in full using your credit card, please go to our secure payment page. If you are having trouble submitting a payment online, please contact us here. Or call 1-800-766-2149 for help.

Payments may also be mailed to:

Smithsonian Customer Service

P.O. Box 8504

Big Sandy, TX 75755

You may also call 1-800-766-2149 if you prefer to pay over the phone.

If you would like to check your payment status, please click here.

If you have already paid your bill, but are still receiving invoices, please keep in mind that it can take up to two weeks for us to receive and process your payment. If it has been less than two weeks since you sent your payment, please disregard any invoices that you receive, as we may be unable to intercept them in the mail. If it has been more than two weeks since you remitted payment, please contact us here.

If you ordered through the subscription agent Synapse, please check with them to ensure that your order was received and processed by calling (877) 755-8550 (9am-10pm EST).

Cancellation

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription online by returning to the main menu and using the “Cancel your Subscription” button.

If you have canceled your subscription, but are still receiving bills, please note that it takes a few days for a cancellation to become effective. Therefore, if you receive any invoices after you have canceled, please ignore them. If you ordered through the subscription agent Synapse, you will need to cancel your subscription directly with them by calling (877) 755-8550 (9am-10pm EST). Or you can go to their customer service website, magcustomerservice.com. Login to your account, click on “My Subscriptions,” select your order and click the “Cancel” link.

If you require further assistance, please contact us here.

Cannot accept online payment

Cannot accept online payment

We may not be able to accept your online payment because we are unable to locate your account information. If we are unable to access your account, we cannot process your payment.

Please contact us here if you require further assistance. Or call 1-800-766-2149.

Cannot accept online renewal

Cannot accept online renewal

There are two reasons we may not be able to accept your online renewal:

  1. You are part of the Membership Automatic Renewal Feature. This is a hassle-free subscription program in which we automatically continue your service without sending you renewal notices. Once you are in this program, there is no need for you to transmit renewal instructions either online or through the mail. Of course, you are always in control of your subscription and may cancel at any time

  2. You have not paid for your current subscription. We are unable to extend your subscription contract until we have received payment for your current service period. You may have an unpaid renewals order open. To check your payment status, please click here. You may also pay your bill online by going to our secure payment page.

If you require further assistance, please contact us here.

Unable to process request

Unable to process request

If we are unable to process your request, it may be because we were unable to locate your account information.

If your account was successfully located, then please make sure you have completed all fields on the transaction form. If required fields are left blank, an error message will be displayed. Simply fill in the necessary fields and resubmit your request.

If you continue to receive error messages, then please contact us at 1-800-766-2149 so that we may better assist you. We apologize for this inconvenience.
