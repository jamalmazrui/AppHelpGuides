Auto-Renewals

About Auto-Renewals

Your listing will automatically renew unless Auto-Renew is disabled. We’ll send a courtesy reminder email 35 days before your renewal date. The exact timing may vary based on your Auto-Renew status. If you wish to avoid automatic renewal of your subscription, it’s best to disable auto-renewal in your Manage Subscription settings before the renewal date.

  • Auto-Renew Enabled: Your card on file will be charged, and your listing will automatically be renewed for another year on your renewal date.
  • Auto-Renew Disabled: Your card on file will not be charged, your subscription will expire, and your listing will no longer be visible to travelers.

How Your Card Is Charged at Renewal

  • If auto-renew is on, we’ll still try to process your renewal using the payment authorization connected to your subscription. In some cases, this authorization can stay active even if the card showing in your account looks expired or isn’t listed anymore.

Enabling/Disabling Your Account’s Auto-Renew

You can turn Auto Renew on or off for your Furnished Finder property or unit at any time. If you turn Auto Renew off, your subscription will not renew automatically. Your listing information will stay saved in your Furnished Finder account after the subscription expires. When you are ready to list your property again, you can reactivate it without starting over.

  • Log in to your Dashboard.
    • If you’re logged in to the app or mobile browser, tap the hamburger menu (☰) in the top right corner.
  • Go to the Account section, and click Manage Subscription.
  • Under your Active Subscriptions, choose the property or unit.
    • If you have multiple units within your property, click the + to expand and manage auto renew for each unit.
  • Click the Auto Renew toggle to the right of the property or unit to turn auto-renew on or off.

Baselane

Accepted Payment Methods with Baselane (Baselane)

Tenants pay rent through a secure payment page hosted by Baselane using:

  • ACH (Bank Transfer)
    • Free when rent is deposited into a Baselane Checking account
    • $2 fee per invoice when rent is deposited into an external bank account
  • Credit Card: A 3.49% processing fee is charged to the tenant.
  • PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App: Available when using a Baselane Banking account to send, receive, or transfer funds.
  • Zelle is not supported at this time.

Baselane’s Payment Processing Times and Payouts

Processing times depend on the payment method and the tenant’s bank, and most payments are completed within 5 business days or less.

When you will receive your funds depends on your Baselane plan.

  • Baselane Smart users: Funds arrive in 2 business days.
    • All Furnished Finder customers get a free 1-year subscription to Baselane Smart, a $240 value.
  • Standard users: Funds arrive in 5 business days For questions about payment timing, please contact Baselane Support.

How to Reach Baselane for Your Online Rent Payments Account

Baselane manages your account for online rent payments. Baselane cannot assist with Furnished Finder account questions, and Furnished Finder does not have access to your Baselane account details or payment information.

You can contact Baselane directly by:

  • Phone: +1 (888) 586-1618, Monday through Friday, 8 AM - 5 PM CT
  • Email: support@baselane.com
  • Chat: Log in to your Baselane account; Monday through Friday, 8 AM - 6 PM CT, Saturday and Sunday, 8 AM - 4 PM CT
  • Baselane’s Help Center Furnished Finder customers receive priority support from Baselane. Resolution times may vary depending on the nature of the issue.

Payment Issues & Bank Statements with Baselane

If a payment fails or is delayed, both the landlord and the tenant will receive an email notification. Baselane reviews and investigates the payment to resolve the issue. If you need assistance with your online rent payments, contact Baselane Support.

Payments will show as Baselane | [Alphanumeric Invoice #] on your bank statement. Tenant names are not included due to bank description limits.

Setting Up Rent and Security Deposits with Baselane

Baselane gives you tools to manage both recurring rent and security deposits in one place.

If you choose to prorate rent, you can do so in Step 3 of the rent collection setup flow. Proration is not calculated automatically; you must enter the prorated amount and due date manually.

Security Deposits

Landlords manage security deposits directly within Baselane. They provide you with tools to:

  • Collect deposits
  • Hold a portion
  • Return funds
  • Issue refunds If you need help using these tools, please contact Baselane Support.

Signing up for Online Rent Payments with Baselane

  • Log in to your Furnished Finder Dashboard.
  • Select Landlord Tools and navigate to the Online Rent Payments section.
  • Create or connect your Baselane account: You will be guided through setting up your secure payment account and linking your bank details directly within Baselane.
  • Enter rent details: Add rent, pet fees, cleaning charges, and any additional payments.
  • Invite tenants: Enter the tenant’s information to send them an invitation to begin making payments.
  • Verify your information: Complete identity verification through Baselane’s secure onboarding process
  • Get paid fast: Receive 2-day rent deposits directly into your account and easily view paid, upcoming, and late payments.

How Should I Price My Property?

A well-priced property attracts the right tenants faster and maximizes your return. Price too high, and your listing may sit empty. Price too low, and you leave money on the table. This guide outlines how to price your rental property and key factors to consider.

A good place to start is the Market Insights page. On the nationwide view, you can see the general price ranges landlords are listing for across the country. When you search by city, you’ll also see the budget ranges travelers are actively searching for, which helps you understand what renters are willing to pay in your specific market.

Please note: A valid email address is required to access our city-level Market Insights.

You can also visit our pricing landing page and blog post to help you determine your monthly rental pricing!

Research the Market

Start by reviewing similar properties in your area. Look for “comps,” properties with similar:

  • Location (same neighborhood or nearby)
  • Size (square footage, number of bedrooms/bathrooms)
  • Amenities (parking, in-unit laundry, pet policies) How to do it: Visit Furnished Finder’s map search, enter your city or zip code, and filter for properties that match your rental’s characteristics. This will help you establish a market baseline for setting a competitive price.

Adjust for Season and Demand

Prices can fluctuate with local seasonality and events. For example,

  • In ski towns, winter is high season.
  • In beach towns, summer rates spike.
  • Major events (festivals, conventions, sports tournaments) can increase temporary demand. Note: Rental rates are set at a monthly level. You won’t be able to adjust pricing month by month, but you can update your monthly rent anytime your rates change.

Know Your Costs

Calculate all property expenses (mortgage, taxes, insurance, maintenance, and utilities) to ensure your rent covers costs and still provides a profit. You can charge a bit more if your rent also includes internet and trash. Estimate the average cost of these and decide if you want to include them in the rent or charge a flat monthly fee.

Set a Nightly Rate for Planning

Even for 30+ day stays, a nightly rate helps you plan and estimate value.

How to calculate:

  • Determine your desired monthly income.
  • Divide by 30 to find a base nightly rate.
  • Multiply by your expected occupancy rate (e.g., 80% = 24 nights/month). If your monthly target is $2,400, your base nightly rate is $80. To adjust for lower seasonal occupancy, you may want to raise the nightly rate or lower it in off-seasons to stay competitive.

Is My Property Address Displayed on My Listing?

No. Your property’s full street address is not publicly displayed on your Furnished Finder listing.

To help protect your privacy and security, Furnished Finder only displays your city, state, and ZIP code to potential tenants browsing the website.

If you choose to share your address with a traveler, it’s entirely up to you when and how you do so.

Fair Housing & Anti‑Discrimination Education

Fair & Consistent Screening Practices

Landlords may screen travelers to protect their property and other occupants. Screening must be fair, consistent, and not based on protected characteristics, such as, race, color, national origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, religion, disability, or family status.

For more information, you can review our Fair Housing & Gender Discrimination Policy.

To keep your screening process fair,

  • Use neutral, business-related criteria, like income and employment, rental history, and credit or background checks, where allowed
  • Apply the same criteria to every applicant.
  • Clearly explain your requirements.

Steps for Consistent Screening

  • Write down your criteria
  • Define income requirements, required documents, how you use references, and whether you use credit or background checks.
  • Use the same process for everyone
  • Ask the same questions, request the same documents, and follow the same review steps.
  • Keep communication neutral
  • Focus on the property and your neutral criteria, not on who the traveler is.
  • Document your decisions briefly
  • For example: “Application declined because income documentation did not meet stated minimum,” rather than vague reasons like “not a good fit.”

Acceptable Screening Criteria

  • Income and employment

Guidance for Booking With a Service Animal or Emotional Support Animal

  • Use filters to narrow your search: Select listings that indicate pets are allowed, as these landlords are already accustomed to hosting animals within their property.

Resolving Login Issues

If you’re having trouble logging into your Furnished Finder account, most login problems have straightforward solutions. This article outlines possible causes and the steps you can take to get back into your account.

Possible Reasons:

  • Incorrect email, username, or password: This is the most common reason customers can’t log in.
  • Unverified phone and email address: Make sure you verify your phone and email.
  • Expired verification code or magic link: This happens if you tried to log in using a one-time 6-digit password or one-time link and it expired before you entered it.
  • Locked account: Your account was temporarily locked after too many failed login attempts.
  • Autofill issues: Your browser or password manager may be filling in outdated credentials.
  • Website-related problems: There may be a temporary issue with the Furnished Finder website.

What to Try:

  • Double-check your email and password: Carefully review what you’ve typed - watch for typos, extra spaces, or incorrect capitalization. Ensure that you’re entering the email address associated with your Furnished Finder account. If you’re entering a username, try your email instead.
  • If you get an error that your email can’t be found: Try entering your username instead of your email address. The login link will still be sent to the email associated with your account.
  • Manually enter your credentials: Don’t rely on saved login info from your browser or password manager, which could be out of date or incorrect. Type everything yourself.
  • Use the most recent verification code or magic link: If you requested multiple codes, only the latest one will work. Check your email for the newest message and enter that code. If you’re unsure, wait a minute and request a new one.
  • Go to the “Forgot your Username or Password?” page and use the steps below to help create or reset your password as an alternative login method.
  • Refresh or reopen your browser: A quick refresh can resolve minor page errors. You can also close the browser and reopen it before trying again.
  • Make sure you’re on the correct website: Always log in from the Furnished Finder Home Page.
  • Log out and log back in: If you’re already signed in on another device or tab, log out completely and log back in. This can help reset your session and resolve authentication issues.
  • “Verify you’re human” message: just follow the instructions on the screen to continue. This is a normal security check that helps keep the website safe.
  • Try another browser or use incognito/private browsing mode: This helps rule out browser extension or autofill problems.
  • If you’re using an Apple device and Safari isn’t working properly, check Apple’s troubleshooting guide here.
  • Try another internet connection: Switching from Wi-Fi to cellular data (or vice versa) can help resolve network-related login issues.
  • Update or restart your browser: Older browser versions may have trouble loading secure (https) login pages correctly.
  • Restart your computer: A full restart can fix background issues that may interfere with logging in.
  • Clear your browser’s cache and cookies: This helps resolve stored data issues.

Questions to Ask During the Booking Process With a Tenant

How Is the Damage Protection Plan Different From Other Damage Protection Options?

Not all damage protection works the same way. The biggest differences are who provides the coverage, who decides claims, and who controls the rental.

Airbnb AirCover

AirCover is included with Airbnb bookings and funded through Airbnb’s booking fees. Airbnb administers its own damage claims, and the damage protection portion is decided by Airbnb. Airbnb alone determines claim outcomes.

Renter-paid Damage Waivers

Damage waivers are typically purchased by the tenant through a booking platform. Coverage is standardized and designed to support the platform’s booking process rather than your property’s specific needs.

Damage Protection Plan

The Damage Protection Plan is purchased by you, the landlord. You choose the damage and liability coverage that fits your property. It’s powered by Rental Guardian and underwritten by US Fire and AmTrust.

Claims are reviewed and adjudicated by licensed professionals at Sedgwick, not Furnished Finder or a booking platform, giving you an independent claims process.

You also keep control of your lease, tenant screening, and rent collection. As with any protection plan, you’re responsible for enforcing your lease. For example, pet damage is covered for pet-friendly properties, but unauthorized pets are considered a lease violation.

Bottom line: The Damage Protection Plan gives you independent, credentialed coverage that’s designed around your property.

These are tactics seen across online rental marketplaces:

  • Fake or copied listings using stolen photos or property details
  • Requests for payment before verification or without a written agreement
  • Phishing emails or messages that imitate legitimate communication
  • Overpayment schemes, where someone sends too much money and asks for a refund
  • Identity misrepresentation, where someone poses as a landlord or renter Understanding these patterns can help you recognize unusual activity early.

Red Flags to Watch For

Be cautious if someone:

  • Pressures you to act quickly or send money immediately
  • Asks to move communication off the platform right away
  • Refuses to provide basic details or documentation
  • Provides contact information that does not match their profile
  • Requests payment via wire transfer, gift cards, or cryptocurrency
  • Sends unfamiliar or suspicious links If something feels unusual, pause and verify before moving forward.

How To Protect Yourself

For Travelers

Before sending payment:

  • Compare the listing price with similar properties in the area

  • Review all listing details and photos carefully

  • Confirm the contact information matches the listing

  • Do not send money without a written lease agreement

  • Use secure, traceable payment methods During communication:

  • Keep conversations on Furnished Finder when possible

  • Request a video call or virtual tour if you cannot view the property in person

  • Be cautious of inconsistent details or unusual urgency

For Landlords

Before confirming a booking:

  • Review the traveler’s profile and inquiry carefully

  • Confirm the inquiry references your specific property

  • Use Furnished Finder’s Tenant Screening tool

  • Be cautious of offers to send payment before lease terms are finalized During communication:

  • Keep conversations within Furnished Finder’s Messaging platform

  • Do not click on unfamiliar or suspicious links

  • Verify email addresses match profile details

  • Be cautious of overpayments or refund requests For additional guidance, visit our blog for a landlord’s guide to spotting scams and protecting your property.

If You Suspect a Scam

What Is a Resident Score?

A Resident Score is a number included in a Tenant Screening report that helps landlords understand an applicant’s credit history and financial responsibility. Landlords use it as one factor when deciding whether to approve a rental application.

The score ranges from 350 to 850 and is based on information such as:

  • Payment history
  • Credit history
  • Credit utilization (how much available credit is being used)
  • Available credit
  • Recent credit inquiries Unlike a traditional credit score, a Resident Score is designed specifically for rental decisions and places greater emphasis on credit behaviors that are associated with paying rent on time.

What Do Resident Score Ranges Mean?

  • 350-559: Poor
  • 560 to 659: Fair
  • 660 to 724: Good
  • 725 to 759: Very Good
  • 760 to 850: Excellent A Resident Score is one part of the Tenant Screening report that landlords may use when evaluating rental applications. A higher score generally reflects stronger credit habits, but each landlord decides what information to consider when making a rental decision.

  • Open Firefox.
  • Click on the three-line menu in the top-right corner.
  • Select Settings.

  • Scroll down to Cookies and Site Data and click Manage Data.
  • In the search box, type the website name (e.g., furnishedfinder.com).
  • Select the site and click Remove Selected.
  • Click Save Changes, then OK.
  • Restart Firefox and try accessing the website again.

Microsoft Edge (Windows)

  • Open Edge.
  • Click the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.

  • Click Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Most Popular Documents

  • Lease Agreement: a legal contract between a landlord and tenant(s) that outlines the terms for renting a property.

  • Lease amendment: a legal document that updates or changes terms in an existing lease without creating a new one.
