Account Management

How do I add or update delivery instructions?

You can add specific delivery instructions — including gate codes, buzzer codes, apartment access details, and drop-off preferences — directly in your account. These instructions are shared with the delivery driver for every order going to that address.

How to add or update delivery instructions

  1. Access Account Settings (the initials at the bottom left side)

  2. Go to Address Info

  3. To update instructions on an existing address, click edit

  4. From here you can select to change:

    • Apt. / Suite / Office
    • Delivery details

  5. On Delivery Options choose any of the predefined default alternatives offer by cookunity:

  • Leave with the Doorman: Perfect if you live in a building with a doorman.

  • Delivery to My Door: Ideal for direct hand-off at your door.

  • Leave at My Door (No Human Contact): For a completely contactless delivery experience.

  1. Finally on Delivery Instructions where you can add more custom information like gate code or other important details. This ensures that our delivery team has all the information they need to get your meals to you safely and efficiently.

  2. If you don’t have any specifics, click the checkbox.

  3. Once you are done, click confirm address.

What to include in your instructions

Be as specific as possible. Examples:

  • “Gate code: 1234. Ring apartment 4B buzzer on arrival.”
  • “Building B entrance — use the side door, not the main lobby.”
  • “Call my cell if you can’t get in: driver has 10 minutes at each stop.”

When do instruction updates take effect?

Updates to delivery instructions apply to future orders placed after the change is saved. They do not apply to orders that are already confirmed or processed.

If your order is already confirmed or charged and you need to update access details for an active delivery, contact our Customer Experience team. We can attempt to relay the information to our delivery partner — but note that we cannot change the delivery address, reschedule, or guarantee specific delivery windows.

My driver didn’t follow my gate code or instructions

Instructions are passed to the driver, but there are situations where they may not be followed or visible at the time of delivery. If your driver didn’t use your access information and your delivery failed or was left in the wrong place, see The driver couldn’t access my building — what happens?

How do I change or update my delivery address?

You can update your delivery address in your account settings at any time — but whether the change applies to upcoming orders depends on your cutoff.

How to update your address (permanent change)

  1. Log in and click your Account icon, (the circle with your initials).
  2. Go to Delivery Info.
  3. Click Add new address or Edit an existing one.
  4. Save and set the new address as your default.

Important: If you have upcoming orders that were placed but not yet charged, you can change the address directly from your Home Page by selecting the date and click update address. Keep in mind that changing your default address will cancel those orders. You’ll need to reselect your meals for those weeks.

I just moved — what should I do?

Update your default delivery address as soon as possible using the steps above. We recommend doing this before your next cutoff so the new address applies to your upcoming order. If your next order has already been confirmed and charged, the address cannot be changed for that delivery — it will go to the address on file at the time of charge.

My address is incorrect on my account

Go to Account → Delivery Info and edit the address directly. If the address is already tied to a confirmed and charged order, contact our Customer Experience team — but note that a full address change for a charged order is not possible as deliveries are already assigned to drivers by zone.

Can I cancel after my order is charged?

Once your order has passed cutoff and has been charged, it cannot be canceled because it has already entered our preparation process. Your delivery will continue as scheduled.

You’ll see the order marked with a tag status Order Confirmed. At this stage, editing options such as changing meals or adding extra meals will no longer be available. Instead, you’ll be able to track your order status until delivery.

I have a pending charge before cutoff cay

You may have noticed a pending amount from CookUnity on your card a few days earlier than usual — sometimes during a week you’ve already received a delivery, or even for a week you’ve skipped. This is not an extra charge. It is a temporary payment hold, and this article explains how it works.

What is a payment hold?

When you confirm an order, your bank reserves the order total on your card to make sure the payment method is valid and the funds are available. The money is set aside, but it has not actually left your account yet. Your bank may display this as “pending,” “authorization,” or “temporary hold.”

The final charge — the one that actually moves the money — only happens at your usual weekly cutoff time: 12:00 PM (local time) 4-6 days before your delivery day depending on your area.

When does the hold appear?

A payment hold may appear a few days before your cutoff. For example, if your cutoff is Saturday at 12:00 PM for a Wednesday delivery the following week, you may see the hold appear as early as the Wednesday or Thursday before your cutoff.

Why did we add this step?

Previously, the charge was only attempted at the exact cutoff time. When a payment failed at that moment — an expired card, an insufficient balance, a bank decline — there wasn’t enough time to fix the issue, and the order was cancelled. Verifying the payment a few days in advance allows us to flag any problems early so your meals still arrive on schedule.

How to tell a hold from a final charge

  • What you see on your statement: Pending / Authorization / Temporary hold; What it means: Funds reserved. Not yet collected. Can be released.
  • What you see on your statement: Posted / Captured / Completed; What it means: Final charge for a confirmed order. Receipt available in Order History.

Common payment hold situations

  1. “I see two amounts from CookUnity this week — am I being charged twice?” Most likely not. One of those amounts is the final charge for the order you are about to receive. The other is the hold for your next order. Once your next order is finalized at its cutoff, only one of these amounts will fully post; the other will be released.
  2. “I skipped this week but I still see a charge.” The amount you are seeing is the hold for an upcoming order, not the week you skipped. If you skip or cancel the upcoming order before its cutoff, the hold is released automatically.
  3. “I cancelled my order but the pending charge is still there.” We released the hold on our side immediately. Depending on your bank, the pending line can take 1-3 business days to disappear from your statement. The funds are not being collected.
  4. “I see multiple attempts on different days.” This usually only happens if you have more than one payment method on file. If the first card cannot complete the hold, our system will automatically try the next card in your wallet to make sure your order is not interrupted. Each attempt is for the same single order — not multiple orders.
  5. “I can’t find a receipt for the pending amount.” Receipts are generated only when the final charge is captured at cutoff. Until then, the amount is a hold, not a completed transaction, so it will not appear in your Order History.
  6. “I edit my order, what happens with the payment hold”. When the order is edited, the original order is cancelled internally. The original hold is voided right away, and a new hold may appear for the updated order total.

I was charged after skipping or pausing my plan

If you were charged for a week you skipped or paused, this is usually because the skip or pause was submitted after your cutoff time for that week.

Once an order passes its cutoff, it is locked and processed — skipping or pausing after that point does not prevent the charge for that delivery.

Why does this happen?

CookUnity processes each order at your weekly cutoff time. Depending on your delivery zone, your cutoff usually happens 4 to 6 days before your delivery day at 12:00 PM local time.

After cutoff, your order enters our preparation cycle. Because every meal is made from scratch, this timeline gives our chefs, marketplace teams, logistics partners, and fulfillment centers the time needed to prepare your order with freshness, quality, and consistency.

Once your order is processed:

  • Our chefs receive the information they need to prepare for upcoming kitchen days.
  • Marketplace readiness begins, including ingredient planning, kitchen schedules, and supply availability coordination.
  • Integrations with operational partners are triggered, including third-party logistics providers.
  • Your meals move through the full production cycle: preparation, cooking, cooling, plating, and sealing.
  • Fulfillment centers prepare to pack and organize each individual order for delivery.

Because this process begins immediately after cutoff, changes submitted after your cutoff time may not apply to the order already being prepared.

This includes:

  • Skipping an order
  • Pausing your plan
  • Updating your delivery day or address
  • Adjusting your plan size

This means: Any skips or pauses submitted after cutoff will apply to future weeks only. (e.g if you skipped on Monday but your cutoff was Sunday night, the charge for that week is still valid.)

I submitted my skip or pause before cutoff — but I was still charged

If you’re confident you acted before the cutoff and were still charged, we want to look into it. We invite you to Contact our Customer Experience team and include:

  • The delivery week and charge amount (e.g., $125 for the week of June 2)
  • The date and time you submitted the skip or pause
  • Whether you received a skip or pause confirmation email
  • A screenshot of the charge, if available
  • Our team will review your account and, if applicable, issue a credit or refund. Resolution times may vary.

Why do I see two charges from CookUnity?

Why do I see two charges from CookUnity?

If you see two CookUnity amounts on your bank statement, you are most likely not being charged twice. One is a temporary payment hold for an upcoming order — the other is the final charge for a current or past order.

Read on to understand what each amount is and when it will resolve.

What is a payment hold?

When you confirm an order, CookUnity places a temporary hold on your card to verify the payment method is valid. Your bank shows this as “Pending,” “Authorization,” or “Temporary hold.”

This is not a completed charge — the money has not left your account.

The actual charge only happens at your cutoff time (12:00 PM, four days before your delivery day). Until then, you may see both the hold and a previous completed charge on your statement at the same time.

“I see two CookUnity amounts this week — am I being charged twice?”

Almost certainly not. Here’s what you’re seeing:

  • Amount 1: The final charge for your current or upcoming delivery (completed at cutoff)
  • Amount 2: A temporary hold for your next week’s order (pending, not yet collected)

Once your next order reaches its cutoff, only one amount will fully post. The hold will either convert to a charge (if the order is confirmed) or be released automatically (if you skip or cancel).

How to tell a hold from a final charge

  • What your bank shows: Pending / Authorization / Temporary hold; What it means: Funds reserved — not yet collected — can be released
  • What your bank shows: Posted / Captured / Completed; What it means: Final charge — receipt available in Order History

If you don’t see a receipt for one of the amounts, it is a hold, not a final charge.

Other common scenarios

  • I skipped this week but still see a charge. The amount is the hold for your next upcoming order, not the week you skipped. If you skip or cancel the upcoming order before cutoff, the hold is released automatically.
  • I cancelled my order but the pending charge is still showing. We released the hold immediately on our end. Depending on your bank, it can take 1–3 business days to disappear from your statement. The funds are not being collected.
  • I see multiple attempts on different days. This happens when you have more than one payment method on file. If the first card can’t complete the hold, our system tries the next card in your Wallet. Each attempt is for the same single order — not multiple orders.
  • I can’t find a receipt for one of the amounts. Receipts are only generated when the final charge is captured at cutoff. A hold won’t appear in your Order History until it becomes a completed charge.

Something still doesn’t add up?

If the explanation above doesn’t match what you’re seeing — for example, you see a hold that doesn’t match any upcoming order, both amounts show as “Posted/Completed,” or a hold remains pending more than 7 business days after you skipped — contact our Customer Experience team and include:

  • Your account email
  • The dates and amounts of both charges
  • Screenshots of your bank statement showing both transactions
  • Whether you received any order confirmation or skip confirmation emails for those weeks

  • Fast Instructions — a quicker option, usually for microwave heating.

Can I use the microwave?
