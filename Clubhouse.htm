Account Settings

Age Verification

To use Clubhouse, you must meet a minimum age requirement, which will depend on the laws in your country. (Generally, that means you need to be at least 13 years of age. In some countries, you’ll need to be older than 13.)

In some cases, to ensure the safety of our users, we may temporarily suspend an account and ask the account owner for more information to help us confirm they’re old enough to use Clubhouse.

If you’ve received an error message requesting additional information regarding your age:

  1. Navigate to our contact form.

  2. Select I want to appeal my account suspension.

  3. Provide as much context as possible so we can help quickly!

Our Trust & Safety team will review your account and request any additional information if required.

Note: It may take up to 3 business days for the team to get back to you after receiving additional information.

You can find out more about age requirements in the Clubhouse Community Guidelines, Terms of Services, and Privacy Policy.

Can I adjust my notification settings?

You can update your notification settings from your user profile!

To update your notifications:

  • Tap your profile pic at the bottom right.
  • Tap on ⚙️settings on your profile.
  • From there, you can select if you would like to pause notifications, or you can access the notification settings to adjust them by:
    • Toggling Chat Notifications.
    • Toggling Direct Notifications
    • Tap on any of the houses you created or are part of, and customize each notification setting.

Remember, Clubhouse is live! Notifications will help you stay engaged with the community, but we encourage you to set your notifications in a way that suits your lifestyle!

Can I use the “Magic Two-Finger Z” gesture with VoiceOver (iOS Users)?

You can use the “Magic Two-Finger Z” gesture in the Clubhouse app to quickly escape, dismiss, or close various windows or actions.

To perform this gesture do a two-finger scrub (move two fingers back and forth three times quickly, making a “z”).

Note: There are some scenarios where the gesture may not apply to prevent any unintentional actions.

You can learn more about Apple’s iOS VoiceOver Gestures by visiting the iPhone User Guide - Learn VoiceOver Gestures on iPhone.

Changing Your Account Email

Adding a verified email address to your account is required if you ever need to change your phone number or are for some other reason unable to log in with the phone number you created your account with.

If you need to update (or add) the email address on your Clubhouse account, you can do that from your Account Settings!

To add or update an email address on your Clubhouse Account:

  1. Tap on your profile at the top of the app.

  2. Tap on the gear icon at the top of your profile.

  3. Tap on your name/user name at the top of the Settings Menu.

  4. Tap on email to open the email settings.

  5. Add your email address and tap verify.

  6. Wait for the verification email to arrive. Be patient! This may take 5-10 minutes to get to you. If it’s longer, make sure to check your spam, and that you’ve entered the correct email address, and are checking the correct inbox.

  7. When you’ve received the email, click the link to verify your email address.

How can I deactivate and delete my account?

We hate to see you leave, but if you decide Clubhouse is no longer for you, you can deactivate and delete your account in a few simple steps.

Once your account has been permanently deleted, it cannot be restored.

To delete your Clubhouse account, follow the steps below.

Note: We cannot delete your account for you. To learn more about data deletion, please visit our Privacy Policy.

  1. Tap your profile pic at the top right.

  2. Tap the ⚙️ settings button.

  3. Tap your name/username.

  4. Tap Deactivate Account at the bottom.

  5. Confirm by tapping I understand. Deactivate Account.

Once you have confirmed, your deletion request has been processed. Your profile will no longer show anywhere on the app.

You will have 30 days to log back into your account to reactivate if you change your mind. When the 30-day period has passed, your account and user data will be permanently deleted.

Logging In and Out of Clubhouse

Logging Into your Clubhouse Account

In order to log in to your Clubhouse account, you’ll need access to the phone number you signed up with, or that you’ve updated your account with. If you need to update your phone number or recover an account if you’ve changed your phone number, see the steps here!

To log in to your Clubhouse Account:

  1. Tap on “Get Started” (iOS) or “Welcome In!” (Android)

  2. Enter your Phone Number

  3. Wait to receive the verification code sent to your phone. This may take a few minutes depending on your connectivity. Having trouble? See the troubleshooting steps here.

  4. Enter the code you received

  5. Proceed to log in to your Clubhouse account!

Logging Out of Your Account

You can log out of your Clubhouse account from the account settings menu!

To Log Out of Clubhouse:

  1. Tap on your profile at the top of the app.

  2. Tap on the gear icon at the top of your profile.

  3. Scroll all the way to the bottom and tap logout.

If you continue to have issues logging in, reach out to our team!

Phone Number Verification

In order to sign up or log in to Clubhouse, you’ll need to verify your phone number. This helps us ensure that everyone on the app is a real person, and keeps our community safe!

How does Clubhouse use my Phone Number?

Clubhouse uses your phone number for safety and security purposes. Your phone number can also be used to sync your contacts, allowing you to find your friends - this is an optional feature.

Verifying your Account

For information about verifying your email address, check out this article.

You’ll need to verify your account when you sign up or log in by receiving a special, one-time code on your phone.

To Sign up/Login and Verify Your Number:

  1. Tap Sign Up or Login

  2. Enter your Phone Number

  3. Wait for the text to be sent to your phone - this may take some time depending on your connectivity.

  4. Enter the one-time code that was sent to your phone

  5. Continue and log in

Issues Receiving Verification Code

Note: We cannot force or manually send you a code. If you’re using VPN, a VOIP, or non-mobile phone number, or if you have a poor/unstable connection, you will have a very hard time receiving a code.

  1. Incorrect or Invalid Phone Number: “Your phone number is incorrect. Please try again.”

Problem: The phone number does not have the correct number of digits for the country code.

Resolution: Check that you entered the phone number correctly. VOIP, and Landline phone numbers are not supported.

  1. Rate Limit/Recently Used: “Our systems have detected high usage of this feature from your account. Please try again later.”

Problem: You’ve tried to request a code too many times in a short period.

Resolution: Wait 20 minutes and try again. The issue will repeat if you continue to request too many times in a short period.

  1. Token Validation: “Login Did Not Pass Token Validation.”

Problem: Your device is unsupported or is running an unsupported operating system.

Resolution: We don’t support Huawei devices, and if you’re running a non-native operating system on your device, you won’t be able to use the app.

Private Profiles

What are Private Profiles?

A Private Profile allows you to limit interactions with other users who you haven’t added as a friend.

In your profile, your friends list, and your Houses will only be visible to your friends.

What about my interactions in others’ Chats?

  • If you create a House chat, other House members will be able to see and respond to it.
  • Only users you’ve added as a friend, who have also added you back, can send you VMs.

How do I add a friend with a Private Profile?

When you add friends who have private profiles, they’ll receive a friend request. Once they accept, you’ll be connected so you can see their activity.

How do I turn on Private Profile?

You can turn on the Private Profile feature from the settings menu.

To make your profile private:

  1. Tap your profile picture at the top right corner of the app.

  2. Tap the settings button.

  3. Tap Your name/username.

  4. Scroll down to the Private Profile section and toggle it on.

  5. Tap Confirm and your profile is now protected!

Can I make my profile public later?

Yes, you have the option to toggle off the Private Profile option at any time from the settings menu.

If you’re logged in, you can update your phone number from the settings menu in your account.

Logging in with a New Phone Number

If you’ve lost access to the phone number you signed up for Clubhouse with, you can recover your account if you’ve added a verified email address.

To Login with a New Phone Number & Verified Email:

  1. Open the Clubhouse app and tap Get started.

  5. Enter the code from the email on the next screen you see on your phone. Tap Next.

  6. You should then see an option to update your phone number.

Friends & Houses

Blocking

What is blocking?

The block feature allows you to moderate who can interact with you on an individual user basis.

How does blocking impact my Clubhouse Experience?

When you block another user on Clubhouse:

  • You won’t be able to add that user as a friend - if they’re already friended, they will be unfriended (but they won’t be notified about that unless you friend them again).
  • The user will still be able to see your profile.
  • You won’t see when blocked people add you as a friend.
  • Blocked users can’t see or join House chats you create or moderate.
  • Other users can’t invite users who are on your blocked list to rooms you’ve created.
  • Blocked users will not be able to see or join any House conversations you create or moderate.
  • You will see a warning before entering a House conversation someone you’ve blocked is already in.
  • If you’re in a conversation, but you didn’t create it, you won’t be alerted to a blocked user also entering that room.
  • Blocked users will not be able to see which users have blocked them.

How do I block another user?

To block a user:

  1. Navigate to the user’s profile (from the room you’re in, from your follower list, or by searching on the Find People to Follow screen).

  2. Tap the “…” at the top right of their profile.

  3. Tap Block.

How can I see who I’ve blocked?

You can see the users you’ve blocked from the Block List in your settings.

How do I unblock someone?

To unblock a user:

  1. Navigate to the user’s profile (from the room you’re in, from your follower list, or by searching on the Find People to Follow screen).

  2. Tap the “…” at the top right of their profile.

  3. Tap Unblock.

How does Blocking work in a House?

House members can’t invite users they have blocked or that they have been blocked by to join a House. House members can invite users who are blocked by other House members.

When a member visits the House, they can see which members they have blocked from the member’s page. Content from blocked users will not be hidden within the House.

Hand Raise for All

All live rooms (House, friend, friend-of-friend, interest-based, and public) have a hand-raise queue that gives moderators the ability to manage who can join the stage

Replays are available for live rooms created in Houses, but they must be turned on during room creation. Here’s what you need to know about replays:

  • You can find your replays in the saved replays section of your settings page or the House settings menu.

  • Turn Off Chat: If chat becomes distracting, moderators can disable it in the room settings.

If chat becomes unruly, you can moderate it by:

  • Turning chat off entirely in the room settings.
  • Deleting individual messages by long-pressing on any message that is inappropriate or not contributing to the conversation.

Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII) & Image Removal

Clubhouse does not allow intimate images to be shared without consent. This page explains how people in the United States can submit a formal removal request under the TAKE IT DOWN Act. If you are outside the United States, you can report NCII content in the app.

What is NCII?

Non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) refers to intimate or sexually explicit images or videos of a real, identifiable person that were shared without that person’s consent. This includes:

  • Real intimate photos or videos shared without the subject’s permission
  • Images taken without the subject’s knowledge (e.g. hidden camera, upskirting)
  • Photos or videos that were originally created with consent, but were shared without consent (for example, images shared in a private relationship)
  • Photos that have been digitally altered, photoshopped, or AI-generated to sexualize an identifiable person
  • Deepfakes: realistic AI-generated images or videos that depict a real person in an intimate or sexual situation

Consenting to the creation of an image does not mean consenting to its distribution. Clubhouse prohibits all of the above.

How to request removal of an intimate image

This process is available to people located in the United States. You do not need to have a Clubhouse account to submit a removal request.

Step 1 — Report in the Clubhouse app

If you have a Clubhouse account, the fastest way to report is directly in the app. You can learn more about how to report incidents here.

How to report depends on the type of content (profile photo, room, chat). Generally, you can tap the three dots or flag icon that appears, and select Report. Choose the Sexual Exploitation option from the menu.

Step 2 — Submit a written removal request via our NCII form

To submit a formal removal request, which is required for us to process a removal request, please complete this form. Your request must include:

  • Your full name and contact information
  • Your electronic signature (by submitting the form, you are providing your signature)
  • A description of the image and where it appears on Clubhouse (e.g., a specific user’s profile, a room name, a chat)
  • A brief statement that you have a good-faith belief the image was shared without your consent

You may also submit on behalf of another person if you are authorized to act as their representative (for example, a parent submitting on behalf of a minor child).

What happens after I report?

We will review your request and act on valid submissions as quickly as possible. You will receive a case number when you submit–keep this, as it lets you track the status of your request and helps with future references to your case. We will notify you when the image has been removed, or explain why if we are unable to act on your request.

We will also make reasonable efforts to identify and remove any identical copies of the reported image elsewhere on Clubhouse.

What if I don’t know exactly where the image is?

Provide us with as much information as you can in your report, including the username of the person who posted the image, the approximate date you saw it, or a description of the content. We’ll do our best to locate it. You are not required to identify every instance of the image.

Reporting outside the United States

Clubhouse’s policy against non-consensual intimate imagery applies globally. If you are outside the United States and an intimate image of you has been shared on Clubhouse without your consent, please report it in the app using the Sexual Exploitation category. We will review and act on reports from all users regardless of location.

Clips: Frequently Asked Questions

What are Clips?

Clips let you grab the best moments from a live room and share them. If something great gets said in a room — a funny line, a sharp take, a moment worth remembering — you can capture just that piece, give it a title, and share it to your profile, the house page, the Clips feed, or straight out to Instagram Stories, TikTok, Snapchat, and more.

Think of it as pulling the highlight out of the full replay so it’s easy to watch and easy to pass along.

Who can make a Clip?

Anyone in a room can make a Clip, as long as:

  • The room is public and the host has replays turned on
    • For more on replays, see here.
  • The host has Clips turned on for that room
  • You’re not browsing anonymously (you’ll need to switch out of anon mode first)

Right now, making and viewing Clips is available on iPhone, in English, for US rooms. We’re starting here so we can get the experience right before bringing it to more places.

Do hosts have to turn Clips on?

Yes. Only the host can turn Clips on or off, either when they’re setting up the room or while it’s live. Clips also depend on replays being on — if replays are off, Clips can’t be turned on either, since a Clip is pulled from that same recording.

When Clips are on, anyone in the room will see a note letting them know the room is being recorded and that short Clips can be shared from it.

How do I make a Clip?

While you’re in a live room:

  • Tap the Clip icon.
  • You’ll see the last few minutes of the conversation as text. Tap to select the part you want — you can adjust the start and end points until it’s just right.
  • Preview the audio to make sure it sounds good.
  • Add a title (or let us suggest one for you based on what was said).
  • Tap Share.

How long can a Clip be?

A Clip needs to be at least 5 seconds, and can run up to 55 seconds. As you’re selecting your moment, you’ll see how much room you have left so you don’t go over.

Where do Clips show up?

Once you share a Clip, it can appear in a few places:

  • The Clips feed, where people can discover new Clips
  • Your profile, if you were a speaker in it
  • The house page, if the room belonged to a house
  • Wherever you choose to share it outside Clubhouse (Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and more)

Does everyone in the Clip get notified?

Yes. If you’re a speaker in a Clip that gets shared, you’ll get a notification letting you know — and you can tap it to go watch. If the room belongs to a house, the house admins get a heads-up too. You won’t be notified for every single Clip made from a busy room, just enough to keep you in the loop without overwhelming you.

Yep. If you’re featured in a Clip, you’ll have the option to send a quick thanks to whoever made it. It’s a nice way to show appreciation when someone captures a moment you’re proud of.

Before your Clip goes live, we run a quick check to make sure the content is appropriate. Most Clips pass right away and post normally. On the rare occasion something doesn’t pass, it just won’t be shared — no big scene, it simply won’t post.

Can I download a Clip or share it outside Clubhouse?

Yes. You can download a Clip that you have made to your phone, or share it directly to apps like Instagram and TikTok. It’ll take a few seconds to prepare the video, and you’ll get a heads-up once it’s ready.

Room creators decide whether to turn on Replays and Clips in public rooms. If you don’t want to be recorded, you should not participate in public rooms where Replays or Clips are turned on.

Again, if you don’t want to be recorded, you should not participate in rooms where Replays or Clips are turned on. If you don’t want to be identified as a speaker in a Clip, you can adjust your settings. If you choose to hide your user info for Clips:

Clubhouse Karma — Frequently Asked Questions

What is Karma on Clubhouse?

Karma helps recognize the top contributors on Clubhouse. It is a simple reputation signal that reflects how positively and consistently you participate in the community. It reflects both who you are (your profile being set up, people vouching for you) and how you engage on the app right now (such as your recent participation in rooms and Houses).

Higher Karma means you’re contributing value to the community. Thanks for that!

What’s the point of Karma?

Karma helps us maintain a trusted and authentic community on Clubhouse while recognizing community members who participate meaningfully.

Some features, communities, and programs on Clubhouse may have minimum Karma requirements. This helps ensure real people are participating and reduces fraud and trust & safety issues.

How is Karma calculated?

We combine two types of signals:

  1. Profile Karma

A stable score component based on basic setup and identity cues—like having a profile photo, writing a bio, participating in Houses, maintaining a good standing in the community, and generally showing signs of being a real, constructive member of the community. This part does not reduce over time.

  1. Engagement Karma

A dynamic score based on how you participate day-to-day—listening, speaking, moderating, hosting, inviting others, receiving positive reactions, and generally contributing to conversations. This part does reduce over time, so recent activity matters most.

Why do parts of Karma reduce over time? To keep things fresh and fair. If someone was active six months ago but not today, their Karma will reflect that. Your engagement score ensures the Karma score shows recent behavior, not just past history.

We don’t share the exact formula or weights to protect the system from being gamed.

But good, authentic participation always helps.

Where can I find my Karma score?

Your Karma score can be found on your Clubhouse profile. While in your hallway, select your avatar in the top right corner. You’ll then visit your profile page where your Karma score can be found the right to your friends total.

What kinds of things increase my Karma?

You’ll gain Karma from genuine, constructive participation—examples include:

  • Spending real time listening in rooms
  • Speaking for meaningful amounts of time
  • Hosting or moderating conversations that others stay for
  • Receiving positive reactions from people who are engaged in the room
  • Showing up to your scheduled rooms
  • Being consistently active over days and weeks
  • Inviting people who actually join and participate
  • Participating in Houses and communities
  • Writing a thoughtful profile and keeping it up to date
  • Building your friend network
  • Filing legitimate Trust & Safety reports

In general:

If it helps the conversation or helps the community grow in a healthy way, it probably helps your Karma. Shortcuts don’t work. We have checks in place to identify and prevent the gaming of Karma scores.

What kinds of things lower my Karma?

A few categories can reduce Karma:

  • Being removed/kicked from rooms
  • Repeatedly receiving negative reactions from other users
  • Trust & Safety violation notifications against you that are upheld
  • Being blocked by many people
  • Signals that suggest low-effort, harmful, or bot-like behavior

Does Karma reward big rooms more?

Larger rooms naturally generate more interactions, but the system includes safeguards so large audiences don’t create runaway advantages. Quality of engagement matters more than size alone.

Why don’t you show the exact numbers or formula?

Because publishing the specifics would allow people to game the system—leading to inflated, meaningless scores. Instead, we keep the details internal and reward authentic, positive participation.

Why do I see my Karma change even though I didn’t do anything?

Engagement Karma reduces with time. If you take a break from the app, the recent-activity portion of your score will gradually decrease. Your Profile Karma remains stable.

Does being a Clubhouse Plus subscriber help my Karma?

Consistency matters, and long-term Plus subscribers who remain active receive a small daily bonus to reflect sustained investment in the community.

What’s the best way to think about Karma?

Think of it less like a scoreboard and more like a trust signal.

If you’re showing up, contributing, connecting, and being a good community member, your Karma will reflect that—automatically.

Understanding the Currency System

Clubhouse uses a two-currency system to manage rewards:

Coins (Virtual Spending Currency)
  • Purchased by users with real money through in-app purchases via the App Store or Google Play
  • Are used to send Premium Reacts
  • Non-redeemable, have no cash value, cannot be transferred between users
  • Treated as final sale
Gold (Virtual Earning Currency)
  • Earned from Clubhouse based on your platform contributions and engagement
  • Cannot be spent in-app or transferred to other users
  • Represents Clubhouse’s discretionary reward to you for your contributions
  • Is initially earned in a pending state, and is sSubject to hold periods, clearance, and potential freezes or reversals
  • Will be paid out to real cash by Clubhouse on a monthly cycle once payout eligibility requirements are met

Settlement Status and Hold Periods

Gold earnings from Clubhouse don’t become available for payout immediately. They go through a settlement process:

  • Pending: All new Gold earnings start in Pending status (you will see this in your earnings dashboard on your profile page)
  • Hold Period: Gold remains Pending for 14-30 days
  • Cleared: After the hold period, Pending Gold becomes cleared and eligible for payout
  • Other Statuses: Gold can also be Frozen, Reversed, Expired, if suspicious activity is detected, which is at Clubhouse’s sole discretion. Gold can also be Paid.

Why Hold Periods? Hold periods provide time to handle refunds, chargebacks, and fraud detection related to the underlying Coin purchases that triggered platform activity. This protects both the community and Clubhouse.

Gold Expiration

Gold may expire if: 365 days have passed since earned AND account has been inactive for 90+ days. Any activity in the app (including just opening it) will maintain your account as active and reset this clock.

  • Freeze or reverse Gold for any reason
  • Change program terms at any time
