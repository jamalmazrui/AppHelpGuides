Google Tasks help

This guide holds Google’s own help for Google Tasks, gathered from support.google.com on 14 November 2026. It covers tasks and lists in the Tasks app and beside Gmail, Calendar, Chat and the Docs editors. Some of it repeats what the Google Calendar guide says about tasks there.

Google writes the same article once for each device it runs on, so where an article differs by platform the heading says which one it is for: Computer, or iPhone and iPad. Google’s Android editions are left out, as this collection covers Windows, the web, iPhone and iPad, and Alexa. The categories are Google’s own topics: Google no longer puts a trail above each article, so each article is filed under the topic its help centre lists it in.

Get started with Google Tasks

Add Google Tasks to your home screen

To manage your tasks from the home screen without opening the Tasks app, you can add a widget.

Add the Tasks widget to your home screen

Important: Depending on your Android phone or tablet, some of these steps may be different.

  • On your Android phone or tablet, touch and hold the home screen.
  • Tap Widgets.
  • Tap Tasks.
  • Touch and hold a Tasks widget:
    • List: Find, create, and manage tasks in the widget
    • New Task: Adds a shortcut to create a new task in the app.
  • Drag the widget to an empty space on the home screen, then release it.

Resize the Tasks widget

Important: You can only resize the “List” widget.

  • Touch and hold the widget on your home screen, then release it.
    • An outline with dots appears on the sides of the widget.
  • To resize the widget, drag the dots.
  • When you’re done, tap outside the widget.

Move the Tasks widget

  • Touch and hold the widget.
  • Drag the widget to an empty space on the home screen, then release it.

Remove the Tasks widget

  • Touch and hold the widget.
  • Drag the widget up to Remove, then release it.

Add Google Tasks to your home screen (iPhone and iPad)

To manage your tasks from the home screen without opening the Tasks app, you can add a widget.

Add the Tasks widget to your home screen

Important:

  • You can add widgets on an iPhone or iPad running iOS 14 and up.
  • To add the widget, download the Google Tasks app.
  • If you recently installed the Tasks app, you may need to open the app before the widget appears in the list of widgets.
  • On your iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the home screen.
  • At the top left, tap Add .
  • In the list of widgets, tap Tasks.
  • To select the widget size, swipe right or left.
  • Tap Add Widget.
  • At the top right, tap Done.

Animation: How to add the Tasks widget

Add the Tasks widget to Today View

  • On your iPhone or iPad, swipe right on the home screen until you find a list of widgets.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Edit.
  • At the top left, tap Add .
  • In the list of widgets, tap Tasks.
  • To select the widget size, swipe right or left.
  • Tap Add Widget.
  • At the top right, tap Done. Tip: To hide your tasks when the screen is locked, open Settings Face ID & Passcode or Touch ID & Passcode, then turn off Today View and Search.

Move the Tasks widget

  • Touch and hold the widget.
  • Tap Edit Home Screen.
  • Drag the widget to another location on the home screen, then release it.
  • At the top right, tap Done.

Remove the Tasks widget

  • Touch and hold the widget.
  • Tap Remove Widget Remove.

Add or edit a task

When you create a task, you can:

  • Add details.

  • Set a date and time.

  • Add a deadline.

  • Make it repeat on certain days.

  • Add subtasks. Important:

  • Across all your lists, you can create up to 100,000 tasks.

  • For any list, you can create up to 20,000 uncompleted tasks.

Create a task based on a message

  • On your computer, open Google Chat.

    • You can also open Chat from your Gmail account.

  • Open a direct message.

  • Hover over a message.

  • Click More Add to Tasks. Your task appears in the last list you view. Tips:

  • When you add a date to your task, the task also appears in Calendar.

  • If you edit the task in Tasks, the Chat message stays the same.

  • To navigate back to your Chat message from Tasks, below the task, click the chat’s name.

Create a group task
  • In Chat, open a space.
  • At the top, click Tasks Add space task.
  • Enter your task details and include:
    • A title.
    • The date and time.
    • A description.
  • Optional: To assign the task to a space member, click Assign enter the member’s name.
    • When assigned, the assignee receives an email, and the task appears in their personal task list.
  • Click Add. Learn how to create group tasks in Chat.

Create a task from Google Docs

Important: You can only add tasks from Google Docs if you have an eligible Google Workspace plan. Learn more about tasks in Google Docs.

  • On your computer, open Google Docs.
  • Open a document.
  • Enter @task.
  • Press Enter.
  • Enter your task details.
  • Assign the task to yourself or others.
    • When assigned, the assignee receives an email, and the task appears in their personal task list.
  • Click Assign as a task. Tip: To quickly assign tasks from a checklist, to the left of a checklist item, click Add to Tasks Enter the assignee and date.

Change a task

  • From your computer, open Tasks in Calendar.

  • Click the task you want to change.

  • Change the task info. Tips:

  • You can also manage your tasks from the side panel.

  • To change the date and time of the next repeating task in a series, click the date.

  • To add a subtask, next to the task, click Task options Add a subtask.

  • To move a task to a different list, click Task options choose a list. Learn more about lists.

Mark a task as complete

  • From your computer, open Tasks in Calendar.
  • To the left of a task, click Complete .

Delete a task

  • From your computer, open Tasks in Calendar.
  • Point to the task you want to delete.
  • Next to the task name, click Task options Delete.
    • For repeating tasks, click Delete all. Tip: To remove all complete tasks from a list, at the top of the list, click List options Delete all completed tasks.

Learn about task notifications

  • If you add a date and time to your task, you get notifications at the scheduled date and time.
  • Tasks with a date also appear in the Calendar app.
  • For tasks without a time, notifications appear at 9 AM.
  • For tasks with a deadline, you get a notification to complete the task on the day of the deadline at 9 AM in your local time.

Learn about repeating tasks

  • To repeat a task, select the date and time Repeat .
  • Tasks with subtasks can’t repeat.
  • In Calendar, a certain number of upcoming instances of a repeating task will appear on the calendar grid, and as time passes, new ones will be added automatically.

Manage Google Tasks notification settings (iPhone and iPad)

Tip: To get notifications, add a date and time to your task. Learn how to add or edit a task.

Turn on task notifications

Important: You can receive task notifications in the Google Tasks and Google Calendar apps. If both of the apps are installed, only one app notifies you. For example, if you have the Calendar app and the Tasks app installed, only the Tasks app notifies you.

  • On your iPhone or iPad, open the settings app.
  • Tap Notifications.
  • In the app list, tap Tasks.
  • Turn on Allow notifications. Tip: If you don’t have the Tasks app, you can change task notifications for Calendar. Learn how to update your notifications for Calendar.

Fix delayed or missing task notifications

If you set up a Focus on your iPhone or iPad, you may not get notifications. To keep notifications for the Tasks app on, turn on Time Sensitive Notifications:

  • On your iPhone or iPad, open the settings app.
  • Tap Focus.
  • At the top, tap the desired Focus.
  • Under “Allow Notifications,” tap Apps Add apps Tasks Done.
  • Turn on Time Sensitive Notifications.

Use Dark theme in Google Tasks

To check tasks more easily in Google Tasks, you can change the background theme.

Turn on Dark theme for Tasks in Calendar

Important: When you turn on Dark theme in Calendar, it also applies to Tasks in Calendar and in the side panel.

  • On your computer, open Google Calendar.
    • Check if you’re in Tasks in Calendar. If so, at the top, click Switch to Calendar .
  • At the top right, click Settings menu Appearance.
  • In the “Appearance” window, select Dark.
  • To apply the changes, click Done.

Turn on Dark theme for Tasks in Gmail

  • On your computer, open Gmail.

  • At the top right, click Settings .

  • In the “Quick settings” menu, next to “Theme,” click View all.

  • In the theme window, select Dark.

  • At the bottom right, click Save.

  • Refresh your browser. Tip: Certain themes allow you to personalize the background of specific Gmail elements, such as the Tasks side panel, separately from the overall Gmail background. This allows for combinations like a dark background for Tasks and a light background for your inbox. To change the text background:

  • In the theme window, select a theme.

  • In the bottom right of the theme window, click Text background .

    • If you don’t get this option, you can’t change the text background.

  • Select Dark.

  • Refresh your browser.

  • On the right, click Tasks .

    • Your tasks in Gmail should now match the chosen background.

    • Your personal task list
    • Shared spaces, like a chat space or document
    • Google Calendar, if the task has a date and time

  • You receive notifications when tasks are:
    • Created
    • Assigned, reassigned, or unassigned
    • Completed or marked as incomplete
    • Deleted without being completed
  • In Chat, you receive notifications about the tasks in the space.
  • In Google Docs, you receive email notifications, which includes the email address of the assignee. You also get notifications when the date or title changes. Tip: For any task changes you make yourself, you won’t get notifications.

Learn about deleted shared tasks

When a shared task is deleted, it’s removed from your personal task list and the shared space.

  • If the task is in Chat: Any related messages remain in the space until you delete them.
  • If the task is in Google Docs and you:
    • Have edit access:
      • The task no longer shows in the document, but the checklist item and text remain.
    • Don’t have edit access:
      • The task shows as deleted next to the checklist item.
      • Any collaborators with edit access can accept these changes.
      • Tasks continue to show in the list stream until the deletion is accepted. Important: To review deleted tasks, go to “Version history” in the document. Learn how to find what’s changed in a document.

  • In Chat: All tasks are deleted from that space and their assignee’s personal task list.

If a Google Account is deleted:
  • In Chat: Shared tasks assigned to you are unassigned but remain in the space.
  • In Google Docs: Shared tasks assigned to you remain in the document but are assigned to an unknown user.
If you lose access to the space or document:
  • Your tasks remain assigned to you. Changes you make are reflected in the shared space.
    • Tip: In Google Docs, a collaborator with edit access has to accept your changes before they’re applied.

Learn what to expect when your Keep reminders move to Tasks

In general, Keep reminders move to Tasks with the same data and are visible on your Keep note and in Tasks. Here are some key points to remember when your Keep reminders move to Tasks:

  • Location-based reminders in Keep: You can no longer create or get location-based reminders. To find your location reminder data:
    • On your device, open the Tasks app .
    • Select a Keep reminder that was moved to Tasks.
    • Review the description.
  • Limits on tasks: You can have up to 100,000 tasks. If the number of Keep reminders is more than the task limit, the oldest reminders don’t migrate to Tasks.
  • Long reminders: If your Keep reminder is too long, its title is shortened after it migrates to Tasks.
  • Pending tasks: You can find a list of all “Pending tasks” from the last 365 days in the “All-day” section of your calendar for the current day.
  • Repeating tasks: The maximum interval for repeating tasks in Calendar is 1000 days, weeks, months, or years. For example, if you set a task to repeat every 2,000 days, Calendar changes it to every 1,000 days.
    • Reminders that don’t repeat and are older than a year are added to an “Old Google Keep Reminders” list.
  • Tasks beyond the year 3000: Any tasks with dates beyond the year 3000 are adjusted to the year 2900.
  • Work or school accounts managed by an administrator:
    • If you’re an administrator, make sure that the “Tasks service” is turned on for your organization. Learn how to turn on Tasks for your organization.
    • If the “Tasks service” is off, your organization’s reminder data doesn’t migrate to Tasks.
    • Reminders for users with suspended Workspace licenses don’t migrate to Tasks.
    • If your organization allows Google Takeout, you can export your reminders data before it’s deleted.

How Google reviews Marketplace apps

When a developer submits an app to the Marketplace to be publicly available, Google reviews the submission to make sure the app meets our guidelines. The developer must provide information about the app’s features and how it works, in addition to links to help users get started or to contact the developer for support. Google also conducts regular reviews for the most popular apps. For more information, see About app review.

Additionally, apps that use Google APIs to access user data might have to complete a verification process before the app is made available on the Marketplace. Whether verification is needed depends on what type of user data the app accesses and the degree of access that’s needed. For more information, see How do I submit for verification?

How apps are organized & ranked

The Marketplace displays relevant apps based on a user’s interests and needs. The apps are ranked based on:

  • App-experience quality and editorial value: Apps in the Editor’s choice category are curated by Google. For more information, see Editor’s choice.
  • Relevancy: Search results are based on how relevant the app’s name and description are to the search term, the app’s popularity, and its user-experience rating. Popularity and user experience have similar importance.
  • Popularity: Ranking is based on the number of users who installed the app. Apps with the most installations are in the Most popular category.
  • User experience: The number of ratings and average rating are used to order apps in the Top rated category. Google doesn’t verify user reviews and ratings. However, if a review violates our policies, it will be removed. Users can also report abusive reviews.

  • Relevancy: Search results are based on how relevant the app’s name and description are to the search term, the app’s popularity, and its user-experience rating. Popularity and user experience have similar importance.
  • Popularity: Ranking is based on the number of users who installed the app. Apps with the most installations are in the Most popular category.
  • User experience: The number of ratings and average rating are used to order apps in the Top rated category. Google doesn’t verify user reviews and ratings. However, if a review violates our policies, it will be removed. Users can also report abusive reviews.

Issues with multiple Google Accounts

If you log into multiple Google Accounts at the same time, you might have access issues with your Apps Script projects, add-ons, and web apps.

Multi-login, or being logged into multiple Google Accounts at the same time, isn’t supported for Apps Script projects, add-ons, or web apps.

To fix multi-login issues, try one of the solutions below:

  • Log out of all your Google Accounts, then sign in to one that has the Apps Script project, add-on, or web app you want to use.
  • Open an incognito window in Google Chrome, or another private browsing window, and sign in to the Google Account that has the Apps Script project, add-on, or web app you want to use.

You can attach Google Drive files to your tasks in Google Calendar. Learn how to use Google products side by side.

Add a Google Drive file to a task

To attach a file when you create a new task or edit an existing one:

  • On your computer, open Google Calendar.

  • You can:

    • Create a new task.
    • Open an existing task.

  • Click Add a Google Drive attachment.

  • Select a file from your Google Drive.

  • To add the file, click Insert Save. You can also attach a file on an existing task through the side panel:

  • On your computer, open Google Calendar.

  • At the bottom right, click Show side panel .

  • On the right, click Tasks .

  • To the right of the task, click Task options Add attachment.

  • From your Google Drive, select a file.

  • Click Insert.

Open or remove a Google Drive file

  • To open a file:
    • On your computer, open the task.
    • Select the file.
  • To remove a file:
    • Open the task.
    • Click Edit Remove attachment.
      • If you open the task from the right-side Tasks panel, click Remove attachment Edit .
    • Click Save.

  • If you have a work or school account, you can only add or open attachments if your administrator turns on Google Drive for your organization.
