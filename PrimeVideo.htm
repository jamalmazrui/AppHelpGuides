Ads in Prime Video

Cancel Prime Video Ad Free

You can cancel Prime Video Ad Free via the website, or through the Prime Video app.

Cancel Ad Free through Prime Video Settings on the website.

  • Go to Your Account.
  • Select Cancel Ad Free, then confirm.

Cancel Ad Free on the Prime Video app for Fire TV, smart TVs, game consoles, and set-top boxes.

  • Go to Settings and select Prime.
  • Select Cancel Ad Free, then confirm.

Cancel Ad Free on the Prime Video app for Android, iOS, and Fire Tablet

  • Go to Settings and select Prime & Subscriptions.
  • Select Cancel Ad Free, then confirm.

After your billing cycle ends, you’ll start to see limited advertisements while watching Prime movies and TV shows. If you pay for your Ad Free subscription through Apple, any subscription cancellations must be done at least 24 hours before your subscription is due to renew, otherwise you may be charged.

How Can I Provide Feedback on Advertisements?

If you have feedback about advertisements on Prime Video, you can share it with us via our feedback form.

To submit feedback related to an advertisement, please use our feedback form.

Your feedback is important and will help us continue to improve the Prime Video experience.

Sign Up to Go Ad Free

Prime members can sign up for Prime Video Ad Free through the Prime Video website.

Prime Video movies and TV shows include limited advertisements. The Ad Free subscription is a monthly subscription, with customers in India also having an annual Ad Free subscription option. Currently, it isn’t possible to switch between monthly and annual Ad Free subscriptions.

Where available, you can subscribe to Prime Video Ad Free on the Prime Video app for Fire TV, smart TVs, game consoles and set-top boxes.

  1. Go to Settings and select Prime
  2. Select Go Ad Free
  3. Select Start Subscription

Once your subscription has started, profiles linked to your account will no longer see advertisements while streaming on-demand movies and TV shows from Prime. ²

Some titles include promotional trailers that run before a movie or TV show. These trailers can be skipped but can’t be removed with Prime Video Ad Free.

What is Prime Video Ad Free?

With Prime Video Ad Free, you can watch most Prime Video movies and TV shows without advertising interruptions.

Prime Video Ad Free provides advertisement-free streaming through a subscription that you can cancel at any time. Your subscription removes ads from all Prime Video profiles that are on your account.

Note: Prime Video Ad Free is currently only available as a monthly subscription, except in India, where it is also available as an annual subscription.

Some content will continue to include advertising, such as free ad-supported programming, live TV, and sports events. You can identify the free ad-supported content by looking for “watch with ads” or “with ads” labels on the show’s detail page.

Questions & Answers

1. Is my NBA League Pass subscription transferrable between Prime Video and the NBA app?

Yes, if you subscribed to NBA League Pass on Prime Video, you can use that subscription to watch NBA League Pass on the NBA app, and vice versa. To connect your existing NBA League Pass subscription to your Prime Video account, go here 0.

2. How do I link my existing NBA League Pass subscription to Prime Video?

To link your existing NBA League Pass subscription to Prime Video, go here 0 and use your existing NBA ID to log in. Once logged in, follow the on screen prompts to connect your NBA league pass subscription and watch on Prime Video.

3. Why should I link my NBA League Pass and Prime Video accounts?

Customers who link their accounts can watch live games on League Pass in the same app as Prime national broadcasts and, if applicable and subscribed, their local team broadcasts through channels like FanDuel Sports Network. Also, by managing all NBA content in one place, customers will get a consistent premium watch experience for every NBA game on Prime Video.

4. Does linking accounts include WNBA League Pass?

No, linking accounts does not include WNBA League Pass.

5. What NBA account information will be shared with Amazon?

By linking your account, you agree to share your subscription type (League Pass, League Pass Premium, Team Pass) and active status.

6. How do I unlink my Prime Video and NBA accounts?

You can go to your linked accounts page here 0 to manage or remove your linked accounts.

7. What countries is account linking available in?

Account linking is available in every country where NBA League Pass is offered on Prime Video.

8. How do I resolve issues with my NBA account?

For issues with your NBA account, please contact NBA support support.watch.nba.com.

9. Still need help?

Streaming Quality Standards for Live Streaming of Social Interest Events on Prime Video in Italy

Streaming Quality Standards for the Live Streaming of Social Interest Events on Prime Video in Italy

Prime Video is always committed to grant to its customers the best possible experience, including with regard to live streaming of specific events that are considered of “social interest”. To this aim, Prime Video also measures certain Quality of Experience (QoE) metrics when its customers in Italy watch live stream events of social interest made available by Prime Video, such as UEFA Champions League matches.

Quality Standards We Monitor

To meet our goal to always ensure a high live streaming quality to our customers, we monitor the following key Quality of Experience (QoE) metrics:

Event Access Metrics

  • MAE (Malfunctioning in accessing the event)
  • MAE shouldn’t exceed five failed access attempts in a row

Buffering Performance

  • RRIC (Rebuffering ratio determined by connection)
  • RRIC shouldn’t exceed 5% of the total event duration

Resolution Standards

Your minimum resolution (Rmin) requirements are based on your internet connection speed (no more than 10% of the duration of the event should be delivered at values lower than the ones below):

  • For speeds above 3 Mbps:
    • The minimum resolution should be 540p or higher
  • For speeds above 6 Mbps:
    • The minimum resolution should be 720p or higher

Review Your QoE Metrics

You can access your QoE metrics through your Prime Video account. To find them:

  • Go to Prime Video website
  • Select Account & Settings
  • Select on Watch History
  • Find and select your specific social interest live event
  • Open the Stream Quality Metrics dropdown

Your QoE metrics become available in your “Watch History” within 24 hours after the event ends made available by Prime Video. If your Watch History doesn’t show any QoE metrics, or if it shows “Pass” indicators, it means that the QoE metrics for the specific title event of social interest met our target streaming quality standards. If it shows “Fail,” it means that the above QoE metrics haven’t been met.

Note: QoE metrics are linked to the specific event and thus, if you select “delete” on a certain event in the Watch History page, all of the relevant QoE metrics for that event will be deleted as well.

  • Ensure that you’re not using the same account on multiple devices - you can only stream the same title to two devices at a time.

Factory resetting your device removes all account information and downloaded content from all apps, not just Prime Video. Please ensure that you have taken backups (wherever possible) and are able to log in to any accounts or apps that you currently have on your device.

Updating Network Settings on Your Connected Device

If the Prime Video app isn’t able to connect to the internet, you may need to update your DNS settings.

Please check your device’s guide for more detailed steps.

  1. Open the Settings menu on your device, and find a section for Network Settings.
  2. Find the section for DNS configuration. This may appear as Advanced or Manual IP settings.
  3. Change the DNS server settings to one of these options: - Google DNS: 8.8.8.8 (primary) and 8.8.4.4 (secondary); or - Cloudflare DNS: 1.1.1.1 (primary) and 1.0.0.1 (secondary)
  4. Save your settings, then retry accessing the Prime Video app.

For more help in changing DNS settings, you may need to contact the company that made your device.
