Pandora in the Car

Pandora can be controlled directly through the dash of nearly 200 models. If you don’t have one of these models, you can still listen to Pandora in your car by connecting your mobile device via AUX cord or Bluetooth. For more information about manufacturers we’ve partnered with that allow controlling Pandora from the dash, click here﻿. Otherwise, learn how to connect Pandora in your car with the instructions below: #### Getting started in the car 1. Download the Pandora app on your Android or iOS smartphone. 2. Connect your smartphone to the vehicle’s audio system: iOS devices via USB (newer models may allow connection via Bluetooth) 3. Android devices via Bluetooth (Android phones may not be compatible with all audio systems) 4. Open the Pandora app on your phone. 5. Select the Pandora icon from your screen.

Connect with Bluetooth on iOS

  1. Open your phone’s Settings.
  2. Find Bluetooth, and toggle Bluetooth ON.

The device you’re connecting to should then be detected and appear under the Bluetooth setting. #### Connect with Bluetooth on Android Make sure you have Bluetooth for devices checked in the Settings menu of the Pandora application. 1. Go to the Profile tab and tap the Settings gear in the top right. 2. Select Advanced. 3. Under Bluetooth for devices, make sure there is a check next to Launch from car.

Disable Notifications on Android

To disable notifications, turn off the Launch from car setting within the Pandora app. 1. Go to the Profile tab and tap the Settings gear in the top right. 2. Select Advanced. 3. Under Bluetooth for devices uncheck the box next to Launch from car.

Pandora on Apple HomePod

You’ll need to meet the following minimum software requirements in order to enjoy Pandora directly from your Apple HomePod device: - iOS or iPadOS 15.0 - HomePod Software Version 15.0 - Downloaded Apple Home app

Setting up Pandora on Apple HomePod Use Voice Commands on Apple HomePod Explicit Content filtering on HomePod Listening History on HomePod Volume Normalization on HomePod Removing Pandora from HomePod Troubleshooting Pandora and HomePod #### Setting up Pandora on Apple HomePod To set up Pandora on Apple HomePod from a mobile device: - Open the Pandora app on your iOS device. - Go to Profile and tap Settings. - Next select Connect with HomePod. - Then tap Use in Home.

If you have more than one HomePod at home and would like to hear Pandora on another or all of your devices, just say: - “Hey Siri, play this everywhere” or - “Hey Siri, play this in the Family Room”

Use Voice commands on Apple HomePod

  • Request a Podcast:

“Hey Siri, play [podcast] on Pandora”

  • “Hey Siri, play [artist] radio on Pandora.”

  • “Hey Siri, create a [artist] station on Pandora.”

  • “Hey Siri, I like this.”

  • “Hey Siri, I don’t like this”

  • “Hey Siri, skip this song on Pandora.”

  • “Hey Siri, pause/play/resume Pandora.”

  • “Hey Siri, make it louder,” or “Hey Siri, make it quieter.”

  • To play playlists they’ve collected, as well as albums, and songs: “Hey Siri, play Highway Companion by Tom Petty on Pandora.” (You won’t be able to create or edit playlists through Siri, but you can do that from the Pandora app and your changes will carry over.)

  • To add content to their collection: Hey Siri, add [song]/[podcast]to my collection” or “Hey Siri, add this to my collection” while the content is playing. (Adding artists or playlists to your collection is not currently supported.)

Make Pandora your primary music and podcast service and you won’t have to say “on Pandora” after each command. Anyone with access to your HomePod will be able to play content from your account, however, if you enable Recognize My Voice then only you will be able to Thumb up/down or add to your collection. If it is turned off, no one will be able to Thumb or collect music. #### Explicit Content filtering on Apple HomePod In addition to respecting your account level setting in the Pandora app, you can choose to apply explicit content filtering on individual HomePod speakers. 1. Open the Home app on your iOS device and long press the HomePod speaker where you want to apply content filtering. 2. Tap the gear icon. 3. Under Music & Podcasts toggle Allow Explicit Content off.

You can hide your listening history by adjusting the setting from your iOS device: 1. Open the Home app and long press the HomePod speaker where you want to apply content filtering. 2. Tap the gear icon. 3. Under Music & Podcasts, toggle Update Listening History off.

Volume Normalization on Apple HomePod

You can adjust your settings to maintain a consistent volume from your iOS device: 1. Open the Home app and long press the HomePod speaker where you want to apply normalization. 2. Tap the gear icon. 3. Under Music & Podcasts, toggle Sound Check on

Removing Pandora from Apple HomePod

  1. Open the Home app on your iOS or iPadOS device.
  2. Tap the home icon at the top left of the screen.
  3. Next select Home Settings.
  4. Tap your iCloud avatar to view personal settings
  5. Select Pandora and then Remove Pandora from Home

Troubleshooting Pandora and Apple HomePod

This issue can be specific to the network you’re using to access Pandora, so let’s start by rebooting your modem and router.

If you’re still having the same problem, then please try the following steps: - Check to see if there are any updates available for your HomePod and Pandora apps. Update both as necessary. - Restart your HomePod device. - Try removing and re-adding your Pandora account.

Pandora on Apple TV

Install on Apple TV

In order to setup Pandora, you will need to have an Apple TV 4K or Apple TV HD device.

Open the App Store on your Apple TV, search for Pandora and select the Get button.

You will then have the option to sign in with your email or create a new account.

Sign into your account, and your stations should appear on your Apple TV screen. #### Pair with controller Refer to Apple’s Use Bluetooth accessories with your Apple TV article for more info on how to pair your controller. #### Pause/Skip/Thumb Press the play/pause button on the remote or select the album art of the currently playing track. From the Now Playing screen use your remote to select the skip icon. You can also give current tracks a thumb up or down from this screen, and thumb previous tracks by swiping through the album art. #### Create a station Select Search and type the name of an Artist or Track in the text-box. From there, select the name of the Artist or Track you would like to base your station on. You can also view and add our current Popular Stations from the initial search screen. #### Delete stations Go to My Stations. Click and hold the station you want to delete and confirm. #### Edit Thumbs If you’re still within your current listening session, simply tap the thumb again to un-thumb. Or you can: 1. Go to Now Playing. 2. Swipe through the album art of the previous tracks until you locate the track. 3. Select the highlighted Thumb icon.

If this happened in a previous session, you’ll need to visit the station’s details from your mobile device or browser to remove the thumb. #### Station sorting Go to My Stations. In the upper-right corner, click Recent to sort by recently listened or A-Z to sort alphabetically. #### Explicit Content Filtering Explicit Content filtering is on by default for in-home products like Apple TV devices, since they are frequently used in family settings. To adjust the Explicit Content settings within the Pandora app on your Apple TV: 1. Go to Settings > Account Settings > Content Settings. 2. Toggle over to Allow Explicit Content and switch it On or Off. 3. Enter your Pandora account password to confirm changes.

Switch accounts

Go to Settings > Switch Account and sign into your alternate Pandora account. #### Sign out Go to Settings and then Sign Out. #### Troubleshooting on Apple TV If Pandora is not working properly, try the following. Restart the your Apple TV device: - Open your Apple TV Settings. - Select System.  - Select Restart.

If your Apple TV remains unresponsive, try restarting your Apple TV with your remote. If you continue to have problems, uninstall and reinstall the Pandora app. Finally, try a few standard network troubleshooting steps: - Connect directly via an ethernet cable rather than wirelessly (if you have this option on your device). - Reboot your modem and router. - Restart your device.

Pandora on Apple Watch

Setting up Pandora on Apple Watch Streaming Pandora on Apple Watch Bluetooth Pairing with Apple Watch Offline Mode for Apple Watch Troubleshooting Apple Watch Troubleshooting Apple Watch Offline Mode**** #### Setting up Pandora on Apple Watch In order to set up Pandora on your Apple Watch, you will need to have the following: - Apple Watch series 3 or newer with WatchOS 6. - iPhone with iOS 13. - The latest version of Pandora.

Next, make sure your iPhone has been paired with your watch. To download the Pandora app onto your watch from your iOS device: 1. Go to the Watch app on your phone. 2. Scroll down and find Pandora. 3. Install the app. If Pandora appears under “Installed On Apple Watch” list, tap and make sure it is set to “Show App on Apple Watch.”

To download the app directly from your Apple watch: 1. Press the Digital Crown on the side of the watch to get to the Home screen and select the App Store. 2. Swipe to the top of the screen, use Scribble or Dictate to Search for the Pandora app. 3. Tap Get to install. (You may be asked to enter your Apple ID password to proceed.)

Keep in mind that installing the Pandora app directly onto your watch will not automatically install the app to your mobile device. Once the app is installed you can set a shortcut (or “Complication”) to launch the Pandora app from your watch face. To do that: 1. Long-press your watch face. 2. Tap Customize. 3. Swipe right (until you see an option to select icons). 4. Tap the tile you’d like to use. 5. Then use the crown to scroll and select Pandora.

Streaming Pandora on Apple Watch

You can choose whether you prefer to stream Pandora directly from your watch or your mobile device, as long as your Apple Watch is connected to your iPhone. To do that: - Go to the home screen by swiping from left to right. - Scroll down the screen and select Playing from… - Tap Watch or Phone when prompted to select the device.

Note: Apple Watch Series 1 and 2 are not currently supported to steam independently of a mobile device, however, they can still be used to control playback from their mobile device when connected. In addition, cellular or wi-fi connectivity is required to launch and enjoy the Pandora app directly from your watch. #### Bluetooth Pairing with Apple Watch Make sure to pair your headphones with your Apple Watch when using the standalone app. Listeners using headphones connected to their mobile device that move out of range of a tethered connection will need to re-pair their headphones with their Apple Watch to listen to content on the go. If you have AirPods or headphones with Bluetooth 5.0, your device should pair automatically. For help connecting your AirPods to your watch, click here. #### Offline Mode for Apple Watch The Pandora app for Apple Watch supports offline listening for Pandora Plus, Pandora Premium and Premium Family subscribers. You can add content you’ve downloaded for offline listening on your iPhone to your Apple Watch. Please note that you’ll need to keep your offline content downloaded on your iPhone within the Pandora app. - Pandora Plus subscribers will automatically have their three most-listened-to stations along with Thumbprint Radio downloaded (as long as there is enough space available on the Apple Watch). - Pandora Premium subscribers, on the other hand, are only limited by the capacity of their Apple Watch, and can download as many stations, albums, songs or playlists as they like.

To make offline content available on the Pandora app for Apple Watch: 1. Download your offline content to the Pandora app on your iPhone 2. Ensure your iPhone and Apple Watch are paired through Wi-Fi or Bluetooth 3. Allow Pandora to refresh in the background (Go to the Watch app on your iPhone and then General > Background App Refresh) 4. Dock your Apple Watch on its charger to speed up downloading

Tip: When listening offline from your watch, be sure to use thumbs. Your offline content will automatically sync with your iPhone when your watch is charging and any thumbs you’ve provided will be recorded. #### Troubleshooting Apple Watch First, try restarting your Apple Watch. Press and hold the side button to power off, then do the same to power on again. If you are still having trouble, try the following steps to uninstall and reinstall the Pandora app on your watch: 1. Go to the Watch app on your iPhone. 2. Scroll down and find Pandora. 3. Tap Pandora then disable the “Show App on Apple Watch” toggle. 4. Finally, switch the toggle back on to begin reinstalling the app.

Troubleshooting Apple Watch Offline Mode

If your offline content has been removed from the Pandora app on your iPhone, it will be removed from your Apple Watch as well. If that does not appear to be the issue, check to see if you have enough storage on your Apple Watch. You may need to remove some items to create space. - To check how much space is available on your Apple Watch, from your iPhone go to the Watch app then General > About > Available.

If it doesn’t appear to be storage related, reset the download process on your Apple Watch: 1. Power off and restart your watch 2. Ensure you iPhone and Apple Watch are connect via Bluetooth and same Wi-Fi network 3. Allow Pandora to refresh in Background (Watch app on iPhone > General > Background App Refresh) 4. Dock your Apple Watch on its charger

If the issue continues after waiting some time for the download process to begin, try uninstalling then reinstalling the Pandora app using the Watch app on your iPhone. 1. Go to the Watch app on iPhone. 2. Scroll down and find Pandora. 3. Tap Pandora then disable the “Show App on Apple Watch” toggle. 4. Finally, switch the toggle back on to begin reinstalling the app.

If these steps do not help, please contact us with with the following info: - What iPhone model you have - What version your Apple Watch is on (Settings > General > About) - Any additional details about the issue you’re experiencing.

Basic voice controls on Google Assistant

Here are some basic requests you can ask your Google Assistant. Keep in mind that if you have Pandora set as your default music provider, you won’t need to say “On Pandora” - Request a station: “OK Google, play ____ station on Pandora.” - Create a station: “Hey Google, create a ____ station on Pandora.” - Personalize your listening with your Thumbs: “OK Google, Thumb up/down this song.” - Hear your greatest hits: “OK Google, play my Thumbprint Radio on Pandora.” - Skip a song: “Hey Google, skip” or “OK Google, next song.” - Play an album, playlist or specific track (if you have Premium): “Hey Google, play”Truth Hurts.” - Pause: “Hey Google, pause the music.” - Resume play: “OK Google, continue playing.” - Adjust volume: “OK Google, turn it up/down.” - Set the tone with a genre, mood or activity: “OK Google, play Music for Cooking on Pandora” or “OK Google, play a Hip Hop station on Pandora.” - Stop playing Pandora: “Hey Google, stop the music.”

Enable voice controls in Spanish

You can enable Spanish support on your Google Home device from the Google Home app. 1. Open the app and tap the account icon in the bottom right corner of the screen. 2. Under Google Assistant, tap More settings. 3. Select Assistant and then Languages. 4. Tap Add a language, and select Español (Estados Unidos) from the list.

Offline Mode and Downloads

This feature is only available to our current Pandora Plus, Premium, or Premium Family subscribers, and only for iOS or Android mobile devices, and Apple Watches. 

Frequently asked questions: How to: #### How does offline listening differ depending on my subscription level?  Pandora Plus subscribers: Plus subscribers cannot choose which stations they’d like available for offline listening. Instead, Pandora will download up to three of your top stations and your Thumbprint Radio. By default, these stations will be downloaded while your device is connected to Wi-Fi. You can give a thumbs up, thumbs down, skip, or replay while in Offline Mode, just as you would with any other station. Pandora will periodically review this feedback to further tune your stations. Each week, we will revisit the stations we’ve stored for offline listening and swap in stations that are eligible to be cached offline. Available offline stations will always be your most-listened-to stations.

For Premium or Premium Family subscribers: Premium subscribers have the ability to download many songs, albums, stations, or playlists from Pandora’s library for offline listening. Licensing restrictions may apply. #### How much storage do I need to download content? Offline content can use up anywhere from 300 MB-700 MB of device space depending on the type that’s been downloaded. For example, classical music may take up more space than a pop station. Because we know you care about the space on your phone, Pandora will never use the last 500 MB of storage available. When Pandora detects that there is not enough storage on your device for all of your offline stations, we will download as many stations as we possibly can. Keep in mind that storage limits on Apple Watches may differ. #### What happens to my downloads if my subscription ends? Downloaded stations, albums, songs or playlists (depending on your subscription level) will be available  within the Pandora app while your subscription is active. What are skips? How do I skip a song? What are replays? Why can’t I skip or replay this song? #### ****What are skips?**** Skips allow you to move past a song you don’t want to listen to and onto the next song in your station. A skip is also triggered if you Thumb Down a song. However, skipping a song is not the same as giving a Thumb Down. - By skipping a song, you will only move to the next song in the playlist. It will not remove the song from your station altogether. - By giving a Thumb Down to a song, you not only will skip the song, but also prevent that particular song from appearing on that station again. - By choosing “I’m Tired of this Track,” you temporarily retire the song from showing up on that station. It will return to your station after some time. Please keep in mind that this functionality is only available from mobile devices for free listeners and Pandora Plus subscribers. It is available to all subscription levels on our website, however.

****How do I skip a song?****

All Pandora listeners receive skips.
