Activate Hulu after getting a bundle subscription

If you signed up for one of the bundle plans or one of the Hulu + Live TV with Disney+ and ESPN Select plans directly through Hulu, you can sign in to your Disney+ and ESPN accounts using the same MyDisney email and password you used to subscribe.   If you’re new to Hulu and subscribed to a bundle plan through DisneyPlus.com, you’ll need to activate your Hulu account to complete the signup process. An activation link is presented during signup and sent via email, but you can also take action from your Disney+ Account page:   1. Log in to your Disney+ Account page and select Billing Details 2. Select Watch Now next to Hulu 3. Create your new Hulu account and start streaming!

  When you activate your Hulu account, you should use the same login email address and password you used to sign up for a bundle plan. Going forward, you’ll use the same email address and password to easily sign in to services and experiences across The Walt Disney Family of Companies. Temporarily pausing your Hulu subscription is a great option if you know you won’t be using your account for a little while – like if you’re taking a summer vacation or a business trip.* Keep in mind that when you pause your subscription, it takes effect on the first day of your next billing cycle. - How do I pause my Hulu subscription? How do I pause my Hulu subscription? 

To pause your Hulu subscription: 1. Visit your Account page 2. Locate the Your Subscription section 3. Click Pause next to Pause Your Subscription at the bottom of that section 4. Set the duration (up to 12 weeks) 5. Click Submit

Your Hulu subscription will resume automatically after the specified duration and your credit card will be charged for your subscription on a recurring monthly basis. If you choose to cancel while your account is paused, you will not be charged. You can visit your Account page and reactivate your subscription at any time. **This option is not available for annual subscribers, subscribers billed by a third party (excluding Amazon and Roku), subscribers with an account on hold, subscribers using gift cards, or subscribers on a promotion.* ##### How long can I pause my Hulu subscription for? You can pause your Hulu subscription for up to 12 weeks before we automatically attempt to resume your subscription using the payment method you have on file. #### How do I restart my Hulu subscription? To resume your Hulu subscription after pausing: 1. Visit your Account page 2. Locate the Your Subscription section 3. Click Restart Subscription next to Pause Scheduled at the bottom of that section 4. Click Cancel Pause

You will be billed immediately with the payment method on file. Once payment is successfully processed, you’ll immediately be able to stream again. ##### Can I end my pause early? Yes – you can resume your Hulu subscription at any time by following the steps to unpause above.

Deleting my Hulu account

Deleting your Hulu account permanently removes account information that is specific to Hulu (including all profiles, watch lists, privacy settings, and marketing preferences associated with Hulu). This action does not affect other streaming services or your MyDisney account. Deleting your Hulu account removes your access to the service and ability to stream content. IMPORTANT: You must cancel your subscription before requesting deletion of your Hulu account or you may continue to be charged. Once your Hulu account has been deleted, you will not have access to the service and will not be able to cancel your subscription via the website or app. If you are subscribed to a bundle plan, you will also need to cancel your bundle subscription prior to deleting your Hulu account. Deleting your Hulu account will not delete your Hulu or ESPN account. If you are billed through a third-party partner, you will need to cancel your subscription through them. Select a topic from the following list to learn more: - How do I delete my Hulu account? - What does canceling my Hulu subscription do?

There’s no place like Home — which couldn’t be more true for Live TV subscribers. Your Home network is an important factor in determining which local and regional channels you can stream on Hulu. What is a Home network, and why do I need to set one up?

Your Home network is the internet connection that you designate as your “Home” when you subscribe to one of our Live TV plans.* You’ll need to set your Home network within 30 days of signing up to make sure you’re able to stream the correct local channels on your favorite devices. You can set up or change your Home network in just a few simple steps. **Setting a Home network is only required for Live TV plans — Hulu and Hulu Premium subscribers do not need to set one.* #### What kind of internet connection do I need? You’ll need to use a residential, non-mobile internet connection when you set your Home network. Please note that mobile hotspots and shared Wi-Fi networks (like a dorm, office building, etc.) do not meet this criteria, and therefore can’t be used as your Home network. If you have limited or no access to this kind of connection, we recommend a Hulu or Hulu Premium plan instead. #### Can I watch Hulu outside of my Home network? Yes, but there are some important things to keep in mind: - You can watch content in the Hulu streaming library on most supported devices while you’re away from home - Live TV channels and content may vary depending on your physical location - Access to Live TV (including DVR) or Premium Add-ons may be interrupted in the following circumstances: You’re using a mobile device and haven’t checked in at your Home location in the past 30 days - You’re using a living room device outside of your designated Home network

If you’re having trouble accessing Live TV, DVR, or Premium Add-ons, try the following: - Mobile device: Check in next time you’re connected to your Home network. If you are having trouble and need immediate assistance, please contact us. - Living room device: Use one of your available changes* to update your Home location. If you’re not able to update your Home network, contact us so we can take a closer look on our end.

**You can change your Home network up to four times per year.* #### I have two houses. Can I use Hulu in both? Our Live TV plans are intended for single-home use. If you have two houses, or plan to use Hulu in multiple locations, one of our other plans may be a better fit. If accessing live TV in both places is important to you, we’d recommend signing up for a second Live TV account. #### I travel a lot for work. Can I still use Hulu while I’m on a trip? You’ll be able to stream Hulu on your mobile devices while you are traveling within the U.S. — just make sure you check in at Home before you leave. You’ll need to do this once every 30 days to avoid any interruptions in service. While you’re traveling, you’ll be able to watch the same national channels and regional sports networks that you can at home, but the local affiliates you’re offered may change based on your physical location. This lets you keep tabs on your hometown teams while still getting the local news and weather that’s relevant to your trip.

I was charged an overdraft fee

Some banks charge overdraft fees when there are insufficient funds in your account to cover a transaction. This may bring the account to a negative balance. If a recurring Hulu payment occurs on a date when the account doesn’t have sufficient funds, your bank may cover the payment and charge you for it. We only have control over charges made by Hulu, in this case, the monthly total amount for the plan and any add-ons you subscribe to. For this reason, your bank is the only one able to reverse any overdraft fees. For more information about overdraft fees, please contact your bank or financial institution.

I’m getting an error message stating I’m not at Home

The most common cause of this error message is that** you’re using a living room device that is connected to a network other than the one that was set as Home. Since our live TV plans are intended for single-home use, living room devices must be connected to your Home network in order to access Live TV (including DVR) and Premium Add-on content. Other possible causes include: - Connecting to a mobile network - Using a shared Wi-Fi network (dorm, office, etc.) - Switching your Internet Service Provider (ISP)  - Changing your IP address - Connecting to a network that is detected as a mobile network**

You can clear the error message by using one of your available Home changes* to update your Home network. If you connect the device back to your original Home network after updating it, you’ll get the “not at home” error again — follow those same steps to resolve it. If you’re not able to update your Home network, one of the following workarounds may be helpful: - Watch shows and movies from the Hulu streaming library on most supported devices - Try watching Hulu on a mobile device instead - Use Hulu as your TV provider to watch on other network apps and websites - Contact us so we can take a closer look on our end

Troubleshooting the invalid region message or error 16 on iOS

If you are seeing either Error 16 or the “Your account is not valid in this region” message on your iOS mobile device, this likely means that our server flagged your IP address as being routed through an anonymous proxy or VPN. Hulu’s content rights are restricted to the United States. We need to be able to determine which connections are originating domestically, and therefore we cannot support the use of VPNs or proxies. If you are seeing this error and are not using an international IP address, VPN or proxy, there are a few things you can do to troubleshoot: - Check your Wi-Fi proxy settings: Open the Settings app on your iPad or iPhone, tap the Wi-Fi button, and then check the details of the connected Wi-Fi network by tapping the blue circle button at the right end of that row. Scroll to the bottom of the screen, and make sure that the HTTP Proxy option is set to Off. Once that’s done, try playing the video again. - Check your Configuration Profiles: Open Settings, and then tap General. Scroll to the bottom, then tap Profiles. (If you do not see the Profiles button, please move on to the next troubleshooting suggestion.) If you have any configuration profiles listed on this page, tap the profile, then tap Delete Profile to remove. Then, try playing a video on Hulu. - Check if your Wi-Fi network is using a “transparent proxy”: To test, try joining a different Wi-Fi network, or try to use a cellular network (if this applies to your device/data plan) by turning off Wi-Fi in Settings.

Am I eligible for a free trial?

Free trials are available on select services for new and eligible returning subscribers who have not subscribed to Hulu in the past 12 months. #### What Hulu plans offer free trials? Free trials are currently available for the Hulu + Live TV, Disney+, and ESPN plan. #### How long does a free trial last? The length of your free trial period depends on the subscription that you’re signing up for. Trial lengths for our plans and add-ons are subject to change and don’t reflect any special promotional offers. You can easily view the end date for your free trial on your Account page. #### Can I change plans during my free trial? Making changes to your subscription while on a free trial may result in a charge. If you are interested in changing your plan, please follow these steps. #### How do I cancel during my free trial? Select Cancel under the Your Account section and follow the on-screen instructions 3. Once you’re done, you’ll receive a confirmation email from us

Will I see prorated charges?

The free trial length for our plans and select add-ons vary. In some circumstances, if one trial ends before another one, or if a free trial happens to end before your next regular billing date, you will see a prorated charge from us. From there on out, your monthly recurring total will reflect the total charges for the plan and add-ons that you’ve signed up for. #### What happens after my free trial ends? Once your free trial ends, you will automatically be charged the current monthly rate of the plan you selected when you signed up.   If you decide Hulu isn’t right for you, be sure to cancel before your trial ends. If you don’t cancel, you’ll be charged once your free trial ends, and your subscription will renew until you cancel. #### How do I change my plan after my free trial?**** You can easily change your plan at any time by going to your Account page. From there you can explore any of our different plans, add-ons, and additional features.
