Accessing Content

Episodes cut off after just a few seconds

This issue is usually do to a poor internet connection or a Wifi network which prevents or throttles streaming services.

To avoid it, make sure you are on a network that has adequate bandwidth and click the downward facing arrow icon on the bottom right of an episode to fully download it to your device before listening. You can access your downloaded episode under “Library” > “Downloaded Episodes”

I downloaded the app but can’t access +PLUS content

If you only see a message saying “nothing to see here” in the app, then you are not logged in with a valid +PLUS account.

Click the top right icon to bring up the login page. If after doing so you still do not see any content, then verify that your subscription is still active and that your last payment was not declined by your bank.

I am being double-billed

It is not possible to be billed more than once a month using our automated system.

If you see several line items on your credit card statement, please ensure that they are not marked “PENDING”, “AUTH”, or “AUTHORIZATION”. If so, these are not charges and no funds have been taken from your account. Please contact your credit card issuer for more details about what authorizations are and how they work.

If you think you may have two subscriptions with two different emails: please reach out to support@swordandscale.com with the full name on the account so we can help you locate / cancel duplicate accounts.

I got a message saying my billing did not work.

Our system automatically charges you on the same day of the month which you subscribed. It will automatically attempt to charge you up three times within a 72 hour window. If the final charge attempt is rejected, your account will be deactivate. However you can reactivate it at any time by simply logging in, selecting a tier, and entering your new billing information.

I registered on Apple Podcasts or Spotify for ad-free

Apple Podcast and Spotify respectively offer a subscription services called “ ad-free”

This is separate from our +Plus app/website and unfortunately does not transfer over/or qualify for our yearly perks (which are limited to high tier Plus members)

With ad-free on Apple Podcasts you get all the same content at the same time; however, you can only get access to Plus episodes via Apple podcasts or Spotify.

If you’re a subscriber to ad-free on Apple Podcasts and having trouble locating Plus episodes on Apple podcasts, you can search “Sword and Scale Plus” in the search bar

You’ll have to cancel your Plus Lite subscription.

You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period, however, to avoid being double billed: please wait until the billing period is over

to subscribe to PLUS

Managing Your Account

How do I cancel?

You can cancel where you originally signed up:

If you signed up on our website:

You can unsubscribe anytime by logging in to your account, selecting the user icon on the top right, and going to “Settings” > “My Subscription”. Find the tier you’re subscribed to and click the “Unsubscribe” button below it.

If you used PayPal: You can cancel recurring billing in your PayPal subscriptions

If you registered using ApplePay, then unsubscribe by going to your iCloud settings by clicking your name in the settings app, and then selecting “Subscriptions”. Find S&S under the active subscriptions list and click “Cancel Subscription”.

If you registered via Google Pay: You can unsubscribe by logging into your Google Play manager and canceling your subscription there

You will continue to be able to access +PLUS content until your billing period expires.

Mike is the author of this solution article.

How do I change to a different +PLUS Tier?

If you registered on our website: You can change tiers anytime by logging in and going to your account settings.

Simply log in, select the user menu on the top right and go to “Settings” > “My Subscription”.

If you subscribed directly on the app: You can change tiers on the app after logging in on the top right > Settings > My subscription> Change Tiers
