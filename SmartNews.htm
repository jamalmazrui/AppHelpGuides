Account

How can I delete my account?

How to delete my account

All of the following data will be deleted.

  • Channel settings
  • Recommended content based on your interests
  • Followed topics
  • Browsing history
  • All app settings
  • Account information
  • Other data you have provided

If you delete your account, all the history and other information associated with the account will be deleted as well.

Data stored on our server will be deleted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

How to Delete
  1. Delete data stored in the app.
    • SmartNews app version 22.2.30 (iOS) / 23.10.40 (Android) or later:
      Open this link on your smartphone. By following the instructions on that screen, data in the app will be deleted immediately and data stored on our server will be deleted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
    • SmartNews app version prior to 22.2.30 (iOS) / 23.10.40 (Android):
      Please send your inquiry through this form mentioning that you would like to delete your data.
  2. Delete data stored on web browsers.
    • Safari on iOS:
      Referring to this information, please go to the iOS settings app and perform “Remove All Website Data” for Safari.
    • Chrome on Android:
      Referring to this information, please go to the Chrome app settings and clear “Cookies and Site Data” for “All Time.”
    • The other web browsers:
      Please delete the Cookies and Site Data in the app you are using. Note that some apps might use different terms instead of “Cookies” and “Site Data.”
  3. Delete data stored on other devices you have.
    • Please perform the above 1 and 2 on the other devices as well. The data associated with your account will be deleted by the operation on your first device. To delete the data associated with the other devices, the operations are required on the other devices as well.
  4. Stop signing in to SmartNews with your Apple ID (if you used “Sign in with Apple.”)
    • Referring to this information, please stop using “Sign in with Apple.”

How do I log in with my email?

Our email login uses a password-less design to keep your account secure without requiring you to remember a password. This allows you to access your account via a code or link sent directly to your email address. When you enter your email address in the app, we will send a one-time code or a login link directly to your inbox. Simply click that link or enter the code in the app to be logged in automatically.

We recommend using the email login option for the most consistent experience across your devices.

How to Sign In or Create an Account

The process is the same whether you are a new user or returning to an existing account:

  1. Open the SmartNews app on your mobile device.
  2. Tap the Profile icon.
  3. Tap one of the 3rd party icons (Google, Apple, or Facebook) or select Email.
  4. Enter your email address or follow the login credentials for your chosen 3rd party account.

If you chose email login:

  • Check your email inbox for a message from SmartNews.
  • Tap the login link in that email or enter the one-time code provided into the app.
Important Note on Missions

Mission points are specific to each individual account. Please be aware that mission points cannot be transferred between accounts.

How to read shared articles when redirected to the SmartNews download page

If you click on a shared news link and are redirected to the SmartNews download page, don’t worry! You can still access the article without downloading the app.

How to Read the Article:

On the download page, locate the button in the top right corner or at the bottom of the article preview labeled “Read Full Article”.
Tap this button, and you’ll be redirected to the original publisher’s website to view the article on your computer.

Some people may not see this page, and this is expected! Your phone may be able to jump directly to our app if SmartNews is already installed on your phone.

I can’t login to SmartNews

If you are having trouble, please simply try the login process again. This solves the issue most of the time. If the login link is still not working or you are seeing an error, please try the following:

  • Request a new link: Links expire quickly for security. Try fresh login process again.
  • Check your connection: Ensure you have a stable internet connection via Wi-Fi or cellular data.
  • Update the app: Visit the App Store or Google Play Store to ensure you are using the latest version of SmartNews.
  • If you are stuck in a loop where the link just opens the app but does not log you in, try copying the entire URL from the email and pasting it into your mobile browser like Safari or Chrome instead of clicking it.
What if I receive the email on a different device than my phone or tablet?

If you’re currently checking your email on a computer but would like to log in on your mobile device or another device where you’ve received emails, please follow these steps:

  1. Open your email account on the mobile device where the SmartNews app is installed.
  2. If you cannot access that email account on your mobile device, forward the login email to an address you can open on that phone or tablet.
  3. Tap the link or copy the code directly on the target device. Because these links are time-limited for security, you must open the URL quickly on the device where you want to use SmartNews.

Why am I getting an error code when verifying my email login?

If the app displays an error message immediately after you enter your email verification login code, your account has most likely triggered a temporary protective lock.

The Cause: This error typically happens when too many security requests are generated in a short period.

The Fix: Do not repeatedly request new codes, as this will extend the system block. Please wait a full 24 hours before attempting to log in again to let the automated security window reset.

Manage your phone number on SmartNews

How to add or authenticate your phone number

To redeem rewards or secure your account, you may be asked to authenticate your phone number.

  1. Open the SmartNews app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon in the top right corner.
  3. Under Account, tap Manage Phone Number.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to enter your mobile number and enter the verification code sent via SMS.
How to remove your phone number If you need to de-authenticate or remove a number from your account:

Open the SmartNews app and go to the Profile tab.

  1. Tap the Settings (gear) icon in the top right corner.
  2. Select Account.
  3. Tap Manage Phone Number.
  4. Find the phone number section and select De-authenticate.

Please note that you must have a verified phone number linked to your account to redeem gift cards. This authentication step is required to ensure secure delivery of your points and rewards.

Name change on profile

Q: I want to change my name on my profile. What should I do?

You can easily change your username at any time by following these steps:

  1. Open the SmartNews app and go to setting on your right top corner
  2. Navigate from Account > Edit profile
  3. Enter a new Display Name of your choice.
  4. Hit Complete to save your changes.

Your new username will now be updated.

Q: I changed my name, but it’s not showing correctly on my profile. What should I do?
If you’ve recently changed your name and it hasn’t been updated on your profile page, try logging out and logging back into your account. This can help refresh your profile and display the correct name.

Q: What should I do if I still see the old name after logging in again?
If your profile still shows the old name after logging back in, please wait a little while and try again. It may take some time for changes to fully reflect. If the issue persists, feel free to reach out to our support team for further assistance.

Where Did My Profile Tab Go?

The layout of the app header can change depending on your scrolling activity or active feed view. If you cannot see your profile option in its usual spot, please follow these quick instructions to reveal it.

How to Reveal Your Profile Tab

To find your profile details, settings, and account history, use the following action steps:

  • Check the Bottom Navigation Bar: Look for the profile tab in the bottom bar, or drag down on the top channel tab to make the top section appear (with the search bar and Profile icon).

  • The Quick Reveal Drag: If you are currently browsing an individual news channel and the top menu disappears, place your finger near the top of the screen and drag down anywhere on the active channel feed. This action will force the hidden header area to slide down, displaying the profile icon directly next to the search bar.

Still Cannot Find the Profile Icon?

If dragging down on your channel tab fails to reveal the search bar and profile icon, run through these quick display fixes:

  1. Force-Close the App: Fully shut down SmartNews from your mobile device’s active background multitasking menu and reopen it to reload the user interface layout.

  2. Verify App Updates: Go to the App Store or Google Play Store and ensure you are running the most recent version of SmartNews. An outdated application build can occasionally cause structural interface items to misalign.

  3. Verify Your Device Compatibility: Ensure your smartphone or tablet meets our operational baseline software requirements (iOS 17.0 or above / Android 9.0 or above).

Still Need Help?

Please contact our support desk through the contact options in your device store, and provide your unique Device Token using these steps:

  1. Open SmartNews and look for the profile tab in the bottom bar, or drag down on the top channel tab to make the top section appear (with the search bar and Profile icon).

  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon in the top right corner. Scroll all the way to the bottom and long-press (hold down) on the version information. This will allow you to copy a unique code.

  3. Please paste it into your reply.

Why isn’t my phone verification code arriving?

If you requested an SMS code and it hasn’t arrived, you are most likely capped by our daily security limit. To protect your data from spam or brute-force attacks, our system limits verification requests per phone number. Please wait a full 24 hours before trying again to allow the security cap to reset.

Update to Autoplay Video Ads Setting - May 2024

At SmartNews, we’re always working to improve your experience in our app. We want to let you know about a change we’re making to the autoplay video ads setting.

What’s Changing?

Starting with version 24.5.10 (iOS) and 24.5.30 (Android) of the SmartNews app, we’re removing the option to turn off autoplay for video ads. This means that all video ads will now autoplay when they’re visible on your screen, and will pause when you scroll past them.

Why Are We Making This Change?

We’ve decided to make this change for a few key reasons:

  1. Consistency: By having all video ads autoplay when in view, we can provide a more consistent and predictable experience across the app.
  2. Alignment with Industry Standards: Most apps and websites now use autoplay for video ads. By adopting this approach, we’re aligning SmartNews with what users expect based on their experiences in other apps.
  3. Simplified Experience: Removing the autoplay setting simplifies the app and ensures all users have the same experience, making it easier for us to continue improving SmartNews.
What Does This Mean for You?

If you previously had autoplay video ads turned off (set as “Never” or “Only on Wi-Fi”), you’ll now see video ads start to play silently as you scroll to them. The videos will pause when you scroll past them, and you can always tap on a video to unmute it if you’re interested in the content.

We believe this change will create a more consistent experience for all our users. We understand that some users may have preferred to keep autoplay off. However, there are limitations from our third-party video ad partners where SmartNews doesn’t have full control to guarantee it not being autoplayed.

Going Forward

We’re committed to making SmartNews the best it can be, and we believe this update is a step in that direction. We appreciate your understanding as we work to improve the app experience for everyone.

Thank you for being a valued member of the SmartNews community. If you have any questions, please visit our Help Center or contact our support team directly.

Why can I not see local contents?

We currently support local features for zip codes within the United States, excluding Puerto Rico and Northern Mariana Islands.

If you are having trouble accessing zip codes within this region, please try the below troubleshooting:

  • Reset your zip code
  • Clear cache, restart your app

Why is the SmartTake Channel gone from my app?

The SmartTake Channel was discontinued because it wasn’t as widely used as other features. This change allows us to focus on improving the parts of SmartNews that bring the most value to our users.
Thank you for your support - we’re always working to make SmartNews the best experience possible.
