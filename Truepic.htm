Android

Add or Edit a Note in a Photo on an Android Device

Add or edit a text note on a photo in the Truepic Vision app on Android to provide context for the reviewer. Notes are visible to the team reviewing your inspection.

  • Open the photo by tapping the image in the Truepic Vision app.
  • Tap the + Add Note button at the bottom of the screen.
  • Type your note or description in the textbox.
  • Tap Save to apply your note.
  • To edit an existing note, tap the Edit Note button and change your text in the textbox. This option appears only if a note already exists.

App Is Asking Me to Log In, Enter My Email, or Create an Account (Android)

If opening your Truepic Vision Smartlink prompts a login screen or email entry instead of launching the inspection, follow these steps to fix it.

If you open your Truepic Vision smartlink and see any of the following, this is an error — not a normal step in the inspection process:

  • A screen asking you to log in
  • A prompt to enter your email address and/or phone number
  • A request to create or set up an account
  • A screen asking you to verify your email No account, login, password, or email verification is ever required to complete an inspection. The Log In button in the Truepic Vision app is only for Truepic employees and partner company staff. If you accidentally land on the login screen, no verification code will be sent — just go back to your smartlink to continue.

What the normal flow looks like

  • You receive a smartlink (starting with app.truepicvision.com) via text or email
  • Tap the link directly on your phone
  • Your browser opens to a Ready to get started? page with a Copy & Continue button
  • Tap Copy & Continue — the app opens and your inspection begins If you’re being asked to log in or enter account details, something went wrong at step 2 or 3. The steps below will fix it.

Steps to fix it

Tap the original link again
  • Go back to the original text or email and tap the smartlink directly. Do not copy and paste it into a browser.
Check if your browser is in Desktop Mode
  • If you see a QR code instead of the Ready to get started? page, your browser may be in Desktop Mode.
  • In Chrome, tap the icon with three dots in the menu bar.
  • Look for the Desktop site checkbox. If it is checked, uncheck it to switch to the mobile site. If it is already unchecked, your Chrome is correctly using the mobile site.
  • If you’re not using Chrome, other browsers have a similar Desktop Mode setting. Check your browser’s settings, or copy the link into Chrome and follow the steps above.
Switch between Wi-Fi and cellular data
  • Some Wi-Fi routers block the smartlink redirect. Turn off Wi-Fi and use cellular data only (or vice versa), then tap the link again.
Disable VPN or security apps
  • If you have a VPN, content filter, or DNS filtering app, turn it off. Then uninstall the Truepic Vision app, tap the link again, and reinstall from the Play Store when prompted.
Use the most recent link
  • If you’ve received multiple inspection requests, make sure you’re using the newest one — older links may be expired or closed.
Check for app updates
  • Go to the Google Play Store and make sure you have the latest version of the Truepic Vision app installed.

Still not working?

If none of these steps resolve the issue, contact Truepic Support in-app.

Can’t Download App Without Payment Method on Android Device

Google Play may require a payment method to download free apps in some regions. This is a Google requirement — not a Truepic one. The Truepic Vision app is free to download. If you cannot download the app because you are told a payment method is needed, it could be because you have not previously downloaded an app with your Google account.

How to download without a payment method:

  • Open Google Play Store on your Android device.
  • Search for Truepic Vision.
  • Tap Install.
  • When prompted for payment, tap Skip to proceed without adding a payment method. If any cost is shown for downloading the Truepic Vision app, contact Truepic Support before proceeding, as it could indicate your device is compromised.

Delete an Image or Video on an Android Device

Delete a photo or video from your inspection in the Truepic Vision app on Android before submitting.

  • Select the photo or video you want to delete from the Inspection page.
  • Tap the trash icon in the upper-right corner of the screen: Note: You cannot delete photos after the inspection has been submitted.

Why Can’t I Delete a Media File?

Photos, videos, or other media cannot be deleted from the Truepic app while uploading. During the upload process, the system temporarily disables the delete function, so you will not see the trash icon.

Solution

Wait for the upload to complete before attempting to delete media. Once the upload finishes, you will regain access to the delete feature.

General Tips for Handling Stuck Uploads or Pending Media

  • Ensure a stable internet connection to prevent uploads from getting stuck.

  • Avoid closing the app during an upload process.

  • If an upload remains stuck, restart the app or your device before reinitiating the upload. Contact Truepic support for additional assistance with specific upload issues. Note:

  • Some companies do not allow deletions in the app.

  • No images or videos can be deleted after submitting your inspection to the company who requested it, unless they send you a new link to alter your submission.

  • You cannot delete images or videos showing a Pending status. These have not yet been uploaded to Truepic servers. You can either submit without the pending media or retake it and allow it to upload. If retaken, contact support to ensure the updated inspection is submitted correctly.

Enable Location Services and Precise Location on an Android Device

The Truepic Vision app requires Location Services and Precise Location to verify where photos are taken. Here’s how to enable them on Android:

  • Open the Settings app.
  • Tap Location and turn on Use Location.
  • Tap Location Services.
  • Find Location Accuracy and turn it on.
  • Return to the Truepic Vision app and try again — the location error should be resolved.

Fixing Camera/Microphone Permissions for the Truepic Vision Web App - Android

Camera/Microphone Permissions Error: Web App

This information pertains to the Vision Web App only. Links for this web app begin with web.truepicvision.com.

If your link begins with app.truepicvision.com or visionbytruepic.com, or if you have been asked to use the Truepic Vision app, this information will not be applicable.

If you see a “Camera/microphone permission denied” error in the Truepic Vision Web App on Android, follow the steps below. Chrome is the only supported browser for the Vision Web App on Android.

Why does Truepic need these permissions? Camera and microphone access lets Chrome capture and submit photos and videos for your inspection.

Quick fix

  • Open Settings > Apps > Chrome > Permissions and enable both Camera and Microphone.
  • Return to the Truepic Vision Web App, tap the tune icon in the address bar (to the left of the URL), select Permissions, and confirm Camera and Microphone are set to Allow.
  • Refresh the page and tap Allow Access when prompted. If that doesn’t resolve the error, follow the full steps below.

Here’s what the error looks like:

Part 1: Enable permissions in Android Settings

Step 1. Open the Settings app and tap Apps.

Step 2. Tap Chrome.

Step 3. Tap Permissions.

Step 4. Tap Camera. Set it to Allowed or Ask every time.

Step 5. Tap Microphone. Set it to Allowed or Ask every time. Microphone must be enabled for Camera to work.

Part 2: Enable permissions in Chrome site settings

Step 6. Return to the Truepic Vision Web App in Chrome. If you can’t find it, tap the link you received.

Step 7. In the address bar, tap the tune icon — the settings sliders icon to the left of the URL.

Step 8. Tap Permissions.

Step 9. Confirm both Camera and Microphone are set to Allow.

Step 10. Tap back on the page, refresh, and tap Allow Access under Camera/Microphone permissions.

Step 11. When Chrome asks for camera permission, tap Allow.

Still not working?

  • Force-close Chrome: swipe it away from your recent apps, then reopen it.
  • Restart your Android device.
  • Contact support with a screenshot of the error.

Getting Started Guide for Android Devices

Introduction

This guide walks you through setting up and using the Truepic Vision app on an Android device for the first time. Truepic Vision is a digital inspection platform that provides a quick and convenient way to send verified, trustworthy images and videos directly to the requester. The Truepic Vision app is a camera application available exclusively on iOS and Android devices.

If you were instructed to use the Truepic Vision app by one of our clients, wait until you have received either an email or text message (or both) with a link that will take you directly to your inspection request for submission.

Note: If you are inspecting somewhere with poor or no signal, first open your inspection link and complete the full app setup (download the app, grant Camera and Location permissions, and reach the inspection screen) while on a connected network. Then leave the app open on the inspection screen for 20 seconds to sync. You can go offline after that to take photos, but photos won’t fully upload until you return to a connection. If the required inspection location has no signal, you may run into issues.

You received your Smartlink, now what?

To begin, have your smartphone or tablet running stock Android handy so you can tap on the link in the text message or email you received. There is no username or password required to log in. If you received the link through email on a desktop, you can forward it to, or open it on, your smart device.

  • Open the text message or email with the link on your smartphone. Tap on the link.
  • You will be taken to a new screen, reading “Ready to get started?”. Click the Copy & Continue button.
  • A message will open, saying “Unique link copied! Opening your app store…”. A prompt may ask for permission to take you to the Play Store. Click Open in Play Store.
  • In the Play Store, click Install or, if you’ve downloaded the app previously, Open.
  • Once downloaded, open the Truepic Vision app. Your request should open for you, including your name. If it does not, you may see this screen — click the paste button to paste the link from the email or text you received:
  • Once the app recognizes you, it will display your name and the company that requested the inspection. Click the check box to accept the Acceptable Use Policy, End User Agreement, and Privacy Policy, then click Get Started.
  • A screen will show letting you know permissions are needed. Press Enable Permissions at the bottom.
  • The Truepic Vision app requires Camera and Location (including Precise Location) permissions. Press Allow Camera Access and then allow the privileges on the following popup to approve camera permissions.
    • If While using the app is selected from the popup, the permissions will be granted for the app any time you use it.
    • If Only this time is selected from the popup, the permissions will be granted just this time, until the app is closed.
  • Press Allow Location Access and then allow precise location on the popup to approve location (including precise location) permissions.
    • If While using the app is selected from the popup, the permissions will be granted for the app any time you use it.
    • If Only this time is selected from the popup, the permissions will be granted just this time, until the app is closed.
  • Once all permissions are given, you may briefly see a “Preparing your inspection…” page.
  • You will then see the Inspection screen, with the list of items/questions the company has set up for your case. They may be grouped, where you need to click the down arrow to see all of the items in that group.
Using the App

The app will show the list of questions from the partner company who asked you to use Truepic Vision.

If the company sending you the inspection allows you to take a photo or video for that question, you can switch between those modes with the toggle at the top of the screen. Otherwise, you will only have one of those options. Please note that if you take a video, your phone may ask you to approve microphone permissions.

You will also see a plus (+) and minus (-) indicator, which turn green when you line them up by tilting your phone. When these line up green, the phone’s camera is level.

Editing Photos

Change Photo Category

To change the category or question a photo is in, tap the drop-down at the top, and then tap the correct category/question.

Add Note

To add a note or description, tap the + Add Note button and type in the text box.

Edit Note

To edit a note or description, tap Edit Note and change your comment in the text box. You will only see this option if a description already exists.

Deleting Photos

What’s Next?

Follow these steps to ensure successful completion and submission of your inspection in the Truepic Vision app:

  • Review all your answers and ensure that photos or videos meet the requirements before submitting.
  • If you encounter issues with media capturing or submission, restart the app or check your internet connection before proceeding.
  • Once you have answered all the required questions (noted with an asterisk * in the app) and captured all necessary photos or videos, tap the Finish Inspection button. Do not do this until you are completely done — you will not be able to alter or add anything after submitting.
  • The app will automatically send your completed inspection to the company that requested it. They will review your answers and reach out directly with questions or next steps.

Still having trouble?

If you encounter any issues while setting up or using the app, contact the company that sent you the inspection request. They can provide additional guidance specific to your situation.

Why am I seeing “Camera Disabled” in my Android app?

The “Camera Disabled” message in the Truepic Vision app on Android means the app cannot access your camera. This error prevents you from capturing photos or videos within the app.

Quick fixes — try these first

Most “Camera Disabled” errors are caused by one of these three issues:

  • No data connection: Move to an area with strong mobile data coverage. The camera will enable automatically once connected.
  • Camera permission denied: Open Settings → Apps → Truepic Vision → Permissions → Camera and toggle it On.
  • Developer Mode enabled: Open Settings → Developer Options → toggle the switch to Off → restart your device.

Data connection

The camera can be disabled if you have no data connection initially. Move into an area with a strong connection until the “Camera Disabled” alert disappears and the camera shutter is enabled. You can return to the previous location after this point without seeing the error again.

Camera permissions

If the quick fixes above don’t work, verify your app permissions:

  • Open your device’s Settings app.
  • Tap Apps or Applications.
  • Find and tap Truepic Vision.
  • Tap Permissions.
  • Ensure Camera is toggled On.

Developer Options

If your phone is in Developer Options mode, disable it:

  • Open your phone’s Settings app.
  • Tap Developer Options in the settings menu.
  • Tap the slider button at the top right to Off.
  • Restart your phone. Why Developer Options causes this: The Truepic Vision app requires a standard, unmodified Android device to ensure the integrity of its verification system. Developer Options signals that the device may be in a modified state, which prevents the app from enabling the camera.

If you cannot disable Developer Options: The app will not function on this device while Developer Options is enabled. You will need to complete your inspection on a different Android or iOS device that does not have Developer Options turned on. Your inspection link is not tied to a specific phone — you can forward it to any compatible device. If you do not have access to another device, contact the company that sent your inspection request to discuss alternatives.

Initializing Camera

If your app displays “Initializing Camera” for more than a few minutes:

  • Restart your device.
  • Turn off Bluetooth immediately after restart.
  • Ensure no other apps are running in the background.
  • Open Truepic Vision and try again.

Clear app data and reinstall

If the above steps don’t resolve the issue, delete your app data and reinstall:

Note: Uninstalling the app removes all inspection answers you’ve provided so far but can resolve the “Camera Disabled” error.

  • While the app is installed, open Settings → Apps or Applications.
  • Find and tap Truepic Vision.
  • Tap Storage & Cache.
  • Tap Clear storage.
  • Uninstall Truepic Vision.
  • Restart your device.
  • Tap the smartlink from your text or email to reinstall Truepic Vision.

Still seeing “Camera Disabled”?

If the error persists after trying all steps above:

  • Restart your device once more.
  • Reinstall the app if you haven’t already.
  • Contact support: Include a screenshot of the full “Camera Disabled” message, your Android device model, and Android version. We’ll investigate further.

How to Fix Camera and Microphone Permissions in the Truepic Vision Web App on iPhone or iPad

This information pertains to the Vision Web App only. Links for this web app begin with “web.truepicvision.com”.

If your link begins with “app.truepicvision.com” or “visionbytruepic.com”, or if you have been asked to use the Truepic Vision app, this information will not be applicable.

If you click on a photo or video question in the Vision Web App and receive the error “Camera/microphone permission denied”, this is likely because certain camera permissions are not set for the browser on your device. Please note that the only browsers supported when using the Vision Web App are Safari and Chrome. Please choose which browser you are using below in order to receive instructions related to how to fix this issue.

  • Safari - iOS (iPhone/iPad)
  • Chrome - iOS (iPhone/iPad)

Safari - iOS (iPhone/iPad)

  1. The error will likely display like in the screenshot below.

  2. Go to the Settings app on your iOS device, and select “Apps”.

  3. Go to “Safari”.

  4. Scroll to the bottom of this list, and find Camera.

  5. Click on Camera and ensure it is set to either “Ask” or “Allow”.

  6. Go back one screen, and find Microphone. Microphone permissions must not be set to deny, or the Camera permissions will not work.

  7. Click on Microphone and ensure it is set to either “Ask” or “Allow”.

  8. Go back to the Truepic web app in Safari, where you started; if you cannot find the Safari app, please click on the link you received asking you to use the web app again.

  9. Check the Site Settings (square icon with two lines underneath) on the address bar:

  10. Then click the … button at the bottom right of the screen.

  11. Scroll down to the site settings. Camera, Microphone, and Location must all be set to “Ask” or “Allow”.

  12. When complete, press Done, click on the webpage again, and refresh the webpage. Click the camera permissions or photo question again. A popup should appear, asking for permissions. Click Allow.

The camera error should now be successfully resolved, and you can move forward with using the Vision Web App.

If you are still running into issues after trying these steps, please contact us.

Chrome - iOS (iPhone/iPad)

The error should display like in the screenshot below.

  1. Go to the Settings app on your iOS device, and select “Apps”.

  2. Go to “Chrome”.

  3. Locate both Camera and Microphone and ensure they are both enabled (green). If either is not enabled, enable them; Microphone permissions are required for Camera permissions to work.

  4. Go back to the Truepic web app in Chrome, where you started; if you cannot find the Chrome app, please click on the link you received asking you to use the web app again.

  5. Refresh the page, and click on the Allow Access button under Camera/Microphone permissions again.

  6. Chrome should prompt you to allow camera permissions. Tap Allow.

The camera error should now be successfully resolved, and you can move forward with using the Vision Web App.

If you are still running into issues after trying these steps, please contact us.

Am I Using an iOS or Android Device?

This article helps you identify your device type and check whether it meets Truepic Vision’s requirements.

The Truepic Vision apps (both web app and native app) work on iOS and Android smartphones and tablets.

Quick Reference

  • iOS devices → Apple App Store

  • Android devices → Google Play Store

  • Computer/Laptop → Truepic Vision Web App iOS Device Requirements

  • iOS devices are made by Apple. These include iPhones and iPads.

    • The Truepic Vision native app runs on most iPhones or iPads running minimum iOS 16 and above (including iOS 26), though devices running operating systems in beta are not supported.
    • The Truepic Vision web app is compatible with Safari or Chrome on iOS devices. The default browser on iOS devices is Safari, but Google Chrome is available to download from the App Store.
    • Jailbroken or modified iOS devices are not compatible with the Truepic Vision native app or web app. Restore your device to factory settings or use a different compatible device.
    • No Apple computers are compatible with the Truepic Vision web app or native app. Android Device Requirements

  • Android devices can be made by many different manufacturers (Google, Samsung, OnePlus, Huawei, etc).

    • The Truepic Vision native app runs on most Android phones or tablets running stock (original manufacturer) Android 10 and above; the Play Store normally runs hardware checks to verify the device is compatible.
      • Devices using a custom OS, like FireOS for Amazon Fire tablets or GrapheneOS on select Google Pixels, are not compatible with the Truepic Vision native app.
      • Jailbroken, rooted, or modified Android devices are not compatible with the Truepic Vision native app or web app. Restore your device to factory settings or use a different compatible device.
    • The Truepic Vision web app is compatible with the Chrome browser on Android devices.
    • No laptop or desktop computers are compatible with the Truepic Vision web app or native app.

Can I add more photos after I submitted my inspection?

No — once you submit your inspection, you cannot add more photos directly. Contact the organization that sent you the inspection and ask them to send a new request.

Why? Truepic Vision locks submissions marked as finished or past the expiration date set by the sending company in order to preserve the verified integrity of the inspection. To add more information or photos, reach out to the point of contact who sent the request. They can send you a new link, and you can capture additional photos and videos.

Can I forward a Smartlink to someone else?

Yes, you can forward a Smartlink to someone else. The new recipient will be able to complete the inspection using the same link.

Smartlinks are not tied to the initial phone number or email they were sent to. If you receive a Smartlink but want to share it with someone else, forward the text message to that person. They will be able to access the Smartlink.

Smartlinks are only compatible with Apple or Android smartphones and tablets. If you received a link via email on your computer, open it on a mobile device.

If you can’t access the original email on your mobile device or are experiencing issues downloading the Truepic app, follow these troubleshooting steps:

  • Locate the Inspection Email: Find the email containing the Smartlink in your inbox.
  • Forward or Copy the Smartlink: Forward the entire email to a personal email address accessible on a different device, or copy and paste the Smartlink into a new email to yourself.
  • Open the Link on a Supported Device: Access the forwarded or self-sent email on a device compatible with the Truepic app (Apple or Android). Paste the link into the device’s mobile browser.
  • Follow Redirection: The Smartlink will guide you to the appropriate app store to download the app or directly start the inspection process.

Delegating the Inspection

Can I upload existing photos?

In most cases, no — Truepic Vision requires photos to be taken live in the app to ensure verified, tamper-proof media. However, some inspections are configured to allow gallery uploads for specific question types.

The ability to upload existing photos depends on how your company configured the inspection in our system. If they enabled this option when creating your case, you may be able to upload existing photos (ones not taken in the Truepic Vision app). Otherwise, you must take all photos and videos using the camera within the Truepic Vision app to submit an inspection.

How to tell if gallery upload is available: If your inspection supports gallery uploads, you will see a gallery icon when capturing a photo.

To get started, tap the link you received, download the Truepic Vision app, and take the necessary photos through the app.

Can I upload photos from my computer?

No — photos cannot be uploaded from a computer. Truepic Vision requires all media to be captured live on a mobile device to ensure verified, tamper-proof inspections.

If you need to complete an inspection, open the Smartlink you received on your smartphone or tablet.

How can I resolve location errors in the Truepic Vision app?

If the Truepic Vision app shows a location error, it usually means location permissions are not enabled or precise location is turned off on your device.

Quick Fix

  • Enable Location Services on your device settings.
  • Grant Truepic permission to access Precise Location data.

Common Location Errors

You may see the following error while using the Truepic app: “Camera disabled; Not at location, relocate to:”, followed by the registered address.

Your submitted photos may be rejected with reasons like:

  • “Photos are not from registered location”
  • “Photo was taken at the wrong location”
  • “Photo was not taken at the registered address” These errors arise from discrepancies between your GPS location and the registered address on file.

Why These Errors Happen

There are two primary causes:

  • Incorrect Registered Address: The app flags your location as wrong if the address in the requesting organization’s system is incorrect.
  • GPS Mismatch: The Truepic Vision app checks the GPS coordinates of your photo against the registered inspection address using precise geolocation data (not cell tower data). If GPS data does not match the registered address, the app disables the camera or flags photos.

How to Resolve Location Errors

Follow these steps based on your situation:

For Incorrect Registered Address
  • Verify the address: Confirm the inspection address in the requesting organization’s records is correct. An incorrect address will disable the in-app camera or flag all submitted photos.
  • Request address update: Contact the requesting organization and ask them to correct the inspection address in their system. Truepic cannot make this update on their behalf.
  • Retake photos: Once the organization updates the address, return to the correct location and retake required photos in the app.
For GPS Mismatch

If you are at the correct registered address and still see “Camera Disabled: Not at Location,” or you have confirmed the address is correct with the requesting organization, contact Truepic Support. We will verify your location and move forward appropriately.

Still Seeing Location Errors?

If you have followed all steps above and continue to experience location errors, reach out to Truepic Support with:

  • The registered address for your inspection
  • Your current device location
  • Screenshot of the error message

No. GPS and address information cannot be manually altered.

A photo is stuck uploading and I can’t retake it. What can I do?

If a photo is stuck uploading in the Truepic Vision app, here’s how to resolve it and continue your inspection.

This article explains what to try first, what Truepic can and cannot recover, and what to do if you can no longer return to the location to retake the photo.

What this usually means

If a photo is stuck uploading, it means the file has not fully reached Truepic’s servers yet. When that happens:

  • You may see the photo marked as Pending, Not synced, or repeatedly failing to upload (e.g., progress resets or never reaches past a certain percentage).
  • You may not be able to delete that photo in the app.
  • Truepic Support typically cannot recover that exact photo if it never finished uploading.

Try these steps first

  • Close the app completely.
  • Make sure the app is fully closed and not still running in the background.
  • Switch your connection.
  • If you were on Wi-Fi, turn Wi-Fi off and try cellular data instead. If you were on cellular data, connect to a strong Wi-Fi network and try again.
  • Reopen the app and tap Retry.
  • Open the inspection again, tap the stuck photo, and use the Retry option if it appears.
  • Keep the app open while it retries.
  • Do not force close the app, switch away from it repeatedly, or lock your phone while the upload is retrying. Wait at least 60 seconds before checking status.
  • Move to a stronger connection.
  • Move to an area with better service or a more stable Wi-Fi network before the upload completes.

If the photo still will not upload

If the retry continues to fail, the only reliable next step is to retake the photo.

If the original file never finished uploading, it typically cannot be recovered by Truepic Support.

If you cannot return to retake the photo

If you no longer have access to the property, unit, vehicle, or item you were photographing, contact support through the app.

If you are able to retake the photo later, Support may be able to help with the inspection submission once the replacement photo uploads successfully.

How to prevent this

Before submitting photos, move to a location with a stronger data connection to ensure all uploads complete successfully.

Seeing “Limit reached — please proceed to next item”? This is a different issue — it means you have taken the maximum number of photos allowed for that question, as set by the company that sent your inspection. This is expected. Tap through to the next question and continue your inspection or delete the photo/video in order to retake it.

What is Truepic?

Truepic is a digital inspection platform that lets organizations request and receive verified photos and videos from recipients.

How it works:

  • Organization sends a Smartlink via text or email

  • Recipient takes photos and videos through the Truepic Vision app

  • Organization reviews verified results. Key features:

  • Verified media capture with integrity verification

  • Location confirmation recorded with each submission

  • Tamper detection to ensure authenticity

  • Centralized dashboard review and management Getting started:

Once you receive your Smartlink from a partner company, choose your access method:

  • If your link starts with web.truepicvision.com, you will use our web app in a browser on iOS or Android. Explore our Web App content for guidance.

About the app: The Truepic Vision app does not immediately save images to your device or access existing photos. You Don’t Need an Account to Use Truepic Vision

You don’t need to create an account to use the Truepic Vision app. Access is granted through the Smartlink you receive — just tap the link and follow the setup steps. There is no password or sign-up process for end users.

How it works

  • The company requesting the inspection sends you a Smartlink via SMS or email
  • Tap the link directly on your phone or tablet to open it
  • Your browser opens to a “Ready to get started?” page — tap Copy & Continue
  • The Truepic Vision app opens and your inspection begins Native app links begin with app.truepicvision.com. Web app links begin with web.truepicvision.com. Either way, the process is the same — just tap the link.

Haven’t received your link yet?

Truepic does not create or send inspection requests. If you’re expecting one but haven’t received it, contact the company that asked you to complete the inspection and ask them to resend it. Also, check your spam or junk folder.
