How do I cancel my subscription?

  • Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026

We are sorry to see you go! If you need our technical support to continue enjoying your subscription, please reach out to us through email at help@curiositystream.com. In order to easily cancel your subscription, review your bank statement to confirm how you are being charged for Curiosity Stream and follow the appropriate method below. If your subscription was set up via iTunes, Google Play, Roku, or Amazon, you’ll need to head over to their respective platforms to manage or cancel your subscription. Cancel my subscription through Curiosity Stream (for accounts created directly through our website) 1. Visit Your Account page. 2. Scroll to the bottom of the page to the Plan and Payment section and select Manage Plan on the right-hand side. 3. Select Cancel Plan in the top right corner. 4. Follow the prompts to cancel your plan. Cancel my subscription through Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) Click here for instructions to cancel your Curiosity Stream subscription through the Apple App Store. Look for Curiosity Stream after following the steps provided by Apple. Cancel my subscription through Google Play Store (Android) Click here for instructions to cancel your Curiosity Stream subscription through the Google Play Store. Look for Curiosity Stream after following the steps provided by Google.

Cancel my subscription through Roku 1. Press the Home button on your Roku remote. 2. Use the directional pad to navigate the channel grid and highlight the Curiosity Stream channel. The directional pad is plus-shaped and is in the center of your Roku remote. 3. Press the Star button on your remote to open the options menu for Curiosity Stream. 4. Select Manage subscription to view the renewal date and access additional options. 5. To unsubscribe, select Cancel subscription. A message appears telling you how long you can continue to access the content and services. 6. Confirm by selecting Cancel subscription. A new message appears confirming the cancellation, along with a reminder of the date when you will no longer have access to the subscription. 7. Select Done to save your changes.

Cancel my subscription through Amazon To cancel an Amazon Appstore subscription on an Android device: 1. Launch the Amazon Appstore application. 2. From the Appstore menu, select Subscriptions. 3. From the My Subscriptions screen, select Curiosity Stream Subscription. 4. From the Subscription screen, you can turn auto-renewal off to cancel your subscription. To cancel a Kindle Fire subscription: 1. From your Kindle Fire device Home Screen, select Apps. 2. Select Store. 3. From the store menu, select My Subscriptions. 4. Select Curiosity Stream. 5. Turn auto-renewal off to cancel your subscription. Thank you for your curiosity!

How do I pause my subscription?

  • Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026

Traveling a lot this summer? No time to watch new documentaries? We offer features to work with your lifestyle such as offline playback on mobile devices, but we also completely understand if you need to take a break! When you pause your subscription, it takes effect on the first day of your next billing cycle. You will not be charged for the duration of months you choose to pause your subscription. To pause your Curiosity Stream subscription: 1. Visit your Account page. 2. Scroll to the bottom of the page to the Plan and Payment section and select Manage Plan on the right-hand side. 3. Select Pause Plan in the top right corner. 4. Choose the duration you would like to pause your subscription (1, 2, or 3 months). 5. Select Continue to confirm you have paused your account.

At the end of the specified pause period, your subscription will reactivate and your monthly payment will be processed automatically. If you choose to cancel during the pause period, you will not be billed. You may reactivate your subscription on the Account page any time. Please note: This option is only available if you are a monthly subscriber and your credit card is billed directly through Curiosity Stream. Unfortunately, pause is not currently available for 3rd party registered accounts such as Apple, Google, Amazon, and Roku.

Taxes Applicable to Your Subscription

  • Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026

Certain jurisdictions require Curiosity Stream to charge a sales tax, value-added tax (VAT), or similar such tax to subscribers who are located within a taxing jurisdiction. Starting in February 2016, Curiosity Stream will add the applicable tax, if any, to the subscription plan price for only those subscribers located in a taxing jurisdiction. Subscriber location is determined based upon the billing information provided by the subscriber. For those subscribers located in a jurisdiction that does not charge an applicable tax, no amount will be charged beyond our subscription plan price. Some states such as Florida have a communications services tax. You can learn more about it here. For subscribers located outside of the United States, the amounts billed may vary over billing periods due to fluctuating foreign currency rates and currency conversion fees. International customers are subject to taxes applicable to their country.

What are the benefits of a Curiosity Stream annual standard plan?

  • Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026

What is Kids Mode?

  • Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026

What is Kids Mode?

Kids Mode is a viewing mode within your account settings that excludes films that may not be appropriate for young children. - It is found within your account settings and can be toggled on or off. - This setting is account-wide when enabled. So, when you enable Kids Mode, content inappropriate for young children will be excluded from all Curiosity Stream channels, apps, and devices that utilize your Curiosity Stream account.

If you wish to turn on Kids Mode, visithttps://curiositystream.com/login and select the “Your Account” page from the top right corner drop-down menu. On the Account page, scroll to the SETTINGS section ( this will be toward the middle of your Account). You will see an option to “Turn Kids Mode On”. Does your HDMI cable and port support 4k? In order to deliver true 4K resolution to your display, you’ll need an HDMI connection (both an HDMI cable and an HDMI port) that does not reduce or degrade the video signal to 1080 or lower. HDMI version 1.4 and above offers 4K support (some devices require HDCP 2.2). You may need to consult your owner’s manual to determine which HDMI ports on your display support 4K video input.
