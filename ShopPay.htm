Shop Pay help

This guide holds Shopify’s own help for Shop Pay, gathered from help.shop.app in November 2026. Shop Pay saves your payment and shipping details so you can check out faster at stores that use Shopify. It covers paying online and in stores, the cards it accepts, its settings and security, and Shop Pay Installments, which splits a purchase into payments.

Shop and Shop Pay share one help centre, so this collection splits it in two: Shop.md for the Shop app and ShopPay.md for paying with Shop Pay and Shop Pay Installments. Each section opens with Shopify’s own overview of it. Shopify’s help for store owners is a separate guide, Shopify.md.

Managing your Shop Pay Installments payments settings

Make a manual installments payment for your order

If you want to make an early payment, late payment, or pay off your remaining balance on an order, then you can do it manually.

There are no penalties for making an early installment payment. For interest-bearing loans, you can save on interest fees when you pay off the remaining balance early. However, while there are no late fees, late payments might affect your eligibility to pay for orders with Shop Pay Installments in the future.

On this page

  • Make a manual payment in the Shop app
  • Make a manual payment on a web browser

Make a manual payment in the Shop app

You can make a manual payment in the Shop app.

Steps:
  • From the Shop app, go to the tab.
  • Tap the order that you want to make a payment for.
  • Tap Make payment.
  • Enter the amount that you want to pay.
  • Tap Pay now.

Make a manual payment on a web browser

You can make a manual payment on a web browser by signing in to your Shop Pay account.

Steps:
  • On a web browser, go to shop.com.
  • Click Sign in, and then follow the prompts to sign in to your Shop account.
  • Click the Account icon.
  • In the Installments section, click the order that you want to pay.
  • Click Make a payment to open the payment dialog.
  • Select or enter a payment amount.
  • In the Payment method section, use the drop-down arrow to select your preferred payment method.
  • Click the Pay button.

Manage your automatic payment settings

After your first payment, Shop Pay automatically charges your method of payment for installment payments going forward, either bi-weekly, or monthly.

You can change your automatic payment method on a Shop Pay Installments order while it’s in repayment. However, you can’t use Capital One cards, Chase credit cards, or prepaid debit cards as a payment method. If you’ve opted into monthly payments, then you can only pay with a debit card.

Going forward, the new payment method will be used for payments on this order. If you want to update your payment method or multiple Shop Pay Installments orders, then you need to change your automatic payment method for each order.

On this page

  • Change your automatic payment method in the Shop app
  • Change your automatic payment method on a web browser

Change your automatic payment method in the Shop app

  • From the Orders tab in the Shop app, tap the order that you want to change the payment method for.
  • Tap View Installments, and then tap Manage.
  • Select the new payment method that you want to use. If you need to add a new payment method, then tap Add card, and complete the required fields.

Change your automatic payment method on a web browser

  • On a web browser, go to shop.com.
  • Click Sign in, and then follow the prompts to sign in to your Shop account.
  • Click the Account icon to see your account settings.
  • In the Installments section, select the order that you want to update your payment method for.
  • Click Manage to open the payment method dialog.
  • Select a payment method from the drop-down menu, or use a different card.
  • Click Save.

Managing your Shop Pay settings

Change your Shop Pay phone number

To change your phone number, you need to sign in to Shop on shop.com through a web browser.

On this page

  • Considerations for changing your Shop Pay phone number
  • Change your Shop Pay phone number

Considerations for changing your Shop Pay phone number

Before you change your Shop Pay phone number, review the following considerations:

  • You can only use a mobile phone number with Shop.
  • You can only change your phone number one time within 24 hours.
  • If you can’t verify your Shop account using your existing phone number, then you can verify by email. However, to ensure that your payment details are protected, this option removes all of your payment information from your Shop account.
  • If you change your Shop Pay phone number, then you also need to update your phone number with Affirm before you can pay for another purchase using Shop Pay Installments.

Change your Shop Pay phone number

  • On a web browser, go to shop.com.
  • Click Sign in, and then follow the prompts to sign in to your Shop account.
  • Click the Account icon, and then click Sign in & security.
  • Click Change next to your existing phone number.
  • If you’re prompted with the Confirm it’s you dialog, do either of the following to verify your existing phone number:
    • To confirm using your existing mobile number, click Send code, and then enter the 6-digit security code sent to your phone number.
    • If you don’t have access to your existing mobile number, then click I don’t have access to this phone number. In the Change phone number dialog, check I understand that changing this number will delete my payment information.
  • In the Update mobile number dialog, enter the new phone number that you want to use, and then click Continue.
  • In the Confirm it’s you dialog, enter the 6-digit security code that you receive to your new phone number.

After you change your Shop Pay phone number, you’re automatically logged out from all of your devices using Shop Pay.

Edit your Shop Pay shipping addresses and payment methods

You can edit your shipping addresses and payment methods in the Shop app, or during checkout. You can save up to 10 credit cards, and 20 shipping addresses in your Shop Pay wallet.

On this page

  • Update your payment methods
  • Update your shipping addresses
  • Update your default shipping address

Update your payment methods

You can update your payment methods in the Shop app.

Steps:

  • From the Shop app in the tab, tap the Account icon, which is your profile picture or initials.

  • In the Payment methods section, tap Add card.

  • Do any of the following:

  • To add a card, fill in the Card number field, and then follow the prompts to fill in the remaining required fields.

  • To delete a credit card, tap on the credit card that you want to delete, and then tap Delete.

  • To change your billing address on an existing card, delete and then re-add your card.

  • Optional: If you have a co-branded card, then you can change your processing network by deleting and re-adding your card.

  • Optional: When you have multiple saved credit cards, you can assign a nickname to your credit card for display instead of showing only the last 4 digits. This feature is available for updating credit card information during checkout, and is unavailable in the Shop app.

Update your shipping addresses

You can update your shipping addresses in the Shop app. The following limitations exist for shipping addresses:

  • You can store a maximum of 20 addresses. To add a new one after reaching the limit, you’ll need to delete an existing address.
  • You can’t change the shipping address on an order that’s already been placed. If you need to update the shipping address after purchase, then contact the store you bought from. If the order has already shipped, then contact the carrier.
  • If an order is edited after it appears in the Shop app, then those changes aren’t reflected on the order receipt in the Shop app.
Steps

  • From the Shop app in the tab, tap the Account icon, which is your profile picture or initials.

  • Tap Addresses.

  • Do any of the following:

  • To edit a shipping address, tap the address that you want to change. Edit the fields that you need, and then tap Save.

  • To add a shipping address, tap Add address, enter your address details, and then tap Save.

  • To delete a shipping address, tap the address, tap the trash can icon next to the address, and then tap Delete.

  • Optional: To set a default shipping address, tap the address, select Default address, and then tap Save.

After you update your shipping address, it displays when you check out using Shop Pay on future orders. To update your shipping address for an order that’s already been placed, you need to contact the store that you purchased from.

Update your default shipping address

You can store up to 20 shipping addresses in your Shop Pay account and set one as your default address. You can add or edit addresses in the Shop app, at checkout, or by logging into your Shop Pay account online.

The default address is set automatically using the following parameters:

  • If you set a default address, then this is used as your default shipping address at checkout.
  • If you don’t set a default address, then your last used address is used as the default.
  • If you have only one address, then it’s automatically set as your default.
  • If you’re a new user, then your first saved address becomes your default.

Your default shipping address determines the currency that’s shown while you shop in the Shop app.

If you want to change the currency you see in the Shop app, or update the default shipping address at checkout, then set a different address as your default in the Addresses section of your Shop account.

Manage your Shop Pay subscriptions

You can manage your Shop Pay subscriptions by signing in to your Shop account in a web browser. If you’re on a mobile device, open this link in your mobile web browser rather than the Shop app. This includes viewing subscription details and updating your payment method for each subscription individually. You can also click Manage subscription to access your customer account through the store that you purchased from to make a payment, pause, or cancel your subscription.

Steps:

  • Sign in to your Shop account in a web browser.
  • Click your account icon.
  • In the Account tab, click Subscriptions.
  • Click the subscription that you want to manage.
  • Do any of the following to manage your subscription:
    • Update your subscription payment method. Changing your default payment method doesn’t automatically update your existing subscriptions, so you need to update the payment method for each subscription:
      • Click Update payment method.
      • Select or add a new payment method for your subscription.
      • Click Save.
    • To make a payment, pause, or cancel your subscription, click Manage subscription. This redirects you to your customer account on the store that you purchased from, where you can make changes to your subscription.

On this page

Deleting a card from Shop Pay

If you delete a payment card from your Shop Pay account, then your active subscriptions aren’t canceled. Your subscriptions continue to bill according to their existing schedule, even after the card is removed from your Shop Pay wallet.

When you delete a card that’s linked to an active subscription, you’re notified that your subscriptions won’t be canceled. After the card is deleted, you receive an email with a summary of your active subscriptions and a link to manage them.

To cancel a subscription, click Manage subscription on your subscriptions page.

Send in-store receipts to Shop

If you make an in-store payment with a payment card that’s saved in your Shop account, then you can receive your digital receipt in the following ways:

  • In the Shop app, your receipt displays in the Orders tab.
  • You can also receive your receipt at the email address or phone number that’s associated with your Shop Pay account, when available.

In-store receipts are activated by default for each card that you use with Shop Pay at a retail store.

When in-store receipts are turned on and you make a purchase at a store that accepts Shop Pay, you can select Shop app as your receipt option at checkout. After you select Shop app one time, your future receipts are sent to your Shop app automatically. This applies to purchases made with any payment card saved to your Shop account, at any store that accepts Shop Pay.

When the in-store receipts setting is turned on, your name and email address are shared with the store that you’re shopping at so that you can view your in-store order in the Shop app.

You can turn in-store receipts on or off for each card from your Shop account.

Steps:

Shop app

  • In the Shop app, tap the Account icon.
  • In the Payment methods section, tap the card that you want to adjust settings for.
  • Tap In-store receipts.
  • Turn the setting on or off.

shop.com

  • On a web browser, go to shop.com.
  • Click Sign in, and then follow the prompts to sign in to your Shop account.
  • Click the Account icon, and then click In-store receipts.
  • Find the card that you want to adjust settings for, and then turn the setting on or off.

Shop Pay

Accepted cards that you can use with Shop Pay

To use Shop Pay, you need to have a supported payment card that the online store that you’re purchasing from supports. You can usually find the list of supported payment methods on the payment screen at checkout.

In addition to credit and debit cards, Shop Pay supports the following local payment methods when they’re available from the store and processed by Shopify Payments:

  • Bancontact
  • BLIK
  • EPS
  • iDEAL | Wero
  • MobilePay
  • Multibanco
  • Przelewy24
  • Swish
  • TWINT
  • USDC

Which payment methods you can use depends on your location and the store’s settings. You can’t use local payment methods to pay for subscriptions.

Some participating stores also let you use health savings account (HSA) and flexible spending account (FSA) payments with Shop Pay for eligible products. You can pay with a supported HSA or FSA card. You can also use a standard credit card and request reimbursement from your HSA or FSA. HSA/FSA payments are available only when the store, products, checkout, and card meet the requirements. If the HSA/FSA funds option isn’t displayed at checkout, then the order isn’t eligible for HSA/FSA payment with Shop Pay.

If you’re having trouble using Shop Pay with your card, then you can troubleshoot by confirming the following information:

  • If applicable, make sure that you’ve activated your card before using it.
  • Check that your billing information matches your bank information on file. For example, the zip code needs to match the billing address.
  • Make sure that you’re using a supported debit or credit card. For example, some prepaid cards aren’t supported.
  • Payment cards issued in India aren’t supported by Shop Pay.
  • If you’re using Shop Pay Installments, then make sure you’re not using a Capital One card, Chase credit card, or prepaid debit card.

Check out with Shop Pay on an online store

Shop Pay is the default checkout experience when you make a purchase in the Shop app or on shop.com. You can also check out using Shop Pay on participating online stores for a faster checkout experience.

If you’re recognized as being signed in with Shop, then the Shop Pay button displays the last four digits of your primary payment method.

After you authenticate or check out using Shop Pay on an online store, you’re automatically signed in with Shop on that store. After you’re signed in, you can access your customer account to manage your orders, customer profile, and features such as loyalty points, when available.

Steps:

  • On an online store that offers Shop Pay as a payment method, select the Shop Pay button on the product page, in your cart, or at checkout.

  • Enter your email address, or select the passkey icon in the email field.

  • Do one of the following:

  • If you selected the passkey icon, then follow the prompts to sign in using your saved passkey.

  • If you set up Shop Pay, then you’ll receive a six-digit SMS verification code to the phone number on your Shop Pay account. In the Confirm it’s you section, enter your six-digit code.

  • If you’ve previously verified your Shop email address when you set up Shop Pay, then you can select Send me a code by email instead.

  • Optional: In the Ship to section, select Set as default to designate an address as the default shipping destination for orders paid with Shop Pay.

  • Optional: If the store offers health savings account (HSA) and flexible spending account (FSA) payments and your order is eligible, then select I want to use HSA/FSA funds for this purchase. You might need to complete a short health survey before you can complete your purchase.

  • Review your order before it’s final.

  • Select Complete purchase to finalize your order.

Make a purchase using Shop Pay at a retail store

At participating retail stores that offer Shop Pay as a payment option, staff will show you a QR code at checkout. You can scan the QR code and complete payment on shop.com. Purchases made at a retail store using Shop Pay can be completed on shop.com only, not in the Shop app.

Steps:

  • Scan the available QR code at the register using your phone’s camera.
  • Tap the link from the QR code to open the checkout on shop.com.
  • Review your details, and then tap Pay now.

When your order is complete, you can view the order confirmation page in your browser. You can also select a receipt option on the store’s device at the register.

Paying for HSA and FSA-eligible purchases using Shop Pay

Some online stores let you use Shop Pay to buy eligible health-related products. These stores can offer a health savings account (HSA) or flexible spending account (FSA) checkout flow. You can pay with an HSA or FSA card, or use a standard credit card and request reimbursement from your HSA or FSA. HSA/FSA payments with Shop Pay are powered by Truemed, which reviews product eligibility and medical qualification for eligible purchases.

HSA/FSA payments aren’t available for every store, product, or order.

On this page

  • Requirements for HSA and FSA payments
  • Check out with HSA or FSA payments
  • Health surveys and Letters of Medical Necessity (LMNs)
  • Troubleshooting HSA and FSA payment availability

Requirements for HSA and FSA payments

To use HSA/FSA payments with Shop Pay, the following requirements need to be met:

  • The store offers HSA/FSA payments with Shop Pay.
  • Your order contains eligible products.
  • You’re logged in to Shop and check out with Shop Pay.
  • You use a supported HSA or FSA card, or a standard credit card for reimbursement.
  • The store is based in the United States, the checkout is in USD, and you’re located in the United States.

You can’t use HSA/FSA payments with Shop Pay for every type of purchase. HSA/FSA payments aren’t available with Shop Pay Installments or subscriptions. Orders that include both eligible and non-eligible products aren’t supported. To use HSA/FSA funds, remove the non-eligible products from your cart.

Check out with HSA or FSA payments

If HSA/FSA payment is available for your order, then the HSA/FSA funds option is displayed during Shop Pay checkout.

Steps:
  • At checkout, select Shop Pay.
  • Log in to Shop and complete Shop Pay verification.
  • Select I want to use HSA/FSA funds for this purchase when it’s available.
  • Review your order.
  • Select Complete purchase. If your purchase requires a health survey, then select Continue to health survey instead.
  • If you selected Continue to health survey, then complete the survey. Your checkout continues automatically after you submit it.

Health surveys and Letters of Medical Necessity (LMNs)

Some HSA/FSA purchases require a short health survey before payment can be completed. The health survey is provided during the Shop Pay checkout flow. You have 72 hours to complete the survey. Your order is placed after you complete the survey.

If your purchase qualifies after review, then Truemed provides a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN). An LMN is typically ready in about 4 hours. Depending on the time of day and your state, it can take 1–2 days. Your order might not ship until the qualification review is complete.

After you place an order that requires an LMN, you receive an email at the email address on your order that says your order is in review.

When your LMN is approved, you receive an email from Shop at the email address on your order. The email includes a link to log in to Truemed, where you can download your LMN. You need a Truemed account to download your LMN.

Troubleshooting HSA and FSA payment availability

If the HSA/FSA funds option isn’t displayed during Shop Pay checkout, then your order doesn’t meet the requirements for HSA/FSA payment with Shop Pay.

This can happen for the following reasons:

  • The store doesn’t offer HSA/FSA payments with Shop Pay.
  • One or more products in your order aren’t eligible.
  • Your order includes both eligible and non-eligible products.
  • Your order includes a subscription.
  • Shop Pay Installments is selected.
  • Your payment card isn’t supported.
  • The store, checkout currency, or your location doesn’t meet the requirements.

Rejected medical qualification

If your medical qualification isn’t approved, then you receive an email that says your LMN wasn’t approved. The email includes a link to your order status page. If your payment hasn’t been collected and your order hasn’t started shipping, then your order is canceled and your payment authorization is voided. You also receive an email from the store that your order was canceled. For questions about your order, contact the store where you placed the order. For questions about your payment card, contact your card issuer.

Use a discount code or gift card at checkout

During checkout, tap Add a discount code or gift card, and then enter the code and tap Apply. Before checking out, review your order to make sure the discount code or gift card was applied.

To redeem multiple gift cards at checkout, you can add each gift card code individually. You can also use a discount code in combination with a gift card at checkout. However, you can only apply one discount code per order at checkout.

Using Shop Pay

Shop Pay is a wallet that helps you check out faster on participating stores by automatically filling in your saved email, payment, and shipping details.

Creating a Shop Pay account

You can create a Shop Pay account in either of the following ways:

  • You enter your mobile phone number at a store’s checkout that offers Shop Pay, which saves your details for faster future checkouts.
  • You set up Shop Pay in the Shop app.

Your Shop Pay account is linked to your phone number and email address, so if you save your details when checking out at a store that offers Shop Pay, this creates a Shop Pay account. Because this happens during checkout, it’s easy to forget. This is why you might receive a verification code, or have an account that you don’t remember setting up. If you don’t want a Shop Pay account, then you can opt out of Shop Pay or delete your Shop account.

Information saved to Shop Pay

The following information is saved and securely encrypted for your future purchases:

  • Email
  • Up to 20 shipping addresses
  • Credit card details for up to 10 credit cards, including billing addresses
  • Mobile phone number

Keeping your information secure

The following precautions protect your saved payment and personal information:

  • Your saved cards are encrypted by Shop Pay.
  • Your saved payment details are stored by Shop Pay, not by each store you purchase from. Your full card number isn’t shared with individual stores.
  • Your shipping and billing details are shared with a store only after you place an order.
  • Your saved information meets payment industry security standards. To learn more, refer to Shopify security.

For details about what information Shop collects, and how it’s kept secure, refer to the Privacy for Users of Shopify Apps policy. For more about shopping safely, refer to Shopping with confidence on Shop.

Managing your Shop Pay information

After you set up Shop Pay, you can manage your credit card and billing information from the Payment methods section in the Shop app. You can manage your shipping addresses from the Addresses settings in the Shop app. Learn more about managing your payment and shipping details.

To view and control the information that Shop has access to, refer to Manage your data and privacy settings and Managing your Shop account and data.

Overview: Shop Pay Installments

Shop Pay Installments is available to eligible customers in Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the United States. On participating stores, you can pay for an order over time in installments.

Shop Pay Installments eligibility and requirements

You can use Shop Pay Installments when you meet the eligibility criteria and use an accepted payment method. If your order requires shipping, then you also need to use a supported shipping address.

1. Eligibility criteria

  • Location and billing address: You must reside within Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom (UK), or the United States (US). Your payment card must have a billing address in the same country.
  • Age requirement: You must be at least 18 years old to use Shop Pay Installments in Australia and most other regions. If you reside in any of the following areas, then you must be at least 19 years old to use Shop Pay Installments:
    • In the United States:
      • Alabama
      • Nebraska, if you’re a ward of the state
    • In Canada:
      • British Columbia
      • New Brunswick
      • Newfoundland and Labrador
      • Northwest Territories
      • Nova Scotia
      • Nunavut
      • Yukon
  • Shop Pay: You need to have a Shop Pay account to use Shop Pay Installments.
  • Currency: Your transaction must use AUD, CAD, GBP, or USD, based on where you reside. Another currency prevents the installment option from being available.
  • Order value: Your order total must be between the following amounts including shipping and taxes, depending on your location:
    • $50 AUD and $30,000 AUD in Australia
    • $35 and $30,000 CAD in Canada
    • £50 and £30,000 GBP in the United Kingdom
    • $35 USD and $30,000 USD in the United States
  • Prohibited items: Orders can’t include any form of currency, including virtual and digital currencies such as gift cards.

2. Payment method restrictions

  • Accepted cards: You can pay using a supported debit or credit card, depending on your location. Monthly payments require a debit card. In Canada and the United States, credit cards are accepted only on installment plans of 4 payments. In Australia and the United Kingdom, debit cards are the only supported payment method.
  • Excluded cards: Capital One and Chase credit cards, as well as all prepaid credit and debit cards, aren’t accepted.
  • Digital wallets: Transactions using Shop Pay Installments aren’t compatible with digital wallets such as Apple Pay.
  • Single store purchase: Purchases must be from a single store. Orders combining products from multiple stores aren’t eligible for Shop Pay Installments.
  • Final approval: Even if all criteria are met, final approval for Shop Pay Installments is determined by Affirm on a case-by-case basis.

3. Compatibility and address limitations

  • Shipping addresses: Orders that require shipping can’t be delivered to P.O. boxes. In the United States, orders also can’t be delivered to army post office (APO) addresses.

4. Updating personal information

  • Mandatory updates: Changes in your personally identifiable information (PII), such as your phone number or email address, require updates with both Shop Pay and Affirm before a new installment purchase can be made. Payments to existing orders can still be processed during this update period.

5. Payment details and options

  • Order total display: Interest fees owed aren’t included in the order total displayed in the Shop app.
  • Installment options: Product pages list the option to pay in 4 installments in Australia, Canada, and the United States. In the United Kingdom, product pages list 3 monthly installments. Checkout might offer other monthly installment options.

6. Account modifications

  • Opting out: You cannot opt out of Shop or Shop Pay while there are active installments being paid. Opting out is only possible after all payments are completed.

7. Credit considerations

  • Credit adjustments: Any credit freeze or lock must be lifted before Affirm can process your application. These actions can be implemented online, by phone, or mail.

8. Purchase type and shipping limitations

  • Subscriptions and B2B: Shop Pay Installments cannot be used for subscription orders or business-to-business (B2B) transactions.
  • Shipping for physical products: If you’re ordering physical products, then the shipping address must be within Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, or the United States. Customers in US territories, including Puerto Rico, aren’t eligible for Shop Pay Installments. Digital products don’t require a shipping address.

Shop Pay Installments overview

Shop Pay Installments lets eligible customers pay for an order in smaller increments over time. It’s available in Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Shop Pay Installments is powered and serviced by Affirm and is only available in some stores. You can use Shop Pay Installments for orders between the following amounts, depending on your location:

  • $50 AUD and $30,000 AUD in Australia
  • $35 CAD and $30,000 CAD in Canada
  • £50 and £30,000 GBP in the United Kingdom
  • $35 USD and $30,000 USD in the United States

How Shop Pay Installments works

When you choose Shop Pay Installments at checkout, you’re applying for a loan through Affirm. Affirm might ask you for additional information to review your application. If approved, then Affirm splits your purchase into several smaller payments.

Payments are due either every 2 weeks or monthly, depending on the plans available for you to choose from at checkout. In Australia, you can choose 4 interest-free payments due every 2 weeks, or monthly payments over 6, 12, or 24 months with interest from 0% to 36% p.a.

Depending on your location, the purchase amount, and the plans available, a down payment might be required at the time of checkout. You can also choose to pay off your loan early without any penalties. For interest-bearing loans, you can save on interest fees when you pay off the remaining balance early.

Shop Pay Installments is offered in partnership with Affirm, who services your loan. Review the following pages on the Affirm Help Center to learn more about Affirm and Shop Pay Installments, depending on your location:

  • About Shop Pay Installments (Australia)
  • About Shop Pay Installments (Canada)
  • About Shop Pay Installments (United Kingdom)
  • About Shop Pay Installments (United States)

What to expect after applying for Shop Pay Installments

After you apply for Shop Pay Installments, Affirm will review your application. You’ll receive an email from Affirm (shoppay@shop.affirm.com) to the email address you use for Shop Pay, letting you know whether you’re approved. This email might go to your spam or junk folder, so remember to check those folders too.

If you’re not approved, then the email will explain why and might include next steps, if applicable. Keep in mind that you may be approved the next time you apply, as your eligibility is determined by Affirm on each individual purchase attempt.

If you’re approved, then your payment schedule displays in the Shop app, and you’ll receive email and SMS text reminders (if applicable) before each payment. You can also turn on push notifications in the Shop app. You can view and manage your Shop Pay Installments orders in the Shop app.

You can’t customize your payment schedule, but you can make early payments. You can also make manual payments in the Shop app or when logged into Shop on your browser.

Shop Pay Installments plans

Affirm offers several Shop Pay Installments plans. Affirm determines your eligibility for each plan, and the types of plans offered can change. At checkout, you can review all available options. If you’re eligible to use Shop Pay Installments, then you can pay over time for orders between the following amounts, depending on your location:

  • From $50 to $30,000 AUD in Australia
  • From $35 CAD to $30,000 CAD in Canada
  • From £50 to £30,000 GBP in the United Kingdom
  • From $35 USD to $30,000 USD in the United States

Your payment options and schedule depend on your purchase amount. A down payment might be required. You can’t change the type of payment plan after you’ve completed your checkout. To request a refund or cancellation for your order, contact the online store directly.

Understanding payments, refunds, and troubleshooting with Shop Pay Installments

With Shop Pay Installments, you have a payment schedule set when you order. You can request a refund but during the refund process, you’ll still need to make payments that are due. You can update your payment method for existing and future Shop Pay Installments orders.

Payment schedule

Depending on where you make your purchase, the purchase amount, and the available plans, a down payment might be required at the time of checkout. After your first payment, you’re charged either every 2 weeks or monthly, depending on the payment option that you chose. Payments every 2 weeks are available in Australia, Canada, and the United States. Monthly payments are available in Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the United States. You’re reminded by email and SMS text (if applicable) before each payment is charged to your card. You can also turn on push notifications in the Shop app to be reminded about upcoming charges. You can’t customize your payment schedule.

Late fees and credit reporting

There are no late fees associated with Shop Pay Installments orders. Although there’s no impact to your credit when you apply to use Shop Pay Installments, your loan and payment information might be shared with credit reporting agencies.

Refunds on orders paid with Shop Pay Installments

Contact the store you purchased from to request a refund. They can let you know whether a return is possible and how much the refund would be, according to their own policies. As you wait for your refund, you need to continue to make any payments that are due.

The following are key details you need to know about getting a refund when you’ve used Shop Pay Installments:

  • Refund timing: If a refund is processed by an online store, then it will appear on your original payment method within 3 to 10 business days, and your installments balance and payments will automatically be updated.
  • Refund application: The refund is first applied to any outstanding installment balance of the order. Any remaining refund amount is then returned to your original payment method.
  • Refund notification: You will receive an email from Affirm explaining how the refund was allocated between your remaining balance and your payment method.

Updating payment methods

Here’s how to update your payment methods for both future purchases and existing installment orders:

  • For future purchases: Updating the payment method in your Shop Pay account affects only future purchases with Shop Pay. This change will not impact the payment method for existing installment orders.
  • For existing installment orders: To change the payment details for ongoing installment payments, you must update the payment method specifically for each existing Shop Pay Installments order.

Troubleshooting installment orders

If you’re encountering issues with your installment orders, consider the following common problems and solutions:

  • Order not visible: If you cannot see your installment order in your Shop Pay account or the Shop app, then check that you are logged in with the same email address used for the order. This is the email where you received the order confirmation.
  • Overpayments: In cases where you’ve accidentally overpaid on your total installment balance, Affirm will automatically refund the payment method you used. If the overpayment occurred on a single monthly payment, then the additional funds are credited towards your total installment balance. Alternatively, you can request a refund for the excess amount by completing a form in Affirm’s Help Center for the United States, Canada, or the United Kingdom, depending on your location.
