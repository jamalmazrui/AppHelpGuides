Troubleshooting If you’re having trouble using AirPlay, please review our Supported Devices for alternative ways to watch on a compatible device or TV. If your device isn’t supported or AirPlay isn’t working as expected, try the following: - Check that you have a stable internet connection - Confirm that both your device and the AirPlay destination are connected to the same Wi‑Fi network

NBA Play

What is NBA Play? NBA Play is a central hub where fans can enjoy a variety of daily interactive games, all in one place. These games let you test your NBA knowledge, make predictions, and compete for fun. The games include NBA Blast, NBA Hoop Connect, NBA Rank, NBA IQ, NBA Full Court Guess and NBA Player Path. New challenges are available regularly, so there’s always something new to play. Where can I access NBA Play? Fans can access NBA Play on NBA.com here and via the Discover menu in the NBA App. On the NBA App 1. Sign in to the NBA App 2. Navigate to the Discover tab 3. Select NBA Play

What games are available? To see specific games available and how to play them, visit here. What time do new games go live?  New versions of games launch at 7am ET each morning. Can I play games multiple times in one sitting? Anyone can play each game once per day. NBA ID members can sign in to play additional games or previous versions of games. Create your free NBA ID at nba.com/ID. Can I share my scores? Yes, fans can share their scores upon completion of a game through the ‘share’ button What is the NBA Blast Leaderboard? With an NBA ID, fans can join the NBA Blast Leaderboard to compare your results to other fans. If you would like to remove your display name from the Leaderboard, please reach out to the support team here. Can I earn a badge for participation? NBA ID members will earn the “Player” badge the first time they play a game, and the “Player Weekly Streak” badge can be earned each week by playing 3 times in one week. Make sure you’re signed in with your NBA ID so your progress is counted. You can admire all the badges you’ve earned in your Hall of Fandom, located at id.nba.com/badges. You may need to sign in with your NBA ID to gain access. I played a game. Why don’t I see the “Player” badge in my Hall of Fandom? First, make sure you’re signed in with your NBA ID when playing any games to make sure your badge progress is counted. If you’re sure you’re signed in, wait up to 24 hours for your badge to appear as we update the Hall of Fandom daily with your latest hardware. What is the Box Score Feature?  The NBA Play Box Score feature aggregates your stats across all NBA Play games to include your career games played, win-loss record, highest career streak and your NBA Play Weekly Streak badge. In order to build your personalized NBA Play box score, you must be an NBA ID member. How does the new NBA Play Archive work?  NBA ID Members will have access to unlimited NBA Play games as part of the “Archive”. Fans will be able to filter the games based on game type or level of difficulty: easy, medium, hard and expert. If I play a game in the archive, does it count towards my box score?  Yes, games played within the NBA Play archive can count towards your overall box score. How does the streak work?  A fan can build their streak for any of the 6 daily games. The streak for a specific game will appear upon completion of an individual game. Your overall daily career-high streak will be featured in your box score on the NBA Play hub. How do I earn the ‘Player Weekly Streak’ badge?  NBA ID members are eligible to earn the ‘Player Weekly Streak’ badge for completing any game within NBA Play for three distinctive days of the week measured Monday to Sunday. If a fan maintains their weekly streak week over week, their badge will accumulate. If I’m having trouble with a game, where can I reach out to? Data Privacy Requests

The NBA is made up of many organizations, including individual leagues and teams, each of which manages fan data separately. To ensure your request is directed to the correct group, we provide a centralized privacy portal that automatically routes your submission to the proper organization based on the information you provide. Pausing a League Pass Subscription

When you pause your subscription, you’ll continue to have access through the end of your current billing period. Once the pause begins, you won’t be charged during the pause period, and your subscription will automatically resume when your selected pause period ends. #### How Do I Pause My Subscription? To pause your eligible monthly subscription: 1. Sign in to your NBA Account on NBA.com. 2. Navigate to My Account. 3. Select Manage Subscription. 4. Choose Pause Subscription. 5. Select a pause duration between 1 and 4 months. 6. Confirm your selection.

The pause will begin at the end of your current billing period, and you’ll continue to have access until that date. #### Who Can Pause Their Subscription? Subscription pausing is available for monthly League Pass subscriptions purchased directly through NBA.com. The following subscriptions cannot be paused: - Season-long subscriptions - Subscriptions purchased through Apple, Google, Roku, or other in-app purchase providers - Subscriptions purchased through third-party providers

What Happens When My Subscription Is Paused?

Once your pause begins: - You won’t be charged during the pause period - You won’t have access to League Pass content - Your subscription will automatically resume when the selected pause period ends

Can I Resume My Subscription Early?

You can resume your subscription at any time from your NBA.com account. If you choose to end your pause early: - Your subscription will resume immediately - You’ll be charged right away - A new billing date will be established based on the date your subscription resumes

Pricing and Promotions

When your subscription resumes, you’ll be charged at the current subscription price. Please note that promotional offers and discounts do not pause with your subscription. Any promotional period will continue based on its original timeline even while your subscription is paused. For example, if you’re receiving a discount for a limited promotional period, the promotion will expire based on its original end date, regardless of any subscription pause.

Generating a HAR file

What is a HAR file? A HAR file is a log of all network requests and responses made by a web browser during a browsing session. Our team may ask fans to provide HAR files when troubleshooting because the HAR files can help our development teams diagnose unexpected technical issues that may occur when using NBA.com. The steps to generate a HAR file for commonly used browsers are provided below. Chrome 1. Visit the page where you are experiencing trouble 2. Open the Chrome menu (⋮) at the top-right of your browser window, then select More Tools > Developer Tools. The Developer Tools will open as a docked panel at the side or bottom of Chrome. 3. Click on the Network tab. Select the option to Preserve log. 4. The recording should autostart and you will see a red circle at the top left of the Network tab. If not, click the black circle to start recording activity in your browser and refresh the page you are on. The goal is to replicate the problem you’ve been experiencing while Google Chrome is recording activity. 5. Once you’ve encountered the problem successfully while recording, select the download icon on the right side of the top panel to download and save the HAR log.

Firefox 1. Visit the page where you are experiencing trouble 2. Select the Firefox menu (Three horizontal parallel lines) at the top-right of your browser window, then select More Tools > Web Developer Tools. The Web Developer Tools will open as a docked panel at the side or bottom of Firefox. 3. Click on the Network tab. Before starting the capture, be sure to enable Persistent Logs. This option is accessible after clicking on the gear icon via the Web Developer Tools toolbar. 4. The recording will autostart once you start performing actions in the browser. If not, refresh the page you are on. The goal is to reproduce the problem you’ve been experiencing while Firefox is recording activity. 5. Once you have reproduced the issue and you see that all of the actions have been generated in the Developer Network Panel, right-click anywhere under the “File” column and click on “Save All as Har”

Safari 1. Visit the page where you are experiencing trouble 2. Select the Developer menu, then select Show Web Inspector 3. Click on the Network tab. 4. The recording will autostart once you start performing actions in the browser. If not, refresh the page you are on. The goal is to reproduce the problem you’ve been experiencing while Firefox is recording activity. 5. Once you have reproduced the issue, click the Export icon and save the HAR file

Edge 1. Visit the page where you are experiencing trouble 2. Select the Edge menu (⋮), then select More Tools > Developer Tools 3. Click on the Network tab. 4. The recording will autostart once you start performing actions in the browser. If not, refresh the page you are on. The goal is to reproduce the problem you’ve been experiencing while Edge is recording activity. 5. Once you have reproduced the issue, right click within the Network tab and select “Save all as HAR”
