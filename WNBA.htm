Data Privacy Requests

The WNBA is made up of many organizations, including individual leagues and teams, each of which manages fan data separately. To ensure your request is directed to the correct group, we provide a centralized privacy portal that automatically routes your submission to the proper organization based on the information you provide. This helps ensure your request is processed securely and in compliance with data privacy laws.  If you would like to delete your account or request that your personal information be removed, you can submit a data privacy request through our official portal. Once submitted, you request will be reviewed and fulfill according to the applicable regulations. The WNBA Privacy Policy outlines these practices in more detail and explains how personal data may be handled differently across the WNBA Family. To learn more about how the WNBA and its affiliated leagues, teams, and partners collect, use, and protect your personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.

If you cannot restart your player through the system settings, try unplugging your device. 1. Unplug the player’s power adapter plug from the outlet that it is connected to 2. Wait for 20 seconds 3. Plug your device back in and then wait for your device to restart

Apple TV: 1. Select Settings from the Apple TV Main Menu 2. Under Settings, select System 3. Scroll down and select Restart 4. If you can’t restart your Apple TV because it’s unresponsive, press and hold the Menu and Back buttons on the remote for six seconds 5. Release the buttons when you see the indicator light blink rapidly 6. Unplug the Apple TV power cord for five seconds then plug it back in

Amazon Fire TV: 1. Remove the power adapter plug from the back of your Fire TV 2. Wait at least twenty seconds 3. Plug the device back in then turn it back on

When enabled, scores, records, and other game‑revealing details are hidden across key areas of the app to create a spoiler‑free viewing experience. Blackouts

In countries where WNBA League Pass is available, all games are available live, except in the US and Canada, where local and national blackouts apply. Blackout restrictions exist because local and national content providers have certain exclusive rights to televise live games and content. What games are blacked out? In the US, nationally televised games on ABC, ESPN, ESPN2, Disney+, CBS, Paramount+, NBC, Peacock, USA Network, Amazon Prime Video, and locally televised games of your local WNBA team(s) in your area will not be available to watch live on WNBA League Pass. 1. Games that are nationally blacked out will be available for viewing on WNBA League Pass the following day at 6am ET.  Games on ION will be available to watch live on WNBA League Pass and following the conclusion of the game, will be available to watch on-demand through WNBA League Pass the following day starting at 6am ET.

In Canada, nationally televised games on TSN, SN, CTV, and CTV2 will not be available to watch live on WNBA League Pass. Games on a national provider in Canada that are blacked out live will be available for viewing on WNBA League Pass shortly after the conclusion of the game. Blackouts are specific to your current location, which is determined by the IP address or location services (if turned on) of your device. Within the US, a zip code is used to determine what teams are covered by your local broadcast and subsequently blacked out on WNBA League Pass. If your location changes, your blackout restrictions for live, locally televised games could also change. The lists of US and Canada nationally televised games that are not available live on WNBA League Pass are subject to change and may be updated at any time. Where can I view blacked out games? Games that are not available to watch live in your market on WNBA League Pass are available through either your local broadcast station, a regional sports network in your area, or a US national TV distributor such as ABC, ESPN, ESPN2, Disney+, CBS, Paramount+, NBC, Peacock, USA Network, and Amazon Prime Video. Please check the schedule page here for details on where games are available in your market. Distributors are subject to change. If you are in the US, enter your zip code on the packages page here to confirm any local blackouts. In the US, nationally blacked out games that are not available live on WNBA League Pass will be available for viewing on-demand at 6am ET the following day. Locally blacked out games that are not available live on WNBA League Pass will be available for viewing on-demand shortly after the game concludes. In Canada, nationally blacked out games that are not available live on WNBA League Pass will be available through a national TV provider such as TSN, SN, CTV, or CTV2. Please check the schedule page here for details on where games are available in your market. Distributors are subject to change. WNBA All-Star and Championship of The Commissioner’s Cup The WNBA All-Star Friday Night event, the WNBA All-Star Game, and the championship game of the Commissioner’s Cup will not be available to watch live on WNBA League Pass in the US or Canada. These games and events will be available for viewing on-demand on WNBA Leagues Pass after they have concluded.
