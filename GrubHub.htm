Grubhub splits its help four ways by audience: diners here, restaurants on a separate site, delivery partners on a third, and corporate accounts on a fourth. Only the diner estate is included, and it is small: Grubhub answers most questions through a chat assistant rather than a library of articles, so what follows is the written guidance that exists, not a summary of a larger collection.

The pages are the ones Grubhub’s own help wizard offers: how Grubhub and Grubhub Plus work, memberships including the student, bank and Lyft partnerships, and the pick-up benefit. Grubhub’s privacy policy and terms of use are not included: they render inside the ordinary site chrome rather than the help wizard, so a copy would carry cookie vendor lists and page scaffolding with it. They remain on Grubhub’s site.

Grubhub+ for Amazon Prime members

What is the Grubhub+ offer for Amazon Prime members?

Amazon Prime members can enjoy Grubhub+ free ($120 annual value), as long as they have an active Prime membership and their Grubhub and Amazon accounts are linked. No expiration date or monthly Grubhub+ membership fees.

What is included in Grubhub+?

Grubhub+ is Grubhub’s membership program. Members get exclusive benefits like $0 delivery fees and lower service fees on delivery orders that meet the subtotal minimum (before tax, tip and fees) from Grubhub+ eligible merchants (the “Subtotal Minimum”), 5% back in Grubhub+ Credit on eligible pickup orders, and access to exclusive offers. Additional fees may apply. The current Subtotal Minimum is $12, except for those participating in Grubhub+ Student, who are subject to a $15 Subtotal Minimum. Subtotal Minimums are subject to change.

See the full Grubhub+ terms and conditions for more information.

How can I redeem my free Grubhub+ membership through Amazon Prime?

  1. Go to <0> and click “Activate Now”
  2. Sign in to Amazon to link your Grubhub account
  3. Agree to the terms and conditions to connect your Prime and Grubhub accounts
  4. Agree to share your Prime membership status with Grubhub
  5. Start enjoying all the benefits of free Grubhub+

What if I’m not a Prime member?

This offer is only available to current Prime members. To join Prime and redeem your Grubhub+ offer, you can sign up using the steps above, then experience all that Prime has to offer, including exclusive shopping, savings, and entertainment benefits.

What if I already have a Grubhub+ membership?

Existing Grubhub+ members can redeem this offer. Sign in or sign up for Prime on <0> and follow the steps above to activate this offer.

If your Amazon account matches the email address of your Grubhub account and you have a paid Grubhub+ membership at the time of signup, your paid membership will be canceled and your free Grubhub+ through Amazon Prime will begin on the calendar day immediately following the end of your then-current billing cycle.

If the email of your current Grubhub account does not match your Amazon account, you will need to cancel your paid or trial Grubhub+ membership in the Grubhub app, on 0, or by contacting Grubhub customer support for assistance in canceling your paid Grubhub+ membership.

What if I’ve already redeemed the Prime Grubhub+ offer?

No action is needed if you’ve previously redeemed the Prime Grubhub+ offer. You can continue enjoying your Grubhub+ benefits, now with no expiration date.

How many Grubhub+ accounts can I claim with my Prime membership?

You are only able to claim one Grubhub+ membership per Amazon account. If you have already activated a Grubhub+ membership for Amazon Prime or Amazon Prime Student members, then your Grubhub+ will apply to that Grubhub account only and cannot be transferred to other Grubhub accounts without unlinking your Amazon and Grubhub accounts.

How do I unlink my Amazon and Grubhub accounts?

You can visit the Manage apps & data services in the Manage Your Data section on the Your Account page of <0> to remove the preexisting linkage.

What if I cancel or pause my Prime membership?

If you cancel or pause your Prime membership you will lose your access to free Grubhub+ through Amazon Prime when that cancellation or pause goes into effect.

If you choose to restart your Prime membership, you will need to redeem this offer again by visiting <0>.

If you decide not to continue your membership with Amazon Prime, you can always sign up for a paid Grubhub+ membership to continue receiving Grubhub+ benefits at 0.

Who do I contact if I have an issue connecting my account?

First, verify that you are logged in to Amazon and that your Prime membership is active, then attempt to redeem the offer by visiting <0>.

For questions about your Prime membership, contact Amazon customer service.

For questions about your Grubhub account or Grubhub+ membership, contact Grubhub customer support.

What if I previously redeemed the Prime Student offer for a free Grubhub+ membership?

Prime Student members who have already redeemed the Grubhub+ Student offer will continue to receive free Grubhub+ until they graduate. If you decide to purchase a Prime membership once your Prime Student membership expires, you will continue to receive Grubhub+ for free with no action required. If you do not purchase a Prime membership when your Prime Student membership expires, you will lose access to your free Grubhub+ membership. You may regain your free Grubhub+ membership by purchasing Amazon Prime and ensuring your Grubhub and Amazon accounts are linked.

Will my free Grubhub+ membership convert to a paid membership?

Your free Grubhub+ membership with Amazon Prime will never convert to a paid membership. If you deactivate your Amazon Prime account, you will not be auto-enrolled into a paid Grubhub+ membership.

How do I cancel my free Grubhub+ membership through Amazon Prime?

You will have free access to Grubhub+ with your Prime membership as long as you have Prime and your Grubhub and Amazon accounts are linked. To cancel your free Grubhub+ membership, please visit the Manage apps & data services in the Manage Your Data section on the Your Account page of <0>. From there you can unlink your Amazon and Grubhub accounts, which will cancel your free Grubhub+ membership through Amazon Prime. You can also contact Grubhub customer support to help cancel your Grubhub+ membership.

Will my personal data be shared between Amazon and Grubhub?

By linking your Amazon and Grubhub accounts, you agree that Amazon will share your Prime membership status information with Grubhub for the purposes of enabling your Grubhub offer and that Grubhub may share offer redemption and/or cancellation information with Amazon.

What if my Amazon or Grubhub account is deleted?

Deletion of your Grubhub or Amazon account will result in the termination of your free Grubhub+ membership for Amazon Prime members. Currently, there isn’t a way to restore your Grubhub+ account once your Grubhub or Amazon account is deleted. If you would like to rejoin Grubhub+, you can create a new Grubhub or Amazon account then redeem this offer again by following the steps listed above. Subject to Grubhub and Amazon terms of use.

Grubhub+ Student Membership

Grubhub+ Student Membership is a special promotional membership program for college students who are affiliated with select universities through Grubhub Campus Dining. Grubhub+ Student Membership allows access to the following benefits:

  1. $0 delivery fees and lower service fees on eligible delivery orders that meet the subtotal minimum at select off-campus restaurants
  2. 5% back in Grubhub+ credit per eligible pickup order, which can be used on future Grubhub orders (except on-campus orders)
  3. Exclusive offers on eligible orders
  4. Matched Donate the Change donations from Grubhub+ restaurants. Terms apply

How do I cancel my membership?

You can cancel your Grubhub+ membership at any time. If you cancel, your membership benefits will continue through the end of the current billing cycle. If you cancel during a trial period, your benefits will end immediately. To cancel your Grubhub+ membership:

iOS and Android App Users:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Tap on the Account icon
  3. Navigate to Grubhub+ membership menu option
  4. Select Manage Membership button
  5. Tap Cancel membership
  6. Confirm Cancel membership

Desktop Users:

  1. Navigate to grubhub.com or seamless.com
  2. Log in to your account
  3. Click on the drop-down menu in the upper right-hand corner
  4. Navigate to Grubhub+ menu option
  5. Click Cancel membership
  6. Confirm Cancel membership

How does Grubhub+ work?

What is Grubhub+?

Grubhub+ is our standard monthly membership program. Grubhub+ members get unlimited $0 delivery fees and reduced service fees on eligible delivery orders of $12+ from restaurants, convenience stores and more in the Grubhub+ network, can earn 5% back in Grubhub+ credit per eligible pickup order, have access to exclusive offers and more!

How does it work?

After joining, look for the Grubhub+ badge next to a restaurant’s name or use the Grubhub+ filter to search for participating restaurants. Order from any of these restaurants and you will receive $0 delivery fees and lower service fees on eligible orders of $12 or more. There are no special codes required and no limits on how many orders you can place per month. You also can earn 5% back in Grubhub+ credit per eligible pickup order, which can be used on future Grubhub orders.

Are there any exceptions?

Orders containing alcohol do not qualify for Grubhub+ benefits. Certain order types (including catering orders, and scheduled team orders) may not qualify for Grubhub+ benefits. However, you’ll still receive your $0 delivery fees and lower service fees on eligible orders from Grubhub+ merchants.

Grubhub+ Student is a free membership program for active students, faculty, or staff of a qualifying college campus affiliated with Grubhub Campus Dining. Orders placed using a Grubhub+ Student account may be subject to different restrictions, order minimums, and eligibility requirements than Grubhub+ to receive benefits. Additionally, on-campus orders may not qualify for Grubhub+ benefits, including, without limitation, 5% Grubhub+ credit on pickup orders and $0 delivery fees. To learn more about Grubhub+ Student, visit grubhub.com/about/campus.

How do I join?

It’s easy and you can use your benefits immediately. Join Grubhub+ today!

How does my pick up benefit work?

How does my Grubhub+ pickup benefit work?

5% pickup credit applicable on eligible pickup orders only and is calculated based on the qualifying order subtotal. Credit will appear as a “Grubhub+ Credit” payment method at checkout. Grubhub+ Credit is redeemable on eligible future Grubhub orders only. Grubhub+ Credit is not transferable, may not be exchanged for cash, expires 90 days after most recent credit is issued, may not be applied toward purchases containing alcohol, and is subject to additional restrictions. Grubhub+ Credit cannot be accrued or redeemed on on-campus dining orders. Subject to merchant and delivery ability.

What about my $10 monthly pickup benefit?

Previously, paid Grubhub+ subscribers received a $10 monthly credit to be used on pickup orders. However, we have changed that benefit to 5% back in Grubhub+ Credit on every eligible pickup order to be used on future Grubhub Orders. For members with an existing pickup benefit, that benefit will cease following the end of the member’s next full billing cycle after announcement of this change on July 25, 2023.

What are the restrictions of the pickup benefit?

Earn Rules:

  • Eligible on Grubhub pickup orders (excludes on-campus orders). Can be earned on Restaurant and Convenience orders. Valid on Group Orders and earning will go to host of order.

  • The 5% credit is calculated based on order subtotal only (excluding tax, fees, tip, promos). No minimum order amount restriction.

  • Grubhub+ Credit will be added to the member’s account after making an eligible pickup order and expires 90 days after issuance.

Redemption Rules:

  • Users can apply Grubhub+ Credit to and order during checkout flow via the “Your Payment” selection

  • Grubhub+ Credit can be used on future delivery or pickup orders on the Grubhub platform (excluding on-campus merchants).

  • Grubhub+ Credit cannot be redeemed on orders containing alcohol

  • Grubhub+ Credit must be used within 90 days of last issuance (otherwise it will expire).
