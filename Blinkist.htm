Bulk inviting users and managing groups via CSV

With a CSV file upload, you can:

  • Invite a large number of learners to your account.
  • Assign learners to new or existing groups.
  • Create new groups.

How to use the CSV

  • Navigate to the “Invite New Learners” page.
  • Click “Upload CSV” and download our official template.
  • Fill out the template using the following rules:
    • Column A (Email addresses): Enter the email addresses of the learners you want to invite.
    • Column B (Groups): Enter the name of the group you wish to add these learners to. This can be an existing group, or a new group you’d like to create. If you don’t need to assign a user to any group, leave the group column empty.

Important Rules for your CSV

Group creation: If you enter a group name that doesn’t exist yet, Blinkist will automatically create that group for you. Double check your group names to make sure to avoid mistakes.

Formatting: Group names are not case-sensitive and not space-sensitive (e.g., “Sales Team” and “sales team” are treated as the same group in order to avoid un-intended duplicates).

Multiple Groups: To assign a learner to more than one group, separate the names with a semicolon (;) (e.g., Marketing; Berlin; Managers).

​

Exclusions: If you leave Column B empty, the learner will be invited to your organization but won’t be assigned to any group.

​Please note: This CSV tool can only be used to add learners and creating groups, but not to remove learners or delete groups.

How can I add and remove learners from a group?

Adding learners to a group

If you wish to bulk add learners to multiple groups at the same time, see the article: Bulk inviting and assigning groups via CSV.

If you’re focusing on a single group, you can add learners directly from that group’s management page:

​

Option 1: Adding learners in manually in the interface

From the Learners & Plans page, click on “Manage Groups” and select the group you wish to edit.

  • Click the “Add Learners” button on the top right corner.
  • Select “Add from emails”.
  • Enter the email addresses of the learners you want to add. You can also include email addresses that are not part of your Blinkist account yet.
  • Click “Confirm”.
  • If the learner is already in your organization, they will be added immediately. If they are new, they’ll receive an invitation email, and will be added to the relevant group as soon as they join your account.
  • ​
Option 2: Using the Group-Specific CSV upload for adding a large list of people to one specific group
  • Click the “Add Learners” button on the top right corner.
  • Select “Add from CSV file”.
  • Upload a CSV file that contains only one column with the learners’ email addresses.
  • Our system will process the list and automatically assign all valid email addresses to this group.
  • If the learner is already in your organization, they’ll be added immediately. If they are new, they’ll receive an invitation email, and will be added to the relevant group as soon as they join your account.

Removing learners from a group

To remove one or more learners from a group, follow these steps:

  • Go to Learners & Plan > Manager Groups,

  • Find the group you’d like to edit, and open it.

  • In the group page, locate the learner(s) you’d like to remove.

  • Tick the checkbox next to their name(s).

  • In the pop-up that appears at the bottom of the screen, select Remove from group.

An additional account on your Premium plan: how does it work?

Premium Sharing works similar to a family plan, in that you can invite someone to share a spot on your subscription. If they accept your invitation, the other person will get complete Premium access to Blinkist.

Are there any additional costs for me or the person I invite?

No. Sharing a plan is no additional cost to you, besides the regular cost of your subscription, and it’s free to the person you invite for as long as you’re sharing it with them. You can then recommend titles and share favorites to each other.

Will the invited person and I get access to each other’s accounts?

No, you won’t! You decide what to share.

You and the person you invite to your plan, will indeed get full Premium access to the Blinkist library and features. However, you’ll both get to keep your personal accounts separate, so that your personal library and activity will remain private.

You’re in full control; only titles you actively recommend to them or are recommended to you will be seen by the other person.

​Does the other person have to already have a Blinkist account to be added to my Premium?

No. They can create a new account for themselves if they don’t yet have one.

Understanding Session Types

What’s the difference between Coaching Sessions and Expert Sessions?
  • Coaching Sessions are interactive workshops led by professional coaches focusing on skill-building topics like leadership, communication, productivity, and personal development. These sessions include interactive exercises and focus on actionability and putting learnings into practice.
  • Expert Sessions are conversations with authors from our Blinkist library. These are presentations plus Q&A-style discussions where you can engage directly with the minds behind the books you love.
How do I know which type of session I am registering for?

Check the banner and/or session description when you register! Coaching Sessions will be hosted by our leadership coaches while Expert Sessions will feature an author name and book title in the description.

Replays and Session Materials

Which sessions are recorded? Will I get a replay?
  • Coaching Sessions: Not recorded. If you attend, you will receive the presentation slides via email after the session.
  • Expert Sessions: Recorded and available as replays! You will receive the recording via email after the session as long as you register, whether you attended or not.
Why aren’t Coaching Sessions recorded?

Coaching Sessions are designed to be interactive, live experiences where participants engage in real-time exercises and discussions. To create a safe, confidential space for learning and sharing, we do not record these sessions.

The good news: Coaching Sessions are repeated several times throughout the year, so you will have multiple opportunities to attend if you miss one!

How do I receive slides or replays?

Both slides and recordings are sent automatically via email from [email protected] within 24 hours after the session.

Pro tip: Check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the email! Some users expect it to come from a Blinkist email address, but it will come from Livestorm, our webinar platform. Consider adding [email protected] to your contacts.

Blinkist Spaces Community Guidelines

Spaces is the first way for Blinkist users to interact with each other on our platform, so you can spark even more understanding with a broad range of viewpoints from not just our diverse content but also other users. To ensure you have the best experience interacting with each other and discussing ideas we ask you to be respectful to others and abide by the following guidelines, and to always obey the law.

We use a mixture of automated and manual review processes to enforce these guidelines, and aim to be fair and objective so that everyone feels safe, heard, and valued, regardless of their background and identity.

Respect

We want to foster constructive, insightful, and inclusive sharing and discussions at Blinkist, and above all using respect as a core basis for how to interact with others. The best discussions come from diverse perspectives, but those discussions can only spark productive understanding if they’re also:

  • Kind and welcoming
  • Considerate of other users and their viewpoints/backgrounds/communication styles
  • Appreciative of others sharing their opinions and ideas
  • Using constructive language when disagreeing with/criticizing ideas and not attacking the other person
  • Refraining from vulgar language or sexually explicit content

Restricted content

We do not tolerate any speech that promotes harmful or hateful ideas. This includes things such as:

  • Clear threats or promotion of violence against others
  • Harassment or abuse of individuals
  • Organizing of illegal activities
  • Dis- or misinformation that leads to harmful outcomes
  • Clear promotion of hate against certain groups based on attributes such as religion, gender, ethnicity, race, cultural background, sexual orientation, age, physical abilities & appearance, body size, or based on any other ground The sharing of external links that breach the above will also be considered in the same manner.

​

Privacy & identity

To create an authentic community within Spaces each user creates a profile that they use to interact others, which should:

  • Represent their true selves and not pose as another individual or entity

  • Use an appropriate username that abides by our community guidelines On top of that, users should respect the privacy of individuals and not reveal to others personal information about Blinkist users or other individuals without their consent. This can include things like:

  • Personal ID or passport information

  • Contact information and addresses

  • Online credentials

  • Financial or medical history General information on how we process your data in connection with Blinkist Spaces can be found in our Privacy Policy.

Spam

When interacting with others, please be mindful of the frequency of your actions. Blinkist Spaces should be a place to have authentic sharing and discussions, and we may restrict spammy behavior like the following:

  • Using automated scripts to interact frequently with others in a short amount of time
  • Targeting a particular user with frequent unwanted communication
  • Creating multiple accounts for the same user
  • Generally spammy behavior.

Reporting & consequences

Our teams are constantly monitoring the behavior on our platforms through automated and manual processes, but we also ask you to tell us whenever you or any individual feels unsafe or is at risk due to activity in a Space.

  • You can report any activity in a Space that violates our Community Guidelines by sending an email to [email protected] from your registered Blinkist email address.

  • Once the issue is reviewed we may take actions against certain users such as removing from Space, blocking general usage of Spaces, or taking further actions.

What’s Blinkist Spaces and how does it work?

Blinkist Spaces is a way to organize and curate your favorite titles, whether for yourself or with others. A Space can be for a topic like Productivity or Mindfulness, or shared and dedicated to a group’s interest, like a book club or a parents group. You can create as many Spaces as you’d like, only private for you, or with other people.

When you create a Space, you can easily add or remove titles, notes, and members.

In a Space, Blinkist paid members can both give and receive recommendations to all members in that Space, as well as add new members to a Space, or remove them. Blinkist Basic members of a Space can read or listen to the titles recommended by users with a paid subscription—even if the title is normally only accessible to paid members.

Does it cost me or the person I invite to a Space?

No. Inviting someone to a space is free. Blinkist Basic members, or even those who are not yet Blinkist members, can still be invited to and participate in a Space.

Who should I invite to a Space?

Blinkist Spaces members tell us they like inviting a partner, family members, friends, or colleagues. We recommend someone with whom you can exchange ideas, share your perspectives, and talk about what you’ve learned.

Please note: if you have a Blinkist Business account, you can only invite colleagues within your organisation to a Space. You won’t be able to invite someone with a personal subscription like your family of friends. This is meant to protect the security and data of your and other Blinkist Business customers.

Does the other person have to already have a Blinkist account to use Spaces?

No they don’t. You can invite them to your Space, and they’ll be able to sign up for a Blinkist account of their own to join the Space.

If you have a Blinkist Business account: anyone within your organization can invite or join a Space created by another colleague within your organization, they can simply create an account upon creating/joining a Space if they haven’t already. You won’t be able to invite someone with a personal subscription to a Space you created with your Blinkist Business account.

Do the other people in my Spaces have access to my library and activity (and viceversa)?

No, they don’t. You’re in full control; only titles you actively recommend to them or are recommended to you in a Space will be seen by the other people there. You can also create a Space for just yourself, without inviting anyone to it.
