  4. Tap Manage accounts.
  5. Tap Manage next to the account you want to remove.
  6. Tap Move out of this accounts center.
  7. Tap Move account.

How to dismiss a persistent notification for the Meta AI mobile app on Android

476 Likes

Updated:15 weeks ago

Certain voice control features and media import settings require the Meta AI mobile app to run in the background. Android OS displays a persistent notification to let you know which apps are active. You can dismiss this notification by swiping it away. The Meta AI mobile app will continue running in the background if you dismiss this notification. #### Turn off auto-import media and voice control

If you’re on an older version of Android OS and are unable to dismiss the notification, you’ll need to turn off auto-import in order to remove the persistent notification.

To turn off auto-import:

  1. From the Meta AI mobile app, tap Glasses in the top right.
  2. Tap Device settings.
    • If you have multiple devices paired with the Meta AI mobile app, swipe left or right to select the device you want to adjust settings for before tapping Device settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Media.
  4. Tap the toggle next to Auto-import media to turn this feature off.

Once you turn off auto-import, all photos and videos taken with your glasses will need to be manually imported to the Meta AI mobile app.

A factory reset will delete all captures on your glasses and will require you to unpair and then re-pair your glasses with the Meta AI mobile app. For Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, see instructions in the next tab.

To factory reset your glasses:

  1. Make sure your glasses are out of their case, charged and powered on.

If you’re experiencing issues using your charging case, a factory reset might help. You can also use these steps for Meta Ray-Ban Display charging cases (see instructions in the next tab for details).

  1. Remove your glasses from the case.
  2. Press and hold the button on the back of the case for more than 16 seconds to factory reset the case.

How to force restart Meta Ray-Ban Display and Meta Neural Band

10 Likes

Updated:5 weeks ago

[Embedded video omitted]

If you’re experiencing issues using Meta Ray-Ban Display or Meta Neural Band, a force restart may help. Force restarting will not delete your captures on your glasses and won’t require you to unpair and re-pair your glasses or band to the Meta AI mobile app again.

To force restart Meta Ray-Ban Display:

  1. Close both arms.
  2. Turn off your glasses by sliding the power switch away from the lenses, so that you see the red line.
  3. While holding the capture button, turn on your glasses by sliding the power switch toward the lenses, and when the LED turns red, release the capture button.
  4. After about 17 seconds, your glasses will be ready to use.

To force restart Meta Neural Band:

  1. Press and hold the button.
  2. Wait for the LED to completely fade out and turn off before releasing the button, which should take about 8 seconds.

If you force restart your device and are still experiencing problems, you can try a factory reset for Meta Ray-Ban Display or Meta Neural Band, or contact Meta Store Support.

  1. From the Meta AI mobile app, tap Glasses in the top right.
  2. Tap in the top right.

  4. Power on your wrist band by pressing and holding the button for 2 seconds until you see the LED flash white and the band vibrates.

  3. Tap General.
  4. Tap Unpair wristband.
  5. Tap Unpair.

Note:

  • Unpairing your band will not change or erase any settings.
  • You may also need to forget your band from your phone’s Bluetooth connected devices menu to complete unpairing.

Troubleshoot microphone issues on AI glasses

63 Likes

Updated:6 weeks ago

If your glasses are not responding consistently when you say “Hey Meta” or if people can’t hear you on a call, follow these troubleshooting steps:

  1. From the Meta AI mobile app, tap Glasses in the top right.
  2. Tap Device settings.
    • If you have multiple devices paired with the Meta AI mobile app, swipe left or right to select the device you want to adjust settings for before tapping Device settings.
  3. Tap Meta AI.
  4. Tap “Hey Meta” preferences.
  5. Ensure the toggles next to “Hey Meta” and Wake sound are turned on.
  6. Try a voice command (example: “Hey Meta, how much battery is left?”).
    • If the voice command was recognized, you should see the notification LED blink white in response. When the Wake sound toggle is on, you will also hear a sound when voice controls activate in response.
  7. If voice controls still aren’t responding, toggle off “Hey Meta” and Wake sound, then turn them back on and try another voice command.
  8. If voice commands still aren’t working, go to Device settings, swipe down and tap Microphone troubleshooting.
  9. Tap the toggle next to Microphone adjustment to turn this feature on, then try another voice command.
  10. If voice commands still aren’t working, try unpairing and re-pairing your glasses. If that doesn’t solve the issue, you can try a factory reset, or contact Meta Store Support.

Troubleshoot pairing issues with AI glasses

22 Likes

Updated:3 weeks ago

If you are unable to pair your AI glasses to the Meta AI mobile app, try these troubleshooting steps:

  1. Make sure glasses are charged, turned on, and placed in the case.
  2. Charge glasses for at least one hour or until case LED is green.
  3. Press and hold the pairing button on the back of the case for 5 seconds. Release when case LED pulses a blue light.
  4. Check if your AI glasses show up in your phone’s Bluetooth connections. If they do, forget the connection, then try pairing your glasses again.
  5. If that doesn’t solve the issue, try deleting and reinstalling the Meta AI mobile app on your phone.
  6. If that doesn’t solve the issue, try performing a manual factory reset for your glasses.

If the LED on your glasses is continuously blinking white during pairing, you can force restart your glasses.

How media storage works with AI glasses and the Meta AI mobile app

21 Likes

Updated:15 weeks ago #### Before importing media

When you capture photos and videos with your AI glasses, your media is stored on your glasses until you import them into the Meta AI mobile app. During import, while your media is transferred from your glasses to your phone, media is temporarily stored as a part of the Meta AI mobile app in a cache. The cache will automatically clear itself shortly after importing, and you can manually clear the cache in Device settings.

To clear your Meta AI mobile app cache:

  1. From the Meta AI mobile app, tap Glasses in the top right.
  2. Tap Device settings.

  3. Plug the other end of the cable into a wall adapter or powered USB port.

  4. The LED on the stand will briefly light up white to confirm it’s powered on. #### Charge your glasses

  5. Close the temple arms on your glasses.

  6. Place the glasses onto the charging dock.

  7. The LED on the stand will light up to confirm charging has started. #### If the LED doesn’t light up when docking your glasses, try the following troubleshooting steps:

The LED on the charging stand shows the current charging status:

  • LED color: White (solid); Status: Stand is powered on
  • LED color: Orange (solid); Status: Charging in progress — battery between 20% and 95%
  • LED color: Green (solid); Status: Charging nearly complete — battery above 95%
  • LED color: Red (blinking); Status: Error — liquid detected or other issue (see note below)

Note: Blinking red means liquid has been detected on the charging contacts. Remove your glasses and dry both the glasses and the stand with a soft cloth. Wait a few minutes before docking again. #### Charging times

  • Charge level: 0% to 50%; Estimated time: 22 minutes
  • Charge level: 0% to 100%; Estimated time: 75 minutes

  1. Dock your glasses on the stand for at least 5 minutes.
  2. The update will begin automatically.
  3. Keep your glasses on the stand until the update is complete (about 30 seconds).

How do I return my AI glasses to the Meta Store?

4 Likes

Updated:4 weeks ago

If you purchased AI glasses (such as Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta HSTN, or Oakley Meta Vanguard) from the Meta Store and want a refund, you can return them within 30 days of delivery. The return process has 4 steps: submit a return request, receive your Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) email with a prepaid shipping label, package your glasses safely, and ship the package to Meta. This guide walks you through each step.

If your AI glasses are defective and you want a repair or replacement instead of a refund, see AI glasses warranty and support

Important: This guide is only for AI glasses purchased from the Meta Store. If you purchased your AI glasses from Ray-Ban, Oakley, LensCrafters, or another retailer, contact the retailer where you made your purchase for their return process. #### What you need before starting an AI glasses return

  • Confirm you are within the 30-day return window: You have 30 days from delivery to initiate a return, and 14 days after starting your return request to ship the product back. Check your order confirmation email for the delivery date. If you are outside the return window, you will not be able to request a refund. See the Meta Store return policy for full details.
  • Find your Meta Store order number: Locate the order number in your order confirmation email. You will need this order number to start the return request on meta.com.
  • Confirm you purchased from the Meta Store: This return process only applies to AI glasses bought directly from the Meta Store at meta.com. If you purchased your AI glasses from Ray-Ban, Oakley, LensCrafters, or another retailer, contact that retailer directly for their return process. #### Start a return request for AI glasses on meta.com

Self-service return (recommended)

  1. Go to meta.com/my/orders and sign in to your Meta account.
  2. Find the order containing your AI glasses.
  3. Click Return item.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete your request. #### Contact Meta Store Support to start a return

If you’re unable to use the self-service option, contact Meta Store Support to initiate your return. #### Receive your RMA email with a prepaid shipping label

After submitting your return request, you’ll receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) email within 1-3 business days.

Your RMA email includes:

  • Prepaid shipping label(s).
  • UN3481 battery label (required for lithium battery shipments).
  • Instructions to prepare and package your device.
  • A list of exactly what items to return.
  • Return shipping address.

Note: Don’t ship your glasses until you receive this email.

If you didn’t receive your email, check your spam or junk folder. If more than 3 business days have passed, contact Meta Store Support. #### Package your AI glasses safely for return shipping

Once you have your RMA email, package your glasses for safe shipment.

AI glasses contain lithium batteries and must be packaged correctly to avoid fire hazards during transit.

Follow the How to package AI glasses safely for returns guide for step-by-step packaging instructions, including the critical safety step. #### Ship your AI glasses return package
