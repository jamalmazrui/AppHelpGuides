What is For You on TikTok?

For You is a personalized feed of content based on your interests and engagement. The For You feed is the first feed you’ll see when you open TikTok. The more you use TikTok, the more your For You feed will reflect your interests and show you creators and content you’re likely to enjoy.

Why do you see certain posts on TikTok?

The For You feed recommends content based on several factors. To learn why a post was recommended to you, we share information alongside each post to help you understand why content appeared in your For You feed. To learn why a post is recommended to you: 1. In the TikTok app, tap the Share button on the side of a post in the For You feed. 2. Tap Why this post. We’ll list some reasons why a post shows up on your feed, such as: ༚ You commented on, liked, shared, or watched similar posts. ༚ This post is popular in your country. ༚ This post was created recently. ༚ This video is longer and you seem to enjoy longer videos. ༚ You’re following this creator. If your content is not suitable for a broad audience, we may make it ineligible for the For You feed and may also make it harder to find in search.

How to manage your For You feed

You can use several features to help influence the content you see on your For You feed or have new content recommended to you, including: • Not interested: If you don’t care for a specific post, you can share feedback that you’re not interested, and we’ll show you fewer posts like that. • Refresh your feed: You can refresh your For You feed to help us reshape your For You feed recommendations.• Filter keywords: You can use filters to remove content containing specific words and their variations, and hashtags from your For You and Following feeds.• Manage topics: You can tailor the content on your For You feed by adjusting your preferences for different topics.Learn more about how to report content on TikTok.

Footnotes are written and rated by eligible contributors and must comply with our Community Guidelines. The Footnotes program is powered by a system that finds a broad level of agreement between people who usually have different opinions. Footnotes display on short-form videos if they meet the threshold for “helpful.”

How to enroll as a Footnotes contributor

TikTok’s mission is to inspire creativity and bring joy. We have a wide variety of content and creators to discover, so you can find what’s most interesting and relevant to you. How to tell if an account is verified on TikTok

A verified badge is the blue check mark symbol that appears next to a TikTok user’s account name in search results and on the profile.

How to get verified on TikTok

You can complete a TikTok verification application to be considered for a verified badge. We consider a number of factors before granting a verified badge, such as whether the account is active, authentic, notable, and unique. We don’t consider the number of followers or likes on an account when granting a verified badge. Location information collected by TikTok

If you’re at least 18 years old, in a region where your device’s Location Services are available for the TikTok app, and you’ve chosen to turn on Location Services in TikTok and in your device settings, we’ll receive either your approximate or precise location from your device’s Location Services, depending on which one you’ve selected. If you choose to turn on Location Services, TikTok receives location information from your device’s GPS and other signals:

  • Approximate location from Location Services: Typically, TikTok receives an area of 1-4 square miles or 3-10 square kilometers. This helps support features like showing locally relevant content without determining your position more accurately.
  • Precise location from Location Services: Typically, TikTok receives information about an area of 100-1000 square feet or 10–100 square meters, which is smaller than approximate location. This provides higher location accuracy and can support features that rely on more specific local relevance. Where precise location is available, you can turn this setting on or off at any time through your device’s Location Services.

Learn more about location information for your device in the Google Account Help Centeror Apple Support.In regions where your device’s Location Services are not available for the TikTok app, or when you turn Location Services off, we will continue to estimate other approximate location information based on your device or network information, such as SIM card region, IP address, and device system settings.
