Calendar feed setup and sync

Calendar feed setup and sync

Modified on Tue, Mar 24 at 2:06 PM

The TripIt calendar feed dynamically displays your TripIt itineraries in your desired calendar application.

If you’re not seeing your TripIt calendar feed URL, it may be currently disabled.

How to enable your calendar feed URL.

TripIt app:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Select Settings.

  • Tap Calendar Sync.

  • Enable the calendar feed toggle. TripIt website:

  • Click the Profile icon (top right of any page).

  • Click Settings.

  • Select Calendar Feed.

  • Enable the calendar feed toggle. How to locate your TripIt calendar feed in the TripIt website.

  • Click the Profile icon (top right).

  • Select Settings.

  • Click Calendar Feed (left navigation bar).

  • From here you can copy calendar feed URL to add to the calendar of your choice. How to add your calendar feed URL to your desired calendar.

Apple calendar:

  • Choose File.

  • Click New Calendar Subscription.

  • Paste your TripIt calendar feed URL.

  • Click Subscribe.

  • Make your selections.

  • Click Ok. Google calendar:

  • Open Google Calendar.

  • Click (+) next to Other calendars.

  • Choose From URL.

  • Paste your TripIt calendar feed URL

  • Select Add Calendar. iOS:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Tap Settings

  • Select Calendar Sync.

  • Tap Add TripIt to your calendar. Android:

First step:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Tap Settings.

  • Tap in the Calendar Sync section.

  • Make sure the Enable Calendar Feed and Subscribe toggle are both turned on. Second step:

  • Open the Google Calendar app.

  • Tap your profile icon (top right).

  • Select your TripIt calendar feed.

  • Tap Manage Accounts.

  • Make sure the TripIt account toggle is turned on. Please note: There are currently two steps as Google now requires non-Google accounts to be manually enabled.

Microsoft Outlook 2010:

  • Click Open Calendar.

  • Select From Internet…

  • Paste your TripIt calendar URL.

  • Click OK. Please note:

  • The refresh rate of the feed varies dependent on your calendar type (15 minutes to 24 hours).

  • The feed is read only (not editable).

  • Lotus Notes and Outlook 2013 (and later) are not supported by the TripIt calendar feed. Reset your TripIt calendar feed

If your calendar is not syncing or updating properly, you may need to refresh your TripIt calendar feed. Please keep in mind that that some calendar applications will refresh on their own (Google Calendar, for example, can take up to a day before you see changes), so you may want to wait. But if the problem doesn’t clear up, try these steps:

TripIt app:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Tap Settings.

  • Select Calendar Sync.

  • Tap Reset Calendar Feed URL.

  • Remove the old TripIt calendar feed URL and add the new one. TripIt website:

  • Click the Profile icon (top right).

  • Select Settings.

  • Click Calendar Feed (left navigation bar).

  • Click Reset Calendar Feed URL.

  • Remove the old calendar feed URL and add the new one.

You can export an individual trip to your calendar.

Exporting a trip to your calendar is most valuable when you want to add a trip to your main calendar, add a shared trip (not a traveler), or are adding trips over 90 days old.

At this time, exporting a calendar file is only available on the TripIt website.

  • From the Trips page.
  • Click More Options.

  • Download the calendar file and Save.
  • Open your calendar and go to File > Import.
  • Import your saved .ics file. Please note that this method of getting your trips to your calendar must be done on a per trip basis. It is also a one time export only so changes to your TripIt itineraries will not be reflected in TripIt events on your calendar.

Past trips on your calendar

Past trips on your calendar

Modified on Fri, Mar 20 at 11:29 AM

Your calendar feed displays your trips for the last 90 days.

For performance reasons, we do not show trips older than 90 days on your calendar feed. However, you can still view all of your past trips from within your TripIt account in both the TripIt website and TripIt app.

If you would like to have all your past trips in your calendar for record keeping purposes, one option is to download the calendar file.

Here’s how:

  • Sign into account on the TripIt website.

  • From the Trips page, click ‘More Options’ under the trip you’d like to export to your calendar.

  • Select “Export trip to calendar”. Caveats:

  • It’s a one time export so trip changes will not automatically update in your calendar.

  • It must be done on a per trip basis.

  • Also, be sure to follow the instructions exactly. We find that customers sometimes open the file instead of importing it.

Wrong times displayed in calendar

Wrong times displayed in calendar

Modified on Tue, Mar 24 at 4:18 PM

If the wrong times are displayed in your calendar, it’s usually due to two reasons:

  • You need to update the “Automatically Adjust Time Zones” setting.
  • Your travel plans are in the wrong time zone. Update Automatically adjust time zones.

We’ve found that it works best for Google calendars to have automatically adjust time zones enabled and for other calendars to have it disabled.

Here’s how:

TripIt website

  • Click the Profile icon (top right).

  • Select Settings.

  • Click Calendar Feed.

  • Update Display plans in local times. TripIt app:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Tap Settings.

  • Select Calendar Sync.

  • Update Auto Adjust Time Zones. Make sure your plans are in the correct time zone.

When a travel plan is added, we use the address field to map he time zone. If the location or time zones are not correct in nearly all cases you can fix the problem by adding at minimum the city and country. If you have it, the street addresses is preferable.

Please note:

  • If your Google calendar has automatic adjust time zones enabled, this means your calendar will convert all event times to your current time zone setting. However, as long as you change your calendar’s settings when you travel to reflect the local time zone, these items will show up in the correct time slot.
  • If you change your calendar feed’s settings it may take some time for your calendar app to update its cached feed data.
  • You can force your calendar’s cache to clear by refreshing your feed’s URL. Please view our Refresh your calendar feed help page for instructions. After refreshing, resubscribe to the new URL, wait 15 minutes, and your calendar should be updated.

Authorizing Inbox Sync

Authorizing Inbox Sync

Modified on Thu, Sep 24 at 11:36 AM

Inbox Sync automatically adds travel plans from your email inbox to your TripIt itinerary

Simply connect your Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook, Microsoft 365, Hotmail, and Yahoo inboxes with your TripIt account. Once connected, your travel plans are automatically added to your TripIt account. You never have to remember to forward an email again. Inbox Sync can be activated in the TripIt app and TripIt website.

TripIt app:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Tap your name (top of screen).

  • Tap the email address you wish to turn on Inbox Sync.

  • Enable the Inbox Sync toggle. TripIt website

  • Click your Profile icon (top right of any page).

  • Click Account.

  • Select Profile.

  • Click on the email address(es) you wish to turn on Inbox Sync.

  • Click the blue “Activate Inbox Sync” button. Need to reactivate Inbox Sync?

If Inbox Sync was deactivated for any reason and you’re having trouble turning it back on, removing TripIt’s access may help.

Google

  • Visit your Google Account’s third-party connections page.

  • Select TripIt.

  • Click Delete all connections you have with TripIt.

  • Re-enable Inbox Sync. Microsoft

  • Visit the Microsoft Account Permissions page

  • Find TripIt under “Apps and services that can access your data”.

  • Click Edit.

  • Click Remove these permissions.

  • Re-enable Inbox Sync. Yahoo

  • Visit Yahoo.com and click your profile icon (top right).

  • Select Manage your Account from the dropdown list.

  • On this new page, click on Security.

  • Under External connections, click on TripIt

  • Click Remove Access

  • Re-enable Inbox Sync. Unable to activate Inbox Sync?

Google

If you have Google’s Advanced Protection Program enabled, this feature may prevent third-party apps, including ours, from connecting with your inbox. The only workaround is to disable Advanced Protection. If you’d like to keep this feature, you can always add trips by forwarding your confirmation emails to plans@tripit.com.

Microsoft 365

Your company can block connections to applications such as TripIt. If your company has blocked TripIt, you will see a message similar to this when trying to enable Inbox Sync:

This means you will not be able to turn on Inbox Sync on your Microsoft 365 email address. Your company controls whether you see this message. You can select a different email address to add to your TripIt account or, as an alternative to Inbox Sync, you can always forward your travel confirmation emails to plans@tripit.com.

Notes

  • Inbox Sync will not give TripIt access to your email account password.
  • Inbox Sync is an optional feature you elect to authorize and is not required to use TripIt.
  • Once authorized, import is not immediate. You can expect your travel bookings to be imported within 24 hours
  • At sign up, TripIt will look for travel-related emails you received in the last 7 days, regardless of travel date (future or past).
  • Please make sure your travel confirmation emails are in your main inbox and not a folder or label.

Inbox Sync not working

Inbox Sync not working

Modified on Thu, Dec 11, 2025 at 2:00 PM

Below are a few reasons why our Inbox Sync feature may not be working for you.

  • Confirmations not in main inbox: Your confirmations are not in your main email inbox but filtered into folders automatically. We only scan your main/primary email inbox.
  • We scan several times per day: Your email inbox has not yet been scanned yet. TripIt will scan your inbox multiple times a day for travel plans (as Inbox Sync is meant as a convenience, there is no option to manually initiate an import).
  • Problem with email format: We support most travel confirmation emails; however, there are a few situations that could cause a failure and we’re unable to automatically import your confirmation email. If that could be the case, please forward your confirmation email to plans@tripit.com. If you receive a problem with the submission email, please see our Problem with your TripIt submission help page for more information.
  • Trip item location: Your restaurant confirmation is for a location within 50 miles of your home location.
  • Inbox Sync disabled: Some email providers may periodically require you to renew TripIt’s permissions. If it’s not renewed in time, Inbox Sync may be disabled. Simply reactivate Inbox Sync to restore functionality. If you experience issues, removing and re-authorizing TripIt’s access may help. Check our Authorizing Inbox Sync page for more details.

Inbox Sync posting other traveler’s trips

Modified on Tue, Mar 24 at 1:57 PM

Inbox Sync security

Inbox Sync security

Modified on Thu, Sep 3 at 2:05 PM

Security for Inbox Sync

Opt In: Inbox Sync is an optional feature users can choose to activate and can be turned off at anytime.

User secure standards: When you setup your Gmail, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Outlook, or Yahoo! Mail account, TripIt never asks for or stores your password. TripIt uses OAuth to authorize access to travel emails from your Gmail, Google Apps, Microsoft 365, Outlook, and Yahoo! Mail inbox, without ever needing your sign-in credentials. You can revoke OAuth access at any time, either from your TripIt account, or directly from Google. TripIt’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google Accounts will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Secure HTTPS for import: TripIt uses https (secure communication) to scan the headers of your inbox to look for travel plans. If TripIt finds a travel plan, it will parse the contents of the email for trip related information. If you have an existing trip with overlapping dates, TripIt will add the plan to your trip. If the plan is new, TripIt will create a new trip. All communication between your email account and TripIt is always secure.

Revoking Authorization: You can revoke OAuth access at any time, either from your TripIt account, or directly from your email provider.

SAP Concur: Personal and business trips

SAP Concur: Personal and business trips

Modified on Thu, May 21 at 12:34 PM

Whether it’s a business trip, a family vacation, or a quick weekend away, TripIt will organize all your information in seconds.

When you connect your SAP Concur and TripIt accounts, trips added in Concur Travel are automatically synced to TripIt. When trips are added in TripIt, you can customize which trips you want to share from TripIt to SAP Concur for every email address within your TripIt account.

How it works:

TripIt app:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Tap your name (top of screen).

  • Tap the email address you want to update.

  • Adjust the “Share Plans with SAP Concur” toggle. TripIt website

  • Click the Profile icon (top right)

  • Select Account.

  • Select Trip Sharing/Inner Circle.

  • Update the “Share Plans with SAP Concur’”box next to the email address you want to update. How it works

When Share Plans with Concur is turned on for a specific email address, all travel plans added to TripIt from that email address will be automatically shared with SAP Concur. You can turn automatic sharing off if you would prefer to not share your TripIt plans with SAP Concur.

Benefits of automatically sharing plans with SAP Concur

Sharing your business trips with SAP Concur saves you time by helping you get your expense reports started. Once a travel plan has been added to your TripIt account, a note will be created in Concur Expense reminding you about the expense.

Personal trips

You can enjoy the benefits of TripIt Pro beyond your business trips when you use TripIt to organize personal trips. Since you’ve connected your TripIt account to your SAP Concur profile, you’ll want to make sure personal trips don’t flow into Concur Travel.

Travel plans always importing into new trips?

Within your TripIt account, each email address is configured to ‘Share Plans with SAP Concur’ or not. When you forward a confirmation email to plans@tripit.com from an email address set to automatically share with SAP Concur, it will only import into an existing trip (with overlapping dates) which is also set to ‘Share with SAP Concur’, otherwise a new trip is created. The same logic applies to an email address set to ‘Not Share with SAP Concur’, it will not import into an existing trip which is ‘Shared with SAP Concur.’ You can update your email settings to avoid this in the future.

How to manually share a trip with SAP Concur

Even if you’ve turned off the option to Share with SAP Concur, you can still share an individual business trip with your SAP Concur account.

TripIt app:

  • Select the trip you want to share with Concur.

  • Tap the 3 dots (top right).

  • Tap Edit Details.

  • Enable the “Share with SAP Concur” toggle

  • Tap Save. TripIt website:

  • Select the trip you want to share with Concur.

  • Click Edit Trip Info (below the trip name).

  • Check the “Share with SAP Concur” box.

  • Click Save. How to manually create a trip

When you manually add a trip in TripIt, you can choose whether to share the trip with SAP Concur by turning on or off the ‘Share with SAP Concur’ toggle.

If personal travel plans end up in Concur Travel?

If a personal trip was shared with your SAP Concur account, you can remove it at any time by following these instructions:

  • Sign in to concursolutions.com.
  • Select the Travel tab.
  • Click Trip Library.
  • Find the trip you would like to remove.
  • Click Remove.

Glossary of TripIt Pro flight alerts

Modified on Tue, Feb 18, 2025 at 2:47 PM

TripIt Pro members can receive flight status notifications via email, push and SMS.

Here is what each notification means:

  • Arrival: Tells you what time your flight arrived at its destination and will display your baggage claim information if available.
  • Canceled: Your flight has been canceled. You should contact your airline or travel vendor to reschedule.
  • Check-in Reminder: A reminder to check into your flight is sent 24 hours prior to departure.
  • Connection at risk: There may not be enough time to catch your connecting flight.
  • Connection summary: Tells you how much time you have to get to your connecting flight.
  • Delayed: Your flight will depart at a later time than originally scheduled.
  • Departure summary: Tells you what time your flight is departing its origin city and is sent 4 hours prior to departure.
  • Gate change: Your flight is departing or arriving at a new gate number.
  • Possible delay: There is a chance your flight is delayed so you should check with the airline.
  • Possible pull-in: There is a chance your flight is departing earlier than the delayed time alert so you should verify with the airline.
  • Pulled-in: When a delay alert was initially sent but your flight is now departing earlier than the delayed time alert or closer to the original departure time.

How to reduce your carbon footprint

How to reduce your carbon footprint

Modified on Thu, Apr 17, 2025 at 9:33 AM

How can I limit my impact on the environment when I travel?

There are plenty of steps you can take to limit your environmental impact before, during, and after your travels.

Before traveling:

  • Select your destination wisely:
    • Some destinations are more sustainable than others. Exploring lesser-known places or traveling during less busy times makes less of an impact on local ecology, increases traffic to businesses that aren’t located in popular tourist areas, and helps strengthen and support a sense of community for locals.
    • Look up a location’s walkability score. Doing so will help you choose accommodations that are within walking distance to nearby amenities such as restaurants, shops, and cafés. You might also want to consider the distance to public transit stops as well as bike-share and electric scooter-share pick-up points, as these are all eco-friendly options for getting around your destination.
  • Reconsider when you travel:
    • Just like choosing where to travel, choosing when to travel can have a big impact too. Traveling during off-peak seasons means fewer tourists and lower prices, more flexibility and options, and it’s better for destinations that are historically overrun during the busy season. You’re also more likely to experience a place like the locals do—with only a few more people than the locals, too.
  • Be intentional about your accommodations:
    • Book an accommodation with a green building certification. Third-party certifications, such as Leadership in Energy Efficiency and Design (LEED), provide travelers with the peace of mind that the building—whether it be a hotel, house, apartment building, or any form of accommodation in between—meets the standards for sustainable and renewable energy initiatives.
    • Look for vacation rentals that offer or support eco-friendly practices, such as using green cleaning products, providing recycling or composting, encouraging guests to use public transportation or providing bicycles, or utilizing green energy (e.g., solar panels).
  • Plan what you pack:
    • Try not to overdo it! Less weight on the airplane equals less gas used to get from point A to point B.
    • Pack sustainable products: such as biodegradable soap, reef-safe sunscreen, reusable bottles, tote bags to carry purchased items, etc.
    • All sunscreens are not created equally. If you’re traveling to a tropical area, consider purchasing reef-safe sunscreen that is specially made to take less of a toll on the local ecosystems. While traveling:
  • Shopping:
    • Consider the souvenirs you purchase. Will it have lasting use, or will it end up in the landfill in a year’s time? Instead of buying a cheap key chain, shot glass, or trinket, invest in higher quality products that will last.
    • Shopping in locally owned stores will not only help curb your climate impact by creating less need for products to be shipped around the world but will also help support the local economy.
    • Support local businesses that partake in sustainable practices. Eat at restaurants that compost their food scraps. Check out local farmers markets for freshly grown and locally sourced items.
  • Getting around:
    • Walk or take public transportation. Not only will walking lessen your carbon footprint, it will also help you become more familiar with the destination you are visiting.
  • Activities:
    • Seek out opportunities to support your destination’s sustainable tourism efforts. For example, choose to support eco-conscious tour companies that employ local, full-time staff and highlight local businesses along your tour.

Connection at risk warning

Steps to find coordinates:

  • Right-click a place or area on the map.

  • This will open up a pop-up window with the coordinates. Acceptable formats for coordinates:

  • Degrees, minutes and seconds (DMS): 41° 24’ 12.1674”, 2° 10’ 26.508”

  • Decimal degrees (DD): 41.40338, 2.17403 Steps to find Plus Code:

  • Enter the location or coordinates into Google Maps

  • In the left-hand panel, you’ll see a string of numbers and letters. This is the Plus Code.

  • Copy the code and Paste it into your trip item’s Address field

Neighborhood safety scores

Modified on Tue, Mar 24 at 1:52 PM

TripIt provides hyperlocal safety scores for neighborhoods around the world.

Neighborhood Safety Scores represent risk on a 1–100-point scale, from lowest to highest. A composite score represents the overall risk for a location. Separate scores are available for daytime and nighttime. Travelers can find safety scores for upcoming trips (not available for past trips) for their lodging, rental car, restaurant, train station, ferry terminal, shuttle and activity locations in the TripIt app.

Neighborhood Safety Scores can also be broken down into categories, such as:

  • Overall safety: objective measure of the safety quality at a location reflecting a average of the sub-categories below:

    • Health and medical: environmental pollution and contamination, illnesses, regulation of sanitary conditions, and access to medical care
    • Physical harm: including muggings, drug activity, gang prevalence, and police presence
    • Women’s safety: including verbal harassment, physical assault, violence directed against women, and adequacy of women’s support facilities

  • Basic freedoms: including political unrest, limited political rights, and incidences of riots and protests

  • Theft: including petty theft, pickpocketing, burglaries, auto theft, and grand theft

  • LGBTQ safety: including likelihood of harm or discrimination against LGBTQ persons or groups and level of caution required at location. How to get started:

  • Select your trip in the TripIt app.

  • Tap a lodging, rental car, restaurant or activity within your itinerary.

  • You’ll see the safety score next to ‘Location Safety’ and ‘Neighborhood Info’ (rated from lowest risk to highest risk).

  • Tap the safety score link to view additional details.

  • Tap ‘Show risk scale’ to display what each color and score means. Please note: A Neighborhood Safety Score will not display if data isn’t available for a certain location.

Neighborhood Safety Scores are powered by our partner GeoSure, which analyzes metadata captured from hundreds of trusted sources to determine destination safety. This data includes statistics from the World Health Organization, Interpol, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, country and city crime statistics, and more. GeoSure also takes into account user reports about on-the-ground safety conditions. Through its proprietary algorithm that combines hundreds of variables specific to each neighborhood, GeoSure produces standardized, customized safety metrics.

What is Personal Risk Level?

You can set your preferred level of personal risk to your choice of lowest, low, average, high, and highest risk. When the overall nighttime risk level of a location is above your personal risk level, you will see an indicator in the trip itinerary — in the trip summary alongside the plan and in the detail screen of the plan. You can adjust your personal risk level, and you can turn this functionality on and off in Profile.

How to get started:

Travel Documents

Travel Documents

Modified on Tue, Apr 28 at 10:25 AM

Store key travel document information in your TripIt account.

Keep your travel document information in one place so it’s easily accessible while traveling. This feature is available in the TripIt app.

Add a travel document

  • Tap Account (bottom right).
  • Tap Travel Documents.
  • Tap (+) button to add a new document. Edit or delete a travel document

Android

  • Long press the travel document.

  • Edit or delete the travel document. iOS

  • Tap the 3 dots (right of travel document)

  • Edit or delete the travel document. Travel Document options include Passport, Driver License, US Permanent Resident Card, Global Entry, US Military ID, US Re-entry Permit, NEXUS, TSA Pre, CLEAR, and SENTRI. There is also a generic template (Other) to place additional document information.

Benefits:

  • All important travel document details are at your fingertips.
  • Allows you to input and store information for yourself and your fellow travelers, so everyone’s travel information is in one place.
  • TripIt encrypts all sensitive information stored on our servers. Instantly add a passport by scanning it with your camera

You can easily add a passport to Travel Documents by scanning it with your camera. When adding to Travel Documents, simply choose the “Capture with Camera” option.

Note: iOS users can also add a US-state driver license, US State ID or US Re-Entry this way.

TripIt Pro Passport Renewal Reminder

TripIt Pro members can receive reminders before their passport expires. Please view our help page for more information.

Who else can view your travel document information?

No one else can see your Travel Document information including your Inner Circle (Pro feature). Even if you have shared your trip with someone on TripIt, they do not have access to this information.

Please note: First time access or apps that have been signed out since last accessing will require a PIN, Face recognition (Android) or Face ID (iOS). Please see our help page for additional information. We recommend never storing financial data and passwords.

Travel Documents pin

Travel Documents pin

Modified on Sun, Mar 22 at 4:52 PM

Accessing your Traveler Documents in the TripIt app requires a PIN, Face recognition (Android) or Face ID (iOS).

Anything you add to Travel Documents, such as passport and driver license numbers are encrypted and protected behind a 4-digit PIN, Face recognition (Android) or Face ID (iOS)

How to set up:

  • Tap Account (bottom right).

  • Tap “Travel documents”.

  • You will be prompted to enter your TripIt password before you can set up your PIN or enable Face ID/Face recognition. Please note:

  • Trivial PINs are not allowed (e.g. 1111, 1234, 2222, 4321, etc.). You will also be asked to enter you PIN twice to ensure the right entry.

  • You will be able to access your travel documents for as long as you are active in this section. If you leave the travel documents section for 15 minutes or force quit the TripIt app, you will be required to re-enter your PIN to re-access.

  • For safety and security reasons, a PIN is tied to only one device for your TripIt account. When you use a different device to access Travel Documents for the first time, you will be required to set up a PIN for that device. You may, however, use the same PIN for a different device. When you sign out of the TripIt app, the PIN is deleted. Because we leave you signed in to the app when you launch in or out of TripIt, your PIN will not expire automatically. If you decide to sign out of the TripIt app, you will need to set up a new PIN when signing in and accessing travel documents or travel contacts again.

Reset PIN

You can reset your PIN by clicking on the Forgot PIN? link on the PIN entry page. You will also be asked to reset your PIN after 3 unsuccessful attempts.

Unfiled Items

Unfiled Items

Modified on Mon, May 11 at 8:55 AM

Travel plans that aren’t associated with a trip are placed in the Unfiled Items section of your account.

Unfiled Items are accessible on the TripIt website and TripIt app. You can move an unfiled item to an existing or new trip at any time.

Examples of Unfiled Items:

  • Your confirmation email or other information sent to plans@tripit.com is not processed.
  • You have unchecked the Put new plans into trips box. If unchecked, your new travel plans will go into your Unfiled items folder on the Trips page.
  • You’ve moved a plan from a trip to Unfiled Items. Move an unfiled Item into a trip

TripIt website.

  • Click Unfiled Items from the Trips page.

  • Click the three dots (right of plan).

  • Click Move.

  • Move to an existing or new trip.

  • Select the plan type (flight, lodging, etc.).

  • Enter the plan details (contents from original email can be located at the bottom of page).

  • Click Save. TripIt app:

  • Tap Unfiled Items (bottom of screen).

  • Select the plan you want to move into a trip.

  • Tap “Create Plan”.

  • Select the plan type.

  • Enter the plan details.

  • Tap Save.

  • Select to move this plan to an existing or new trip. Delete an unfiled Item:

TripIt website:

  • Click Unfiled Items from the Trips page.
  • From here, you can choose to delete all of your unfiled items or specific ones. Android: Hold down on the specific unfiled item to navigate to the delete option.

iOS: Swipe to the left of the specific unfiled item to navigate to the delete option.

Using Point Tracker

Using Point Tracker

Modified on Tue, Mar 24 at 10:20 AM

TripIt Pro’s Point Tracker keeps track of your award programs.

We’ll track your total miles or points and any other details, such as status level, we can find for that program. Point Tracker currently supports more than 150 reward programs, including air, hotel, transportation, parking, dining, and credit card rewards. If you don’t see your favorite program, let us know!

TripIt website.

  • Click the profile icon (top right).

  • Click Account.

  • Click Point Tracker.

  • Enter your award program(s). TripIt app:

  • Tap the Pro tab (bottom of screen)

  • Select Point Tracker.

  • Enter your award program(s). If your preferred program is not currently available in Point Tracker, you can manually add it in the TripIt app by selecting ‘Other’.

American, Delta, Southwest and United:

To track your balances for the American, Delta, Southwest and United, just forward the reward-program summary emails you receive from those airlines to points@tripit.com, much like you can forward your travel plans to plans@tripit.com. We’ll update Point Tracker with your latest balance so you can track all your miles and points in one place.

  • Be sure to send us only your monthly or quarterly program statements. Typically those emails will include the words Summary, Statement, or Report in the subject line. If you currently do not receive a monthly or quarterly email summary you can request to do so in the email settings/subscriptions area of the AA, DL, WN and UA websites.
  • Point Tracker does not support other emails from your airline such as special promotions (which may also include your account balance) or non-original email formats (e.g. copy and paste into email, hand typed itinerary, printed from Web browser, scans).
  • If you have Inbox Sync enabled, it will automatically add these programs to Point Tracker. Track all of your and your friends and family reward accounts.

Point Tracker allows you to add up to 10 individual accounts in each reward program, so you can track your personal, business, and family accounts together. To help keep your accounts distinct, you can give each account a nickname, like Personal Avis account or Nick’s Hyatt points. To add a nickname for your account, just visit Point Tracker and click the Edit link next to the account you want to update.

Weather display

Weather display

Modified on Wed, May 13 at 10:23 AM

Weather forecasts can be viewed in the TripIt app

Real-time weather forecasts will be displayed with other travel details within 10 days of the current date in the TripIt app.. Historical weather data will be shown for your destination more than 10 days out. TripIt uses IBM technology to provide AI-derived insights from The Weather Channel to help travelers make more informed choices before and during their trips.

Key info:

  • Within ten days of your travel plan, you’ll see forecasted high and low temperatures for that day, plus an icon that indicates the weather conditions, such as cloudy, thunderstorms, windy, etc.

  • More than ten days out, your travel plan will show historical averages for the high and low temperature. Note that historical average weather data is not the same as a weather forecast, which can vary greatly from historical conditions.

  • Weather will display in Fahrenheit or Celsius based on your TripIt Settings. If this needs to be updated, please view our help page for instructions.

  • Weather forecasts on our mobile apps are currently available for lodging, restaurants, car rentals and activities with an address/location.

  • Weather is not displayed for past trips. For Pro users:

  • Pro check-in emails will include a 5-hour weather forecast for the departure location and a 5-day weather forecast for the arrival location.

  • Pro departure summary emails include a 5-day weather forecast for the arrival location. Please note: Weather is not yet offered on our website.
