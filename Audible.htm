Apply erotica filter

apply-adult-content-filters

The erotica filter helps to hide any titles that Audible has categorized as erotica from showing up in your search results and recommendations. As long as you’re signed into your account and you’ve turned the filter on, this content will be hidden from your search results and recommendations.

Note:

  • Even with the filter turned on, you may still see some content in your search results or recommended to you that you consider erotica. No filter is 100% accurate, and opinions may vary on whether content is erotica.
  • By default, the erotica filter is turned off.

How do I apply erotica filters to my search results and recommendations?

On desktop
  1. Go to Account details.
  2. Select Settings.
  3. Select the checkbox for the Erotica filter.
On mobile web
  1. Select your username in the site’s top navigation.
  2. Select Update settings.
  3. Select the checkbox for the Erotica filter.
In the app
  1. Go to your Profile tab.
  2. Select the Settings gear icon.
  3. Select Discovery preferences.
  4. Toggle the Erotica filter on.

To provide feedback on the erotica filter or to report an issue, please contact us. Note: Please ensure you’re logged into your Audible account to contact us.

How can I browse chapters or podcast episodes in the player?

You can browse chapters or podcast episodes right from your player screen in the app or on your Cloud Player, or you can use Alexa voice commands to control Audible with any Alexa device.

In the app player
  1. In the player, select the Chaptersmenu icon.
  2. Select the chapter or episode you want to hear.
On Cloud Player
  1. In the player, select the icon labeled ChaptersorEpisodes.
  2. Select the chapter or episode you want to hear.

How can I browse audiobook chapters or podcast episodes on my Fire TV?

You can browse audiobook chapters or podcast episodes with the buttons on your paired Fire TV remote.

Browse with your remote
  1. Next to the title you want to hear, select Listen Now.
  2. When the player is open, select the Menu button on your remote.
  3. Select Chapters.
  4. Use the up and down buttons on the directional pad of your paired remote to navigate to a specific chapter.
Browse with Alexa voice commands
  1. Press the microphone button on your remote.
  2. Try one of the following voice commands:
  • “Alexa, next [chapter or episode].”
  • “Alexa, previous [chapter or episode].”
  • “Alexa, go to [chapter or episode] number [7].”
  • “Alexa, go to last [chapter or episode].”

How can I browse audiobook chapters or podcast episodes on my Fire Tablet?

You can browse chapters or podcast episodes right from your player screen in the app.

In the app player
  1. In the player, select the Chapters menu icon.
  2. Select the chapter or episode you want to hear.

Cancel membership

cancel-membership

You can unsubscribe our membership by visiting Account details on the Audible site. Please note: Deleting the Audible app will not cancel a membership.

You can cancel your membership on the Audible website anytime. You’ll still have access to any titles you bought with credits, credit card, or debit card. If you have credits but don’t want to lose them when you cancel, you may be eligible to pause your membership.

Note: There are different steps for canceling if you are billed through the App Store or Google Play Store.

How do I cancel my membership on the Audible site?

On desktop
  1. Select your username from the Audible desktop site’s top navigation.
  2. Select Account details.
  3. Select the Cancel membership link.
  4. You’ll be asked to confirm cancellation. Select Continue to cancel until you reach the cancel confirmation page.

Note: After you cancel, you’ll get an email confirmation. Your membership status on your Account details page will also reflect this change.

On mobile web
  1. Select the ≡ main menu (hamburger button).
  2. Select your account.
  3. Select Cancel membership.

Note: After you cancel, you’ll get an email confirmation. Your membership status on your Account details page will also reflect this change.

How do I cancel my Audible membership billed through Apple or Google?

Audible memberships billed through Apple or Google cannot be canceled through the Audible site and must be canceled through the App Store and/or Google Play Store. For specific instructions to cancel, view the following articles:

  • Manage App Store Subscription
  • Manage Google Play Store Subscription

What happens if I cancel?

At the end of your final billing cycle, Audible Premium members will lose access to all unused credits, member benefits, including exclusive discounts and included titles in the Plus Catalog.

Note: Credits received through an App Store membership or a Google Play Store membership do not expire and will remain in your account after cancellation. Extra credits purchased in the App Store and Google Play Store do not expire.

Can I still access my Library when I cancel?

You can listen to titles you purchased with a credit, credit card, or debit card anytime. They’re yours to keep and will always be available in your Library whether you’re a current member or not.

You can download titles you’ve purchased as many times as you want, even if you cancel your membership. However, you’ll lose access to any included titles you’ve downloaded that are in the Plus Catalog. Any titles that were included in the Plus Catalog will be locked.

Notes:

  • You may also continue to use your account to purchase new titles without a membership.
  • You can purchase locked titles if you’d like to own them or become a member again to regain access. If no purchase options are available, we no longer carry the title.

Do I keep my credits when I cancel?

No. Your unused credits will be lost along with your other membership benefits. Any title you get with a credit is yours to keep, though, so use your credits before the end of your final billing period.

If you switch to the Audible Plus plan, you will retain all the credits you have accrued. These credits will retain their expiration date.

Note: Credits received through an App Store membership or an Audible Google Play membership do not expire and will remain in your account after cancellation. Extra credits purchased in the iOS and Google Play apps do not expire.

Can I purchase titles after I cancel?

Yes, you can purchase titles with or without an Audible membership. Any title you purchase is yours to keep, regardless of your membership status.

Change your marketplace

change-your-marketplace

Your Audible marketplace determines the titles, pricing, and membership plans available to you. You can change your marketplace at any time to browse content and see pricing tailored to your country.

Note: Your language preference and your marketplace are separate settings. Changing your marketplace does not automatically change the language you see on Audible. To update your language, visit Set your language preference.

How do I change my marketplace?

You can change your marketplace from the country selector on Audible.

Please note that you can only switch between countries within your marketplace region — to move to a different region (e.g., from Germany to France), please contact us.

On desktop:

  1. Go to the Audible site.
  2. Select the country and currency indicator in the top navigation (it shows your country’s flag and currency code).
  3. Select Change marketplace.
  4. Choose your preferred country from the list.
  5. Select Update.

Notes:

  • If you have an active Audible membership, your membership plan will be updated for your selected country.
  • To complete a marketplace change, you’ll need to update your payment method with a valid billing address in your selected country.

On mobile web:

  1. Select the main menu (three horizontal lines).
  2. Select the country and currency indicator (it shows your country’s flag and currency code).
  3. Choose your preferred country from the list.
  4. Select Update.

In the app:

  1. Go to your Profile.
  2. Select the Settings gear icon.
  3. Select Marketplace.
  4. Choose your preferred country.
  5. Confirm your selection.

What happens to my membership when I change my marketplace?

If you’re a current Audible member, here’s what to expect:

Your membership plan will be updated. You’ll be enrolled in the equivalent plan available in your new marketplace. Your new billing amount will reflect local pricing in the currency of your new country.

The change takes effect on your next billing cycle. Your current plan remains active until your next renewal date. At that point, your new marketplace plan and pricing will begin.

Your credits carry over. Any unused credits in your account will remain available and can be used before they expire, regardless of your marketplace.

Note: Coupon balances cannot be transferred across marketplaces.

What happens to my library?

Your purchased titles stay in your library and remain accessible after you change your marketplace. However, some titles may become unavailable for playback if they are geo-restricted in your new country or region. If this happens, you’ll see a message letting you know the title isn’t available in your area.

What do I need to complete a marketplace change?

To complete a marketplace change, you’ll need:

  • A billing address in the country you’re switching to.
  • A payment method issued in that country.

If you don’t have a local payment method or billing address, you can still browse content in another marketplace, but you won’t be able to complete the switch or sign up for a membership there.

Note: If your Audible membership is managed through the App Store or Google Play Store, you need to cancel your current subscription and re-subscribe in your new country. For cancellation steps, visit Manage iOS App Store subscription or Manage Google Play Store subscription.

How do I pause my Audible membership billed via the Apple App Store?

Pauses which can be activated on Audible memberships billed via the Apple App store always last exactly 30 days; it is not possible to resume normal membership before the 30 days have passed. Follow these steps to pause your Audible membership billed via the Apple App Store:

  1. Open the Audible App for iOS
  2. Select the Profile tab.
  3. Go to the Membership tab and select Manage.
  4. Select Pause Membership.
  5. Confirm your selection.

Note:

  • If you are an Audible Standard member billed through the App Store, your pause starts on your next billing date, giving you time to use your current monthly listen before the pause begins.

How do I cancel my Audible subscription in the App Store?

  1. Open your App Store or iOS deviceSettings.
  2. Selectyour name.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Select Audible.
  5. Select Cancel Subscription.
  6. Select Confirm.

Note: If you don’t see “Cancel Subscription,” your membership is already canceled and won’t be renewed.

How do I edit my Audible subscription payment method?

Your App Store payment method applies to all App Store subscriptions and not just for Audible, but you can change it at any time.

  1. Open your App Store or iOS deviceSettings.
  2. Selectyour name.
  3. Select Payment & shipping.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions to add or edit your payment method.

How do I switch my Audible subscription in the App Store?

  1. Open your App Store or iOS deviceSettings.
  2. Selectyour name.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Select Audible.
  5. Select See all plans.
  6. Choose a plan.
  7. Confirm your new subscription.

What happens after I cancel my App Store Audible subscription?

You can still enjoy your Audible membership for the time that you’ve already paid. For example, if you subscribe to an annual membership on January 1 for $159.99 and decide to cancel your membership on June 1:

  • You’ll have access to all of your member benefits until December 31.
  • You won’t be charged for another annual subscription on the following January 1.

Manage Two-Step Verification

manage-two-step-verification

Amazon’s Two-Step Verification is a security feature that adds a second layer of account authentication when signing in to your favorite Amazon sites like Audible.

Note: If you sign in with a username (ex: john123), you can’t enable Two-Step Verification.

In this article:

  • How do I turn on Two-Step Verification on my account?
  • How do I turn off Two-Step Verification?
  • How do I recover my account if I lose access to my Two-Step verification method?
  • How do I recover my account if Two-Step Verification fails?

How do I turn on Two-Step Verification on my account?

  1. Go to your Amazon account.
  2. Select Login & security.
  3. Select Edit beside Two-Step Verification (2SV) Settings.
  4. Select Get Started.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions.

How do I turn off Two-step Verification?

  1. Go to your Amazon account.
  2. Select Login & security.
  3. Select Edit beside Two-Step Verification (2SV) Settings.
  4. Select Disable beside Two-Step Verification.
  5. Enter the code sent to your phone number, or generated through the authenticator app.
  6. Select Verify code.

How do I recover my account if I lose access to my Two-Step Verification method?

If you don’t have access to your phone number, authenticator app, or backup credentials for Two-Step Verification, you can:

  1. Sign in from a trusted device on your Amazon account to bypass the Two-Step Verification code.
  2. Go to the Amazon.com Contact Us page to recover your Two-Step Verification information.

How do I recover my account if Two-Step Verification fails?

To regain access to your account:

  1. Go to Two-Step Verification Account Recovery.
  2. Follow instructions to upload your ID document.
  3. Ensure your name, address, and issuing authority are visible.
  4. Cover any sensitive information like account or ID numbers.

Note: Verification takes 1-2 days. We’ll email you when Two-Step Verification is disabled. You can then access your account with just your password.

How do I add a new payment method?

You can add a new payment method on the Audible site.

  1. Select your username in the site’s top navigation.
  2. Go to Account details.
  3. Select Payment.
  4. Select Add a card.
  5. Enter your credit card information in the spaces provided.
  6. Select Add a new payment.

Note:If your payment method is entered incorrectly 3 times, you may temporarily be prevented from adding a new payment method. If this occurs, you will be able to try again after approximately 30 minutes.

How do I edit my payment method?

You can add a new payment method on the Audible site.

  1. Select your username in the site’s top navigation.
  2. Go to Account details.
  3. Select Payment.
  4. Select Edit next to the card you’d like to update.
  5. Edit the information in the open text fields.
  6. Select Save.

Note: If you need to change the credit card numbers, remove the card first, then re-add it.

How do I set a credit card as my default payment method?

  1. Select your username in the site’s top navigation.
  2. Go to Account details.
  3. Select Payment.
  4. Select your card in theDefault column.

Note: If the default card on file can’t be charged, any card currently in your Amazon account will be used as a backup card.

How do I manage my backup payment method?

Backup payment methods allow us to help you avoid disruptions of your membership if there’s a problem with your default payment method. Changes you make to these settings will impact Audible and any other Amazon products and services.

  1. In your Amazon account, go to Your Payments.
  2. In the top navigation, select Settings.
  3. Select Manage back-up payment method.
  4. From here, you can do several things:
  • Toggle backup payment methods on/off.
  • Review and update the payment methods on file using the checkboxes.

How do I delete a credit card?

To avoid lapses in your membership, you can’t delete a credit card from the Audible website without having another card on file. Additionally, if a credit card is deleted from Audible, it will also be deleted from your Amazon account.

  1. Select your username in the site navigation.
  2. Go to Account details.
  3. Select Payment.
  4. Select Delete card next to the card you want to delete.
  5. Select Delete card to confirm.

Note: If the card is currently set for your Audible membership, you must first select or add a different card as your default payment method before trying to remove your credit card.

What payment methods does Audible accept?

Audible accepts the following payment methods:

  • Visa
  • MasterCard
  • American Express
  • Discover
  • Prepaid debit cards
  • Amazon Store Card
  • Amazon Secured Private Label Credit Card (PLCC)
  • Amazon Gift Cards (only for US purchasing Audible content on Amazon.com or the Amazon app)

We don’t support any other method of payment, including PayPal.

Listen in Dolby Atmos

listen-in-dolby-atmos

Unlike stereo where sound is panned left to right, Dolby Atmos provides a true spatial sound experience. Sounds are individually placed and moved in three-dimensional space, giving you the feeling that the sound is all around you, even above and behind you.

Note: Playback in Dolby Atmos results in larger file sizes. If you’re streaming, using Wi-Fi is the way to go to use less data. If you’re downloading a Dolby Atmos title, make sure you have plenty of space on your device.

How do I listen to Audible titles in Dolby Atmos?

You can listen to any title marked with the Dolby Atmos badge. Spatial audio is automatically turned on in the Audible app for devices that support Dolby Atmos playback.

To check if spatial audio is turned on:

  1. Go to your Profile.
  2. Select the Settings gear icon.
  3. Select Data & storage.
  4. Check that the Spatial audio is toggled on.

Note: If you want to listen to a title you’ve already downloaded in spatial audio, you must delete and re-download that title.

Which devices are compatible with Dolby Atmos?

Your spatial audio setting is on by default in the Audible app for iOS. You’ll need to use a Dolby Atmos-compatible device and headphones when listening.

iOS devices

Your Apple device must be updated to iOS 15.1 or later and be connected to a set of supported headphones unless otherwise stated.

  • iPhone XS or later (via the device’s built-in speaker)
  • iPhone 7 or later
  • iPad Pro 12.9-inch (3rd generation or later)
  • iPad Pro 11-inch
  • iPad Air (3rd generation or later)
  • iPad (6th generation or later)
  • iPad mini (5th generation or later)

Note: Supported headphones include AirPods (3rd generation), AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, or Beats Fit Pro. You can control spatial audio and head tracking from your iOS Control Center.

Android devices

Some Android devices do not support Dolby Atmos at this time. No specific headphones are required.

To see if your Android device supports Dolby Atmos playback:

  1. Go to your Profile.
  2. Select the Settings gear icon.
  3. Select Data & storage.
  4. Check if the Spatial Audio toggle is available and turned on.

Notes:

  • If the toggle is available and turned on, your device supports Dolby Atmos playback. If the toggle is missing or grayed out, your device does not currently support Audible playback with Dolby Atmos’.
  • Google Pixel devices are not supported at this time. In January 2023, Google announced its spatial audio feature for Pixel 6, Pixel 6 Pro, Pixel 7, and Pixel 7 Pro devices running Android 13. This spatializes Dolby Digital Plus, but it does not support Dolby Atmos—which is Audible’s spatial audio offering.
Fire Tablets

Dolby Atmos playback on Audible is supported on Amazon Kindle Fire HCX 8.9 via connected headphones and on the Amazon Fire HD 10.

Note: Fire Tablets running Fire OS 7 no longer support Dolby Atmos playback. Your audiobooks will still play in stereo. Only the Dolby Atmos experience has been removed.

Audible Cloud Player
  • For desktop, spatial audio playback is supported on MacOS 10.12+ with Safari 10+.
  • For mobile web, spatial audio playback is supported on iOS 11+ with Safari 9+.

Note: The spatial audio setting is only turned on for iPhones in the 7 series or later with iOS version 15.1 or later.

What titles are available in Dolby Atmos?

All Audible titles marked with a Dolby Atmos badge are available in Dolby Atmos. You’ll also see a Dolby Atmos badge in the app player when you’re playing a Dolby Atmos title.

How do I listen to a title I already own in Dolby Atmos?

If you’d like to download the Dolby Atmos version of a title you own, you’ll need to remove the old version from your device first. Once removed, you can re-download it to experience the Dolby Atmos version. You won’t lose your listening position when you do this.

Are Dolby Atmos titles available in stereo?

If your device supports Dolby Atmos but you prefer playback in stereo, you’ll need to perform the following steps:

Step 1: Turn spatial audio off
  1. Go to your Profile.
  2. Select the Settings gear icon.
  3. Select Data & storage.
  4. Toggle spatial audio off.
Step 2: Re-download your title
  1. Go to your Library.
  2. Select your title’s 3-dot menu.
  3. Select Remove from device.
  4. Once removed, re-download the title.

Will Audible titles in Dolby Atmos play on Apple Podcast or Amazon Music?

No, Audible titles in Dolby Atmos are only available through the Audible app at this time.

Use Page Sync

use-page-sync

Page Sync lets you seamlessly switch between reading your physical book and listening to the audiobook. With the corresponding audiobook open in the Audible app, simply scan a page with your phone’s camera, and your audiobook automatically jumps to that exact spot—no manual searching needed.

In this article:

  • What is Page Sync?
  • How do I use Page Sync?
  • Which titles work with Page Sync?
  • Troubleshooting

What is Page Sync?

Page Sync uses your phone’s camera to recognize text on the page you’re reading, then automatically matches your audiobook to that exact spot.

How do I use Page Sync?

  1. Open  the audiobook in the Audible app that corresponds to the physical book you want to scan.
  2. Select the 3-dot More menu.
  3. Select Page Sync.
  4. Grant camera permission (first-time only).
  5. Hold your camera over any page of text in your physical book.
  6. The app automatically captures and recognizes the text.
  7. Your audiobook jumps to the matching position.

Note: Keep the page flat and well-lit for best results. Make sure the text is fully visible in your camera frame. The app provides real-time feedback on text readability.

Which titles work with Page Sync?

Page Sync is available for certain English-language audiobooks Your audiobook doesn’t need to be downloaded to use this feature. You can access Page Sync from the player overflow menu.

Not supported:

  • Podcasts
  • Audible Originals without book equivalents
  • Kids Profiles
  • Children’s picture books

Note: Page Sync works with any edition of your physical book as long as the text matches the audiobook.

Troubleshooting

If Page Sync isn’t working:

  • Check your internet connection (Page Sync requires an active internet connection)
  • Ensure camera permissions are enabled in your device settings
  • Make sure the text is fully visible in your camera frame
  • Improve lighting and keep the page flat

Note: Different book editions may have different page numbers. Page Sync works best when your physical book edition closely matches the audiobook version.

Create a Kids Profile

create-a-kids-profile

There’s no minimum age requirement for a Kids Profile and anyone with an Audible account with an Amazon login can create a single Kids Profile to share titles for kids and featured editorial lists with children right from their Library—regardless of membership and as long as you, as the account holder, have access to the titles.

This will allow kids to listen to only the titles you choose. You can share titles you’ve purchased with cash or credit as well as any titles for kids included in your membership, and there’s no limit to how many titles you can share.

Kids Profile is only available in the Audible app for iOS and Android and on the Audible website. If you are using Audible on Fire Tablet, Kindle or Echo device, please create a Child account within Amazon Family Library.

Notes:

  • Your child only has access to titles you directly share with them.
  • Some titles are restricted from sharing, such as audiobooks with erotica content, podcasts with ads and titles with Virtual Voice narration.
  • Kids Profiles don’t get access to credits or selections. You’ll need to purchase titles with your credits first, then share it to your Kids Profile.

What is a Kids Profile?

Kids Profile is a new feature for Audible customers that helps parents share their love of stories with their children right from the Audible app and the Audible website. With access to only those titles and lists that you choose to share, your child can easily listen from the Audible app whenever and wherever they choose.

How do I create a Kids Profile?

You can create a Kids Profile from the Profile tab in the Audible app or on the Audible site from your Account details.

In the app
  • Go to your Profile.
  • Select Create a Kids Profile.
On desktop
  • Go to the Audible site and sign in.
  • Select Account details.
  • Select Profiles.
  • Select Create a Kids Profile.

If you want to learn more about who to log into your Kid Profile after it was created please visit Listen with a Kids Profile.

Can I create more than one Kids profile?

No. You can only create 1 Kids Profile per Amazon Family or Audible account. If you’re part of an Amazon Family and a Kids Profile has already been created for a member of your Amazon Family, you won’t be able to create a new one until the existing profile is deleted.

Can multiple kids listen to my Kids Profile?

Yes. You can create 1 Kids Profile per account that can be accessed on multiple devices. Multiple children can use this same Kids Profile simultaneously without interrupting each other’s listening progress.

Why was a Kids Profile automatically created on my account?

Audible may have received notice that you are, or the user of your device is, under the age of 18. New legislation requires online services to provide enhanced protection for users under 18. If the app store account accessing the Audible app is registered to a child under 18, the Audible app experience may now default to the Kids Profile in certain US states. The Kids Profile is a kid-friendly space where you can share only the titles you choose from your Library. You can continue to access your Audible account and manage any titles you’d like to share with your Kids Profile from the Audible app on your personal device or the Audible site.

For questions on how the app store for your device applies age signals, please contact your app store customer service.

Understand error codes

understand-error-codes

If you see an error code while using Audible, it can help you identify and resolve the issue quickly. Error codes appear when there’s a problem with playback or downloading.

In this article

  • Where you’ll see error codes
  • Playback error codes
  • Download error codes

Where you’ll see error codes

Error codes appear as red banner notifications at the bottom of your screen in the Audible app. The message shows a description of the issue first, followed by the error code in parentheses—for example, “There was an issue with playback (Error Code IP-2).” You may see these error messages on different screens throughout the app, including the player, title detail pages, and your Library.

Playback error code

Playback error codes start with IP- or AP- and appear when there’s an issue playing your audiobook or podcast.

For a full list of playback error codes and troubleshooting steps, view the reference guide below.

  • Error Code: IP-1 / AP-1; Issue: Authentication issue; What to do: Try playing the title again—this usually resolves automatically. If it continues, sign out and back in to your account. For third-party podcasts, check back later if the issue persists.
  • Error Code: IP-2 / AP-2; Issue: No network connection; What to do: Connect to a stronger network when attempting to stream content. Verify your device is not in Airplane mode and has an active internet connection.
  • Error Code: IP-3 / AP-3; Issue: Title not found; What to do: If this happens with an Audible title, try playing again; For third-party podcasts, the content may be temporarily unavailable.
  • Error Code: IP-4 / AP-4; Issue: Streaming restricted; What to do: Your settings are blocking streaming without Wi-Fi. Connect your device to Wi-Fi or change your settings to allow streaming over cellular data.
  • Error Code: IP-5 / AP-5; Issue: Audio interference; What to do: End any phone calls or close other apps using audio then try playing again.
  • Error Code: IP-6 / AP-6; Issue: Media services reset; What to do: Try playing the title again—this usually resolves automatically.
  • Error Code: IP-7 / AP-7; Issue: Authorization Failure; What to do: Try playing the title again—this usually resolves automatically.
  • Error Code: IP-8 / 8P-8; Issue: Content Not Eligible; What to do: Confirm the title is in your Library and that your account has access.
  • Error Code: IP-9 / AP-9; Issue: Requester Not available; What to do: Verify your membership is active in your account settings.
  • Error Code: IP-10 / AP-10; Issue: Not available in your region; What to do: This title isn’t available in your current location, check which marketplace you’re using.
  • Error Code: IP-11 / AP-11; Issue: Temporary playback issue; What to do: Wait for 24 hrs and try again.
  • Error Code: IP-12 / AP-12; Issue: Device not compatible; What to do: The AirPlay device doesn’t support this audio format. Try playing on your phone or another compatible device.
  • Error Code: IP-13 / AP-13; Issue: No recap available; What to do: Recap content isn’t available for your current position in the title.  This is normal at the beginning of a book.
  • Error Code: IP-14 / AP-14; Issue: Recap not available for your account; What to do: The recap feature may not be available with your current membership plan.
  • Error Code: IP-15 / AP-15; Issue: Recap not available for this title; What to do: This title doesn’t support the recap feature, not all titles have recap content available.
  • Error Code: IP-4562/AP-4562; Issue: Decoding issue; What to do: Downloading the title in a different audio quality or streaming the title instead of downloading it.

Download error codes

Download error codes start with ID- or AD- and appear when there’s an issue downloading your audiobook or podcast.  For a full list of download error codes and troubleshooting steps, download the reference file below.

  • Error Code: ID-0 / AD-0; Issue: Download was cancelled; What to do: If you didn’t cancel, try downloading again.
  • Error Code: ID-1 / AD-1; Issue: Server error; What to do: Try downloading again.
  • Error Code: ID-2 / AD-2; Issue: Download link issue; What to do: Try downloading again.
  • Error Code: ID-3 / AD-3; Issue: File location issue; What to do: Try downloading again. If issue continues, delete the title and download it again.
  • Error Code: ID-4 / AD-4; Issue: Download link expired; What to do: The download will retry automatically. If it fails, try downloading again.
  • Error Code: ID-16 / AD-16; Issue: Not enough storage; What to do: Free up space on your device. Remove unused apps, photos, or downloaded titles.

If you’re still experiencing issues after trying these troubleshooting steps, please contact us for additional help.
