This guide gathers Shipt’s official help for members — the people ordering groceries and other goods for delivery. It was taken from Shipt’s own help site on 29 July 2026.

Shipt runs as a website and a phone app; there is no Windows program and no Alexa skill. Help for shoppers, the people who do the shopping and driving, is a separate audience and is not included here.

The sections below are Shipt’s own categories. Shipt publishes each category as a single page holding all of its questions and answers, so this guide is built from those pages: each question below is one of Shipt’s own, with its own answer beneath it. Where the same question appears in more than one category, it is kept once.

Shipt repeats its telephone number and chat invitation at the foot of most answers. Those have been left where Shipt put them, because the number is often the actual answer to the question asked.

Can I order alcohol?

We offer alcohol delivery in select cities from a variety of retailers. If you are in the coverage area for this service, beer, wine, and spirits may be available for delivery. A $7 delivery fee may be applied to alcohol orders from select retailers.

Please note that you must be 21 or older with a valid ID to place or receive an alcohol order. Due to state and federal regulations, our shoppers are legally required to confirm the recipient of an order with alcohol is 21 or older by reviewing a valid ID at delivery, regardless of the recipient’s apparent age. The recipient must also sign for the delivery on the shopper’s phone.

Valid Forms of ID:

  • Driver’s license or identification card* from any US state or Puerto Rico.
  • Temporary driver’s license or identification card with a photo and scannable barcode.
  • US passports**
  • US military IDs**

Shipt will not be able to honor a request for alcohol on a “dry” order (an order that does not already include alcohol). If you’d like to have alcohol delivered after you’ve already placed a dry order, please place a new order for the alcohol.

For a complete explanation of our alcohol policy, please see our terms of service.***

*In North Carolina, out-of-state identification cards cannot be accepted as a form of ID.

**US passports and US military IDs cannot be scanned with the Shipt app, so they will need to be manually verified before completion of the order.

***Shipt and shoppers with Shipt are required to follow each state’s specific laws and regulations for alcohol delivery, which may include order limits. Alcohol delivery is prohibited to college campuses (including dormitories and other on-campus housing), military bases, correctional facilities, high schools, middle schools, elementary schools, daycare centers, places of worship, and ABC licensed premises. If you live in a zip code within proximity to a prohibited area, alcohol delivery may be restricted. If you have questions or need assistance with an order containing alcohol, you can contact Shipt Support through the Live Chat feature at the bottom of this screen or by calling (205) 502-2500.

Can I choose my shopper?

Because shoppers have the flexibility to choose when and where they shop with Shipt, you are unable to select a specific shopper to complete your order.

However, if you have a stellar experience with a shopper, rating them 5 stars in the app will give you the option to add them as a preferred shopper! This will increase the likelihood of them receiving your orders in the future. You can also go back to any past 5-star order and add that shopper to your preferred shopper list.

You can manage your list of preferred shoppers at any time via the Account page on the Shipt app or website. Shoppers will receive a notification if you add them to your preferred shopper list, but they will not be notified if they are removed.

How do I rate my shopper?

After your order is delivered, you’ll be prompted to rate your shopper as well as your overall Shipt experience the next time you log into the Shipt app or website.

You can rate your shopper and experience on a one-to-five-star rating system. Based on the number of stars that you choose, there will be additional feedback options you can select such as “Good communication” or “Item issue.” Ratings can be edited via the Orders section in the app up to 7 days after they are submitted.

If you rate your shopper with 5 stars, you’ll have the option to add them as a preferred shopper, increasing the likelihood that they will claim your orders in the future. Shoppers rated 2 stars or below will not receive offers for your orders in the future.

What are the requirements to become a shopper?

  • At least 18 years old
  • Valid mailing address
  • Valid U.S. driver’s license, Social Security Number, and auto insurance
  • A reliable, operable vehicle
  • Ability to lift 50 pounds

Can I drive someone else’s car while shopping with Shipt?

You do not have to own the car you drive while shopping with Shipt. However, you do have to be insured on the policy that covers that car. If you are not an insured driver on the policy for a car, you cannot use that car while working with Shipt.

I can’t complete the sign-up process to become a shopper

  • We are sorry about that! If you are unable to complete the sign-up process, you first need to double-check that every required field has been filled out.

If you are certain every field has been filled out, then one of two issues may be occurring.

1) Phone Number - This field accepts only numeric values and will not accept any dashes, dots, or slashes. Please check to make sure you are entering your phone number in the following format: ##########

Ordering

How do I add things to my cart?

Easy! Find the item you want delivered, and tap the plus sign to add it to your cart. You can also click on the item to see a more detailed description, and then select the plus sign to add it to your cart.

When an item is in your cart, you will see a number in the bottom right-hand corner of the item image and on the cart icon in the top right-hand corner of the Shipt app or website.

Is there a minimum purchase amount for orders?

Nope, there is no minimum for orders! You can shop as often as you want, and delivery is free for all orders over $35.*
*Note: Shipt membership fees and one-time delivery fees vary by market. Deliveries under $35 with a Shipt membership will incur a $7 fee. All orders with alcohol (where available) may incur a $7 alcohol fee.

Is there a maximum number of deliveries allowed in a day?

Nope! You can order as much as you want!

What if I can’t find something I want in the app?

No worries! If you don’t see an item in the app or have something unusual for us to pick up for you, use the Special Request option. Be as detailed as possible with brand, size, flavor, and other descriptors that will help your shopper identify the exact item you want.

Please note, any special requests will display a $6 price as a placeholder. The actual price of the item will be reflected in the digital receipt you are emailed upon delivery.

Shoppers with Shipt are happy to deliver special items for you whenever possible. Please note that some items are restricted from delivery and cannot be fulfilled as a special request. You can find a list of restricted items here.

What items are you restricted from delivering?

Due to legal restrictions, there are several items we cannot deliver at this time including but not limited to:

  • Tobacco products
  • Cannabis, CBD, and/or hemp-derived products including but not limited to oils, tinctures, and topical products
  • Any over-the-counter medication that contains pseudoephedrine
  • Lottery tickets
  • Movie tickets
  • Toll passes
  • Large pieces of furniture
  • Propane tanks over 20 pounds
  • Guns and ammunition
  • Dry ice
  • Fireworks and sparklers
  • Gift cards
  • Items from in-store vendors such as Starbucks or Target Café
  • Live animals such as fish, birds, or hamsters
    • Live insects such as crickets and mealworms from PetSmart can be delivered
  • Prescription medications
  • Certain heavy items*
  • Alcohol, including non-alcoholic beer and wine **
  • Energy drinks***

*Certain products may have quantity limits due to their weight

  • **Alcohol delivery is only restricted in select markets.*

***Shoppers will not be able to deliver energy drinks with Shipt in the Palmdale/Lancaster CA metro only because the law puts an age restriction on energy drink delivery, and Shipt’s platform cannot support ID verification for this product at this time.

Can a shopper pick up my prescription medication?

I am having problems with the app. How do I fix it?

1. Verify that your phone has been updated to the latest version of its operating system.

2. Verify that your Shipt app has been updated to the latest version.

3. If both your phone’s operating system and your Shipt app are up to date, then try closing and reopening the app.

  • iPhone: On an iPhone X or later, an iPad with iOS 12 or later, or iPadOS, from the Home screen, swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause slightly in the middle of the screen. On an iPhone 8 or earlier, double-click the Home button to show your most recently used apps
    • Swipe right or left to find the app that you want to close.
    • Swipe up on the app’s preview to close the app.
  • Android: Select the ‘Recent Apps’ icon or press and hold the ‘Home’ button until the Recent Apps list appears. Swipe the open app towards the right of the phone. To exit this feature, by selecting the ‘Recent Apps’ icon again.

4. Try to open your Shipt app again by clicking on the Shipt icon. If the app still does not work, then try turning off the phone completely and turning it back on.

  • To power off an iPhone: Press and hold the Power and Volume Down buttons until the option ‘slide to power off’ appears. Slide the power button to the right and wait while the phone turns off. Then turn the phone back on by holding the Power button.

  • To soft reset an iPhone: Press and hold the ‘Sleep/Wake’ button (the thin rectangle button) and the ‘Home’ button at the same time for at least 10 seconds until the Apple logo appears. Let go of both buttons. The phone will continue through the usual process of starting up.

  • To power off an Android: Press and hold the ‘Power’ button until the ‘Device Options’ appear. Select ‘Power Off’. The phone will shut down. Then turn the phone back on by holding the ‘Power’ button.

5. Try to open your Shipt app again. If the app still does not work, then try reinstalling the app.

  • iPhone: Select and hold the Shipt app until a small ‘x’ appears on the upper left corner. Select the ‘x’. When the confirmation window appears, select ‘Delete’. The app will disappear from the Home Screen. Select the ‘Home’ button to exit from the feature. Then go to the app store to select the Shipt app to install it again.
    • https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shipt-same-day-delivery/id971888874 - Click on this link to access the Shipt app in the Apple App Store. Click on “Get” to download the app. If your app is out of date, you will need to click on the blue Update button to ensure you are using the latest version.
  • Android:Open the ‘Settings’ app. Select ‘Applications’. Select ‘Application Manager’. Select the Shipt app. Select ‘Uninstall’. When the confirmation window appears, select ‘OK’. The app will disappear. Then go to the Google Play store to select the Shipt app to install it again.
    • https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shipt.groceries&hl=en\_US -
      Click on this link to access the Shipt app in the Google Play Store. Click on “Install” to install the app. If your app is out of date, you will need to click on the green Update button to ensure you are using the latest version.

6. Once your Shipt app has been installed, try to open your Shipt app again. If the app still does not work, please contact us.

2-Factor Authentication

You can add enhanced security to your account by enabling 2-Factor Authentication. This requires a unique code to be sent to your email or SMS every time you log into your account.

To enable 2-Factor Authentication:

  • Navigate to your account page and select 2-Factor Authentication.
  • Select the method you would like to receive your verification code, and select Send code.
  • Enter your unique authentication code, and select Verify.
  • Once you have enabled 2-Factor Authentication, you will receive a new unique code to your email or SMS every time you log into your account.

Please note that if you haven’t used your Shipt account in over a year, we will automatically apply 2-Factor Authentication to your account in order to help protect your information. If you ever want to disable** 2-Factor Authentication, you can do so by following the same steps above and selecting Turn Off**.

Shipt provides several features to help you protect your personal information including 2-factor authentication (2FA), password requirements, and a masked number system to conceal your phone number when you communicate with shoppers.

I’m logged in, but I don’t see my order history. Did something change with my account?

If you’re logged in with the correct credentials, nothing should look different. If your order history or membership status seem off, that means you most likely logged in with the wrong email.

If you use an Apple device, check to make sure you didn’t use the private relay feature that generates an email address with @privaterelay.appleid.com when you registered with Shipt. If you registered with private relay, but logged in with your real, non-Apple email, you wouldn’t see the same account history. You can find more information about this here.

Regardless of how it happened, it’s a quick fix—just log out and then back in with the right email address.

I logged in with my Apple ID, but the email address on my account is xxxxx@privaterelay.appleid.com. Is something wrong?

You may have opted into the “Hide my email” setting when logging in with Apple, which generates a random @privaterelay.appleid.com email address.

While it might look strange, you should continue using your Apple login to avoid creating a second account.
