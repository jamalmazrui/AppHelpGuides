What is “sponsored content”?

Selected organisations sponsor New Scientist content in return for the display of their name and logo. New Scientist retains full editorial control, giving no sponsor any influence over any content, including choice of topics, the views expressed, or the style of presentation.

Where can I find out about New Scientist ’s circulation or readership?

New Scientist is a science magazine for everyone, both young & old, amateur & professional. For more details, please see our media information.

Data

Are my personal details secure?

We use your personal information provided to process your subscription order. New Scientist is committed to protecting your privacy and we abide by a strict Privacy Policy. You can be secure in the knowledge that your personal and payment information is always kept secure. Our subscription bureau is PCI compliant level 1 which is the same level as a bank.

How do I unsubscribe from emails or update my other contact preferences? (Data)

Log in to update your email preferences here. If you’ve forgotten your password, you can reset it here. Once you’re logged in, click on “Account” then “Contact preferences”. Here you can check or uncheck the relevant boxes. Once you’ve made your selections, click the blue ‘Update’ button at the bottom. Please note that these changes can take up to 24 hours to take effect.

You can also unsubscribe from individual emails by clicking on the “Unsubscribe” link at the bottom of each email. Please note this will only unsubscribe you from that specific mailing list. For example, if you click to unsubscribe from the health newsletter, but also receive the daily newsletter, you will be unsubscribed from the health emails but continue to receive the daily.

Delivery

Do you use plastic to send subscription copies of New Scientist?

  • Print magazines to the UK are sent in home compostable film that complies to BSEN13432. You are therefore able to put this into your compost bin, use as a food waste caddy liner or dispose of a in a green waste collection bin. From time to time UK copies are wrapped in Polyair™. This film is made from 100% recyclable, carbon neutral material. It is PAS2050 carbon neutral certified and can be recycled
  • Print magazines to Australia and New Zealand are wrapped in a bioWrap plastic wrap which is recyclable. We recommend you check whether your local council collects plastic wrap with its recycling but if not, the wrap can be recycled with plastic bags at larger supermarkets
  • Print magazines to US and Canada are sent without any additional packaging
  • Print magazines to the rest of the world are sent in paper wrap We encourage all our customers to reuse, compost, or recycle packing materials where possible. We regularly review the options to ensure we balance sustainability with cost.

How long will delivery take of my subscription copy?

  • UK: 2-3 days (Delivery should be Thursday-Saturday)
  • Europe: 4-8 days
  • USA/Canada: 2-3 days
  • Central & South America: 3-4 weeks
  • Africa & Asia: 3-4 weeks
  • Pacific Rim (Australian edition): 1-2 weeks
  • Middle East: 2-3 weeks

How often is New Scientist published?

New Scientist is published weekly. There are 51 issues per year with a double issue during Christmas. New Scientist is on sale Thursday of each week with a cover date of the following Saturday.

My issue has not arrived, what do I do?

We are sorry to hear that your issue did not arrive. We would be happy to send a replacement. You can order a replacement in my account under missing issue, or contact our customer service team.

My magazine has arrived damaged, what do I do?

We’re sorry to hear that your issue arrived damaged. We would be happy to send a replacement. You can order a replacement in my account under missing issue, or contact our customer service team.

When will my first issue arrive?

If you do not select a future date your first magazine will be the next issue available. We are unable to start a subscription for a previous issue or the latest copy on sale in shops. You can view your first or next issue in my account. We will confirm your start issue in your welcome email or letter.

Delivery time depends on the country your subscription is being sent to please see below.

While you wait for your first issue, you have immediate digital access at newscientist.com in the New Scientist app.

Digital Access: New Scientist app

Are the digital issues available after my subscription ends?

If you downloaded issues they will still be available once your subscription ends. However, if change your device or there is an app upgrade which removes these issues, they will no longer be available and you won’t be able to redownload them without an active subscription.

Can I access the issues on more than one device?

You can download a digital subscription on up to 5 devices. The access is unlimited as long as the subscription is active. Once the subscription has been cancelled, the digital access is also cancelled.

Can I change the font size and brightness of the articles?

Within ‘Settings’ you’ll find a tools section where you can change font size and enable dark mode. You can also ‘edit timelines’ which will change what article categories you want to see.

Can I keep copies of New Scientist on my tablet or mobile?

Yes. When using our new app, downloaded issues of New Scientist will be stored on your device. Downloaded issues will still be available once your subscription ends. Any downloaded issues are tied to the device, and cannot be transferred to another device.

Can I purchase a digital issue if I am not a subscriber?

Yes, you can purchase individual issues of New Scientist in the app and you can purchase digital copies of our Essential Guides and How to Think About series.

Downloading

Can I read New Scientist when I am not connected to the internet?

In the first instance, you will need a connection to download new issues onto your app. Once you have downloaded an issue, it will then be available for you to read. You can also listen offline by adding articles to your playlist.

Can I read on an Amazon fire device?

Our app is only supported on Amazon Fire devices if downloaded from the Amazon Appstore. Our app is not registered through Amazon Store.

The Google Play Store is not officially supported on Fire devices, so we do not support our app installed through other sources.

Can I save articles to read later?

You can bookmark articles to read later via the bookmark icon in the top right of an article. You can find all your bookmarked articles under the ‘Bookmark’ tab on the bottom ribbon of your screen.

Can I share articles? (Digital Access: New Scientist app)

You can share articles using the share function on each article, but if you share with a non-subscriber they may not be able to read the article.

Do I get access to back issues with my digital subscription?

Issues date back to January 2021 on the New Scientist app.

Do I need a New Scientist subscription to access the app?

Anyone can download and access the New Scientist app. You will require a subscription for unlimited access to all content. Subscriptions can be purchased directly through your app store or via newscientist.com.

How can I access the audio feature?

Open an article displaying the audio icon and click ‘Listen to this article’. This will add the article to your queue.

To listen to the whole issue, under Magazine select ‘Listen to this issue’ This will add the issue to your queue.

How can I close the audio feature?

When you are finished listening, slide left on the audio ribbon at the bottom and click on the X.

How can I display the audio queue?

When the audio ribbon is at the bottom of your screen, click on the title of the article. You can then manage the audio queue, including deleting and reordering items.

How can I switch on dark mode?

Dark mode can be switched on and off under settings. Alternatively, you can set the app to match the dark mode settings on your phone.

How do I cancel my subscription? (Digital Access: New Scientist app)

In the event you wish to cancel your subscription, and your subscription was purchased directly with New Scientist, please log into your account at newscientist.com/my-account, click “Need Help” and then “Cancellation Request”.

Alternatively, contact our customer service team.

If your subscription was purchased directly through the app store, navigate to Settings on your phone or tablet, go to “Subscriptions”. Select the subscription you wish to cancel and tap “Cancel Subscription”.

How do I delete issues from my device?

Issues which you have downloaded will be shown with a tick next to the date. You should click on the tick and then click on the bin icon that appears. To delete all issues at once, go to settings and select ‘Delete all downloaded editions’.

How do I download issues?

If you are a subscriber with app access, make sure you are logged in to the app first. Then, to download an issue, click on the ‘Magazine’ tab on the bottom ribbon of your screen. Then, click the cloud icon underneath the cover image and wait for the download to start. It will have a tick icon when it has been successfully downloaded.

You can also read a PDF version of the magazine by clicking on the magazine cover, then selecting ‘Read as PDF’.

If you are not a subscriber, you can buy single issues by selecting the cover and following the prompts.

How do I find articles I want to read?

When you open an issue from the issue library, you’ll see a table of contents. Tap on a section heading (e.g. News, Features, Views) to browse the articles in that section, or tap on a headline to go straight to the article.

How do I install the New Scientist app?

Visit our app page for more information.

How do I log in to the app?

To log in, tap the ‘Account’ icon in your app, and then ‘Sign in’. Enter the email address associated with your New Scientist subscription and password to access your subscription. If you have forgotten your password you can reset your password here.

If you have purchased a subscription through the App Store, you don’t need to log in.

How do I search for a particular topic or article?

To locate the search function, go to your account within the app. Under tools, you’ll see ‘Search’, click on this and type the word or phrase you’re searching for.

How do I share app logs

  • Go to account settings within the New Scientist app by clicking the icon on the top right-hand corner of the home screen
  • Scroll to the bottom of the settings page
  • Long press the version number until a dialog pops up with the app logs
  • Click the share button (top right-hand corner) Send to our customer service team.

How do you download articles to listen offline?

Curate a playlist using the queue and then click the download icon.

I am a subscriber, but when I log in, I don’t get access to all articles

Please check that you are logged in with the email address that you subscribed with. If the email is correct and you still don’t have full access, please contact us here.

I am having technical problems

If you are having technical difficulties, we suggest you try following these steps:

  • Close and reopen the app – sometimes this can resolve minor issues
  • Restart your device – this can help clear any system errors that may be affecting the app
  • Update the app – ensure you have the latest version by checking for updates in your app store
  • Reinstall the app If the above does not resolve your issue, please contact our customer service team with a copy of your app logs.

I have an app subscription can I access newscientist.com ?

Unfortunately, subscriptions to the New Scientist app purchased from the App Store, Google Play or Zinio do not include unlimited access to newscientist.com.

I have forgotten my password. How do I reset it?

To reset your password, please visit newscientist.com/lost-password. You should then be able to log in again with your new password.

I purchased a subscription through the New Scientist app. How can I cancel it?

Navigate to Settings on your phone or tablet, go to “Subscriptions”. Select the subscription you wish to cancel and tap “Cancel Subscription”.

I subscribe to the print edition. Can I use this app for free?

If you have a print only subscription, you will be unable to access this app for free. You can upgrade your subscription to include app access by contacting our customer service team.

If I delete an issue, can I download it again for free later?

Yes, you can archive an issue and re-download it at any time whilst your subscription remains active. You can also restore any purchases.

Editorial

Can I send in an article for publication in New Scientist ?

The vast majority of the news section of New Scientist magazine is written by staffers but we do publish the occasional freelance piece. Most of our feature articles are written either by researchers (who usually give first hand accounts of work in which they are involved) or by science journalists, but we are open to anyone who can write in our style on topics which we find exciting. We don’t encourage people to send in full-length material out of the blue because we will often not have time to read it. If you have a good idea for a feature article, you should send a short synopsis – no more than 400 words – by email to the features editor. If you are a freelance journalist interested in pitching a news story, please check our Guide for Freelancers for further information. And everyone is welcome to send letters for publication, though you must include a postal address for your letter to be published.

Can I use one of your pictures or contact one of your artists?

Most of the photographs we use come from photo libraries – check the credit next to the picture for the name of the company. The copyright on these pictures does not belong to New Scientist, and we can’t give permission to use them. If you can’t find a number for the photo library in question, you can email the picture desk for contact information. New Scientist does commission a lot of original artwork, but the copyright always belongs to the artist who did the work. If you’re interested in one of these pieces or in the artist who created it, you should contact the art department by email and we’ll pass your request along to the artist.

How can I contact the author of an article?

You can email the section that the article appeared in for further information (Features, News, Letters, Feedback or The Last Word).

More details of how to contact us can be found here.

How can I get in touch with an expert on my illness?

The staff at New Scientist magazine are not medical experts, and as such we are not equipped to advise on medical matters. If you have questions about your illness, please discuss them with your doctor.

How can I get in touch with suppliers about products I’d like to buy?

If an article mentions a product, the article itself will usually contain all the necessary information to contact the research team or company developing the product. It is unlikely that we have any further details.

How can I get my letter published in the magazine’s Letters section? What are the terms and conditions?

Letters to the Editor are for publication. Please include your postal address, telephone number, and an article reference (issue, page number, title).

New Scientist reserves the right to edit letters and use any submissions sent to the letters column in any other format. If you want to send a private message about the content of an article, you can find the appropriate contact details here. Due to the volume of correspondence we receive, we regret that we cannot guarantee a reply.

How do I get permission to reuse New Scientist material?

If you are interested in re-using New Scientist material you should read the syndication page for information on permissible use of content and to ensure you will not be violating copyright.

If you subscribed with a one-off payment, we will send you a renewal notification by post or email approximately 10 weeks before your subscription is due to end, to remind you to renew. Your subscription will start from the next available issue, or you can select a future date when you subscribe. We are unable to start a subscription for a previous issue or the latest copy on sale in shops. You can view your first or next issue in my account.

Will you refund my subscription if I cancel my subscription?

New Scientist will offer you a refund if you cancel during your cooling off period, within 14 days of a payment being taken. We will refund unsent issues.

How do I renew a gift subscription?

If you subscribed with a one-off payment, we will send you a renewal notification by post or email approximately 10 weeks before your subscription is due to end, to remind you to renew. How does a gift subscription work?

You can purchase a subscription as a gift, this will enable the recipient to receive the print issue delivered to their home, plus access the digital subscription, depending on the package you choose.

How does the recipient access their digital subscription?

We will require an email address for the recipient which we request at the point of ordering. If you do not know what this is, the recipient can provide this later at newscientist.com/update-email. You will receive instructions in your confirmation email and you will need to forward this to your gift subscription recipient in order for them to set up their digital subscription.

I bought a gift subscription for Christmas, when will the first issue arrive?

As we don’t want to spoil the surprise for the recipient, all gift orders placed between October & December are automatically enrolled to start in the New Year. You will receive a gift card to gift the recipient on Christmas Day. You can change this during the checkout process by changing your start date.

If I’m giving a gift subscription, will the recipient be notified?

All communication will be sent to you to pass on if you wish. Please note, in order for your recipient to access any digital content, we will require an email address for them which we request at the point of ordering. If you do not know what this is, the recipient can provide this later at newscientist.com/update-email. You will receive instructions in your confirmation email and you will need to forward this to your gift subscription recipient in order for them to set up their digital subscription.

My free subscription gift has not arrived, what do I do?
