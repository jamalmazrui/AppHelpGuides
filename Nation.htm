HOW CAN I CHANGE OR STOP BY MONTHLY DONATION?

HOW CAN I CHANGE OR STOP BY MONTHLY DONATION?

You can update or cancel your donation through our secure recurring gift portal or by calling us at 212-209-5400.

HOW CAN I SET UP A MONTHLY DONATION?

HOW CAN I SET UP A MONTHLY DONATION?

Monthly donations help us save resources and are a convenient way for donors to make a sustained, regular impact. To set up a monthly gift using your credit card or bank account, click here. Alternatively, you can send a voided check to our office or set up your gift over the phone by contacting us at 1-888-749-6171.

I RECEIVED A PHONE CALL OR SOLICITATION ON BEHALF OF THE NATION. HOW CAN I MAKE SURE IT IS NOT A SCAM?

I RECEIVED A PHONE CALL OR SOLICITATION ON BEHALF OF THE NATION. HOW CAN I MAKE SURE IT IS NOT A SCAM?

First, read through through our guide on how to spot fraud. We conduct tele-fundraising campaigns throughout the year. These campaigns are managed by Public Interest Communications (PIC), a prominent and reputable fundraising firm that has offices in Pittsburgh and Salt Lake City. If you are not sure if a phone call or solicitation came from The Nation, please contact us at [email protected].

IS MY GIFT TO THE NATION TAX-DEDUCTIBLE?

IS MY GIFT TO THE NATION TAX-DEDUCTIBLE?

The Nation is not a non-profit organization, so most donations aren’t tax-deductible. Donors interested in making gifts of $5,000 or above can contact us for information about making a tax-deductible gift through our fiscal sponsor.

WHY SHOULD I DONATE WHEN I ALREADY SUBSCRIBE TO THE NATION?

WHY SHOULD I DONATE WHEN I ALREADY SUBSCRIBE TO THE NATION?

Subscriptions cover less than 50% of our costs. We rely on generous donations from our readers to help make up the difference.

WILL I RECEIVE A SUBSCRIPTION TO THE NATION WITH MY DONATION?

WILL I RECEIVE A SUBSCRIPTION TO THE NATION WITH MY DONATION?

For donors who give more than $500 in a calendar year, we offer a complimentary one-year print and digital subscription as a thank you.

How many articles at TheNation.com can I read for free?

How many articles at TheNation.com can I read for free?

We believe that good journalism is worth paying for. It’s true for the content that we pay to read, and it’s true for the content that we produce. In general, we offer three (3) free articles a month. This means that on the TheNation.com, you can read three free articles within a 30-day period. However, we’re always testing the best ways to offer readers our journalism, whether it’s a digital day pass or other types of access. This data allows us to adopt a strategy that supports the financial well-being of The Nation, in turn allowing us to produce even more of the award-winning journalism that our readers have come to expect. As always, thank you for your support!
