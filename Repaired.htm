Nothing else was touched. Dropbox’s two “version history” articles stayed, because those are about a feature of Dropbox and are exactly what a reader wants; so did Alexa’s announcements, LinkedIn’s and Instagram’s “recent changes” articles, and Slack’s announcement-channel help.

Two things I did not do

  • Thirty guides still carry a [table] placeholder where a table was dropped when the guide was built. The content is gone from the file; only a fresh harvest of those products brings it back. Repairing it here would mean inventing content, so I left it.

The rule that such a category folds into Miscellaneous has always been applied when a guide is built and never retroactively. Applying it now would move a few hundred articles between headings across the collection. It is cosmetic and it is your call, so I did not do it unasked.
